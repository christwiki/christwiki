The letter was written at Laodicea on the Syrian coast two months after the capture of [[place:jerusalem]], as the army broke up. It runs through the campaign from Nicaea to the battle of Ascalon and asks the faithful in the West to pray, give thanks and help the returning crusaders.

It is the leaders' own account, meant to be read out in the churches of the West, and it presents the army's successes as God's doing. It names [[person:godfrey-of-bouillon]] as "defender" of the Holy Sepulchre, and it states in one sentence what was done with the people found in Jerusalem.
