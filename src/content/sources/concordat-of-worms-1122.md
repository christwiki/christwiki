The agreement consists of two documents, one issued by each side: a privilege of Pope [[person:callixtus-ii]] for the emperor and an edict of [[person:henry-v-emperor]] for the church. The emperor's charter names the princes by whose counsel it was made. The name "concordat" is later; the texts speak of peace.

The Avalon Project page heads the emperor's charter "Edict of the Emperor Henry IV". That is a slip: the emperor of 1122 was Henry V, who in the text itself speaks of the time "of my father".
