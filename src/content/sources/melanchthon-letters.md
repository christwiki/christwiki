[[person:philip-melanchthon]] wrote to [[person:martin-luther]] and to others from Augsburg during the diet of 1530. The second volume of the Corpus Reformatorum prints these letters in Latin, together with the reports that the envoys of Nuremberg sent home in German.

They are cited here for dates and for what Melanchthon reported at the time. The text is in Latin and is paraphrased.
