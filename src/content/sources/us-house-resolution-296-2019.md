The resolution was agreed to in the House on 29 October 2019. Its preamble speaks of a campaign of genocide against Armenians, Greeks, Assyrians, Chaldeans, Syriacs, Arameans, Maronites and other Christians, and recalls the reports of Ambassador [[person:henry-morgenthau]]. It is a political act of 2019 and is cited in [[event:armenian-genocide]] only as evidence of how a state describes the events.
