The rescript is issued in the names of Honorius and Theodosius II and addressed to Palladius, praetorian prefect. It says that word has reached the emperors of a new teaching whose authors are said to be [[person:pelagius]] and Caelestius, describes that teaching in hostile terms, and orders that the two be driven from the city of Rome first and that anyone found holding or spreading their views be brought before a judge and deported. Its date line reads the day before the Kalends of May, at Ravenna, in the twelfth consulship of Honorius and the eighth of Theodosius, which is 30 April 418; the editors note that one manuscript writes the day as "II kal."

It survives in a Latin collection of canons and papal letters, and the editors say that this collection alone preserves it. There it follows the canons of the council of Carthage of 418 and is followed by the prefects' edict that published it. The collection's heading calls it a rescript "given after the acts of the above-written synod were received". That heading is the compiler's, and the date in the rescript is one day earlier than the date the canons carry.
