The document records a meeting held on 1 September. It gives [[person:cyprian]]'s opening words and then the opinion of each of the eighty-seven bishops by name, ending with his own. All agree that baptism given outside the church is no baptism. The text carries no year. It belongs to the dispute with [[person:stephen-i]] of Rome, who was dead by August 258, when Cyprian reported the execution of his successor Xystus ([[source:cyprian-letters]] 81.1 [80.1]), and Cyprian himself was in exile from 30 August 257 ([[source:proconsular-acts-of-cyprian]]); the latest 1 September that fits is that of 256, the year usually given.

Cyprian's opening speech does not name Stephen. It says that no one present sets himself up as "a bishop of bishops" or forces his colleagues to obey, since each bishop answers to Christ for his own decisions.
