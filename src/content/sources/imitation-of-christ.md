The work is made up of four short books: admonitions for the spiritual life, on the inner life, on inward consolation and on the sacrament of the altar. The text names no author. Its opening chapters set humility and a good life above learning pursued for its own sake.

The oldest evidence for the author cited in this wiki is a manuscript of 1441 written by Thomas à Kempis himself; see [[source:imitation-of-christ-autograph-1441]] for what that manuscript does and does not show. The book is cited here as a document of the circle described in [[event:devotio-moderna]], without deciding who composed it.
