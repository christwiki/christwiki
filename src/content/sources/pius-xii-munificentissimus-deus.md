The constitution has forty-eight numbered paragraphs. It recalls the petitions sent to Rome, the pope's question to all the bishops in 1946 and their almost unanimous answer. It then reviews the witness of the liturgy, of Fathers such as John of Damascus and of later theologians, and says that all of this rests finally on Scripture, which shows Mary as most closely joined to her Son. The definition follows in paragraph 44, and paragraph 45 declares that anyone who wilfully denies or doubts it has fallen away from the Catholic faith.

It is the Catholic Church's own statement of the dogma and of its reasons. See [[event:dogma-of-the-assumption]].
