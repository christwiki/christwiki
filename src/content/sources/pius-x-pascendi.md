The letter is addressed to the bishops of the Catholic Church. Its first and longest part sets out, as a single system, the views on philosophy, faith, dogma, Scripture and church history that the pope attributes to "the Modernists"; the second gives what he takes to be their causes; the third orders remedies, among them the censorship of books and a council of vigilance in each diocese.

It is the pope's account of the views he condemns. It is cited in [[event:the-fundamentals]] as the Catholic Church's official judgment on the movement at the time.
