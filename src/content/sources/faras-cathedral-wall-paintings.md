Faras, the ancient Pachoras, was excavated by a Polish team during the international campaign to rescue the monuments of Nubia before the Aswan High Dam flooded the valley. The museum that now holds the paintings describes Faras as a large urban centre of the medieval kingdom of Nobadia, says that a first cathedral was built in the seventh century and that the cathedral the excavators found was of the eighth, and dates the paintings from the eighth to the fourteenth century.

The paintings are later than the events told in [[event:conversion-of-nubia]] and are not evidence for them. The museum's page is cited in this wiki for the place and its remains. Its statement that the rulers of Nobadia became Christian "around 548" is the museum's summary of the written sources.
