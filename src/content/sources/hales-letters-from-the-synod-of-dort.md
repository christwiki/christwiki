Hales signs himself the ambassador's chaplain and dates his letters from Dort, most of them in both the old and the new calendar. He describes each session soon after it took place: who spoke, what was proposed and how the president, whom he calls the praeses, handled the business.

He writes as an English observer who belonged to neither Dutch party. He was present for the sessions he describes, though the Latin speeches he gives are his summaries. His letters are cited in [[event:synod-of-dort]] for the appearance of [[person:simon-episcopius]] and the other cited Remonstrants on 6 December 1618 and for their dismissal by the president in January 1619.
