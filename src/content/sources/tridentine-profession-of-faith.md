Schaff prints the profession from the bulls of [[person:pius-iv]] *Iniunctum nobis* of 13 November 1564 and *In sacrosancta* of 9 December 1564. It opens with the Niceno-Constantinopolitan Creed and goes on, in the first person, to accept the apostolic and ecclesiastical traditions, Scripture in the sense held by the Church, the seven sacraments, the decrees of Trent on original sin and justification, the sacrifice of the Mass and transubstantiation, purgatory, the invocation of saints, images and indulgences, and it ends with a promise of obedience to the bishop of Rome.

It is a short official summary of the council's teaching, made within a year of its close, and it shows what the papacy thought essential. It adds nothing to the decrees themselves, which remain the source for what Trent taught.
