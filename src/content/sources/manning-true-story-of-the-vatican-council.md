Manning took part in the council and wrote this book seven years later against the accounts of it that had circulated while it sat. He describes the opening on 8 December 1869 and the fourth session on 18 July 1870, and gives the figures of the vote in the last general congregation and of the final vote.

He writes as a partisan of the definition, and his judgments of the minority are those of an opponent. His figures for the votes are those of a member who was present.
