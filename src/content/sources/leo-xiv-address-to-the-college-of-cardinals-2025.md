[[person:leo-xiv]] spoke to the cardinals on 10 May 2025. He asks them to renew their commitment to the path the church has followed since the Second Vatican Council, lists points from Pope Francis's [[source:evangelii-gaudium]] that he wishes to stress, and then gives his chief reason for the name: Leo XIII's encyclical *Rerum novarum* on the social question in the first industrial revolution, and the challenge of a new one. It is his own explanation and is cited for that in [[event:leo-xiv-elected]].
