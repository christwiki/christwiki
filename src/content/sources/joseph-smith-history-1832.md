On its third page the manuscript says that Smith, calling on the Lord "in the 16th year of my age", saw a pillar of light, and continues: "I saw the Lord and he spake unto me saying Joseph my son thy sins are forgiven thee". The words giving his age are an insertion; the editors' note at that place says that they are in the hand of Frederick G. Williams.

The account names one figure, "the Lord", where the history begun in 1838 speaks of two Personages. The wiki reports both as [[person:joseph-smith]]'s accounts; see [[event:latter-day-saints]].
