The text is two paragraphs long. [[person:benedict-xvi]] read it to the cardinals gathered in consistory on 11 February 2013; it is dated the day before. It gives his reason, the decline of his strength with age, states that he acts "with full freedom", names the hour at which the see will fall vacant, and says that a conclave will have to be called.

It is the act of resignation itself and so the primary source for [[event:benedict-resigns-francis-elected]]. It is addressed to the cardinals as an announcement of a decision already made.
