The decree is signed by Lenin as chairman of the Council of People's Commissars and by eight commissars. Articles 1 to 8 separate church and state, guarantee to each citizen the profession of any religion or none, and transfer the registration of births and marriages to the civil authorities. Article 9 separates school and church. Articles 10 to 13 place religious societies under the rules for private associations, deny them the right to own property or to be legal persons, and declare everything they hold to be the property of the people.

It is the text of the law itself and is cited in [[event:russian-revolution-and-the-church]] for its provisions, not for how they were applied.
