The coin weighs 8.31 grams. One side shows a palm branch in a wreath with the Hebrew legend "Shimon Prince of Israel"; the other shows a lyre with the legend "Year 1 of the Redemption of Israel". The museum dates it to AD 132–133.

The rebel government struck its own money, and this is one of many surviving pieces. The coins are evidence, from the rebels themselves, of the title their leader used and of the era by which they counted the years of the war. See [[event:bar-kokhba-revolt]].
