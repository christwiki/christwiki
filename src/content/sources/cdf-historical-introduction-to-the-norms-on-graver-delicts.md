The congregation published this short history when the revised norms were issued in 2010. It says that [[person:john-paul-ii]] decided to include the sexual abuse of a minor under eighteen by a cleric among the offences reserved to the congregation, that the law was promulgated in the letter *Sacramentorum sanctitatis tutela* on 30 April 2001, and that Cardinal Joseph Ratzinger and Archbishop Tarcisio Bertone informed the bishops by a letter of 18 May 2001. It adds that [[person:benedict-xvi]] approved a revised text on 21 May 2010.

It is an official summary, written by the body whose competence it describes, and is cited for the dates and content of the law. The motu proprio itself, linked above, promulgates the norms without setting them out.
