Van Braght's book gathers the stories of those he calls the defenceless Christians, who baptised only on confession of faith, from the apostles to his own century. The title page says it was compiled from chronicles, memorials and testimonies. For the sixteenth century it reproduces older documents, among them a report of [[person:michael-sattler]]'s trial with the charges against him and the sentence.

It is a work of commemoration, published for Mennonite readers more than a century after the first Anabaptists died, and its dates are not always exact: it puts the death of [[person:felix-manz]] in 1526, where the sentence of the [[place:zurich]] court is dated January 1527. It is cited for what its documents say, with the date of compilation in mind.
