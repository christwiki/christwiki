The memoir appeared after [[person:henry-venn]]'s death; the edition used is that of 1882. Its value here lies in the documents it prints. Appendix C gives the papers he issued for the Society on "The Native Pastorate and Organisation of Native Churches", the first of them dated 1851, in which the aim of a mission is said to be a native church under native pastors on a self-supporting system.
