[[person:thomas-clarkson]] published the *History* in 1808, the year after the Act passed. He had been a member of the London committee from its first meeting in 1787, so that much of the book is the report of a participant. It prints documents in full, among them the Quaker petition of 1783, and it summarises the debates in both Houses year by year.

It is a partisan's book. Clarkson writes to honour the cause and those who served it, and he says little good of its opponents. For dates, votes and the texts he reproduces it is a primary source; for motives, his own and other people's, it is one man's testimony.
