The *Acta proconsularia* are short. They give the questions and answers at [[person:cyprian]]'s first hearing before the proconsul Aspasius Paternus on 30 August 257, which ended in his banishment to Curubis; then his recall under the next proconsul, Galerius Maximus, his second hearing, the sentence read from a tablet, and his beheading. The exchanges are set out in the form of official minutes, with each speaker named, and a Christian hand has added the linking narrative and the account of the burial.

The Oxford translators describe the text as the substance of the original record with later incidental additions. It is the source for the dates of Cyprian's exile and death, which it fixes by the consuls of each year. It should be read beside the [[source:pontius-life-of-cyprian]], which describes the same events from among the bishop's companions.
