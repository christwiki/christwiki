Frank Bartleman was in [[place:los-angeles]] before the revival began, met [[person:william-seymour]] at a cottage prayer meeting shortly before 26 March 1906, and went to the meetings at Azusa Street. The book is arranged by date, like a diary, and its dedication is signed at Los Angeles in April 1925.

He writes as a participant, and the book is his own view of events and of the people in them, set down nineteen years afterwards. It is cited in [[event:azusa-street-revival]] for what he says he saw.
