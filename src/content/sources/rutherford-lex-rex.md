The title page describes the book as a dispute for the just prerogative of king and people, containing "the reasons and causes of the most necessary defensive wars of the Kingdom of Scotland" and of the Scots' expedition to help their brethren in England, and as an answer to a royalist tract. It is dated London, 7 October 1644, when its author was a commissioner to the Assembly sitting at Westminster. The first questions ask whether government is by divine law and by the law of nature.

It is cited in the entry for [[person:samuel-rutherford]] as his own statement of his political teaching.
