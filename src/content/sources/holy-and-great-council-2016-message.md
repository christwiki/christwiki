The message is the council's own summary of its work, shorter and plainer than the [[source:holy-and-great-council-2016-encyclical]]. Its preamble dates the meeting to the week of Pentecost, 18 to 26 June 2016, and says that the council was convoked by the Ecumenical Patriarch [[person:bartholomew-i]] by the common will of the primates of the autocephalous churches. Point 1 says that the Orthodox churches are not a federation but one church, and records the proposal that the council become a regular institution meeting every seven or ten years.

The text speaks for the churches that took part. It does not mention that four of the fourteen autocephalous churches were absent; see [[event:holy-and-great-council]].
