The act is the legal record of England's reconciliation with Rome under Mary I. Its preamble says that the realm had "swerved from the obedience of the See Apostolic", that the pope had sent Cardinal [[person:reginald-pole]] as legate, and that the Lords and Commons had declared themselves repentant of the schism and had been absolved. The body of the act then repeals, one by one, the statutes passed against the papal supremacy since the twentieth year of Henry VIII.

It is a document of the side that carried out the restoration and uses that side's language throughout. A large part of it deals with property: it confirms the lay owners of former monastic lands in what they held. Elizabeth I's [[source:act-of-supremacy-1559]] repealed it.
