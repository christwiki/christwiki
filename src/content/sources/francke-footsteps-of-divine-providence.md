Francke writes in the first person, year by year, of the poor-box in his parsonage, the first school, the first orphans and the building of the orphan house, and he records gift after gift arriving when money had run out. The narrative is Francke's own, and its stated purpose is to show the providence of God in the work. See [[event:pietism]].
