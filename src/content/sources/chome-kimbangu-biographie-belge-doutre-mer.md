Jules Chomé was a Belgian lawyer and the author of *La passion de Simon Kimbangu* (Brussels, 1959), a book on the trial; the entry lists it among its sources. He wrote this article in 1966 for the dictionary of colonial biography published by the Belgian academy.

It is a secondary account written forty-five years after the trial by a man who held that Kimbangu was condemned for his religion. It is used in [[event:simon-kimbangu]] because it quotes the words of the judgment, which was not found in a public edition online, and for the course of the trial, the sentence and its commutation. Where it differs from the church's own account, both are given.
