The text is short: a list of six considerations and two resolutions, adopted unanimously according to the document (protocol no. 12 of 1 June 2016). The considerations are the absence from the agenda of topics the synod thought important, the disagreements other churches had declared about texts already approved, the impossibility under the rules of editing texts during the council, the seating of the primates, the placing of observers and guests, and the cost of taking part.

It is the Bulgarian church's own statement of its reasons for not attending the council; see [[event:holy-and-great-council]].
