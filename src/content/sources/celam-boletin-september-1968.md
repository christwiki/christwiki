The front page says that on Saturday 24 August, at 10.30 in the morning in the cathedral of Bogotá, Paul VI inaugurated the second general conference of the Latin American bishops, and that on Monday 26 August the conference began its work at Medellín, which lasted until 6 September. The same page reports that a few days after the conference ended the conclusions of its sixteen commissions and sub-commissions were revised for style and sent to the pope for his approval.

The bulletin is the organising body's own news sheet, printed within weeks of the meeting. It is cited here for dates only. For the conclusions themselves see [[source:medellin-documents-1968]].
