Robert of Courçon was a cardinal and papal legate; the statutes say that the pope had commanded him to see to the condition of the students of [[place:paris]]. They are addressed "to all the masters and scholars at Paris" and refer to "the university masters and scholars" as a body before which offenders must make amends.

They prescribe Aristotle's logic and forbid lectures on his Metaphysics and natural philosophy.
