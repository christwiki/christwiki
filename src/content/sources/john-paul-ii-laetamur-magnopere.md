The letter says that the text published with *Fidei Depositum* in 1992 was in French, and that the Latin "typical edition" now promulgated is the definitive one. It was prepared by a commission of several Vatican departments which the pope appointed in 1993 under Cardinal Joseph Ratzinger, and which examined the many changes suggested from around the world. Those that the pope approved were incorporated, and the letter says that the edition faithfully repeats the doctrinal content of the text of 1992. It is dated from Castel Gandolfo.
