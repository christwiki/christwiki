The work has an introduction, four parts and a conclusion: what Christ has given, how he lived and suffered, what the way into the kingdom is, and how Christ helps those who walk it. It is plain catechesis, built on the Gospel saying about denying oneself, taking up the cross and following Christ. The act of the Russian church that declared its author a saint says that he wrote it in the Aleut language while he was priest on Unalaska and that the Holy Synod allowed it to be printed in Aleut in 1840; the year 1833 usually given for it is not stated in the text read here.

It is cited in [[event:russian-mission-to-alaska]] as an example of what the mission taught, in the missionary's own words.
