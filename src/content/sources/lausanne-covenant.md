The covenant has an introduction, fifteen numbered sections and a conclusion, each section followed by a list of biblical references. It speaks in the first person plural for church members from more than 150 nations gathered at Lausanne. Section 2 affirms the inspiration, truthfulness and authority of the Old and New Testaments as the only written word of God, "without error in all that it affirms". Section 4 defines evangelism. Section 5 expresses penitence for having treated evangelism and social concern as mutually exclusive and affirms that both are part of Christian duty, while saying that social action is not evangelism and political liberation is not salvation.

The text was drafted by a committee chaired by [[person:john-stott]] and revised after comments from the participants (see [[source:stott-lausanne-covenant-exposition]]).
