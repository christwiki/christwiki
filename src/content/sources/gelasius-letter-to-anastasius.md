Gelasius wrote to Anastasius I in 494. The passage translated by Robinson is the part that later ages quoted. It distinguishes the "sacred authority of the priests" from the "royal power", says that the priests carry the heavier burden because they must answer to God for kings as well, and asks the emperor to obey in religion those whose laws he expects the clergy to obey in public affairs. It ends by claiming a special obedience for the bishop of the see set above all others.

The letter is Gelasius's own statement and gives one side of an argument with the Eastern court. It was much quoted in later debates about the powers of popes and emperors.
