The book by [[person:david-du-plessis]] includes a note on his career, as secretary and organiser of the Pentecostal World Conferences from 1949, and his story of meeting John Mackay at Princeton in 1951 and of going to the World Council of Churches' assembly at Evanston in 1954. It is his own testimony, written to encourage the movement he describes. See [[event:charismatic-renewal]].
