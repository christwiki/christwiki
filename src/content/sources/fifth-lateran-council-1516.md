The eleventh session met on 19 December 1516. Its bull abolishes the Pragmatic Sanction of Bourges, a French ordinance limiting papal rights. In doing so it states that subjection to the Roman pontiff is necessary for salvation for all Christ's faithful, cites *Unam sanctam* for this, and renews and approves that constitution, adding that this is without prejudice to the declaration of Clement V beginning *Meruit*.

Its wording speaks of "all Christ's faithful", where Boniface wrote "every human creature".
