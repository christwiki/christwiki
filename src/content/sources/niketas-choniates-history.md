Niketas Choniates wrote the history of the Byzantine emperors from 1118 down to the Latin conquest of 1204 and the years just after it. The passage cited in this wiki comes from his account of the short reign of Alexios V Doukas, whom the crusaders called Mourzuphles. Munro, who translated it, notes that the same chapters include the author's own experiences in the captured city.

The public-domain extract covers only the sack itself: the treatment of icons, relics and the eucharist, the stripping of Hagia Sophia and the suffering of the inhabitants. It is a lament as much as a report, written by one of the defeated, and its language about the Latins is as hostile as [[source:villehardouin-conquest-of-constantinople]] is satisfied. The letter of Pope Innocent III in [[source:innocent-iii-letters]] confirms the main charges from the Latin side.
