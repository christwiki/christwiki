The report was compiled by the council's staff and published under the name of its general secretary, [[person:willem-visser-t-hooft]], whose preface is dated Geneva, September 1948. The introduction sketches the council's origins as its founders saw them, in the missionary movement and the movements called Life and Work and Faith and Order.

It is the council's own record and gives its own view of itself. It is the primary source for what the assembly resolved, for the wording of the Basis and for who was present. See [[event:world-council-of-churches]].
