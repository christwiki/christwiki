Osiander reached [[place:marburg]] on the afternoon of Saturday 2 October 1529 and reports the morning's debate from what others told him and the rest from what he saw. He gives the arguments in order, the passages of Scripture each side used, and the private negotiations on the Monday from which the [[source:marburg-articles]] came.

He writes as one of [[person:martin-luther]]'s supporters to masters who shared his view, and his judgement of [[person:huldrych-zwingli]] is hostile throughout. Zwingli's own account, in a letter to a friend, is in [[source:zwingli-letters]].
