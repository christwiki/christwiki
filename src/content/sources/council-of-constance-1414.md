The decrees are cited by session. The council sat for forty-five sessions between November 1414 and April 1418. Its acts include the bull by which John XXIII convoked it, the decrees of the fifth session on the council's authority (Haec sancta), the condemnations of the teaching of [[person:john-wycliffe]] and of [[person:jan-hus]], the depositions of John XXIII and Benedict XIII and the acceptance of Gregory XII's resignation, the decree on regular councils (Frequens), and the acts of [[person:martin-v]] after his election.

The online page mixes three kinds of text: the decrees themselves, Tanner's short summaries of sessions he does not print, and comments added by the person who put the text online. Only the decrees and Tanner's summaries are used in this wiki, and a summary is called a summary where it is cited. See [[event:council-of-constance]], [[event:western-schism]] and [[event:wycliffe-bible]].
