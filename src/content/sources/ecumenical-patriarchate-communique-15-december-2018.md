The communiqué has three short paragraphs. It announces the completion of what it calls the Unifying Council, founding the new church, and the election of [[person:epiphanius-of-kyiv]]; reports that he telephoned the Ecumenical Patriarch; and says that [[person:bartholomew-i]] invited him to concelebrate at the Phanar on the feast of Theophany and to receive the tomos. It does not say who took part in the council. See [[event:autocephaly-of-ukraine]].
