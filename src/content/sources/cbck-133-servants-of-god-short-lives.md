The biographies were written by the church that is promoting the cause, and they present their subjects as martyrs. The page on Yi Byeok heads his life with the years 1754–1785, lists the manner of his death as illness "or poisoning", and says that he was thirty-one when he died. It says that he discussed Catholic doctrine with his teacher and fellow scholars in the winter of 1779, that after the arrests of 1785 his father shut him up at home and could not make him give up his faith, and that the pressure of his family brought him to his death. It names no documents for these statements.

It is a present-day account, not a record of the eighteenth century. It is cited in [[event:first-korean-baptism]] and on the page of [[person:yi-byeok]] beside [[source:dallet-histoire-de-l-eglise-de-coree]], whose years for the same events differ.
