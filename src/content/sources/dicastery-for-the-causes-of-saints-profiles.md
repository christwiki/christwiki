Each profile is the official summary of a life as established in the process of beatification, and is published with the Latin decrees of the cause. The pages are written to commend their subjects and are not contemporary documents. The wiki takes from them only plain facts that the process itself had to establish: dates of birth, ordination, appointment, arrest and death, and the dates of beatification.

Each citation links to the page of the person concerned.
