The Act tells Innocent's life from his birth in a Siberian village in 1797 to his death as metropolitan of Moscow in 1879, and quotes from his letters. It is a document of the church that honours him, written a century after his death, and it speaks of him and of the peoples he worked among in the language of praise. It is cited in [[event:russian-mission-to-alaska]] and in [[person:innocent-of-alaska]] for dates and offices and as the Orthodox Church's own statement about him.
