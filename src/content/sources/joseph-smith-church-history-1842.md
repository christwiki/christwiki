Smith says at the outset that he wrote the sketch at the request of John Wentworth, editor of the *Chicago Democrat*. It tells of his vision of "two glorious personages", the plates and the Book of Mormon, the organizing of the church on 6 April 1830, the expulsions from Missouri and the building of Nauvoo, and closes with thirteen statements, twelve of which begin "We believe", above his name. The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints prints those statements, with small changes of wording, as the Articles of Faith.

It is [[person:joseph-smith]]'s own summary, written twelve years after the church was organized. It places that event in the town of Manchester, New York, where his manuscript history has Fayette; see the dating note of [[event:latter-day-saints]].
