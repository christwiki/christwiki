The cardinals issued the declaration in August 1378, some four months after they had elected Bartholomew, archbishop of Bari, as [[person:urban-vi]] at [[place:rome]]. It says that armed Romans surrounded and entered the conclave, threatened to kill the electors unless they chose a Roman or an Italian, and so forced an election that was null.

It is the case for one side, written by men who had themselves written to announce Urban's election and coronation before they turned against him; [[person:catherine-of-siena]] and others answered that they had chosen him freely. The source book prints only the central part of a long text. See [[event:western-schism]].
