The report is written in the third person but from the legates' side throughout, and is usually taken to be the work of [[person:humbert-of-silva-candida]]. It gives the day and hour of the excommunication, the third hour on Saturday 16 July, and the legates' movements afterwards. It calls [[person:michael-cerularius]] a heresiarch, says that he falsified the bull in translation and stirred up a riot against the emperor, and ends with the emperor's anger at the patriarch. The Greek account of the same days is in [[source:synod-of-constantinople-1054-edict]], and the two should be read together.
