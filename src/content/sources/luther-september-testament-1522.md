The book is a folio of 222 leaves printed by Melchior Lotter at [[place:wittenberg]], with twenty-one woodcuts by Lucas Cranach in the Revelation. It is called the September Testament from the month of its appearance; a letter of [[person:martin-luther]] written about 20 September 1522 sends finished copies to the court, lacking only the preface to Romans.

The record linked here is the holding library's description of its own copy. It is cited for the book as an object: its title, printer, date and make-up. What Luther says about his translation and about the books of the New Testament is in his prefaces, which have their own record, [[source:luther-bible-prefaces]].
