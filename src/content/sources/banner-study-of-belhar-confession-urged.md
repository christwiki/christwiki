The item reports that the Christian Reformed Church's synod of 2007 urged its congregations to study the Belhar Confession. In passing it explains the name: Belhar is a suburb of Cape Town, where a general synod of the Dutch Reformed Mission Church met in 1982.

It is a reporter's text in a church magazine, written twenty-five years after that synod, and is not a statement of either church. The wiki found no document of the Dutch Reformed Mission Church itself that names the place. See [[place:cape-town]].
