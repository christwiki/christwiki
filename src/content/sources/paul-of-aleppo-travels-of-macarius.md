summary: The travel diary of Paul, archdeacon of Aleppo, who went with his father, Patriarch Macarius of Antioch, to Moscow in the 1650s. He was present while Patriarch Nikon changed the Russian rites and reports what Nikon and Macarius said and did.
---

Paul wrote in Arabic as a visitor from the Greek-speaking East, and he took the side of the reform: Russian customs that differed from those of Antioch seemed to him mistakes to be corrected. He records sermons and synods of [[person:nikon]] that he attended, and he also notes that some of the Russian clergy refused the changes in their hearts and were afraid to say so.
