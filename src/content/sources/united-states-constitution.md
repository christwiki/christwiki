The original Constitution mentions religion once. Article VI requires legislators and officers of the United States and of the states to be bound by oath or affirmation to support the Constitution, and adds that no religious test shall ever be required as a qualification for any office or public trust under the United States. The closing words date the signing to 17 September 1787. See [[event:religious-freedom-in-america]] and [[source:united-states-bill-of-rights]].
