The editors count eighty-two surviving letters and say that their preservation is owed for the most part to Peter of Mladoňovice, the secretary of John of Chlum. Many of those from Constance were written in prison. In them [[person:jan-hus]] speaks for himself, and he speaks as a prisoner who believes he has been wronged.

The editors' linking passages and notes belong to 1904 and are not cited here; only the letters and the declaration of 1 July 1415 are. See [[event:council-of-constance]].
