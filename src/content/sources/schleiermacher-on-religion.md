The five speeches are a defence, an account of the nature of religion, a speech on its cultivation, one on church and priesthood, and one on the religions. Schleiermacher revised the book twice, and the wording differs between editions: Oman's extracts from the first edition speak of religion as "intuition and feeling" and of "intuition of the Universe". The translation quoted in this wiki is of the third edition, so a quotation from the body of a speech gives the wording of 1821; the wording of 1799 is quoted only from Oman's appendix on the first edition.

It is cited in [[event:schleiermacher]] for his argument.
