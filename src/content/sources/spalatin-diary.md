Spalatin was with the elector [[person:john-of-saxony]] at the diet of [[place:speyer]] in the summer of 1526. On Saturday 11 August he wrote down what a man he calls Buschius had said at supper: that six thousand copies of the New Testament in English had been printed at [[place:worms]], translated by an Englishman who was staying there with two companions and who was skilled in seven languages.

The entry is hearsay at one remove, noted on the day, from a town some twenty miles from Worms. It names no one. It is the nearest thing to a contemporary report of the printing of the [[source:tyndale-new-testament-1526]].
