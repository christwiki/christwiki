The declaration is short and personal. [[person:charles-v]] recalls that his forebears, emperors, archdukes of Austria and dukes of Burgundy, were faithful sons of the Catholic Church, and says it is his duty to uphold what they and the councils established. He calls [[person:martin-luther]] a single monk who sets his own judgement against the faith held by all Christians for more than a thousand years, regrets having delayed so long, and resolves to hear him no more, while keeping the safe-conduct.

It states the emperor's own reasons, the day after the hearing, and is the plainest expression of his side of the case.
