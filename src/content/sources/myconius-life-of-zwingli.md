Myconius says that he put forward the name of [[person:huldrych-zwingli]] when a preacher was to be chosen for the Great Minster in [[place:zurich]]. He wrote this short life in Latin in 1532, the year after Zwingli's death in battle, as a letter to a friend. He was in Zurich on the day of the battle and describes the troops leaving and the news arriving that night.

It is the account of an eyewitness to much of what it tells and of a devoted friend, written to defend Zwingli's memory. Its dates are not all exact: it puts Zwingli's birth about 1487, which the editor corrects to 1 January 1484.
