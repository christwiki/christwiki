The piece is printed on pages 17 to 20 of *Widerstand und Ergebung*, the volume of Bonhoeffer's letters and papers from prison that Eberhard Bethge published in 1951. It argues that folly is a moral and social failing more than an intellectual one, and that it spreads under the impression of great power.

It is cited in the entry on [[person:dietrich-bonhoeffer]] as a text of his own from the war years. It says nothing about the events of his life.
