The first part of the volume, "Experience and Views", was first published in 1851, as the preface to the second edition says; the copyright notice of the collected volume is dated 1882. It begins with a short sketch of her conversion and of the Adventist meetings she attended at Portland, Maine, and then relates her visions one by one. A publisher's note on its first page gives her birth as 26 November 1827 at Gorham, Maine, and a note to her first vision says that it was first published in 1846.

The book records what [[person:ellen-white]] said she saw. The wiki reports the visions as her account, as it does with any vision. See [[event:great-disappointment]].
