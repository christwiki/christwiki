The Life was written in Slavonic in the circle of Constantine's brother [[person:methodius]] and their pupils and tells the story from their side. It reports the embassy of [[person:rastislav]], the making of the letters, the disputes with Latin clergy over worship in Slavonic, the reception at [[place:rome]] and Constantine's death, which it dates to 14 February in the year 6377 from creation, that is AD 869.

It survives only in Church Slavonic copies made long after it was composed, and it is a saint's life: the letters are revealed in answer to prayer and opponents are routed in debate. Its details can be checked against the papal letters in [[source:john-viii-letters]] and against [[source:life-of-methodius]].
