The speech was given at the Palazzo della Pigna in Rome when the messenger from the Vatican brought the *biglietto* announcing [[person:john-henry-newman]]'s elevation. It is cited in this wiki for the fact and date of his becoming a cardinal, and it is his own short summary of what he thought his life's work had been.
