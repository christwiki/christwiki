The council issued two sets of texts. The first, *Zelus fidei*, deals with the Holy Land and the crusade. The second is a collection of thirty-one constitutions that [[person:gregory-x]] promulgated on 1 November 1274; the editor's introduction notes that three of them were added after the council and that others, the conclave rule among them, were revised by the papal curia before publication.

Constitution 1 of the second set, which opens with the words *Fideli ac devota*, is the dogmatic statement on the procession of the Holy Spirit. Constitution 2, *Ubi periculum*, is the conclave law; it says expressly that it leaves in force what Alexander III had laid down for papal elections ([[event:third-lateran-council]]). The introduction is modern scholarship and is cited in this wiki only for the editor's account of the texts.
