The minutes report a presentation by Metropolitan Andria of Gori and Ateni, the church's representative in the council's secretariat, a discussion among the bishops and a vote. The difficulties listed include the broken communion between Antioch and Jerusalem, the refusals of Antioch and Bulgaria, the council's document on marriage, which the Georgian delegation had not signed, and the document on relations with other Christians, which the synod had judged on 25 May to contain serious errors.

It is the Georgian church's own record of why it did not attend the council; see [[event:holy-and-great-council]].
