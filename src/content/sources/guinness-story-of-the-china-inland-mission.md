The book was published by the mission, and [[person:hudson-taylor]] himself refers readers to it for the details of the years after 1865. It quotes at length from the letters of the *Lammermuir* party, with the writer, place and date of each given in a footnote, and these letters are the reason it is cited here.

It is the mission's own history, written to commend the work. The narrative that links the letters is hers, a generation after the events.
