The Council published its decisions in four parts in 1918 as a supplement to its Acts. The first determination, dated 4 November 1917 in the Julian calendar, declares that the highest authority in the Russian church belongs to a local council of bishops, clergy and laity, and restores the patriarchate. Later ones define the rights and duties of the patriarch, the synod and the supreme church council, the diocese and the parish, and respond to the measures of the Soviet government on marriage, schools and church property.

These are the church's own acts, adopted while the events described in [[event:russian-revolution-and-the-church]] were taking place. They are cited there for what the Council decided.
