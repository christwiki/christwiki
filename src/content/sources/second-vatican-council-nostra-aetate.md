The declaration has five articles. The second and third speak of Hinduism, Buddhism and Islam and say that the Catholic Church rejects nothing that is true and holy in these religions. The fourth, the longest, recalls the bond between the church and "Abraham's stock". It says that what happened in the passion of Christ cannot be charged against all Jews then living, without distinction, nor against the Jews of today; that the Jews are not to be presented as rejected or accursed by God; and that the church decries hatred, persecution and displays of antisemitism at any time and by anyone.
