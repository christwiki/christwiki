[[person:gregory-palamas]] wrote these nine treatises while his dispute with [[person:barlaam-of-calabria]] was going on; John Meyendorff, who edited the Greek text, dates them between 1338 and 1341 in his introduction to the English selection. They are works of controversy. The second and third groups quote Barlaam's own treatises in order to refute them, so that much of what is known of Barlaam's case comes through his opponent.

The English edition is a selection arranged by theme. It is cited here by triad, treatise and the paragraph at which the selection begins. The work is the main source for [[event:hesychast-controversy]].
