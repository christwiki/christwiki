Leo wrote in [[place:constantinople]] in the 990s. In a digression in his last book he lists the portents and calamities that followed the death of the rebel Bardas Phokas: pillars of fire in the northern sky, which foretold the taking of Cherson by the "Tauroscythians" (his name for the Rus'), a comet, and the earthquake that damaged Hagia Sophia. He says nothing of a baptism. His value for [[event:baptism-of-rus]] is the sequence, which places the fall of [[place:chersonesus]] after Phokas's death and so helps to date it.
