The title page gives the place and date of the sermon and says that it was taken down by a stenographer. In it [[person:harry-emerson-fosdick]] describes the people "who call themselves the Fundamentalists", lists the doctrines he says they were making tests of fellowship, sets a liberal reading beside the conservative one on three of them, and asks for tolerance.

It is a statement by one party to the dispute and describes the other side in its own terms. It is cited in [[event:the-fundamentals]] for what Fosdick said.
