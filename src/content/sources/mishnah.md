The Mishnah arranges the oral teaching of the rabbis in six orders and sixty-three tractates. Tradition names Rabbi Judah the Prince as its editor. Much of it concerns the temple and its worship, set down generations after the temple was destroyed.

It is a legal work, not a history, and it mentions events only in passing. Tractate *Ta'anit* (4:6), explaining the fast of the ninth of Av, lists five disasters that fell on that day, among them that Betar was captured and that the city was ploughed. The notice was written down within about seventy years of the Bar Kokhba revolt and shows how the fall of [[place:betar]] was remembered among Jews.
