The assembly met at Žiča monastery and in Belgrade under Patriarch Irinej. Its communiqué covers the whole of its business; one paragraph deals with Ukraine. It calls the schism there the greatest problem of the Orthodox Church at that time, criticises Constantinople for acting on its own initiative, and states that the Serbian church is in communion only with the Ukrainian Orthodox Church led by Metropolitan Onufriy. It calls the new church a "false-church structure". See [[event:autocephaly-of-ukraine]].
