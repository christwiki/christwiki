The report says that members of the Kimbanguist Church met at Nkamba on the Wednesday before its dateline, that is on 12 October 2022, to keep the seventy-first anniversary of Kimbangu's death, and names the church officers who spoke. It closes with a "brief biography" in the agency's own words: born at Nkamba on 12 September 1887, arrested on 12 September 1921, condemned on 3 October 1921, died at Elisabethville on Friday 12 October 1951.

It is a journalist's report, not a statement of the church, and it names no source for the biography. It is cited in [[event:simon-kimbangu]] as a report of the day on which the church keeps the anniversary and for the days the agency gives.
