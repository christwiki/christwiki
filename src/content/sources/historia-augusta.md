The *Historia Augusta* presents itself as the work of six authors writing under Diocletian and Constantine. Most historians now think that it was written by one author late in the fourth century, and that many of its documents and some of its details are invented, especially in the later lives. The early lives, among them that of Hadrian, are thought to rest on better sources.

It is cited here for the birth dates of Hadrian and Antoninus Pius, for the terms of Antoninus's adoption, and for one sentence about the Jewish war: that the Jews went to war under Hadrian "because they were forbidden to practice circumcision" (*Hadrian* 14.2). Cassius Dio gives a different cause, and the notice stands alone. It was written some two centuries after the event.
