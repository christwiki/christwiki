The constitution has eight chapters: on the mystery of the church, the people of God, the hierarchy, the laity, the universal call to holiness, religious, the pilgrim church and the Virgin Mary. Article 8 says that the one church of Christ "subsists in" the Catholic Church, while many elements of sanctification and truth are found outside its visible structure. Article 22 teaches that the bishops as a body, together with the pope and never without him, hold supreme power over the whole church, and that the pope's own primacy remains intact.

Its third chapter opens (article 18) by saying that the council follows in the footsteps of the [[event:first-vatican-council]], teaches again its doctrine of the Roman primacy, and goes on to declare the doctrine concerning bishops. See [[event:second-vatican-council]].
