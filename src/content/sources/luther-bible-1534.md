The title page reads, in translation, "Bible, that is, the whole Holy Scripture in German. Mart. Luth. Wittenberg", and names the printer Hans Lufft and the year 1534. The museum's record describes a folio in six parts and dates the work of translation from 1522 to 1534.

The record is cited for the object and its date. It is the description of one surviving copy by the museum that keeps it, in the house at [[place:wittenberg]] where [[person:martin-luther]] lived.
