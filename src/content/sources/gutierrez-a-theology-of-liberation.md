Gutiérrez opens with two passages from the introduction to the Medellín documents and then says, in a parenthesis, that the name and the reality of "liberation theology" came into existence at Chimbote in Peru in July 1968, only a few months before Medellín (p. xxviii). His first note adds that his lecture, given to a national meeting of lay people, religious and priests, was published in Lima and then in Montevideo in 1969, and that he gave it again in an expanded form at a meeting at Cartigny in Switzerland in 1969 (p. xxviii, note 1). The rest of the introduction looks back over the twenty years since, and takes up among other things the phrase "preferential option for the poor".

This is the author's own account, written twenty years after the lecture; the lecture itself was not found in an open edition. The body of the book is in copyright and the publisher's excerpt does not include it.
