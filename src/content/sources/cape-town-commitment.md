The foreword says that the congress met at Cape Town from 16 to 25 October 2010 and brought together 4,200 evangelical leaders from 198 countries. The preamble says that the movement remains committed to the Lausanne Covenant of 1974 and the Manila Manifesto of 1989, and observes that at least two thirds of the world's Christians now live in the global south and east.
