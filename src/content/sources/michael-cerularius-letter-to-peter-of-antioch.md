[[person:michael-cerularius]] wrote to win the other eastern patriarchs to his view. He repeats that the legates were impostors, lists the faults of the Latins, and rebukes Peter for a rumour that Antioch, Alexandria and Jerusalem still named the pope in the liturgy. Constantinople, he says, has not done so since the sixth council (chapter 9), a statement that [[source:peter-of-antioch-letter-to-michael-cerularius]] corrects.
