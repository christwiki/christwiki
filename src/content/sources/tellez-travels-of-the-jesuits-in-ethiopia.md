The preface to the English version calls Tellez a learned Jesuit whom his society employed to digest what those of its members who had been in Ethiopia had written, and it names them: Pedro Páez, whose manuscript history reached to his death in 1622; Manuel de Almeida, who was superior of the mission; and the patriarch Afonso Mendes. Long passages are given in Almeida's own words, among them the funeral of Susenyos, at which he was present. The Portuguese original, *Historia geral de Ethiopia a Alta*, was printed at Coimbra in 1660; the English version cited here appeared in 1710 without a translator's name.

The book is the mission's own account. It calls the Ethiopian clergy schismatics and the opponents of the union rebels, and it presents the restoration of the old faith as a defeat. It is cited for the dates and documents the Jesuits recorded, and each use says whose account it is. The Ethiopian court's record is in [[source:ethiopian-abridged-chronicle]].
