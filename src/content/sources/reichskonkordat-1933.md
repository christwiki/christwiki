The concordat has 34 articles and a final protocol. It guarantees the freedom of profession and public practice of the Catholic religion, the church's right to manage its own affairs within the law that applies to all, Catholic religious instruction and Catholic confessional schools, and the protection of Catholic organisations that serve religious, cultural and charitable purposes. In article 32 the Holy See undertakes to bar clergy and religious from membership of political parties.

It is the legal text itself and is cited in [[event:mit-brennender-sorge]] for what the two parties agreed in 1933.
