Flinders Petrie excavated the ruined royal mortuary temples on the west bank at [[place:thebes]] in the winter of 1895–1896 and published this report the following year. It is the first-hand record of the discovery of the [[source:merneptah-stele]]: page 13 describes how the stone was found lying in the first court of the temple of [[person:merneptah]], page 10 gives its material and measurements, and plates XIII and XIV show it in a photograph and a hand copy.

The translation on pages 26–28 is by Wilhelm Spiegelberg, who copied the inscription on the spot. Spiegelberg was the first to publish the text. His English version here renders the key line as "the people of Israel is laid waste". On page 30 Petrie adds his own first thoughts on how the name might relate to the biblical Israelites. Those remarks document the debate that began with the find; they are not a settled reading.
