Ten lines name Sumyafa' Ashwa', his sons and a long list of clans and tribes, and say that they wrote the inscription in the fortress of Mawiyat when they restored its wall, gate, cisterns and approach and fortified themselves there, after returning from the land of the Abyssinians, when the Abyssinians had killed the king of Himyar and his chiefs. The king is not named. Norbert Nebes, whose notes the archive reproduces, takes him to be Yusuf As'ar Yath'ar and converts the date, the month dhu-Hillatan of the year 640, to February 530. The translation of lines 8–9 is disputed in detail, as the archive's notes set out.
