The page gives the full text of the treaty in 27 articles, the financial convention in 3 and the concordat in 45, with the list of annexes. The treaty names its two plenipotentiaries, [[person:pietro-gasparri]] for [[person:pius-xi]] and [[person:benito-mussolini]] for King Victor Emmanuel III, and required ratification by pope and king within four months; the ratifications were exchanged on 7 June 1929.

These are the legal texts themselves. They are cited in [[event:lateran-treaty]] for what the two parties agreed, not for how the agreements worked in practice.
