The Exercises are a handbook of meditations arranged in four "weeks", followed by methods of prayer and sets of rules. The short "Principle and Foundation" near the beginning states the aim of the whole.

In his [[source:ignatius-autobiography]] Ignatius says that he won Peter Faber and Francis Xavier in Paris by giving them the Exercises. The book is cited here for what it teaches. The translator's preface says that the Latin version was formally approved by the Holy See in 1548, and the edition prints Paul III's letter of approval.
