---

The letter answers the movement for a Chinese Catholic church independent of Rome in government, finance and teaching. Pius XII grants that Chinese clergy should in time lead the church and that preaching should be adapted to Chinese culture. He denies that the church in any nation can govern itself apart from the pope, and says that those who adhere to the principles of the "Three Autonomies" cannot be considered Catholics.
