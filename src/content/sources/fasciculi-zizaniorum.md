The title page of the printed edition ascribes the collection to Thomas Netter of Walden. It gathers the charges, condemnations and recantations in the campaign against [[person:john-wycliffe]] and the Lollards, linked by a narrative written against them.

For [[event:wycliffe-bible]] it supplies the setting of the condemnation of 1382: Archbishop William of Canterbury called his bishops and doctors of theology and law to the house of the Friars Preachers in [[place:london]], and an earthquake shook England that afternoon.
