The release calls the killing a war crime and asks that civilians be spared in the air strikes that followed. Its second paragraph says that a video published on 15 February 2015 showed armed men beheading twenty-one men, most or all of them Egyptian Copts working in Libya, on a beach thought to be near Sirte. A later paragraph says that the Egyptians had gone missing at Sirte in December 2014 and January 2015, that photographs of men said to be the missing Egyptians, kneeling on a beach in front of men with knives, had been published earlier in February in the armed group's online magazine, and that the video shows the same setting and seemingly the same victims; that paragraph gives the day of the video's publication as 16 February. The release names no day for the killing.

Human Rights Watch is a non-governmental organisation and had no one at the scene; its account rests on the published images and on reports from Libya and Egypt. The wiki neither describes nor links to the video. See [[event:martyrs-of-libya]].
