Robert and Samuel Wilberforce, both clergymen, wrote the *Life* from their father's papers. They quote his private journal and his letters at length and give the dates of the entries in footnotes.

The extracts are primary evidence for what [[person:william-wilberforce]] wrote. The narrative around them is the work of sons writing to honour their father, and should be read as such.
