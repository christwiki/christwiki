The federation published this notice the day after the death of [[person:christian-krause]]. It gives his birth at Dallgow-Döberitz on 6 January 1940, his ordination in 1969, his years as bishop of Brunswick (1994–2002) and as president of the federation (1997–2003), and his death at Wolfenbüttel on 28 November 2024. It is the federation's own record of one of its officers and is used in this wiki only for those dates and offices.
