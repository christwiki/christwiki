The text is headed "Lund, 31 October 2016" and has five headed sections: "With thankful hearts", "Moving from conflict to communion", "Our commitment to common witness", "One in Christ" and "Calling upon Catholics and Lutherans worldwide". The Holy See's page does not print the signatures; the Lutheran World Federation's report of the day records that [[person:francis]] and [[person:munib-younan]] signed it during the service ([[source:lwf-report-on-the-lund-commemoration-2016]]).

It is a joint text and makes no doctrinal agreement of its own. It names the wish of many to receive the Eucharist at one table and calls the present inability to do so a wound, without proposing a remedy. See [[event:reformation-500]].
