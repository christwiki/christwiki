Boris, baptized from [[place:constantinople]], turned to Rome within two years and sent a list of questions about Christian practice and about the standing of his church. [[person:nicholas-i]] answered them one by one. Most of the answers concern daily life: fasting, marriage, oaths, war, dress. Two bear on the quarrel with the east. Chapter 72 holds out an archbishop, though not yet a patriarch, for Bulgaria. Chapter 92 says that the true patriarchal sees are Rome, Alexandria and Antioch, and that the bishop of Constantinople is called patriarch by the favour of emperors rather than by right.
