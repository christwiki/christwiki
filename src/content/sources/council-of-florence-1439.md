The decrees are cited by the session numbers of Tanner's edition, which begin again at Ferrara. They include the declaration opening the council at Ferrara on 8 January 1438, the decree of 10 January 1439 moving it to Florence because of plague, the definition of 6 July 1439 known from its first words as Laetentur caeli, and the bulls of union with the Armenians (1439) and with the Copts (Cantate Domino, 1442).

Laetentur caeli was issued in Latin and Greek in the name of [[person:eugene-iv]] with the agreement of the emperor [[person:john-viii-palaiologos]] and the Greek delegation. These are the texts of the side that held the union to be valid; for the Greek refusal see [[source:mark-of-ephesus-encyclical]] and [[source:doukas-history]]. See [[event:council-of-florence]].
