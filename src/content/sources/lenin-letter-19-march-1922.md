The letter is headed "Top Secret" and addressed to Molotov for the members of the Politburo, which was about to discuss the resistance at Shuya to the removal of valuables from the churches. Lenin asks that no copies be made. The document was shown at the Library of Congress in 1992 among papers newly released from the Russian archives; the translation ends with the note that it is a true copy and that the original went to the Lenin Institute.

It is a private instruction from the head of the government to his colleagues, and it states his purpose more openly than any public decree. It is cited in [[event:russian-revolution-and-the-church]].
