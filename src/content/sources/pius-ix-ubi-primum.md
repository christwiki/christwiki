The letter recalls the petitions that had reached Gregory XVI and Pius IX asking for a definition, says that the pope has set cardinals and theologians to examine the question, and asks each bishop to order public prayers and to report on the devotion of his clergy and people and on his own judgment. It is dated from Gaeta in the third year of the pontificate.

*Ineffabilis Deus* refers back to it as the consultation that preceded the definition. See [[event:immaculate-conception]].
