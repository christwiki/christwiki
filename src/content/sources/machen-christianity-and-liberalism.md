The preface says that the book grew out of an address given by [[person:j-gresham-machen]] on 3 November 1921 and printed in *The Princeton Theological Review* in 1922 under the title "Liberalism or Christianity". Its chapters set what Machen takes to be the teaching of liberalism against that of historic Christianity on doctrine, God and man, the Bible, Christ, salvation and the church.

It is one side's case in a controversy, made by a participant. It is cited in [[event:the-fundamentals]] for Machen's own argument.
