The Act confirms three resolutions that the Patriarchal Synod had passed on 23 April 1929: that the old Russian rites are salvific, like the new, and equal in honour to them; that the condemnatory expressions used of the old rites, and of the two-finger sign in particular, are rejected and regarded as if they had not been uttered; and that the anathemas which the Moscow councils of 1656 and 1667 laid on the old rites and on those who keep them are abolished and regarded as if they had not been.

It is evidence of how the Moscow Patriarchate itself judged the measures of the seventeenth century three hundred years later. It dates the patriarchate of [[person:nikon]] to 1652–1666. See [[event:nikon-and-the-old-believers]].
