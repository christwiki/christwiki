Ekkehard was a monk who became abbot of Aura on the Franconian Saale. For the years after 1100 his chronicle is written close to the events, and it ends in 1125.

For 1122 he describes the assembly at [[place:worms]], gives the text of both charters and their date, and tells how they were read out in a field by the Rhine. He writes as a churchman glad to see the schism ended. His wording of the papal charter has the bishop receive the regalia "by the sceptre".
