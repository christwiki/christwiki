The letter is the king's side of the breach of 1076, written in his name and sent out with a letter in which a long list of German bishops withdrew their obedience from the pope. It argues that a king anointed by God is to be judged by God alone and may be deposed only for heresy, and it accuses [[person:gregory-vii]] of having gained the papacy by money and violence.

It is a piece of polemic, composed to be read aloud and circulated, and it should be read as a statement of the royal case, not as a report of facts. Henderson's translation gives it the date 24 January 1076.
