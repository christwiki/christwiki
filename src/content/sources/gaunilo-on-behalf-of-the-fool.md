The translation used here heads the reply as the work of Gaunilo, a monk of Marmoutier. He speaks for the fool of the psalm who denies God, attacks only the proof, and ends by praising the rest of Anselm's book. [[person:anselm-of-canterbury]] answered him point by point, and his biographer Eadmer says that Anselm wanted the criticism and his answer copied at the end of the Proslogion from then on. Eadmer speaks only of "a certain person".
