The letter was issued by the Ecumenical Patriarchate on 1 January 1920 and sent, as the World Council's introduction to it says, to the leaders of Christian churches. It holds that closer relations between the churches are not excluded by their doctrinal differences. It asks first for an end to proselytism among Christians, then for the rekindling of love between the churches, and it lists practical steps. Pointing to the League of Nations then being set up, it proposes a "league" or fellowship of churches.

The World Council of Churches, which publishes this translation, calls the letter one of the foundational moments of the modern ecumenical movement. It is the Patriarchate's own statement of why an Orthodox church should seek such a fellowship. See [[event:world-council-of-churches]].
