The articles were drawn up at the end of the colloquy that [[person:philip-of-hesse]] had called at [[place:marburg]]. They cover the Trinity, the person of Christ, original sin, faith and justification, the preached word, baptism, good works, confession, civil government, tradition and infant baptism. The fifteenth, on the Lord's Supper, states what both sides hold in common and then that they have not agreed whether the true body and blood of Christ are bodily present in the bread and wine.

The signatures are those of [[person:martin-luther]], Justus Jonas, [[person:philip-melanchthon]], Andreas Osiander, Stephan Agricola, Johannes Brenz, Johannes Oecolampadius, [[person:huldrych-zwingli]], Martin Bucer and Caspar Hedio. Both parties signed the same text, so it is evidence of what each was willing to say together with the other, not of the full teaching of either.
