The bull follows from [[source:leo-x-exsurge-domine]]. It reports that many of [[person:martin-luther]]'s followers had recanted and that his books had been burned in parts of Germany, but that Luther himself had not withdrawn his errors within the time set and had written and preached worse things. It declares him a heretic, extends the same sentence to those who follow or protect him, whatever their rank, and orders the clergy to publish the excommunication.

It is dated at St Peter's on 3 January 1521. It is the church's formal sentence and gives no new account of Luther's teaching.
