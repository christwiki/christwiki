*Paraclesis* is Greek for "exhortation". [[person:erasmus]] printed it as the first preface of his [[source:erasmus-novum-instrumentum-1516]]. It sets out what he calls the philosophy of Christ, a way of life learned from the Gospels and the apostles' letters, and argues that this learning belongs to all the baptised and not to theologians alone.

The English version linked here was printed at Antwerp in 1529; its translator is not named, and the page gives an excerpt, not the whole. The translator added a few words of his own at the end, as the editor's note points out. The Latin original can be read in the facsimile of the 1516 edition.
