The text is the Holy See's transcript of what [[person:leo-xiv]] said to the crowd on Thursday 8 May 2025. He greets them with the risen Christ's words of peace, thanks Pope [[person:francis]] and the cardinals, calls himself an Augustinian, greets the church of Rome and, in Spanish, his former diocese of Chiclayo in Peru, and ends with a Hail Mary. See [[event:leo-xiv-elected]].
