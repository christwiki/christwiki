[[person:athanasius]] wrote this work while in hiding during his third exile, in the year of the councils it describes. It has three parts: a narrative of the councils of Ariminum and Seleucia; a dossier of the statements of [[person:arius]] and of the creeds that councils had issued in place of the Nicene one, from Antioch in 341 to Constantinople in 360 (15–30); and an argument addressed to those who accepted the Nicene faith but hesitated over its wording.

The value of the work is in the documents. The extracts from the *Thalia* of Arius (15) are among the few passages of his own writing to survive, and several of the creeds are preserved here in their earliest form. The selection and the commentary are those of an opponent, whose purpose is to show that the other side could not agree on what it believed.
