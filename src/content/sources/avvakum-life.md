Avvakum tells his life from his birth in the village of Grigorovo: his years as a priest, his opposition to [[person:nikon]], his exile to Siberia with his wife and children, his return, his appearance before the patriarchs in Moscow and his banishment to Pustozersk, from where he says he is writing. The book is a participant's account and a polemic. Nikon and those who accepted the new books are "apostates" in it, and what they said is reported only as Avvakum remembered it.
