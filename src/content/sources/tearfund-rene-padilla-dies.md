The release, dated 29 April 2021, says that Padilla had died aged 88; that he was born in Ecuador and that his family moved to Colombia when he was very young; and that he was international president of Tearfund from 1998 to 2010, in which time the agency adopted his term "integral mission" for its work. It calls him the father of integral mission.

It is an obituary notice from an organisation he led, and the wiki cites it only for those facts.
