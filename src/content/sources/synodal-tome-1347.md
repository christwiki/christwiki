The tome is headed as a synodal tome confirming the earlier tome against the teachings of Barlaam and Akindynos. In the part that survives the synod says that it acted together with the empress and her son, the emperor John Palaiologos, and the senate; it strikes at one who had received Akindynos into communion and had cast out [[person:gregory-palamas]], strips Akindynos of all priestly ministry, and extends the same sentence to anyone who should afterwards write or speak against Palamas and the monks with him, whom it calls champions of the church (cols 1281–1283). It ends with the statement that it was written and signed in February of the fifteenth indiction (col. 1284). The fifteenth indiction ran from September 1346 to August 1347.

The name of the patriarch does not stand in the columns read for this wiki, where the printed text has gaps. That this is the tome by which Patriarch John was deposed rests on [[source:synodal-tome-1351]], which describes that tome and quotes its closing sentences word for word (§4). Like the other tomes it is the judgment of the side that prevailed. Used for the date in [[event:hesychast-controversy]].
