[[person:john-paul-ii]] spoke at midday in Rome while the representatives of the Catholic Church and the Lutheran World Federation were signing at Augsburg. The address fixes the place and the day from the Catholic side, calls the event a "milestone" and at the same time an "intermediate goal", and speaks of remaining problems still to be addressed. See [[event:joint-declaration-on-justification]].
