The Joint Committee was set up by the [[source:common-christological-declaration-1994]]. This statement, dated 24 November 2017, concludes the phase of its work devoted to the sacraments and is a text agreed by the representatives of both churches.

It is cited in this wiki for one sentence of its introduction. Quoting the declaration of 1994, the committee says that with those words an age-old doctrinal controversy about the Christological implications and consequences of the Council of Ephesus was concluded. The two churches here say together that it was the Council of Ephesus over which they had been divided. The statement does not list the councils that the Assyrian Church of the East receives.
