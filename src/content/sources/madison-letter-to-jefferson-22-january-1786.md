---

Madison sat in the assembly through the session and writes as a participant, the day after it closed. He tells Jefferson that the general assessment had been defeated by petitions from all parts of the state, that the bill passed the House without alteration, that the Senate objected to the preamble and obtained some changes of wording, and that the enacting clauses passed untouched. It is an account of the passage of [[source:virginia-statute-for-religious-freedom]] by a member who was present.
