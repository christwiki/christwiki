A recess was the document in which an imperial diet recorded what it had agreed. That of 1526 says that the electors, princes and estates have unanimously resolved, until a general council or national assembly meets, that in the matters touched by the [[source:edict-of-worms]] each will "so live, govern, and carry himself as he hopes and trusts to answer it to God and His Imperial Majesty".

The sentence does not grant freedom of religion or mention reform. The evangelical princes read it as leaving the ordering of their churches to them; the emperor's proposition to the next diet at [[place:speyer]] complains that it had been expounded at every man's pleasure. Kidd prints this one article.
