Most of the address concerns relations between the Catholic Church and the Church of Scotland. In the last paragraph but one the pope says that he will speak in his native tongue to express his sorrow, that he has read that day of the execution of "twenty-one or twenty-two" Coptic Christians, and that the blood of Christians of every confession is one and confesses Christ. He names this the ecumenism of blood.

These are his words on the day after the deaths were made known, before the number and the identities of the men were settled. See [[event:martyrs-of-libya]].
