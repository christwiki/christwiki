*Der christliche Glaube* is built of short numbered propositions, each followed by pages of explanation. Baillie translated the propositions alone, printing the edition of 1821–22 on the left-hand pages and that of 1830–31 on the right, and says in his note that the work had not then been translated into English. The numbering differs between the editions: the definition of piety is §§8–9 in the first and §§3–4 in the second.

It is cited in [[event:schleiermacher]] for its definitions, which are quoted in Baillie's wording.
