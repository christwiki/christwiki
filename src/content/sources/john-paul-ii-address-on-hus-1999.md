The symposium was the work of an ecumenical commission set up by the archbishop of Prague to study the place of [[person:jan-hus]] among the reformers of the church. Receiving its members in Rome on 17 December 1999, [[person:john-paul-ii]] praised Hus's moral courage and said that he felt the need to express "deep regret" for the "cruel death" inflicted on him and for the conflict and division it left among the Bohemian people.

The address does not lift the council's sentence or pronounce on Hus's teaching. It is cited in [[event:council-of-constance]] as the Catholic Church's own later statement about his death.
