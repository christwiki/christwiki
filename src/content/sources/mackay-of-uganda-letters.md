Mackay was in Buganda throughout the persecution. The book is built from his own letters home, printed with their dates, and from a last survey of the mission's history that reached the Church Missionary Society in April 1890, after his death.

The letters are contemporary and were written under threat. They give the events as an Anglican missionary knew them at the time, with his anger at the king undisguised.
