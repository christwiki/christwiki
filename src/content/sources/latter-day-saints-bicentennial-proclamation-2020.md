The proclamation is issued in the name of the First Presidency and the Council of the Twelve Apostles, and a note at its end says that the church's president, Russell M. Nelson, read it at the general conference in Salt Lake City on 5 April 2020. It affirms that God the Father and Jesus Christ appeared to Joseph Smith, that heavenly messengers restored authority to him, and that the church organized on 6 April 1830 "is Christ’s New Testament Church restored".

It is cited in [[event:latter-day-saints]] as the church's present statement of how it understands itself.
