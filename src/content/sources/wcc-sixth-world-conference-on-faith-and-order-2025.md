The World Council of Churches' page for the conference says that it met at the monastery of St Bishoy at Wadi El Natrun from 24 to 28 October 2025 and was the centrepiece of the council's activities for the 1,700th anniversary of Nicaea. The message thanks the Coptic Orthodox Church and its pope, Tawadros II, for their hospitality, places the meeting in the line of the Faith and Order conferences from Lausanne in 1927 to Santiago de Compostela in 1993, and ends with a call to renew commitment to faith, mission and unity.

It is the conference's own statement. The page for the event was written in part before the meeting and speaks of it in the present and future tenses. See [[event:nicaea-1700]].
