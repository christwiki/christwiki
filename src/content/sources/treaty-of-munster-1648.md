Most of the treaty concerns territory, the restoration of princes and the relations of France with the Empire. On religion it does not repeat the settlement but adopts it: article 49 says that the agreement on church lands and the exercise of religion inserted in the treaty with Sweden is confirmed here as if it were repeated word for word, and that it applies also to those called the Reformed.

The closing clause dates the treaty at Münster in Westphalia on 24 October 1648. It is cited in [[event:thirty-years-war]] together with [[source:treaty-of-osnabruck-1648]].
