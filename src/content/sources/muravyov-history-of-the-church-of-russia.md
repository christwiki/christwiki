The title page describes the author as chamberlain to the emperor and under-procurator of the Most Holy Governing Synod. He writes more than a century after the events of 1721 and as a servant of the institution whose founding he describes, which he presents as a needed reform approved by the whole eastern church. His narrative is therefore a later and interested account, and where the wiki uses it the text says so.

At pages 287 and 288 he gives the letter in which Jeremias, patriarch of Constantinople, recognises the Russian Synod, dated 23 September 1723. The wording quoted in the wiki is that of Blackmore's English translation. Muravyov does not say from what copy he prints it.
