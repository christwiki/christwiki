[[person:lesslie-newbigin]] wrote the book, he says, in the last months of a furlough, and dated its foreword from Madura in November 1947. It answers critics of the scheme and argues the theology of the church's unity and continuity, of the ministry and of the sacraments. It is the work of a participant arguing a case, and is cited here for his own account of himself and of what he believed had happened. See [[event:church-of-south-india]].
