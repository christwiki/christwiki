The annals were written up at or near the Carolingian court by several hands, the later years close to the events. They report what the court wished to have remembered, briefly and from its own point of view. The entry for 826 records the baptism of the Danish king Harald at Mainz, the event from which Rimbert's undated account of [[person:ansgar]]'s first mission can be anchored.
