The tome is written in the name of the patriarch ("Our Modesty") and dated August of the ninth indiction, the Byzantine tax year that ended on 31 August 1341. It tells how [[person:barlaam-of-calabria]] accused the monks, how the council met in the presence of the emperor, what passages of the Fathers were read, and what was decided.

It is a document of one side. Barlaam's arguments appear only as his opponents summarised and quoted them, and the tome itself was later treated as a standard by which others were condemned. It is cited here for the course of the hearing and its verdict in [[event:hesychast-controversy]].
