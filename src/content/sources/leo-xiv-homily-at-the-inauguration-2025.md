[[person:leo-xiv]] preached on John 21 on Sunday 18 May 2025. He recalls the death of Pope [[person:francis]] and the conclave, says that he was chosen "without any merit of my own", and describes the office of Peter as one of self-giving love and of service to unity, to be exercised without becoming "an autocrat". He names a united church as his first great desire. See [[event:leo-xiv-elected]].
