The conference published its own record in the year it met. Volumes 1 to 8 each contain the report of one commission, drawn up beforehand from the answers of missionaries around the world, followed by the speeches made when the report was debated. Volume 9 tells how the conference was prepared, prints the minutes of every session, lists the delegates and gives the addresses delivered in the evenings, among them that of [[person:v-s-azariah]].

It is the conference's account of itself. The volumes are cited in [[event:edinburgh-1910]] for what was said and resolved.
