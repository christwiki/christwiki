The printed title says the sermon was preached at the castle of Allstedt before the dukes and rulers of Saxony by [[person:thomas-muntzer]], minister of the word of God. He expounds Daniel 2, defends true visions and dreams as a way in which God still speaks, and says that a new Daniel must arise to interpret them to the rulers. The princes are told that the sword was given to them to root out the godless; if they will not use it, it will be taken from them.

It is Müntzer's own statement of his programme, a year before the rising in Thuringia, and it mocks an opponent under nicknames that the editors take to mean [[person:martin-luther]]. Whether the printed text matches what was spoken cannot be checked.
