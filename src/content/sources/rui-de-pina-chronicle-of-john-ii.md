Rui de Pina was chronicler to the Portuguese crown. He writes of Kongo from the Portuguese court, where, as he says, the news of the king's baptism arrived. He gives exact dates for the landing, for the baptisms of the lord of Soyo, the king and the queen, and for the building of the first church.

The account is written to the glory of [[person:nzinga-a-nkuwu|the king]]'s Portuguese sponsor, John II, and of the faith. It puts long pious speeches into the mouths of the king of Kongo and his nobles and reports dreams and the finding of a stone cross as miracles. This wiki knows of no Kongo account of 1491 to set beside it; the letters of the king's son Afonso, a generation later, are the nearest thing. See [[event:baptism-of-the-king-of-kongo]].
