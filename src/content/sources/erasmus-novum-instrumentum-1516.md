The book is a folio printed by Johann Froben at [[place:basel]]. Its title page names [[person:erasmus]] as the reviser and says the text was corrected against the Greek and against manuscripts in both languages; the last leaf gives the printer and the date, February 1516. The Greek and Erasmus's Latin stand side by side in two columns, and his notes follow in a separate part. Before the text come a dedication to Pope [[person:leo-x]], dated 1 February 1516, and three prefaces, the first of them the [[source:erasmus-paraclesis]].

The volume is cited here for what it prints and for what Erasmus says in it about his own work. He was already planning a revised edition within months of its appearance, as he told a friend in June 1516, so a reading found in this printing should not be assumed for the later ones.
