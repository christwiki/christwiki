The statement sets out the Moscow Patriarchate's view of the weeks before the council: its proposal of 3 June for an emergency pre-conciliar conference, the Ecumenical Patriarchate's refusal, and the decisions of the churches of Bulgaria, Antioch, Serbia and Georgia, which it quotes. It argues from the rule of consensus that a council lacking any one of the recognised autocephalous churches cannot be held as planned, and ends with six resolutions.

It is one party's account of a dispute and is cited in [[event:holy-and-great-council]] for the Russian church's own reasons. Its summaries of the other churches' decisions agree with those churches' own texts where this wiki has checked them.
