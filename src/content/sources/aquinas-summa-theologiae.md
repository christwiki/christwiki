The Summa is divided into three parts, the second in two halves, and each part into questions and articles; a reference such as I.25.1 means the first part, question 25, article 1. Each article states objections, an answer and replies to the objections.

It is cited in this wiki as a statement of Latin scholastic teaching in the words of [[person:thomas-aquinas]].
