The Charter is written in the voice of the abbot and monks of the New Monastery, that is [[place:citeaux]], and its prologue names Abbot Stephen and his brethren as its authors. It says that they drew it up before the daughter abbeys began to flourish, so that no abbey would be founded in a diocese until the bishop had approved the terms.

It survives in more than one version, and historians disagree about how much of the text goes back to [[person:stephen-harding]] himself; see the note on [[source:exordium-parvum]]. It is cited here for what its provisions say.
