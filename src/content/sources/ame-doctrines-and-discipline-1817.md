The book sets out the articles of religion, the rules of the societies and the government of the new church, following the Methodist pattern. It opens with an address "To the Members of the African Methodist Episcopal Church", a brief statement of the church's rise and progress, which dates the first meeting of the Black Methodists of Philadelphia to November 1787 and records that on 11 April 1816 Allen was set apart for the episcopal office. Its section on slavery provides that no slaveholder shall be received as a member.

The preface was written within a year of the founding by the men who led it, thirty years after the earliest events it describes. It is cited in [[event:richard-allen-and-the-ame]].
