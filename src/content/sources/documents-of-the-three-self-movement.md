The collection was made in New York from the Chinese church press, much of it from the movement's own magazine *Tian Feng*. Most of what it prints is the voice of the movement's supporters, as the editors themselves point out. It also prints [[person:wang-mingdao]]'s pamphlet against the movement and the attacks on him that followed. The editors worked for the Far Eastern Office of the Division of Foreign Missions of the American National Council of Churches, and their introductions and biographical notes are written from outside China.

It is cited in this wiki for the translated documents, each under its own title, and for the editors' notes as their own statements. See [[event:three-self-movement]].
