According to the library record of the edition, the chronicle was begun by Kaspar Braitmichel, who carried it to 1542, and others continued it to 1665. It opens with the history of the church and then tells how the brotherhood began in Switzerland: how [[person:conrad-grebel]], [[person:felix-manz]] and [[person:huldrych-zwingli]] discussed baptism, how the priest [[person:george-blaurock]] joined them, and how Blaurock asked Grebel to baptise him.

It was written later and far from [[place:zurich]], by a community that honoured these men as its founders, and it gives no day for the baptism. How the story reached the chronicler is not known. For the same weeks the [[source:zurich-council-records]] give the authorities' side.
