The excerpt is a few sentences long. Having read what it calls "this proclamation", which the sourcebook identifies as the papal decree against the rites, the emperor writes that Westerners are petty, that none of them is versed in Chinese works, that their religion seems to him no different from small sects of Buddhism or Taoism, and that from now on they should not be allowed to preach in China, to avoid further trouble. It is the emperor's answer in his own terms to a decision taken in Rome about the customs of his empire. See [[event:chinese-rites-controversy]].
