The charter begins with the "terrible crime" in which five of the clergy and laity of [[place:paris]] were killed, sentences the provost of the city, of whom the students had complained, and then provides for the future. Royal officers may not lay hands on a student except in the act of a crime, and must then hand him over to the ecclesiastical judge; each new provost must swear before the scholars to observe this.

It is the first document in the collected charters of the University of Paris, the Chartularium.
