[[person:hudson-taylor]] wrote these chapters for readers who supported his mission, and they are arranged to show answers to prayer. Chapters 18 and 19 tell how he came to believe that a new agency was needed for the inland provinces, what happened on the beach at Brighton on 25 June 1865, and how the first party sailed in the *Lammermuir* the next May.

The book is a founder's memoir, written long after the events and with a devotional purpose. It gives exact dates for the steps he describes.
