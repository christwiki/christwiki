Book 1, by William of Saint-Thierry, covers Bernard's youth, his entry at [[place:citeaux]] and the founding of [[place:clairvaux]]. William knew Bernard: he speaks of things "we used to hear him say". The last books, by Geoffrey of Auxerre, describe Bernard's death; Geoffrey writes as a monk of Clairvaux, which he calls "our valley".

The work was written to honour a man its authors held to be a saint, and it is full of visions and miracles. It is used here for the outline of Bernard's early career and for the dates it gives. The volume of Migne also prints a long editorial commentary on the Life, which is cited in one dating note as scholarship.
