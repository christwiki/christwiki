The bull is dated at Saint Peter's on 16 May 1920. It narrates the life of [[person:joan-of-arc]], calls the trial at Rouen unjust and her death cruel, recalls the rescript of [[person:callixtus-iii]] and the sentence of 7 July 1456, and records the steps of her cause: its introduction in 1894, the decree on her virtues, the miracles accepted, and her beatification. It then gives the formula by which the pope declared her a saint and ordered her memory kept each year on 30 May.

It is a document of 1920 and is cited for the canonization and for the judgment of the modern papacy on the trial of 1431, not as evidence for the fifteenth century. See [[event:joan-of-arc]].
