In the bull *Rex pacificus*, addressed to the masters and students of Bologna, Gregory IX explains that the decretals of his predecessors were scattered through several volumes, repeated or contradicted one another and caused confusion in the courts. He had therefore had them gathered into one volume by his chaplain and penitentiary, brother Raymond, with his own constitutions added, and he ordered that this collection alone be used in courts and schools.

The collection is arranged by subject. Its title on heretics, book 5, title 7, contains the third constitution of the Fourth Lateran Council as chapter 13 and Gregory IX's own decree *Excommunicamus* as chapter 15. The compiler shortened many texts, so the wording here is the wording that became law, which is not always the wording first issued.
