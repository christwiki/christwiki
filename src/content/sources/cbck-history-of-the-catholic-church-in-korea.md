These articles are a modern summary, not a document of the eighteenth century. They are cited in [[event:first-korean-baptism]] as the present-day statement of the church concerned about its own origin: that Koreans took up the faith from books, sent one of their number to Beijing for baptism and formed a community before any missionary came. The articles call the period in which lay leaders acted as priests the "pseudo-hierarchy" and say it was begun in good faith and given up when the bishop of Beijing was consulted.

Two of the articles differ slightly on the date: one puts the baptism of Yi Byeok in September 1784, another the founding of the community in October 1784.
