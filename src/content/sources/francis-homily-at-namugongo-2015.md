The Mass was celebrated in the grounds of the Catholic shrine of the martyrs. The pope names Joseph Mukasa and [[person:charles-lwanga]] and says that the deaths of the Anglican martyrs "testify to the ecumenism of blood". It is cited as a statement by a pope that the Catholic Church honours the Anglicans who died beside its own.
