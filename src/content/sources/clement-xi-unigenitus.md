The title of the Paris printing gives the date and the book: propositions extracted from a book printed in French in several volumes, *Le Nouveau Testament en françois avec des réflexions morales sur chaque verset*, in editions of 1693, 1694 and 1699. The author is not named in that title.

The propositions are quoted from the book and concern grace, the church, the reading of Scripture and excommunication. The censure at the end is given to the whole list together: the propositions are condemned as, among other things, false, scandalous, rash, injurious to the church, suspect of heresy and heretical "respectively", without saying which note falls on which proposition, and as renewing the propositions of Jansen in the sense in which those were condemned.

This record is distinct from the jubilee bull of 1343 that begins with the same word, [[source:clement-vi-unigenitus]].
