Teresa says in her prologue that her confessors commanded her to give this account of her way of prayer and of what she took to be the workings of grace in her. It is not a diary. Chapters 32 to 36 narrate the founding of St Joseph's; in the prologue to her [[source:teresa-book-of-the-foundations]] she says that her confessor García of Toledo ordered her to write that history in 1562, the year of the foundation itself. In chapter 32 she speaks of a vision "nearly six years ago", so that the text as it stands was finished some years after 1562.

The book is the only source for much that it tells, above all for her inner experience, which it reports as she understood it. For outward events, such as the opposition of the city of Ávila and the lawsuit that followed, she is a participant writing within a few years. The editors' footnotes in the translation linked here, which supply names and dates she leaves out, are later scholarship and are not part of her text.
