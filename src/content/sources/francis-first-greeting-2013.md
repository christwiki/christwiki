The text is a transcript of what [[person:francis]] said to the crowd in St Peter's Square on Wednesday 13 March 2013. He speaks throughout as bishop of Rome, prays for his predecessor, whom he calls "our Bishop Emeritus", and asks the people to pray for him before he gives the blessing.
