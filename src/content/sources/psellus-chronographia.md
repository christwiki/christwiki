Psellus was born after Basil II's great wars and wrote about him from what he had heard. On the civil war of 987–989 he notes that a picked force of "Scythians" from the Taurus had lately come to the emperor's aid and was used against the rebels (1.13). He does not say how the emperor obtained them; that is supplied by [[source:yahya-of-antioch-history]].
