The act's own title is "Act Ratifying the Confession of Faith and settleing Presbyterian Church Government". The king, queen and estates declare that they ratify and establish the confession read in their presence "as the publick and avowed Confession of this Church", containing the sum and substance of the doctrine of the Reformed churches, and that they confirm the government of the church by kirk sessions, presbyteries, provincial synods and general assemblies. The text of the Confession follows the act; it is that of the Westminster Assembly, beginning with the chapter on Holy Scripture.

It is cited in [[event:westminster-assembly]] for the legal standing the Confession received in Scotland.
