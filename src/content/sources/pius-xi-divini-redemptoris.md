The letter is addressed to the bishops of the whole Catholic Church. It describes the doctrine of communism and its effects in Russia, Mexico and Spain, sets against it the church's teaching on society, and forbids Catholics to collaborate with it. It is cited in [[event:mit-brennender-sorge]] to show what else the pope published in the same week.
