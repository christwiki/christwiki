The law is dated at Roncaglia in November 1158 and orders its own insertion among the imperial constitutions. It protects scholars on the road and in the places where they study, forbids holding them liable for the debts of their countrymen, and gives a scholar who is sued the choice of his judge.

It names no city. Munro, in the note that introduces his translation, says that it is generally taken to have been made for [[place:bologna]] and may have been obtained by the Bolognese doctors of law, but that it was granted to students in general.
