[[person:martin-luther]] wrote this preface for the first volume of his collected Latin works. In it he looks back over the years 1517 to 1521: the indulgence preaching, his letters to the bishops, the theses, the hearing before Cardinal Cajetan, the Leipzig debate and the summons to Worms. He also describes how his reading of Romans 1:17 changed.

It is a first-hand account, and it is a memory set down twenty-eight years later by a man who asks his readers to excuse how much he once conceded to the pope. Where it can be compared with letters written at the time, the letters are the better evidence for dates and sequence.
