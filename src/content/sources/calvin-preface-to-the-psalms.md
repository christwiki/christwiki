Comparing his trials to David's, [[person:john-calvin]] gives here a connected account of his own life: his father's plans for him, what he calls a "sudden conversion", his withdrawal from France to Basel, the first *Institutes*, the night in Geneva on which [[person:guillaume-farel]] threatened him with God's curse if he left, his expulsion and his unwilling return.

It is first-hand, and it was written some twenty years after the events it describes, by a man explaining his calling to his readers. It gives no dates and few names. The registers of the city of Geneva supply the dates.
