The page gives the secular name of [[person:kirill-of-moscow]], Vladimir Mikhailovich Gundyaev, his birth in Leningrad on 20 November 1946 as the son and grandson of priests, his chairmanship of the Department for External Church Relations from 1989 to 2009, his election as patriarch by a local council of the Russian Orthodox Church on 27 January 2009 and his enthronement on 1 February 2009. It is his own church's record and is used in this wiki for dates and offices.
