[[person:catherine-booth]] takes the objections to women preaching one at a time. She argues that custom is being mistaken for nature, that 1 Corinthians 11 assumes that women prayed and prophesied in the assembly, that the silence required in 1 Corinthians 14 concerns disorderly questioning, and that the prophecy of Joel quoted at Pentecost and Paul's words in Galatians 3:28 put women beside men in the work of the gospel.

The preface says that the argument had first appeared as *Female Teaching* and that this edition drops the controversial passages. The Salvation Army's own chronology gives 1859 for her first pamphlet. It is her own statement of the case for women preachers.
