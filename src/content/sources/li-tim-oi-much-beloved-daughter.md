Ted Harrison interviewed [[person:florence-li-tim-oi]] in Toronto in the spring of 1984, partly in Cantonese and partly in English, and built the book around her words. Her recollections are given in quotation marks and his narrative links them. For the ordination itself the book is her memory at a distance of forty years.

The book also prints documents of the time: Bishop [[person:ronald-hall]]'s letters to Archbishop William Temple of 4 June 1943 and 27 January 1944, in chapter 4, and the resolution of the Chinese House of Bishops of March 1946. Those are contemporary evidence, though they are quoted here in extracts chosen by the author. See [[event:florence-li-tim-oi]].
