Eadmer was a monk of Canterbury who travelled with Anselm in his exile. For those years he writes as an eyewitness: he speaks of "our dwelling" when he describes the hilltop village in Italy where the Cur Deus Homo was finished. For the earlier years at [[place:bec]] he reports what he was told, and says of one story that Anselm himself used to tell it.

The work is a saint's life written by a devoted disciple, and it includes miracles. It is used here for the outline of Anselm's career and for what it says about how the books came to be written.
