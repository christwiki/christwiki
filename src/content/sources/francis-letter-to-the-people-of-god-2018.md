[[person:francis]] wrote the letter in August 2018. Its first section refers to a report made public "in recent days" that detailed the experience of at least a thousand survivors over about seventy years; the letter does not name it, and the figures match the report of the Pennsylvania grand jury ([[source:pennsylvania-grand-jury-report-2018]]). The pope acknowledges that the church did not act in time, and calls the whole church to prayer and fasting. The letter states an attitude and makes no law.
