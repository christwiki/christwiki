The page records that the federation was established in 1947 and held its first assembly at [[place:lund]] in Sweden from 30 June to 6 July of that year, attended by 47 member churches from 26 countries, with Anders Nygren of Lund as its first president. Its list of presidents gives [[person:christian-krause]] the years 1997–2003 and [[person:munib-younan]] the years 2010–2017. It is used in this wiki for those dates.
