The introduction says that more than 3,000 people from about 170 countries had gathered in Manila in July 1989, fifteen years after the congress at Lausanne. The twenty-one affirmations end with the call for the whole church to take the whole gospel to the whole world. The exposition says that evangelism is primary and that words and deeds belong together, and notes that evangelical attitudes to the Roman Catholic and Orthodox churches differ widely.
