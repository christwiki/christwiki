The dedication is to Haimeric, cardinal deacon and chancellor of the Roman church, who had asked [[person:bernard-of-clairvaux]] a question; Bernard styles himself "called Abbot of Clairvaux". The treatise therefore belongs to his years as abbot. Its closing chapters reuse a letter that Bernard had written to the Carthusians.

It is short and argues largely from verses of Scripture. Its subject is the growth of love by stages, from the love of self with which everyone begins.
