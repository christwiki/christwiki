The letter, *Sendbrief vom Dolmetschen*, answers a friend who had asked two questions: why [[person:martin-luther]] had written "by faith alone" in Romans 3, when the word "alone" is not in Paul's text, and whether the departed saints intercede for the living. Luther dated it from "the Wilderness" on 8 September 1530, and it was published with a preface by Wenceslas Link.

Most of it concerns translation. Luther grants that the word is in neither the Greek nor the Latin, argues that German idiom and Paul's meaning require it, and describes how he and his helpers worked. It is his own account of his method, written with open contempt for his critics, whom he calls papists and asses throughout.
