Piccolomini, then bishop of Siena, wrote to his friend Cardinal Carvajal in [[place:rome]]. He confirms what the cardinal had heard about "that miraculous man" seen at [[place:frankfurt]]. He had not seen whole Bibles, he says, but several quires of different books, in very clean and correct lettering that could be read without glasses. Witnesses told him that 158 copies had been finished, though some said 180, and he feared he could not buy one because buyers had been found before the volumes were complete.

The passage quoted does not name the man or the place of printing. It fixes a date by which sheets of the Bible were on show, and it gives the figures then being reported for the number of copies. See [[event:gutenberg-bible]].
