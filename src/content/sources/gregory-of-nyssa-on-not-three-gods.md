Ablabius had put the question in this form: Peter, James and John share one human nature and are called three men, so why are Father, Son and Spirit, who share one divine nature, not three gods? [[person:gregory-of-nyssa]] replies in two ways. Strictly, he says, even "three men" is a loose way of speaking, since the nature is one. More importantly, the three do not act separately: every operation of God toward creation begins from the Father, proceeds through the Son and is completed in the Holy Spirit, so that there is one action and one God. The persons differ only in that one is the cause and the others are from the cause.

The work is undated. It is evidence for Gregory's own teaching.
