Kritoboulos took service under the Ottomans, governed his native Imbros for them from 1456 to 1466, and wrote a history of [[person:mehmed-ii]] covering 1451 to 1467. His account is more favourable to the sultan than any other Greek work and is detailed about the Ottoman dispositions, the guns and the assault. He also mourns the city, and gives figures for the dead and enslaved.

This wiki has not been able to cite an Ottoman eyewitness of 1453 in an openly available translation, and uses Kritoboulos for the view from the sultan's side, with that limitation stated. See [[event:fall-of-constantinople]].
