The conference of 1878 published its conclusions as a letter that embodies the reports of its committees. The report on the Anglican Church's relation to the Old Catholics and others who had left the Roman communion contains a short statement of the principles on which the Church of England reformed itself and a warning about the act of the Vatican Council.

It is cited as the common voice of the Anglican bishops eight years after the definition of papal infallibility.
