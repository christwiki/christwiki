summary: "The Mediterranean world breaks apart. Islam rises, missionaries carry the faith to northern and eastern Europe and to China, and Greek East and Latin West drift toward schism."
---

Monasteries preserve learning and send out missionaries: Boniface to Germany, Ansgar to Scandinavia, Cyril and Methodius to the Slavs. The Church of the East reaches China. In Byzantium the controversy over icons is settled; in the West the papacy allies with the Franks. The mutual excommunications of 1054 mark the estrangement of Rome and Constantinople.
