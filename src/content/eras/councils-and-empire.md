With Constantine the church moves from the margins to the centre of public life. Nicaea, Constantinople, Ephesus and Chalcedon produce the creeds still confessed today; Chalcedon also leaves lasting divisions with the churches of Egypt, Syria, Armenia and Persia. Jerome, Augustine and the Cappadocians shape theology for centuries.
