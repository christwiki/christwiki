The Western church reaches the height of its institutional power and then its deepest crisis, with rival popes and councils. Francis, Dominic and Aquinas, Palamas and Sergius of Radonezh belong to these centuries, as do Wycliffe and Hus. At the end of the era the faith crosses to the Americas and into central Africa.
