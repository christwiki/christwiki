The first eleven chapters of Genesis set the stage for everything that follows: a good creation, humanity made in God's image, a rupture, and a world that needs mending. Later Scripture returns to these chapters constantly. Christian traditions read them in different ways, from literal history to theological narrative, and this wiki places them at the head of the timeline without assigning years.
