The exodus is the founding event of Israel's faith and the pattern the New Testament uses for redemption. Scholars who accept a historical exodus place it either about 1446 BC, from the figure in 1 Kings 6:1, or in the thirteenth century BC, from archaeology and Egyptian records. Around 1208 BC the Merneptah Stele names a people called Israel in Canaan.
