The four Gospels are the main sources, with Paul's letters as the earliest written witness and brief notices in Josephus and Tacitus. The Gospels are not diaries: they arrange Jesus' words and deeds to proclaim who he is, so the order of events inside the ministry is approximate. The crucifixion is dated to AD 30 or 33.
