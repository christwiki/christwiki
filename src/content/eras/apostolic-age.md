Acts and the letters of Paul are the chief sources, anchored by a few fixed points: the death of Herod Agrippa in AD 44, Gallio's term in Corinth in 51–52, the fire of Rome in 64 and the fall of Jerusalem in 70. By the end of the century there are Christian communities from Syria to Italy.
