After a period of local leaders called judges, Israel asks for a king. When that period began depends on the date of the exodus: about 1375 BC on the early date, about 1200 BC on the late one. David makes Jerusalem his capital and receives the promise of a lasting dynasty; Solomon builds the temple. Dates rest on the Bible's regnal figures, counted back from later reigns that Assyrian records fix.
