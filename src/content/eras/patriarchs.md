Genesis 12–50 follows four generations from Mesopotamia to Canaan and finally to Egypt. The dates are traditional: they are worked out from the Bible's own figures, and no inscription names the patriarchs. The promises made here, above all the covenant with Abraham, are the root from which Israel, and later the church, understood itself.
