summary: "How Christians have defined what they believe: the councils from Jerusalem and Nicaea onward, the creeds and confessions they produced, and the teachers who shaped them."
---

When disputes arose, the churches met in council. The first seven ecumenical councils, received by both East and West, defined the doctrines of the Trinity and of the person of Christ. Later councils and confessions belong to particular traditions. This thread sets them side by side and notes which churches accept which.
