Jesus was executed by a Roman governor, and for three centuries his followers had no legal standing. After Constantine the question was reversed: how far should rulers direct the church, or the church the rulers? This thread follows the answers given, from the Christian empire to the investiture contest, the wars of religion and the modern separation of church and state.
