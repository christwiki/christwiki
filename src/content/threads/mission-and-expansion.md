summary: "How a movement that began in Galilee reached every continent: Paul's journeys, the conversion of kingdoms, monks and friars, and the modern missionary movement."
---

From the commission given by the risen Jesus, the faith moved along trade routes east to Persia, India and China as early as it moved west to Rome. This thread follows that spread, the methods used and argued over, and the growth of churches led by the peoples who received the message.
