---
title: "Revival and Unity"
order: 14
summary: "Movements of spiritual renewal, from Pietism and Methodism to Pentecostalism, and the modern search to heal the divisions among the churches."
---

Since the seventeenth century, waves of renewal have crossed confessional lines and created new churches. In the twentieth century a second movement ran alongside them: the effort to recover visible unity, through councils of churches, formal dialogues and agreed statements on questions that had divided Christians for centuries.
