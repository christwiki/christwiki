title: "Monasticism and Religious Life"

summary: "Men and women who left ordinary life for prayer and service: the desert hermits, Benedict's Rule, Athos, the friars, the Jesuits, and the renewal movements that followed."
---

Monasticism began in the Egyptian desert at the end of the third century and became one of the most durable institutions of both Eastern and Western Christianity. Monasteries were centres of prayer, learning and mission, and repeated reforms of the church began inside them.
