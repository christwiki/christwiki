---

Differences of language, politics and theology separated the churches over many centuries. The Church of the East and the Oriental Orthodox churches parted from the imperial church in the fifth century; Rome and Constantinople broke communion in the eleventh. This thread follows the causes, the attempts at reunion, and the steps taken toward one another since 1965.
