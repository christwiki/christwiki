After a series of parables Matthew says that [[person:jesus]] spoke all these things to the crowds in parables and said nothing to them without one, and that this was to fulfil what was spoken through the prophet [[bible:Matt 13:34-35]]. The quotation is the second verse of a psalm [[bible:Ps 78:2]]. The psalm carries the name of Asaph in its heading, in the Hebrew and in the ancient Greek translation alike [[bible:Ps 78:1]] [[cite:lxx]]. Chronicles calls Asaph "the seer" [[bible:2 Chr 29:30]].

In the psalm the speaker calls on his people to hear his teaching and says that he will utter "dark sayings of old", things that the fathers have told and that will not be hidden from their children [[bible:Ps 78:1-4]]. What follows tells of God's deeds for Israel from Egypt to the choice of [[person:david]] [[bible:Ps 78:12, 70-72]].

Matthew's second line differs from the psalm's. For sayings "of old" he has things hidden "from the foundation of the world" [[bible:Ps 78:2; Matt 13:35]]. The ancient Greek translation has sayings "which have been from the beginning" [[cite:lxx]]. In Matthew, the New Revised Standard Version Updated Edition, to which the links here lead, has only "the foundation", without "of the world" [[bible:Matt 13:35]].
