[[person:paul]] goes on, "Therefore he says also in another psalm", and quotes one line. [[person:david]], he says, after serving the purpose of God in his own generation, fell asleep, was laid with his fathers and saw decay; he whom God raised up did not. Through this man, Paul tells his hearers, forgiveness of sins is proclaimed to them [[bible:Acts 13:16, 35-38]].

[[person:peter]] quotes four verses of the same psalm at Pentecost and argues from the tomb of David; Paul quotes the one line [[bible:Acts 2:1, 14, 25-31; 13:35; Ps 16:8-11]]. The psalm, and what its line says in the two translations and in the ancient Greek, are described under [[quotation:ps-16-8-in-acts-2-25]].
