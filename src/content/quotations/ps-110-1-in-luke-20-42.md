In Luke the question comes after some of the scribes have told [[person:jesus]] that he has spoken well, and when they no longer dare to ask him anything [[bible:Luke 20:39-40]]. He asks why it is said that the Christ is the son of [[person:david]]: "David himself says in the book of Psalms", and the opening of the psalm follows. "David therefore calls him Lord, so how is he his son?" [[bible:Luke 20:41-44]] Luke reports no answer; the next thing he tells is a warning to the disciples about the scribes [[bible:Luke 20:44-46]].

Luke names the book of Psalms, which Matthew and Mark do not [[bible:Luke 20:42; Matt 22:43; Mark 12:36]]. The psalm is described under [[quotation:ps-110-1-in-matt-22-43]].
