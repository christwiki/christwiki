The verse is the second of two quotations at the opening of Mark, under an introduction that the translations give differently: "in the prophets" in the World English Bible, with Isaiah named in the New Revised Standard Version Updated Edition, to which the links here lead [[bible:Mark 1:2-3]]. The first quotation, about a messenger, has its own entry: [[quotation:mal-3-1-in-mark-1-2]]. Mark goes on to tell that [[person:john-the-baptist|John]] came baptizing in the wilderness and preaching a baptism of repentance [[bible:Mark 1:4]].

In Isaiah 40 the voice calls for the way of the Lord to be prepared in the wilderness and a highway made in the desert for God [[bible:Isa 40:3]]. Like the ancient Greek translation, Mark's form has the voice itself crying in the wilderness [[cite:lxx]] [[bible:Mark 1:3]]. Where Isaiah speaks of a way for "our God", Mark has "his paths" [[bible:Isa 40:3; Mark 1:3]].

The passage in its own setting is described under [[quotation:isa-40-3-in-matt-3-3]].
