In Mark, [[person:jesus]] is walking in the temple when the chief priests, the scribes and the elders ask him by what authority he acts, and he begins to speak to them in parables [[bible:Mark 11:27-28; 12:1]]. The tenants of a vineyard beat and kill the owner's servants and at last his beloved son; the owner will come, destroy them and give the vineyard to others. Jesus asks, "Haven't you even read this Scripture", and quotes the two verses about the stone. They try to seize him, because they perceive that he has told the parable against them, but they fear the crowd [[bible:Mark 12:1-12]].

The psalm, and the other places where the verse is quoted, are described under [[quotation:ps-118-22-in-matt-21-42]].
