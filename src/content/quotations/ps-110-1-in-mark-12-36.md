In Mark the question is put by [[person:jesus]] as he teaches in the temple, and it is about what the scribes say: how can the Christ be the son of [[person:david]]? "For David himself said in the Holy Spirit", and the opening of the psalm follows. "Therefore David himself calls him Lord, so how can he be his son?" Mark adds that the crowd heard him gladly [[bible:Mark 12:35-37]]. In Matthew the question is put to the Pharisees, who cannot answer it [[bible:Matt 22:41-46]].

The psalm, and its use elsewhere in the New Testament, are described under [[quotation:ps-110-1-in-matt-22-43]].
