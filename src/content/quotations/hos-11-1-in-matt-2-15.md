Matthew tells how [[person:joseph-of-nazareth|Joseph]] takes the child and his mother to Egypt by night and stays there until the death of [[person:herod-the-great|Herod]], and says that this was to fulfil what the Lord had spoken through the prophet [[bible:Matt 2:13-15]]. He does not name the prophet. The words are in [[person:hosea]] [[bible:Hos 11:1]].

In Hosea the sentence speaks of the past. The son is Israel, loved as a child and called out of Egypt, and the next verse tells how the people went away and sacrificed to the Baals [[bible:Hos 11:1-2]]. Exodus speaks of Israel in the same way: [[person:moses]] is to tell Pharaoh that Israel is the Lord's firstborn son and that he must let this son go [[bible:Exod 4:21-23]].

The ancient Greek translation of Hosea has "his children" where Matthew has "my son" [[cite:lxx]]. Matthew's wording agrees with the Hebrew text as modern translations render it [[bible:Hos 11:1; Matt 2:15]].
