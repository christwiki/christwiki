Matthew tells that after the supper [[person:jesus]] and the disciples sang a hymn and went out to the [[place:mount-of-olives]], and that he said to them that all of them would fall away because of him that night, "for it is written", with the words about the shepherd. He adds that after he is raised he will go before them into Galilee [[bible:Matt 26:26, 30-32]]. When Jesus is arrested, Matthew says, all the disciples left him and fled [[bible:Matt 26:56]].

In the book of Zechariah the Lord calls on a sword to awake against "my shepherd", a man near to him: "Strike the shepherd, and the sheep will be scattered". In all the land two parts will be cut off, and a third will be refined like silver and will call on the Lord's name [[bible:Zech 13:7-9]].

In Zechariah the words are a command, "Strike". In the Gospel they are the statement of the one who strikes, "I will strike" [[bible:Zech 13:7; Matt 26:31]]. The ancient Greek translation has a command too, and speaks of shepherds, more than one [[cite:lxx]]. Mark has the saying in the same place: [[quotation:zech-13-7-in-mark-14-27]].
