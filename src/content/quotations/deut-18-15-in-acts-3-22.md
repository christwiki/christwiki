Acts tells that after a man lame from birth was healed at the gate of the temple, the people ran together to [[person:peter]] and John in the porch called Solomon's, and Peter spoke to them [[bible:Acts 3:1-2, 11-12]]. He says that God has fulfilled what he announced through all the prophets, that the Christ should suffer, and calls on them to repent. Moses said, he goes on, that God would raise up a prophet like him from among their brothers, and the quotation follows [[bible:Acts 3:17-23]]. The translations differ on the words that introduce it, which are left out above: the World English Bible has Moses say it "to the fathers", and the New Revised Standard Version Updated Edition, to which the links here lead, does not [[bible:Acts 3:22]].

In Deuteronomy [[person:moses]] tells Israel that the Lord will raise up for them a prophet like him from among their brothers, and that they are to listen to him. The people had asked at Horeb not to hear the voice of God again. The Lord says that he will put his words in the prophet's mouth, and that he himself will call to account whoever does not listen [[bible:Deut 18:15-19]].

The close of the quotation differs from Deuteronomy. There God will require it of the one who does not listen; in Acts that one will be destroyed out of the people [[bible:Deut 18:19; Acts 3:23]]. Leviticus says of anyone who does not keep the day of atonement that he will be cut off from his people [[bible:Lev 23:27-29]].

Stephen quotes the promise of the prophet in his speech [[bible:Acts 7:37]]. In John's Gospel the Baptist is asked whether he is "the prophet", and some in the crowd say it of [[person:jesus]] [[bible:John 1:21; 7:40]].
