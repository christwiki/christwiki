Acts tells that on the next Sabbath almost the whole city gathered to hear the word, that the Jews contradicted what was said by [[person:paul]], and that Paul and [[person:barnabas]] answered boldly. The word of God had to be spoken to them first; since they thrust it away, "behold, we turn to the Gentiles. For so has the Lord commanded us", and the quotation follows. The Gentiles were glad when they heard it [[bible:Acts 13:44-48]]. Here the words are applied to the two speakers, as a command to them [[bible:Acts 13:47]].

In Isaiah 49 a speaker says that the Lord called him from the womb and said to him, "You are my servant; Israel". It is too light a thing, the Lord says, that the servant should raise up the tribes of Jacob: he is given as a light to the nations [[bible:Isa 49:1-6]]. The last line of the verse is not given above. In the World English Bible the servant is to be the Lord's salvation to the end of the earth; in the New Revised Standard Version Updated Edition, to which the links here lead, that salvation is to reach it [[bible:Isa 49:6]].

In Luke's Gospel, Simeon, holding the child [[person:jesus]], speaks of a light for revelation to the nations [[bible:Luke 2:25-32]].
