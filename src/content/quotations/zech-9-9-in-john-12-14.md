In John a great crowd that has come to the feast takes palm branches, goes out to meet [[person:jesus]] and hails him as the King of Israel. Jesus finds a young donkey and sits on it, "As it is written", and the quotation follows [[bible:John 12:12-15]]. John does not say here that anything was fulfilled. He says that the disciples did not understand these things at first, but that when Jesus was glorified they remembered that these things were written about him and that they had done these things to him [[bible:John 12:16]].

John's quotation is shorter than the verse in the book of Zechariah, and shorter than Matthew's quotation of it [[bible:John 12:15; Zech 9:9; Matt 21:5]]. It opens with "Don't be afraid, daughter of Zion", where Zechariah has a call to rejoice [[bible:Zech 9:9]]. It has one animal, a donkey's colt [[bible:John 12:15]].

In Matthew the same verse is introduced as fulfilled, and the verse itself is described there: [[quotation:zech-9-9-in-matt-21-4]].
