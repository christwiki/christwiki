In Matthew, [[person:jesus]] tells the chief priests and the elders of the people, in the temple, a parable. The tenants of a vineyard beat and kill the owner's servants and at last his son. His hearers say that the owner will destroy those men and let the vineyard to others. Jesus asks, "Did you never read in the Scriptures", and quotes the lines about the stone; he adds that the kingdom of God will be taken away from them [[bible:Matt 21:23, 33-43]]. Matthew says that the chief priests and the Pharisees perceived that he spoke about them [[bible:Matt 21:45]].

In the psalm the speaker asks for the gates of righteousness to be opened to him and gives thanks because the Lord has answered him and become his salvation. The stone that the builders rejected has become the cornerstone, and this is the Lord's doing [[bible:Ps 118:19-23]]. The psalm does not say who or what the stone is [[bible:Ps 118:22]]. A few lines on come the words that the crowds cry at the entry into [[place:jerusalem]], the blessing on him who comes in the name of the Lord [[bible:Ps 118:26; Matt 21:9]].

Mark and Luke have the quotation in the same place: [[quotation:ps-118-22-in-mark-12-10]], [[quotation:ps-118-22-in-luke-20-17]]. The verse about the stone is applied to Jesus again in Acts, where [[person:peter]] speaks before the rulers and elders, and in 1 Peter [[bible:Acts 4:5-11; 1 Pet 2:7]].
