In Luke's account the devil leads [[person:jesus]] to [[place:jerusalem]], sets him on the pinnacle of the temple and says, "If you are the Son of God, cast yourself down from here, for it is written", with the lines of the psalm about the angels. Jesus answers that it has been said that the Lord is not to be tested, and the devil leaves him for a time [[bible:Luke 4:9-13]]. This is the third temptation in Luke and the second in Matthew [[bible:Luke 4:3, 5, 9; Matt 4:3, 5, 8]].

Luke's quotation keeps the words "to guard you", which Matthew's does not have. Neither has "in all your ways" [[bible:Luke 4:10; Matt 4:6; Ps 91:11]]. The psalm is described under [[quotation:ps-91-11-in-matt-4-6]].
