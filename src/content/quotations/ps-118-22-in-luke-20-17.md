In Luke, [[person:jesus]] tells the parable of the vineyard to the people. When he says that the owner will come, destroy the tenants and give the vineyard to others, they protest. He looks at them and asks what then is this that is written, and quotes the verse about the stone. He adds that everyone who falls on that stone will be broken to pieces. The scribes and the chief priests know that he has told the parable against them [[bible:Luke 20:9-19]].

Luke quotes the verse about the stone alone. Matthew and Mark add the next verse of the psalm, that this is the Lord's doing [[bible:Luke 20:17; Matt 21:42; Mark 12:10-11; Ps 118:22-23]]. The psalm is described under [[quotation:ps-118-22-in-matt-21-42]].
