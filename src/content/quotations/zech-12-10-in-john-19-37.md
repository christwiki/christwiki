John says that the soldiers' not breaking the legs of [[person:jesus]] happened so that the Scripture might be fulfilled. He adds, "Again another Scripture says", and quotes a line about looking on one who was pierced [[bible:John 19:36-37]]. He has just described a spear thrust into Jesus's side, to which, he says, one who saw it has testified [[bible:John 19:34-35]].

The line is from an oracle about [[place:jerusalem]] in the book of Zechariah. In it the Lord says that he will defend the city, seek to destroy the nations that come against it, and pour out a spirit of supplication on the house of David and the people of Jerusalem; they will look on one who has been pierced and mourn for him as one mourns for a firstborn [[bible:Zech 12:8-10]].

The versions differ on the words. The World English Bible has "they will look to me whom they have pierced", with the Lord speaking. The New Revised Standard Version Updated Edition, to which the links here lead, has "the one" where that has "me" [[bible:Zech 12:10]]. The ancient Greek translation reads "they shall look upon me, because they have mocked me" [[cite:lxx]]. John has "him" and "pierced" [[bible:John 19:37]].

Revelation has similar words of Jesus Christ coming with the clouds: every eye will see him, "including those who pierced him", and all the tribes of the earth will mourn over him [[bible:Rev 1:5-7]].
