John says, after telling how the legs of [[person:jesus]] were left unbroken and how his side was pierced with a spear, that "these things happened" so that the Scripture might be fulfilled [[bible:John 19:32-36]]. He adds, "Again another Scripture says", and quotes a line about looking on one who was pierced [[bible:John 19:37]]. To the spear thrust, he says, one who saw it has testified [[bible:John 19:34-35]].

The line is from an oracle in the book of Zechariah, headed as the word of the Lord concerning Israel, which speaks of [[place:jerusalem]] [[bible:Zech 12:1-2]]. It says that the Lord will defend the people of Jerusalem, and in it the Lord says that he will seek to destroy the nations that come against the city and will pour out a spirit of supplication on the house of David and the people of Jerusalem; they will look on one who has been pierced and mourn for him as for a firstborn [[bible:Zech 12:8-10]].

The versions differ on the words. The World English Bible has "they will look to me whom they have pierced", with the Lord speaking. The New Revised Standard Version Updated Edition, to which the links here lead, has "the one" where that has "me" [[bible:Zech 12:10]]. The ancient Greek translation reads "they shall look upon me, because they have mocked me" [[cite:lxx]]. In John the World English Bible has "him" and "pierced"; the New Revised Standard Version Updated Edition has "the one" there too [[bible:John 19:37]].

Revelation has similar words of Jesus Christ coming with the clouds: every eye will see him, "including those who pierced him", and all the tribes of the earth will mourn over him [[bible:Rev 1:5-7]].
