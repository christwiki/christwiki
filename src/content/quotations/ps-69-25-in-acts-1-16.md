Acts tells that after [[person:jesus]] had been taken up, [[person:peter]] stood up among the disciples, about a hundred and twenty of them, and said that the Scripture had to be fulfilled which the Holy Spirit spoke through [[person:david]] about [[person:judas-iscariot|Judas]], who guided those who seized Jesus. After words about the field that Judas got with his reward, the speech goes on, "For it is written in the book of Psalms", and two quotations follow, joined by "and" [[bible:Acts 1:9, 15-20]].

The first is from Psalm 69. There the speaker, who bears reproach for God's sake, asks God to pour out his anger on his enemies, and says, "Let their habitation be desolate" [[bible:Ps 69:4, 7, 18-25]]. The psalm speaks of many, "their". In Acts the line is said of one man, "his" [[bible:Ps 69:25; Acts 1:20]]. The ancient Greek translation has the plural, as the Hebrew text, as modern translations render it, does [[bible:Ps 69:25]] [[cite:lxx]].

The second quotation has its own entry: [[quotation:ps-109-8-in-acts-1-16]]. John quotes another verse of Psalm 69: [[quotation:ps-69-9-in-john-2-17]]. Matthew tells of the end of Judas with a different Scripture: [[quotation:zech-11-12-in-matt-27-9]].
