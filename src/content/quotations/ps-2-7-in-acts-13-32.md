Acts tells how [[person:paul]] and his companions come to [[place:pisidian-antioch|Antioch of Pisidia]], go into the synagogue on the Sabbath, and are invited to speak after the reading of the law and the prophets [[bible:Acts 13:14-16]]. Paul says that the people of Jerusalem and their rulers had [[person:jesus]] put to death and that God raised him. He goes on: we bring you good news of the promise made to the fathers, that God has fulfilled it for their children by raising Jesus, "as it is also written in the second psalm" [[bible:Acts 13:27-33]]. The word "fulfilled" is said of the promise; the psalm is quoted in support with "as it is also written" [[bible:Acts 13:32-33]].

In the psalm the speaker tells of the Lord's decree, "You are my son", and the nations are promised to him as his inheritance. In the verse before, the Lord says that he has set his king on Zion [[bible:Ps 2:6-8]].

The translations render the second half of the line differently, in the psalm and in Acts alike. The World English Bible has "Today I have become your father"; the New Revised Standard Version Updated Edition, to which the links here lead, has "begotten" [[bible:Ps 2:7; Acts 13:33]].

Hebrews quotes the same line twice [[bible:Heb 1:5; 5:5]]. Paul's speech goes on with two more quotations: [[quotation:isa-55-3-in-acts-13-34]] and [[quotation:ps-16-10-in-acts-13-35]].
