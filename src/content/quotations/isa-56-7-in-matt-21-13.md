Matthew tells how [[person:jesus]] enters the temple, drives out those who sell and buy there, overturns the tables of the money changers and says to them, "It is written", with the words about the house of prayer. The den of robbers follows as his own charge against them [[bible:Matt 21:12-13]].

Both halves are words of Scripture. In Isaiah 56 the Lord promises to bring to his holy mountain the foreigners who join themselves to him and hold fast his covenant, and to accept their offerings, "for my house will be called a house of prayer for all peoples" [[bible:Isa 56:6-7]]. In the book of [[person:jeremiah]] the prophet is told to stand in the gate of the Lord's house and speak to those who steal, murder and swear falsely and then come and stand in that house: has it become a den of robbers in their eyes? [[bible:Jer 7:1-2, 9-11]] The speech goes on to threaten the house with what was done to Shiloh [[bible:Jer 7:12-14]].

Matthew's quotation stops before the words "for all peoples" [[bible:Matt 21:13; Isa 56:7]]. Mark's has them [[bible:Mark 11:17]]: see [[quotation:isa-56-7-in-mark-11-17]], and for Luke [[quotation:isa-56-7-in-luke-19-46]]. John tells of the clearing of the temple with another Scripture: [[quotation:ps-69-9-in-john-2-17]].
