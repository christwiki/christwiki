In Luke, at the table after the supper, [[person:jesus]] reminds the disciples that when he sent them out without purse or bag they lacked nothing, and tells them that now whoever has none should sell his cloak and buy a sword. "For I tell you that this which is written must still be fulfilled in me", he says, and quotes the line about being counted with transgressors [[bible:Luke 22:14, 20, 35-37]]. The sentence that follows the quotation is left out of the words given above, because the translations render it differently: in the World English Bible, "that which concerns me has an end"; in the New Revised Standard Version Updated Edition, what is written about him "is being fulfilled" [[bible:Luke 22:37]]. The disciples answer that they have two swords [[bible:Luke 22:38]].

The line is from the end of the passage about the servant in Isaiah 53: he poured out his life to death and was counted with the transgressors, yet he bore the sin of many and made intercession for the transgressors [[bible:Isa 52:13; 53:11-12]]. Luke tells afterwards that two criminals were crucified with Jesus, one on his right and one on his left [[bible:Luke 23:32-33]].

In the World English Bible the same line stands in Mark as well, as a comment on the two robbers: "The Scripture was fulfilled". The New Revised Standard Version Updated Edition, to which the links here lead, does not have that verse [[bible:Mark 15:27-29]]. Who the servant of Isaiah 53 is has been read in more than one way; the readings are set out under [[event:prophecies-of-return|the prophecy of the suffering servant]].
