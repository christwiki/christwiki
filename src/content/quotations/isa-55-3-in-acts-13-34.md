In his speech at Antioch of Pisidia [[person:paul]] says that God raised [[person:jesus]] from the dead, no more to return to corruption, and that he has spoken thus, and one sentence follows [[bible:Acts 13:14-16, 33-34]].

In Isaiah 55 a speaker calls on those who hear to come to him, that they may live, and promises to make an everlasting covenant with them, tied to [[person:david]]. The next verse says that he was given as a witness and a leader to the peoples [[bible:Isa 55:3-4]]. Further on the chapter marks words as the Lord's [[bible:Isa 55:8]]. Isaiah has the making of a covenant where the quotation in Acts has "I will give you" [[bible:Isa 55:3; Acts 13:34]] [[cite:lxx]].

The translations word the promise differently. In Isaiah the World English Bible has "the sure mercies of David", as the ancient Greek translation has [[cite:lxx]]; the New Revised Standard Version Updated Edition, to which the links here lead, has a "steadfast, sure love" for David [[bible:Isa 55:3]]. In Acts the first has "the holy and sure blessings of David" and the second "the holy promises" made to him, with no word for "sure" [[bible:Acts 13:34]].

Paul joins a line of Psalm 16 to these words: [[quotation:ps-16-10-in-acts-13-35]].
