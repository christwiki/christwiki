At Pentecost [[person:peter]] says that God raised [[person:jesus]], that he has been exalted at the right hand of God, and that he has poured out what the crowd sees and hears. "For David didn't ascend into the heavens, but he says himself", and the opening of the psalm follows. Let the whole house of Israel know, Peter ends, that God has made this Jesus, whom they crucified, both Lord and Christ [[bible:Acts 2:14, 32-36]].

In the Gospels Jesus quotes the verse to ask how the Christ can be the son of [[person:david]] [[bible:Matt 22:41-45]]. In Acts it is quoted for his exaltation at the right hand of God [[bible:Acts 2:33-35]]. The psalm is described under [[quotation:ps-110-1-in-matt-22-43]].
