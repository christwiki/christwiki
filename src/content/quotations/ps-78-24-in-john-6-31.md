In John, on the day after the feeding, the crowd comes to [[place:capernaum]] looking for [[person:jesus]]. He tells them to work for the food that remains to eternal life and to believe in him whom God has sent. They ask what sign he does, that they may see and believe: "Our fathers ate the manna in the wilderness. As it is written, 'He gave them bread out of heaven to eat.'" [[bible:John 6:22-31]] Here it is the crowd that quotes Scripture, in asking for a sign.

Jesus answers the quotation. It was not [[person:moses]] who gave the bread out of heaven; his Father gives the true bread out of heaven; and "I am the bread of life" [[bible:John 6:32-35]].

John does not say where the words are written [[bible:John 6:31]]. In Exodus the Lord says that he will rain bread from heaven, and Moses tells the people that the manna is the bread the Lord has given them to eat [[bible:Exod 16:4, 15]]. Psalm 78 retells it: the people did not believe, and yet God rained down manna on them to eat and gave them food from the sky, or "the grain of heaven" as the New Revised Standard Version Updated Edition has it [[bible:Ps 78:22-24]]. In the ancient Greek translation of the psalm he "gave them the bread of heaven" [[cite:lxx]]. A prayer in Nehemiah says of the same years that God gave bread from heaven for the people's hunger [[bible:Neh 9:15]].
