---
name: Elijah
altNames: [Elias, Elijah the Tishbite]
role: Prophet in Israel, ninth century BC
summary: Prophet from Gilead who opposed the worship of Baal under Ahab and Jezebel. Kings says he was taken up to heaven in a whirlwind. Malachi promises his return, and the Gospels link that promise to John the Baptist.
---

Elijah the Tishbite appears without introduction in the reign of [[person:ahab]], announcing a drought [[bible:1 Kings 17:1]]. Kings tells of his stay with the widow of Zarephath, the contest with the prophets of Baal on [[place:mount-carmel]], and his flight to Horeb [[bible:1 Kings 17:8-19:18]]; see [[event:elijah-on-carmel]]. He condemned Ahab and [[person:jezebel]] for the judicial murder of Naboth [[bible:1 Kings 21:17-24]] and foretold the death of Ahab's son Ahaziah [[bible:2 Kings 1:2-4, 17]]. At the Jordan, the account says, he was carried up to heaven in a whirlwind, leaving [[person:elisha]] as his successor [[bible:2 Kings 2:1-14]]; see [[event:elisha-succeeds-elijah]].

Malachi closes with the promise that Elijah will be sent before the day of the Lord [[bible:Mal 4:5-6]]. In the Gospels Jesus says of John the Baptist that he is the Elijah who was to come [[bible:Matt 11:13-14; 17:10-13]], though John himself, when asked, says he is not Elijah [[bible:John 1:21]]. Elijah appears with Moses at the transfiguration [[bible:Matt 17:1-3]], and James holds up his prayer as an example [[bible:James 5:17-18]].
