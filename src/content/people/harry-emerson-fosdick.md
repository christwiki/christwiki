Fosdick preached "Shall the Fundamentalists Win?" at the First Presbyterian Church, New York, on 21 May 1922; the printed text was taken from a stenographic report [[cite:fosdick-title]]. He said that the Baptist and Presbyterian churches were the two most affected by the Fundamentalist movement, and that many Christians were trying to hold the new knowledge of science and history together with their faith [[cite:fosdick-4]]. He spoke, he said, "from the viewpoint of liberal opinions", and warned liberals too against intolerance [[cite:fosdick-11]]. See [[event:the-fundamentals]].
