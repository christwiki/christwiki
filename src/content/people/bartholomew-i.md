Dimitrios Archontonis was born on the island of Imbros on 29 February 1940 [[cite:bio]]. He was ordained a deacon in 1961 and a priest in 1969, became metropolitan of Philadelphia in 1973 and of Chalcedon in 1990, and was elected Ecumenical Patriarch on 22 October 1991 [[cite:bio]].

The encyclical of the [[event:holy-and-great-council]] names him as the one who convoked the council and carries his signature as chairman [[cite:crete]]. The tomos of 6 January 2019, issued in his name as archbishop of Constantinople and Ecumenical Patriarch, declared the Orthodox Church in Ukraine autocephalous [[cite:tomos]]; the synod of the Russian Orthodox Church had declared communion with his patriarchate impossible in October 2018 [[cite:moscow]]. See [[event:autocephaly-of-ukraine]].

On 28 November 2025 he and Pope [[person:leo-xiv]] prayed together at İznik, where, the pope said, Bartholomew had decided the anniversary of Nicaea should be kept [[cite:iznik]]. The two signed a joint declaration at the Phanar the next day [[cite:declaration]]; see [[event:nicaea-1700]].
