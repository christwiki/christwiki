Thomas says that he came to [[place:deventer]] to study in his youth and that Florentius, the leader of the brothers there, kept him for a time in his house, put him to school and found him lodging [[cite:deventer]]. The chronicle of Mount St Agnes records that Thomas Hemerken of Kempen, in the diocese of Cologne, son of John and Gertrude and brother of the first prior, was invested there on the feast of Corpus Christi in 1406 [[cite:invested]].

The brother who continued the chronicle writes that he died in 1471, on the feast of Saint James the Less, in his ninety-second year, having been a priest for more than fifty-seven years. He says that Thomas copied the complete Bible that the house used and many other books, and composed "divers little books" for the young [[cite:death]]. A volume in his hand, finished in 1441, contains the four books of the *Imitation of Christ* [[cite:autograph]]. See [[event:devotio-moderna]].
