Francke describes himself as minister at Glaucha, a suburb of [[place:halle]]. He tells how a gift left in the poor-box of his parsonage became the fund for a charity school at Easter 1695 [[cite:box]], and how in July 1698 the foundation of an orphan house was laid before he had the means to build it [[cite:building]]. The English editor of the Tranquebar letters calls him "Professor Franck at Hall" and says that gifts for the mission in India were sent to him to be forwarded [[cite:mission]]. See [[event:pietism]] and [[event:tranquebar-mission]].
