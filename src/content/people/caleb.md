Caleb son of Jephunneh represented the tribe of Judah among the twelve scouts [[bible:Num 13:6]]; he is also called a Kenizzite [[bible:Num 32:12]]. When the others said that Canaan could not be taken, he urged the people to go up at once [[bible:Num 13:30]]. For this he and [[person:joshua]] were exempted from the sentence that the whole generation would die in the wilderness, and he was promised the land he had walked on [[bible:Num 14:24]] [[bible:Num 14:30]].

Forty-five years later, at the age of eighty-five, he came to Joshua at [[place:gilgal]], reminded him of the promise made at [[place:kadesh-barnea]] and was given [[place:hebron]] [[bible:Josh 14:6-14]]. He drove out the three sons of Anak from it and gave his daughter Achsah in marriage to Othniel, who took the town of Debir [[bible:Josh 15:13-19]].
