Ananus was one of the five sons of the elder Ananus, all of whom, Josephus says, held the high priesthood as their father had. King [[person:herod-agrippa-ii]] appointed him in the interval after the death of the governor [[person:festus]]. Josephus calls him bold and insolent and a Sadducee. He convened a council, had [[person:james-the-just]] and others condemned as breakers of the law and stoned, and was removed from office after three months when the new governor and the king learned of it [[cite:james]].

Josephus wrote of him twice, and differently. In the *Jewish War* Ananus is the senior high priest who rallies the people of Jerusalem against the Zealots in the temple [[cite:leader]]. When the Idumaeans entered the city they killed him and threw out his body unburied. Josephus there praises him as a just man and a lover of peace, and says that the fall of the city began with his death [[cite:death]].
