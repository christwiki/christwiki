name: Joseph of Nazareth
altNames: [Saint Joseph, Joseph the carpenter]
role: Husband of Mary
summary: The husband of Mary, a descendant of David and a carpenter of Nazareth. In Matthew he is told in dreams to take Mary as his wife, to flee to Egypt with the child and to return. He does not appear in the accounts of the ministry of Jesus.
---

Matthew and Luke both trace Joseph's descent from [[person:david]] [[bible:Matt 1:1-16]] [[bible:Luke 3:23-31]]. In Matthew he is "a righteous man" who, finding [[person:mary]] with child before they lived together, resolves to end the betrothal quietly, until an angel tells him in a dream to take her as his wife and to name the child Jesus [[bible:Matt 1:18-25]]. Further dreams send him to Egypt with the child and his mother and bring him back to settle at [[place:nazareth]] [[bible:Matt 2:13-23]]. In Luke he goes with Mary to [[place:bethlehem]] for the census, presents the child in the temple, and searches [[place:jerusalem]] with her for the twelve-year-old Jesus [[bible:Luke 2:4-5, 22, 41-51]]; see [[event:birth-of-jesus]].

The people of Nazareth know [[person:jesus]] as "the carpenter's son" and as the son of Joseph [[bible:Matt 13:55]] [[bible:Luke 4:22]], as does the crowd at [[place:capernaum]] in John [[bible:John 6:42]]; Luke adds "as was supposed" [[bible:Luke 3:23]]. Joseph does not appear in the accounts of the ministry, and the Gospels do not tell of his death.
