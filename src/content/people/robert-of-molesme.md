The Exordium Parvum calls Robert the first abbot of Molesme, in the diocese of Langres. In 1098 he and some of his monks told the papal legate that they had kept the Rule of Benedict "lukewarmly and negligently" at Molesme and wished to keep it strictly elsewhere. A company of twenty-one monks went to [[place:citeaux]], where he received the abbot's staff of the new house from the bishop of Chalon [[cite:ep-1]].

The monks he had left appealed to Pope [[person:urban-ii]], and the legate, Hugh of Lyon, ordered Robert back to Molesme, with leave for any who wished to go with him. The legate's decree speaks of his "usual inconstancy". Robert gave up the staff, released the monks who stayed from their obedience to him, and returned; those who stayed elected the prior, Alberic, as their abbot [[cite:ep-5]].
