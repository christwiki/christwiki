Graham led the Christ for Greater Los Angeles campaign from 25 September to 20 November 1949, which drew the attention of the national press [[cite:timeline]]. He wrote in 1956 that six weeks before it he had settled his doubts about the Bible by accepting it in faith as the word of God, and that from then on his preaching rested on the words "The Bible says" [[cite:graham-1956]]. The Wheaton College archives trace the founding of *Christianity Today* to his discussions with L. Nelson Bell in 1954 [[cite:founding]]. See [[event:billy-graham]].
