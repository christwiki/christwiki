The first volume of *The Fundamentals* contains an essay by Torrey on the personality and deity of the Holy Spirit [[cite:fund-1]]. The statement of the two laymen in the twelfth volume names him among the original committee that supervised the series and as editor of volumes 11 and 12. It adds that he had for many years been superintendent of the Moody Bible Institute of Chicago, had left it for work as an evangelist in many countries, and was now dean of the Bible Institute of Los Angeles, whose magazine *The King's Business* he would edit as a continuation of the series [[cite:fund-12]].
