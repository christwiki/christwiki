Basil and the restored patriarch [[person:ignatius-of-constantinople]] asked Rome for the council that met at [[place:constantinople]] in 869–870 and condemned [[person:photius]]; the emperor and his sons signed its acts [[cite:first]]. Ten years later it was to Basil and his sons that Pope [[person:john-viii]] addressed the letter recognizing Photius as patriarch [[cite:terms]].

At [[event:council-of-879|the council of 879–880]] Basil stayed away from the earlier sessions. He told the bishops, when they came to him in the palace on 3 March 880, that he had done so in order that no one could say they had agreed out of fear of him [[cite:session6]]. He then signed the council's definition, confirming the seventh council, the promotion of Photius and the annulment of all that had been written or said against him [[cite:signature]]. In August 880 the pope thanked him for warships sent to defend the lands of Saint Peter and for letting the Roman see have the Bulgarian diocese [[cite:thanks]].
