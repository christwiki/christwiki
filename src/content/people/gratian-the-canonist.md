This entry rests on the book alone. Its title is *Concordia discordantium canonum*, "a harmony of discordant canons" [[cite:title]], and the passages in which the compiler speaks in his own voice are headed with the name Gratian. The first of them says that the human race is ruled by two things, natural law and custom, and defines natural law by the rule of the Gospel: do to others what you would have done to you [[cite:d1]] [[bible:Matt 7:12]].
