name: Silas
altNames: [Silvanus]
role: Prophet of the Jerusalem church; companion of Paul
summary: A leading member of the Jerusalem church who carried the council's letter to Antioch and then travelled with Paul through Asia Minor, Macedonia and Greece. Paul's letters call him Silvanus.
---

Silas was one of two "chief men among the brothers" whom the apostles and elders in [[place:jerusalem|Jerusalem]] sent to [[place:antioch|Antioch]] with the letter of the [[event:council-of-jerusalem]]. Acts calls him a prophet [[bible:Acts 15:22, 27, 32]]. After the break with [[person:barnabas|Barnabas]], [[person:paul]] chose him as his companion for the journey that took them to Macedonia [[bible:Acts 15:40]]. At [[place:philippi]] the two were beaten and imprisoned, and Acts counts Silas, like Paul, as a Roman citizen [[bible:Acts 16:19-40]]. He shared the work at [[place:thessalonica]] and Beroea and rejoined Paul at [[place:corinth]] [[bible:Acts 17:1-15]] [[bible:Acts 18:5]]. See [[event:second-missionary-journey]].

Paul names a Silvanus beside [[person:timothy|Timothy]] as his fellow preacher at Corinth and as co-sender of 1 Thessalonians, in the same places and at the same time as the Silas of Acts, and the two are generally taken to be one man [[bible:2 Cor 1:19]] [[bible:1 Thess 1:1]]. The opening of 2 Thessalonians names him too [[bible:2 Thess 1:1]]. 1 Peter is sent "through Silvanus, our faithful brother" [[bible:1 Pet 5:12]].
