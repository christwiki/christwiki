The publisher of the Athens edition of the *Philokalia* in 1893, addressing the monks of [[place:mount-athos]], the Holy Mountain from which Nicodemus is called "the Hagiorite", wrote that Nicodemus came from among them, that he spent his life on the publication of sacred books, and that he contributed much to the *Philokalia* when he published it with [[person:macarius-of-corinth]] [[cite:dedication]]. The catalogue record of the first edition names him as its author together with Macarius and gives his dates as 1749–1809 [[cite:copy]]. The letter of [[person:paisius-velichkovsky]] that describes the making of the collection speaks only of Macarius [[cite:paisius]]. See [[event:philokalia]].
