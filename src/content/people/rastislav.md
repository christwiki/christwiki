The Life of Constantine says that Rastislav took counsel with his nobles and sent to Michael III for a bishop and teacher, since his people had left paganism but had no one to explain the faith in their own tongue, and that he received Constantine with honour and gave him pupils [[cite:embassy]]. The Life of Methodius names him together with Svatopluk as sender of the embassy, and gives him first place among the princes to whom Pope [[person:hadrian-ii]]'s letter on the Slavonic liturgy is addressed [[cite:request]]. Neither Life tells how his reign ended. See [[event:cyril-and-methodius]].
