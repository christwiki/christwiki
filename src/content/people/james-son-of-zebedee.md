James and his brother [[person:john-the-apostle|John]] were fishing partners of Simon [[person:peter]]. [[person:jesus]] called them from their boat, and they left their father Zebedee to follow him [[bible:Mark 1:19-20]] [[bible:Luke 5:10]]. Mark says that Jesus named the two Boanerges, "Sons of Thunder" [[bible:Mark 3:17]]. With Peter, Mark says, they were present at the raising of Jairus's daughter, at the transfiguration, and in [[place:gethsemane]] [[bible:Mark 5:37; 9:2; 14:33]]. Luke tells that the brothers wanted to call down fire on a Samaritan village that refused Jesus [[bible:Luke 9:51-56]], and Mark that they asked for the seats at his right and left in his glory; Jesus answered that they would drink the cup that he drank [[bible:Mark 10:35-40]].

Acts reports that "King Herod", that is [[person:herod-agrippa-i]], killed James with the sword and then, at Passover, arrested Peter [[bible:Acts 12:1-3]]. [[person:josephus]] says that Agrippa ruled Judea for three years under [[person:claudius]] before he died, which places the execution between AD 41 and 44 [[cite:agrippa]]. He is to be distinguished from James the son of Alphaeus, another of the Twelve, and from [[person:james-the-just]], the brother of the Lord.
