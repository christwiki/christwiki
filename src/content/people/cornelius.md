Cornelius was a centurion of the Italian cohort stationed at [[place:caesarea-maritima]]. Acts describes him as devout and God-fearing with all his household, generous to the needy and well spoken of by the Jewish people [[bible:Acts 10:1-2, 22]]. Directed by an angel in a vision, he sent for [[person:peter]], who came from [[place:joppa]], preached in his house, and had him and those with him baptised after the Holy Spirit had come on them [[bible:Acts 10:3-48]]. See [[event:peter-and-cornelius]].

Peter defended what he had done before the church in [[place:jerusalem|Jerusalem]] and appealed to it again at the [[event:council-of-jerusalem]] [[bible:Acts 11:1-18]] [[bible:Acts 15:7-9]]. Acts says nothing further about Cornelius.
