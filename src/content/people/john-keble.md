[[person:john-henry-newman]] calls Keble the "true and primary author" of the movement of 1833 [[cite:newman]]. He says that Keble had won the highest honours of the university as a boy and had then turned to pastoral work in the country, and that his *Christian Year* appeared in 1827 [[cite:newman]].

On 14 July 1833 Keble preached the assize sermon at St Mary's, [[place:oxford]], published as *National Apostasy* [[cite:start]] [[cite:preached]]. It asks whether a nation that throws off the restraint of the church's rules does not deserve to be called apostate [[cite:sermon]]. See [[event:oxford-movement]]. He died at Bournemouth in March 1866 [[cite:death]].
