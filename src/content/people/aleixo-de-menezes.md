The acts of the synod he called style him "Dom Frey Aleixo de Menezes", archbishop metropolitan of [[place:goa]] and primate of the Indies. In his summons he says that Pope Clement VIII had charged him with the vacant bishopric of the Thomas Christians, that his orders were not obeyed there, and that he therefore left his own church and went to Malabar himself to bring them to obedience to Rome and to purge their books [[cite:summons]]. At the synod he was the first to swear the profession of faith, kneeling at the altar [[cite:profession]], and at its close he signed the decrees on his throne, after which the clergy and the lay representatives signed them at a table set in the church [[cite:signing]].

The Carmelite Giuseppe Sebastiani, writing some sixty years later, says that Menezes belonged to the order of St Augustine and calls him the "new apostle" of those peoples [[cite:sebastiani]]. The churches descended from the Thomas Christians judge his synod very differently; see [[event:synod-of-diamper]].
