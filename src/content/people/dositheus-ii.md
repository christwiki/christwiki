The acts of 1672 are headed as those of the synod held "under Dositheus, Patriarch of Jerusalem", and the preamble explains that the bishops had met for the dedication of the church of the Nativity at [[place:bethlehem]] [[cite:occasion]]. The confession opens in his name, "Dositheus, by the mercy of God, Patriarch of Jerusalem", and he signs first, declaring it to be the faith of the Eastern church [[cite:signed]]. See [[event:synod-of-jerusalem]].
