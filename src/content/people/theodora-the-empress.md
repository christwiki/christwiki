When Theophilus died, Theodora governed for their small son together with the eunuch Theoktistos, her brother Bardas and her uncle Manuel. The history compiled a century later at the Byzantine court makes her say that she had always wished to restore the images and had been held back by the strength of the other party [[cite:regency]]. The same history says that she made the pardon of her dead husband a condition, and swore that he had repented and kissed the icons before he died [[cite:pardon]].

The Synodikon gives eternal memory to the emperor Michael and to Theodora, his mother [[cite:synodikon]]. See [[event:triumph-of-orthodoxy]].
