Pius VI dated his letter *Charitas* at [[place:rome]] on 13 April 1791, in the seventeenth year of his pontificate [[cite:charitas-date]]. In it he recounts his letters to the king of France since July 1790 urging him not to approve the Civil Constitution of the Clergy [[cite:charitas-king]], pronounces that law heretical in many of its decrees and in others sacrilegious and schismatic [[cite:charitas-judgment]], and suspends clergy who had sworn to uphold it unless they retract within forty days [[cite:charitas-suspension]].

By the treaty of Tolentino of 19 February 1797 he made peace with the French Republic, renouncing Avignon and the Legations of Bologna, Ferrara and Romagna [[cite:tolentino]]. His successor [[person:pius-vii]] praised the firmness with which he had borne his misfortunes and recorded that he died after providing for the election of the next pope [[cite:diu-satis]].
