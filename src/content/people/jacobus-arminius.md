His children, dedicating his disputations to the magistrates of [[place:leiden]] after his death, call him a native of Oudewater and say that he had served the church of [[place:amsterdam]] as pastor for fifteen years before he was called to the university [[cite:children]].

In his declaration to the States of Holland he said that he disapproved of the teaching on predestination current in the churches and in the university, and gave his own: God decreed to save those who repent and believe, and chose particular persons on the ground of his foreknowledge of who would believe and persevere through grace [[cite:own]]. The nineteenth-century editor of his works, quoting the funeral oration of Petrus Bertius, puts his death on 19 October 1609 [[cite:life]]. The Synod of Dort named him and his followers as the source of the troubles in the Dutch churches [[cite:preface]]. See [[event:synod-of-dort]].
