[[person:philip-melanchthon]] records that Luther was born at Eisleben on 10 November. His mother remembered the day and the hour but not the year; his brother said that the family took it to be 1483. Melanchthon adds that Luther entered the house of the Augustinian friars at Erfurt and came to [[place:wittenberg]] in 1508 [[cite:birth]]. Luther himself wrote in 1545 that he had hated the phrase "the justice of God" in Romans until he understood it, from the words that the just live by faith, as the justice by which God makes the believer just [[cite:preface]] [[bible:Rom 1:17]].

His dispute with Rome began with [[event:ninety-five-theses]]. Pope [[person:leo-x]] declared him a heretic in January 1521 [[cite:decet]], and the Edict of Worms called him a member cut off from the church [[cite:edict]]; see [[event:diet-of-worms]]. His later work appears under [[event:luther-bible]], [[event:peasants-war]] and [[event:marburg-colloquy]]. Melanchthon told the students of Wittenberg that before four o'clock on 18 February 1546 Luther commended himself to God and died amid his prayers [[cite:death]]; the life adds that he was in his sixty-third year [[cite:birth]].
