Dallet, drawing on Korean records, calls Yi Byeok the chief instrument in bringing the gospel to Korea and tells how in 1777 he joined a group of scholars studying Christian books in a mountain temple [[cite:temple]]. When his friend [[person:yi-seung-hun]] was to travel to [[place:beijing]] in 1783, Yi Byeok urged him to seek out the missionaries and bring back books [[cite:mission]]. In 1784 Yi Seung-hun baptized him, and he took the name John the Baptist [[cite:baptism]]. The Catholic bishops of Korea treat his baptism as the starting point of their church [[cite:founders]].

Dallet reports that after the first arrests of 1785 he gave way to his father's pressure, and that he died of an epidemic in the spring of 1786, aged thirty-three [[cite:death]]; the year of his birth is reckoned from that age. See [[event:first-korean-baptism]].
