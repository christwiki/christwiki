Pachomius was born to pagan parents in the Thebaid. His Life says that he was conscripted at twenty, met Christians who fed the recruits, was baptised on his release and trained under the hermit Palamon [[cite:life-youth]]. At [[place:tabennisi]] he heard a call to build a monastery and gathered a community there under a rule [[cite:life-call]]. He died on 9 May in a year the Life does not name, which scholars place in 346 [[cite:life-death]].

Jerome names him, with his successors Theodore and Horsiesius, as the first to found monasteries of the common life in Egypt [[cite:jerome]]. Palladius reports seven thousand monks living under his rule about 420 [[cite:palladius]]. See [[event:pachomius]].
