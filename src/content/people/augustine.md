Augustine was born at Thagaste in Roman Africa and taught rhetoric at Carthage, Rome and Milan [[cite:birth]]. For nine years he followed the Manichaeans [[cite:manichee]]. His conversion in a garden at [[place:milan]] is told in his own *Confessions* [[cite:garden]], and his friend Possidius says that he was baptised by [[person:ambrose]] at Easter [[cite:birth]]; see [[event:conversion-of-augustine]]. His mother was [[person:monica]]. Back in Africa he was made presbyter and then bishop of [[place:hippo-regius]] [[cite:hippo]].

As bishop he signed the decisions of the African councils that listed the books of Scripture [[cite:council]] (see [[event:councils-of-hippo-and-carthage]]), began the *City of God* after [[event:sack-of-rome|the sack of Rome]] [[cite:city]], and for some ten years wrote against the teaching of [[person:pelagius]] [[cite:pelagians]] (see [[event:pelagian-controversy]]). He died on 28 August 430, aged seventy-six, in the third month of the Vandal siege of Hippo [[cite:age]] [[cite:death]]. The year of his birth, 354, is reckoned back from that age.
