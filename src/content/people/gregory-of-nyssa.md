Gregory was a younger brother of [[person:basil-of-caesarea]] and [[person:macrina]]. He says in his memoir of his sister that for a long time he could not visit her because the leaders of the opposing party kept driving him out of his own country [[cite:exile]]. In the short work *On Not Three Gods* he argues that Father, Son and Spirit are one God because their action is one, the persons being distinguished only as cause and caused [[cite:not-three]].

The law of 381 by which Theodosius handed the churches to the Nicene bishops names him, with the bishops of Caesarea and Melitene, as one of those with whom bishops of the region of Pontus must be in communion [[cite:law]], and Socrates records the same arrangement among the decisions of the council of Constantinople in that year [[cite:socrates]]. Jerome heard him read from his work against Eunomius and says that he was still writing in 392–393 [[cite:jerome]]. See [[event:cappadocian-fathers]].
