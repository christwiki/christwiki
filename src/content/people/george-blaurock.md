The chronicle of the Hutterian Brethren calls him George of the house of Jacob, a priest from Chur, and says that he got the name Blaurock from the blue coat he wore. It tells that he came to [[place:zurich]] and talked first with [[person:huldrych-zwingli]], was told of men more zealous than Zwingli, sought out [[person:conrad-grebel]] and [[person:felix-manz]], asked Grebel to baptise him, and then baptised the others [[cite:chronicle]]. A witness told the council in February 1525 that at a gathering in his house at Zollikon Blaurock had shared bread and wine with those who believed [[cite:zollikon]].

On 5 January 1527 the Zurich court had him whipped through the city and banished on oath, on pain of drowning if he returned [[cite:sentence]]. The *Martyrs Mirror* reports under the year 1529 that he was seized in Tyrol and burned not far from Klausen, charged with leaving the priesthood, rejecting infant baptism, the mass and confession, and teaching that the mother of Christ is not to be invoked [[cite:mirror]]. See [[event:anabaptists]].
