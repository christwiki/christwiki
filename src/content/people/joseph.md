Joseph is the first son of Rachel and the eleventh of [[person:jacob]] [[bible:Gen 29:31-30:24]]. His brothers sell him to traders when he is seventeen [[bible:Gen 37:2]] [[bible:Gen 37:28]]. In Egypt he rises from prison to rule the country under Pharaoh at thirty [[bible:Gen 41:39-46]], and the story of the famine and of his family's move to [[place:goshen]] is told in [[event:joseph-and-israel-in-egypt]]. He marries Asenath, daughter of a priest of On, and has two sons, Manasseh and Ephraim [[bible:Gen 41:50-52]]. Jacob adopts them as his own and sets the younger, Ephraim, first [[bible:Gen 48:5]] [[bible:Gen 48:17-20]]. Joseph forgives his brothers [[bible:Gen 50:19-21]] and dies in Egypt at 110 [[bible:Gen 50:26]]. [[person:moses]] takes his bones out of Egypt, and they are buried at [[place:shechem]] [[bible:Exod 13:19]] [[bible:Josh 24:32]].

Psalm 105 calls him a man sent ahead of Israel [[bible:Ps 105:16-22]], [[person:stephen]]'s speech retells how he brought his family to Egypt in the famine [[bible:Acts 7:9-14]], and Hebrews counts his instruction about his bones as an act of faith [[bible:Heb 11:22]]. His dates are worked out from the Bible's own figures; see the dating note at [[event:joseph-and-israel-in-egypt]].
