Alexander became bishop of [[place:alexandria]] after Achillas, in the peace that followed the persecution. When the presbyter [[person:arius]] opposed his teaching, he excommunicated him and wrote to bishops everywhere to explain why; Socrates preserves the letter [[cite:socrates]]. He attended the [[event:council-of-nicaea]], whose bishops commended him to his church as one who had laboured for peace at a great age [[cite:letter]], and whose sixth canon confirmed the authority of his see over Egypt, Libya and Pentapolis [[cite:canon6]]. He died in 328, after sending out that year's Easter letter, and [[person:athanasius]] succeeded him [[cite:index]].
