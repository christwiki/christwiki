He was called Romulus by his parents and was made emperor by his father Orestes, a Pannonian who had once been secretary to Attila and had risen to the rank of patrician [[cite:av]]. Procopius says that the Romans called him by the diminutive Augustulus because he came to the throne as a boy [[cite:procopius]].

When [[person:odoacer]] had killed Orestes and entered [[place:ravenna]], he deposed the boy but spared him on account of his youth, gave him an income and sent him to live with his relatives in Campania [[cite:av]]. Marcellinus names the place as the Lucullan castle and writes that the Western empire of the Roman people perished with him [[cite:marcellinus]]. What became of him afterwards the sources cited here do not say.
