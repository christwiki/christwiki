The elector's preface to the Heidelberg Catechism says only that it was prepared with the advice and cooperation of the whole theological faculty at [[place:heidelberg]] and of the leading ministers [[cite:preface]]. Tradition names Ursinus and [[person:caspar-olevianus]] as its authors; the introduction printed with it by the Christian Reformed Church describes a team in which Ursinus was probably the primary writer [[cite:crc-intro]].

The introduction printed with the English translation of his lectures says that he came to Heidelberg in 1561 as head of a college there, lectured on theology in the university from the next year, and that his lectures on the catechism were edited after his death by his pupil David Pareus [[cite:intro]]. In them he explains the first question, on the believer's only comfort, as the statement of the design and substance of the whole work [[cite:commentary]]. See [[event:heidelberg-catechism]].
