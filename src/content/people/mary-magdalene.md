---
name: Mary Magdalene
altNames: [Mary of Magdala]
role: Disciple of Jesus and witness of the resurrection
summary: A follower of Jesus from Galilee. Mark, Matthew and John name her at the crucifixion and all four Gospels at the empty tomb, and John and the longer ending of Mark say that the risen Jesus appeared first to her.
---

Luke introduces "Mary who was called Magdalene, from whom seven demons had gone out" among the women who travelled with Jesus and the twelve in Galilee and supported them from their own means [[bible:Luke 8:1-3]]. Her second name is usually taken to mean that she came from Magdala, a town on the Sea of Galilee.

She is named in every Gospel's account of the last days. She watched the crucifixion [[bible:Mark 15:40-41; Matt 27:55-56; John 19:25]], saw where Jesus was buried [[bible:Mark 15:47]], and came to the tomb on the first day of the week [[bible:Mark 16:1; Matt 28:1; Luke 24:10; John 20:1]]. In John she stands weeping outside the tomb, meets Jesus, whom she at first takes for the gardener, and tells the disciples that she has seen the Lord [[bible:John 20:11-18]]. The longer ending of Mark also says that he appeared first to her [[bible:Mark 16:9]]. See [[event:crucifixion]] and [[event:resurrection]].

The Gospels do not call her a sinner. They do not identify her with the unnamed woman of Luke 7 or with [[person:mary-of-bethany]] [[bible:Luke 7:36-38; John 12:1-3]].
