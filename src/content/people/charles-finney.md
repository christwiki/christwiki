In the preface to his *Lectures on Revivals of Religion* Finney describes himself as a pastor who no longer has the health to labour as an evangelist and who has been given "some experience in revivals of religion" [[cite:preface]]. The lectures hold that a revival is the result of the right use of the means God has appointed [[cite:revival]] and defend the measures used in his meetings, among them protracted meetings and the anxious seat [[cite:measures]].
