Charles was a son of [[person:pepin-the-short]]. Einhard says that on Pepin's death he and his brother Carloman were made kings together and that he ruled alone after Carloman's death [[cite:accession]]. At the request of Pope [[person:hadrian-i]] he overthrew the Lombard kingdom in Italy [[cite:lombards]]. In the General Admonition of 789 he styled himself the defender and helper of the church and set out a programme for its correction ([[event:carolingian-renewal]]) [[cite:admonitio]].

Einhard describes his devotion to the church of St Peter at [[place:rome]], which he visited four times, and the basilica he built at [[place:aachen]] [[cite:church]]. On his last visit to Rome he received the titles of emperor and Augustus ([[event:coronation-of-charlemagne]]) [[cite:crown]]. He died at Aachen on 28 January 814, in the seventy-second year of his age and the forty-seventh of his reign according to Einhard, and was buried in his own church the same day [[cite:death]].
