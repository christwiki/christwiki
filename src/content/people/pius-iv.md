The [[event:council-of-trent]] reopened on 18 January 1562 in what its acts call the first session under Pius IV [[cite:reopened]], and it closed on 4 December 1563 with a request that he confirm all that had been decreed under his predecessors and himself [[cite:close]]. He did so in consistory on 26 January 1564. The bull of confirmation orders the decrees to be observed, forbids anyone to publish commentaries or interpretations of them without papal authority, and reserves doubtful points to the Apostolic See [[cite:confirmation]]. The profession of faith that bears the council's name comes from his bull *Iniunctum nobis* of 13 November 1564 [[cite:profession]].
