Olevianus is not named in the elector's preface to the Heidelberg Catechism, which speaks of the theological faculty, the superintendents and the leading ministers as those who helped to prepare it [[cite:preface]]. The introduction printed with the catechism by the Christian Reformed Church says that an old tradition credits him and [[person:zacharias-ursinus]] as coauthors, that the work was in fact that of a team, and that his role was the lesser one [[cite:crc-intro]].
