The council's decree of 1667 says that the service books were corrected and printed by command of Tsar Alexei Mikhailovich [[cite:decree]]. Paul of Aleppo saw him stand bareheaded through a sermon of [[person:nikon]] and ask the patriarch to bury, not burn, the icons he had condemned [[cite:icons]].

[[person:avvakum]] says that the tsar asked Nikon not to unfrock him [[cite:siberia]], sent courtiers to urge him to be reconciled with the patriarchs, and at the last asked to be remembered in his prayers [[cite:farewell]]. See [[event:nikon-and-the-old-believers]].
