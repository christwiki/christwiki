The manuscripts of the Epitome of the Formula of Concord close with the place and date, Bergen, 29 May 1577, and six signatures, of which "Iacobus Andreae D." is the first; the same names stand under the longer Solid Declaration [[cite:signatures]]. The preface to the Book of Concord, which names no theologian, describes how the Formula came about: the conference that the elector of Saxony called to Torgau in 1576 and the revision of its draft in the light of the criticisms sent in by the Lutheran princes [[cite:preface]].
