Callixtus acted at [[place:worms]] through legates. Ekkehard of Aura says that he sent the bishop of Ostia, with full powers, and two cardinals to Germany to reconcile kingdom and priesthood, and calls him a kinsman of the emperor [[cite:ekkehard-1122]]. The privilege issued in his name grants [[person:henry-v-emperor]] a place at the election of German bishops and abbots and the right to confer their regalia by the sceptre [[cite:pope]]. The canons of his Lateran council of 1123 forbid simony, the consecration of bishops not canonically elected, and lay disposal of church property [[cite:lateran]].
