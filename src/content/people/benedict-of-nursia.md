Gregory the Great says that Benedict was born in the district of Nursia, studied at Rome and left to live as a hermit in a cave at Subiaco [[cite:youth]]. After founding several small monasteries there he moved to the hill above Cassino, destroyed a shrine of Apollo and built the monastery of [[place:monte-cassino]] [[cite:cassino]]. Gregory reports a visit from the Gothic king Totila, to whom Benedict foretold a reign of nine years [[cite:totila]], which places Benedict at Cassino in the 540s. He wrote a rule for his monks and died standing at prayer in the oratory [[cite:rule]].

The Rule itself says nothing about its author. It describes the monastery as "a school of the Lord's service" [[cite:prologue]]. Gregory gives no dates for Benedict's birth or death.
