The Ethiopian chronicle gives his throne name as Seltan Sagad and traces his descent through his father Fasiladas and his grandfather Ya'qob to Wanag Sagad [[cite:descent]]. It records that he made his residence at [[place:gorgora]] in the sixth year of his reign and at Danqaz in the twelfth [[cite:residence]]. The Jesuit historian Tellez counts his reign from 1607 to 1632 [[cite:reign]].

Tellez says that he publicly professed "the Latin Faith" in 1622 [[cite:profession]] and on 11 February 1626 swore obedience to the pope before the patriarch [[person:afonso-mendes]] [[cite:oath]]. The chronicle tells that in his twenty-fifth year, after defeating a rebel army, he returned to the faith of Alexandria by proclamation and named his son [[person:fasilides]] to reign, saying that he was weak and ill, and that he died on the tenth of Maskaram in his twenty-seventh year [[cite:proclamation]]. Tellez dates his death to September 1632 and gives his age as sixty-one, which is the basis of the birth year shown; he adds that the emperor declared at his death that he died in the Roman faith [[cite:death]].
