Nero sent Porcius Festus to Judaea as the successor of [[person:felix]] [[cite:sent]] [[bible:Acts 24:27]]. Within days of arriving he took up the case of [[person:paul]]. He proposed a hearing in Jerusalem, and when Paul appealed to Caesar he allowed the appeal; he then laid the matter before [[person:herod-agrippa-ii]], saying that he did not know what charge to write to the emperor [[bible:Acts 25:1-27]].

Josephus contrasts him favourably with his successor Albinus [[cite:war]]. He says that Festus moved against the bandits and the *sicarii*, sent troops against a man who had led a crowd into the wilderness, and let a dispute over a wall in the temple go to Nero for decision [[cite:war]] [[cite:office]]. He died in office. The death of [[person:james-the-just]] took place in the interval before his successor Albinus arrived [[cite:death]]. Josephus gives no years for his term; the dates used here are discussed under [[event:paul-arrested-in-jerusalem]].
