As Hugolino, cardinal bishop of Ostia, he signed the privilege of 1216 for Dominic's community at Toulouse [[cite:signed]]. Thomas of Celano says that the women of San Damiano received their way of life from him while he was bishop of Ostia [[cite:ostia]], and Jordan of Saxony that he conducted the funeral of [[person:dominic]] at Bologna [[cite:dominic]]. Roger of Wendover records that he succeeded [[person:honorius-iii]], who died on 18 March 1227 [[cite:elected]]. As pope he canonized [[person:francis-of-assisi]] at Assisi in July 1228 [[cite:canonized]].

In 1231 he issued statutes against heretics and commissioned Dominican friars to inquire after them [[cite:statutes]] [[cite:ille]]. He had the decretals of earlier popes and his own gathered into a single book of law and ordered that it alone be used in the courts and schools [[cite:decretals]]. See [[event:papal-inquisition]].
