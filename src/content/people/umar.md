Umar ibn al-Khattab led the Muslim community as "Commander of the Faithful", the title he bears in the document that al-Tabari quotes as his guarantee to the people of [[place:jerusalem]] [[cite:tabari]]. The Byzantine chronicler Theophanes, who calls him the leader of the Arabs, says that he took the city by agreement after a two-year siege, entered it in dirty garments of camel hair, and in the same year subjected all Syria [[cite:theophanes]].

Theophanes records that he was stabbed to death at prayer by a Persian after ruling for twelve years, in November of the year of the world 6137, which is AD 644 [[cite:death]].
