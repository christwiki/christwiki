His Life, written by his disciple Epiphanius the Wise, says that he was the second of three sons of Cyril and was baptized Bartholomew. It places his birth at the coming of the host of Akhmyl, which the editors date to 1322 [[cite:birth]]; it also says he was seventy-eight when he died, which would put it in 1314 [[cite:death]]. With his brother Stephen he built a church of the Holy Trinity in the forest, and he was tonsured there at twenty-three with the name Sergius [[cite:tonsure]].

The Life tells that on the advice of Patriarch Philotheos of Constantinople he gave his monks a common rule, under which no one owned anything [[cite:common-life]]. It dates his death to 25 September 1392 [[cite:death]]. Its editors give 1452 as the year of his canonization [[cite:about]]. See [[event:sergius-of-radonezh]].
