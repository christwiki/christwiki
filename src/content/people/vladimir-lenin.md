The decree of 20 January (2 February) 1918 on freedom of conscience and on church and religious societies is signed "V. Ulyanov (Lenin)" as chairman of the Council of People's Commissars [[cite:decree]].

On 19 March 1922 he wrote a secret letter to the members of the Politburo about the removal of valuables from the churches. He argued that the famine gave the government its best chance to carry the removal through against the clergy, wanted the proceeds as a fund of several hundred million gold roubles, and asked that the trial of those who had resisted at Shuya end in the shooting of as many as possible of the most influential among them [[cite:lenin]].
