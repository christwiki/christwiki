The English editor of his letters says that Ziegenbalg was born at Pulsnitz in Misnia and educated at Berlin and at the university of [[place:halle]] [[cite:origins]]. He signed the letter that reports the arrival at [[place:tranquebar]] on 9 July 1706 [[cite:arrival]]. He writes that within eight months he could read, write and speak Tamil and that he took the Tamil work while his colleague [[person:heinrich-plutschau]] took the Portuguese [[cite:progress]].

In October 1707 he sent home the four Gospels in Tamil, checked, he says, against the original [[cite:gospels]]. A letter from the mission of September 1714 speaks of the New Testament as translated and reports the Gospels and Acts printed in Tamil [[cite:printing]]. See [[event:tranquebar-mission]].
