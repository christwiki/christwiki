His rescript on the case of [[person:joan-of-arc]] is dated at Rome on 11 June 1455, in his first year as pope. It sets out the family's complaint and commits the cause to the archbishop of Reims and the bishops of Paris and Coutances, who are to take an inquisitor with them, hear both sides and decide what is just [[cite:rescript]]. The editor of the trial records notes that he was Alfonso Borgia, formerly archbishop of Valencia, elected in April 1455 [[cite:rescript]].

The judges gave their sentence as "specially delegated by our most Holy Lord the Pope" [[cite:nullity]]. See [[event:joan-of-arc]].
