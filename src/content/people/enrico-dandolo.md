Dandolo was doge of [[place:venice]] when the French crusaders came to hire a fleet, and he set the terms: a fixed price for transport and, for fifty Venetian galleys, half of whatever was conquered [[cite:treaty]]. When the army could not pay, he proposed the attack on Zara, and then took the cross himself in St Mark's. Villehardouin, who was there, calls him an old man who "saw naught", having lost his sight through a wound in the head [[cite:cross]].

He sailed with the fleet to [[place:constantinople]]. The twelve electors who chose Baldwin of Flanders as emperor after the city fell met in the palace where he was lodged [[cite:election]]. He died there in 1205, before Pentecost, and was buried in Hagia Sophia [[cite:death]]. See [[event:sack-of-constantinople]].
