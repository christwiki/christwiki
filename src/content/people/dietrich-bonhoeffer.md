Among the papers of Bonhoeffer that Eberhard Bethge published in 1951 is a short reflection on folly. Folly, he wrote, is a more dangerous enemy of the good than malice, because evil can be protested against and exposed while against folly there is no defence. He held it to be a human and not an intellectual defect, and observed that every strong display of power, political or religious, strikes a large part of mankind with folly [[cite:folly]].

His arrest and death are told in [[event:execution-of-bonhoeffer]]. The documents cited for [[event:barmen-declaration]] do not name him among those who called the synod.
