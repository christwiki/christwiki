Peter Lombard speaks of his purpose in the prologue of the Sentences. He compares his work to the poor widow's offering to the treasury, says that he undertook it at the urging of studious brethren, and describes it as a short volume in four books that gathers the opinions of the Fathers so that a reader need not turn over many books [[cite:prologue]].

In the fourth book he names seven sacraments of the new law: baptism, confirmation, the eucharist, penance, extreme unction, orders and marriage [[cite:four-two]]. The Council of Trent, in its seventh session, defined the same seven [[cite:trent]].
