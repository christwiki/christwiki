Solomon was the son of [[person:david]] and Bathsheba. The prophet [[person:nathan]] gave him the second name Jedidiah [[bible:2 Sam 12:24-25]]. In David's old age he was anointed king at Gihon, ahead of his brother Adonijah, who had claimed the throne [[bible:1 Kings 1:5, 32-40]]. At Gibeon, Kings says, he asked in a dream for an understanding heart to govern the people, and was promised wisdom, riches and honour [[bible:1 Kings 3:5-14]]. He built the temple in Jerusalem with the help of [[person:hiram-of-tyre]] ([[event:solomons-temple]]). Kings credits him with three thousand proverbs and tells how the queen of Sheba came to test him with hard questions [[bible:1 Kings 4:29-34]] [[bible:1 Kings 10:1-10]].

Kings ends his story with a judgment. His many foreign wives turned his heart to other gods, he built high places for those gods near Jerusalem, and he was told that most of the kingdom would be torn from his son [[bible:1 Kings 11:1-13]]. He reigned forty years [[bible:1 Kings 11:42-43]]. The dates given here follow the usual reckoning, which [[event:david-becomes-king]] explains. In the Gospels Jesus speaks of "Solomon in all his glory" and of "someone greater than Solomon" [[bible:Matt 6:29]] [[bible:Matt 12:42]].
