Wu worked for the national committee of the YMCA, and the American editors of the movement's documents call him the founder of the Three-Self Movement and its chairman from the start [[cite:wu-bio]]. The Communist Party's account says that he and others met Zhou Enlai four times in [[place:beijing]] in May 1950, that he and others, forty in all, issued the manifesto in July, and that the national conference of 1954 elected him chairman of the movement's committee [[cite:party]]. The manifesto asks Christians to support the government and to purge imperialist influence from the churches [[cite:manifesto]]. See [[event:three-self-movement]].
