What is told of Macarius here comes from a letter of [[person:paisius-velichkovsky]], written while the *Philokalia* was at the press [[cite:paisius]]. Paisius calls him the former metropolitan of Corinth and says that from his youth he sought out and copied the books of the fathers on watchfulness and the prayer of the heart, that he searched the libraries of [[place:mount-athos]], above all that of Vatopedi, and that from Smyrna he sent thirty-six such books to [[place:venice]] to be printed [[cite:paisius]]. The catalogue record of the first edition names him, as Macarius Notaras, with [[person:nicodemus-the-hagiorite]] [[cite:copy]]. See [[event:philokalia]].
