Jorge Mario Bergoglio was born in Buenos Aires on 17 December 1936, the son of immigrants from Piedmont [[cite:bio]]. He entered the Society of Jesus in 1958, was ordained a priest in 1969, and became archbishop of Buenos Aires in 1998 and a cardinal in 2001 [[cite:bio]]. The conclave elected him pope on 13 March 2013, and he took the name Francis after [[person:francis-of-assisi]]; the Holy See's biography calls him the first pope to come from the Americas [[cite:bio]]. On the evening of his election he asked the crowd to pray for him before he blessed them [[cite:first-greeting]]; see [[event:benedict-resigns-francis-elected]].

In *Evangelii gaudium* he asked for a church turned towards mission and spoke of a "conversion of the papacy" [[cite:eg]]. In 2016 he met Patriarch [[person:kirill-of-moscow]] at Havana [[cite:havana]] and signed a joint statement with the Lutheran World Federation's president at Lund [[cite:lund]]; see [[event:reformation-500]]. In 2019 he made it a duty for clerics and religious to report sexual abuse and set a procedure for investigating bishops [[cite:vos-estis]]; see [[event:clergy-abuse-crisis]].

He died in the Vatican on Easter Monday, 21 April 2025 [[cite:bio]]. See [[event:leo-xiv-elected]] for the election of his successor, [[person:leo-xiv]].
