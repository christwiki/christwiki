Winfrid grew up in English monasteries and taught at Nursling before he left for the continent [[cite:early]]. The commission he received from Pope [[person:gregory-ii]] on 15 May 719 addresses him as Boniface, the name by which he is known [[cite:commission]]. His oath as bishop, sworn at [[place:rome]] in 722, bound him to the Catholic faith and its unity and to loyalty to Peter and the pope [[cite:oath]].

He worked for more than thirty years in Hesse, Thuringia, Bavaria and the Frankish kingdom, presided as archbishop at the reforming synods held under Carloman, and held the see of [[place:mainz]] [[cite:synods]]. He founded the monastery of [[place:fulda]] and asked to be buried there [[cite:fulda]]. He was killed with his companions in Frisia on 5 June; Willibald gives the year as 755, the tradition of Fulda as 754 [[cite:death]]. See [[event:boniface]].
