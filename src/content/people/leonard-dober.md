The Moravian Church's history prints the paper in which Dober explained his purpose to the congregation at [[place:herrnhut]]. The count's account of the slaves of St Thomas, he wrote, had made an impression on him that nothing could erase, and he had resolved, if another brother would go with him, to offer himself to be a slave in order to tell them of the love of the Saviour [[cite:dober]]. He left Herrnhut on 21 August 1732 with the carpenter David Nitschmann. At Copenhagen a court official asked what "the potter" would live on, and Nitschmann answered that he would provide for them both [[cite:potter]]. They landed at St Thomas on 13 December [[cite:arrival]]. See [[event:moravian-renewal]].
