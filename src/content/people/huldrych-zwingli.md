His friend Oswald Myconius writes that Zwingli was born in the village of Wildhaus in the Toggenburg, about 1487; the editor of the translation corrects the date to 1 January 1484 [[cite:birth]]. Zwingli said of his own preaching at [[place:zurich]] that he began with the whole Gospel of Matthew and had not then heard of those whose follower he was later said to be [[cite:archeteles]]. In 1523 the city council ruled that he should go on preaching as before [[cite:decree]]; see [[event:zwingli-in-zurich]] and, for those who broke with him, [[event:anabaptists]].

In 1529 he debated the Lord's Supper with [[person:martin-luther]] and judged that Luther had been beaten [[cite:vadian]]; see [[event:marburg-colloquy]]. Myconius relates that in the second of the wars named after the monastery of Kappel Zwingli went out with the troops of Zurich as chaplain, on horseback and armed, and was killed in the fighting. The editor of the translation gives 11 October 1531 as the day [[cite:death]].
