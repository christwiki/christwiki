Taizong was an emperor of the Tang dynasty; his reign is the Zhenguan era by which both the Xi'an Stele and the court records date these events. The stele says that he sent a minister to welcome Aluoben in 635, had the Christian books translated in the palace library and examined the teaching himself [[cite:arrival]].

His edict of the seventh month of 638 judged the teaching beneficial and ordered a monastery built in the capital with twenty-one monks. It survives both on the stele and in the Tang collection of state documents [[cite:huiyao]].
