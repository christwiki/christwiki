---
name: Aquila
role: Tentmaker; fellow worker of Paul
summary: A Jew from Pontus who left Rome with his wife Priscilla when Claudius expelled the Jews. The couple worked with Paul at Corinth and Ephesus, taught Apollos, and later hosted a church in their house at Rome.
---

Aquila was a Jew born in Pontus who had been living in Italy. Acts says that he and his wife [[person:priscilla]] came to [[place:corinth]] when [[person:claudius|Claudius]] ordered the Jews to leave [[place:rome|Rome]], and [[person:paul]] lodged and worked with them, since all three were tentmakers [[bible:Acts 18:1-3]]; see [[event:claudius-expels-the-jews]]. They sailed with Paul to [[place:ephesus]] and stayed there, and when the Alexandrian preacher [[person:apollos]] arrived they "explained to him the way of God more accurately" [[bible:Acts 18:18-19, 24-26]].

Paul sends greetings from them and from "the assembly that is in their house" when he writes from Ephesus, and greets them and the church in their house when he writes to Rome, adding that they had risked their necks for his life [[bible:1 Cor 16:19]] [[bible:Rom 16:3-5]]. They are greeted once more in 2 Timothy [[bible:2 Tim 4:19]]. In the New Testament Aquila is never named without his wife.
