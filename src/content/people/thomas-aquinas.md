William of Tocco, who wrote his life for the canonization, says that Thomas was sent as a boy to the schools of [[place:naples]], entered the Order of Preachers there and was held by his family for nearly two years to make him give it up [[cite:youth]]. He studied at Cologne under [[person:albert-the-great]] and was sent on his recommendation to teach at [[place:paris]] [[cite:albert]]. Tocco lists among his writings a commentary on the Sentences, the Summa of theology in three parts and the book *Against the Gentiles* [[cite:books]].

A witness at the canonization inquiry of 1319 testified that Thomas stopped writing after a Mass at Naples and said that all he had written seemed to him straw [[cite:straw]]. Tocco dates his death to the morning of 7 March 1274, in the forty-ninth year of his life [[cite:death]], which puts his birth about 1225. See [[event:thomas-aquinas]].
