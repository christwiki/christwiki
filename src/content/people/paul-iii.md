In the bull *Sublimis Deus* of 1537 Paul III declared that the Indians are truly men and are not to be deprived of their liberty or property or enslaved [[cite:bull]]; see [[event:sublimis-deus]]. In 1540 he approved the [[event:society-of-jesus]], limiting it to sixty members [[cite:jesuits]].

His bull of 22 May 1542 summoning a general council to [[place:trent]] tells of his earlier attempts to hold one at Mantua and at Vicenza and of his journey to Nice to make peace between the emperor and the king of France [[cite:indiction]]. The [[event:council-of-trent]] opened under him on 13 December 1545 [[cite:opening]]. When it resumed in 1551 it was under his successor Julius III [[cite:resumed]].
