Hilda was a great-niece of King Edwin of Northumbria and was baptized with him by Paulinus. Bede says that she lived thirty-three years in the world and thirty-three as a nun, and died on 17 November 680 at the age of sixty-six. Bishop Aidan called her back from East Anglia to Northumbria, where she became abbess of Heruteu (Hartlepool) and then founded the monastery of Streanaeshalch, identified with [[place:whitby]] [[cite:life]] [[cite:founding]].

In her house no one was rich or poor and all things were held in common. Kings and princes sought her advice, and Bede names five men trained there who became bishops [[cite:life]]. At the [[event:synod-of-whitby|synod held there in 664]] she and her community stood with the Irish party [[cite:synod]].
