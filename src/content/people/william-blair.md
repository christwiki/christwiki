Blair sailed for Korea in August 1901 as a missionary of the Presbyterian Church in the U.S.A., and wrote his book on furlough after eight years there [[cite:blair-foreword]]. At [[place:pyongyang]] he worked in the North Pyeng Yang Church and chaired the Pyeng Yang Men's Association [[cite:blair-46]].

During the Bible class of January 1907 he preached on the Saturday night from 1 Corinthians 12:27, on the church as one body [[cite:blair-44]]. On the Tuesday night an elder confessed before the congregation that he had hated Blair, and asked him to pray; Blair writes that he began "Father, Father" and could get no further [[cite:blair-46]]. See [[event:pyongyang-revival]].
