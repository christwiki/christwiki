The Lateran Treaty names Cardinal Pietro Gasparri, Secretary of State, as plenipotentiary of the pope, and he signed it in Rome on 11 February 1929 [[cite:preamble]]. [[person:pius-xi]] told the parish priests of Rome that day that his Cardinal Secretary of State was at that hour signing a treaty and a concordat in the Lateran Palace with [[person:benito-mussolini]] [[cite:address-11]]. See [[event:lateran-treaty]].
