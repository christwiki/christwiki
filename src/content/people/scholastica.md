Gregory says that Scholastica had been dedicated to God from her infancy and came once a year to see her brother [[person:benedict-of-nursia]] at a house belonging to the monastery, not far from its gate. At their last meeting she asked him to stay the night and talk of the joys of heaven. He refused to remain outside his monastery; she prayed, and a sudden storm kept him there until morning. Gregory comments that she could do more because she loved more [[cite:meeting]].

Three days later Benedict saw her soul go up to heaven in the form of a dove. He had her body brought to [[place:monte-cassino]] and laid in the grave he had prepared for himself [[cite:death]]. Gregory calls her a nun and mentions her nunnery, but does not say where it was.
