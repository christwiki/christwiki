Parker's own Latin notes on his life say that he was born at Norwich on 6 August 1504, was sent to Cambridge in 1522 and became master of Corpus Christi College there in 1544. In 1554, under [[person:mary-i-of-england]], he was deprived of his church offices, and he writes that he then lived as a private man, content in his studies [[cite:memoranda]].

Under [[person:elizabeth-i]] he was made archbishop of Canterbury. His note for 17 December 1559 records his consecration and adds a prayer of dismay at the times he had been kept for [[cite:memoranda]]; the London diarist Henry Machyn notes under the same day that the new bishop, Doctor Parker, was "made" at Lambeth [[cite:machyn]]. The printed collection of his letters runs, as its title says, to his death in 1575 [[cite:title]]. See [[event:elizabethan-settlement]].
