His sister Gilberte Périer, who wrote his life, says that he was born at Clermont on 19 June 1623 and died on 19 August 1662, aged thirty-nine years and two months [[cite:life]].

The first of his *Provincial Letters* is dated from [[place:paris]] on 23 January 1656. It reports the proceedings of the Sorbonne against Antoine Arnauld, who had written that he could not find the condemned propositions in the book of [[person:cornelius-jansen]], and it treats that as a question of fact that does not touch the faith [[cite:letter-1]]. In the seventeenth letter he says of himself, "I am not of Port-Royal", and argues that those accused condemn the five propositions and differ from their accusers only on a matter of fact [[cite:letter-17]]. See [[event:jansenism]].
