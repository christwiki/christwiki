The introduction that the Evangelical Church in Germany publishes with the Barmen Declaration lists Martin Niemöller with the pastors Hans Asmussen, Karl Koch and Karl Immer and the theologian [[person:karl-barth]] among the few church leaders who opposed what it calls the church's captivity to National Socialism, and who brought the delegates together at Barmen [[cite:ekd-en]]. See [[event:barmen-declaration]].
