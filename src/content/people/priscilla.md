---
name: Priscilla
altNames: [Prisca]
role: Fellow worker of Paul; teacher of Apollos
summary: Wife of Aquila, with whom she left Rome under Claudius, worked with Paul at Corinth and Ephesus, and hosted churches in her house. Acts calls her Priscilla and Paul calls her Prisca; both often name her before her husband.
---

Priscilla came to [[place:corinth]] from Italy with her husband [[person:aquila]] after [[person:claudius|Claudius]] ordered the Jews to leave [[place:rome|Rome]], and [[person:paul]] lived and worked with them there [[bible:Acts 18:1-3]]. She and Aquila went on with him to [[place:ephesus]]. There they heard [[person:apollos]] in the synagogue, took him aside and explained the way of God to him more accurately [[bible:Acts 18:18-19, 26]].

Paul, who calls her Prisca, names the couple as his "fellow workers in Christ Jesus" to whom all the Gentile churches owe thanks, and mentions the church that met in their house, first at Ephesus and later at Rome [[bible:Rom 16:3-5]] [[bible:1 Cor 16:19]]. In four of the six places where the two are named she comes first [[bible:Acts 18:18, 26]] [[bible:Rom 16:3]] [[bible:2 Tim 4:19]]. See [[event:claudius-expels-the-jews]].
