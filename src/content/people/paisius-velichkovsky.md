The Life written in his community says that Paisius was born in 1722 at Poltava, the son of a priest, and was baptized Peter [[cite:birth]]. It dates his arrival on [[place:mount-athos]] to 1746 [[cite:athos]], and tells how he later took charge of the monastery of Neamț in Moldavia, which he governed together with Secu, and was made archimandrite [[cite:neamt]]. It records his death at Neamț in November 1794, at the age of seventy-two [[cite:death]].

In a letter to Archimandrite Theodosius he describes his own work: comparing the old Slavonic versions of the ascetic fathers with Greek manuscripts, correcting them, and translating other books afresh [[cite:work]]. The Russian editors of his writings in 1847 say that the Slavonic *Dobrotolubiye* printed at Moscow was his translation [[cite:optina]]. See [[event:philokalia]].
