Tiberius was born at Rome on 16 November 42 BC, by the date that Suetonius found in the public records [[cite:birth]]. He was the son of the wife of [[person:augustus]] and succeeded him [[cite:successor]] when Augustus died on 19 August AD 14 [[cite:accession]]. He died at Misenum on 16 March AD 37, in the twenty-third year of his reign and the seventy-eighth of his life [[cite:death]] [[cite:death-suetonius]].

The public life of [[person:jesus]] falls within his reign. Luke dates the first preaching of [[person:john-the-baptist]] to "the fifteenth year of the reign of Tiberius Caesar" [[bible:Luke 3:1]]; see [[event:ministry-of-john-the-baptist]]. Tacitus writes that Christus was executed by [[person:pontius-pilate]] while Tiberius was emperor [[cite:christus]]. [[person:herod-antipas]] named his new city on the [[place:sea-of-galilee]] Tiberias in his honour [[cite:tiberias]].
