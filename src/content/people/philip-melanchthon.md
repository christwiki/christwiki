[[person:martin-luther]] wrote that the elector [[person:frederick-the-wise]] called Melanchthon to [[place:wittenberg]] in 1518 to teach Greek [[cite:preface]]. In 1522 the two went through Luther's German New Testament together before it was printed [[cite:march]], and Luther later named him among those who laboured with him over the book of Job [[cite:translating]]; see [[event:luther-bible]].

At [[place:marburg]] in 1529 he was paired with [[person:huldrych-zwingli]] in the private talks [[cite:zwingli]] and signed the articles [[cite:articles]]. In 1546 he wrote a short life of Luther from what he had heard from Luther and seen himself; it says that Luther posted his theses on the church next to the castle [[cite:life]]. See also [[event:augsburg-confession]].
