The editor of the *Passion* introduces her by her full name, Vibia Perpetua (spelt "Vivia" in the linked translation), as well born, well educated and married, about twenty-two years old, with a father, mother and two brothers and an infant son at the breast [[cite:intro]]. She was a catechumen when she was arrested and was baptised a few days later. In the pages given as her own she tells how her father begged her to give way, how she nursed her child in prison, and how she answered the procurator Hilarianus, "I am a Christian" [[cite:diary]].

She was tossed by a wild cow in the arena, helped [[person:felicity]] to her feet, and was then killed with the sword, guiding the young gladiator's hand to her own throat [[cite:death]]. Tertullian appeals to her vision of paradise [[cite:tertullian]].
