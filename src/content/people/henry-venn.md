Venn was born at Clapham on 10 February 1796, the son of its rector [[cite:birth]]. As honorary secretary of the Church Missionary Society he issued in 1851 a minute on the ordination of native teachers [[cite:minute]]. Its tenth paragraph says that the ultimate object of a mission is "the settlement of a native Church, under native pastors, upon a self-supporting system", and that the "euthanasia of a mission" comes when the missionary can hand all pastoral work to them and move on to "the regions beyond" [[cite:minute]].

The Society's historian records that when it seemed that Europeans could not live on the Niger, Venn's plan was a purely African mission under an African bishop, and that he persevered against the doubts of the English missionaries in West Africa [[cite:plan]]. See [[event:samuel-ajayi-crowther]]. He died on 13 January 1873 [[cite:death]].
