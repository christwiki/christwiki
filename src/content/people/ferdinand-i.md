The preface of the Augsburg Confession recalls that Ferdinand, king of Bohemia and Hungary, represented the emperor at the diet of [[place:speyer]] the year before [[cite:speyer]]. In the recess of the diet of 1555 he styles himself king of the Romans and explains that he has taken on the diet's business in place of his brother [[person:charles-v]], whom illness kept away [[cite:recess]]. By the powers the emperor had given him he settled the point on which the estates could not agree, what should become of the sees and benefices of clergy who left the old religion [[cite:reservation]], and dated the recess at Augsburg on 25 September 1555 [[cite:dated]]. See [[event:peace-of-augsburg]].

When the Council of Trent closed in December 1563 the fathers acclaimed "the most serene Emperor Ferdinand" [[cite:acclaimed]].
