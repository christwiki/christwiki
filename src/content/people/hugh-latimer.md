What follows is John Foxe's account, written by an admirer. [[person:henry-viii]] made Latimer bishop of Worcester. When the Six Articles came in he resigned the see, and he was later held in the Tower until [[person:edward-vi]] became king. Under Edward he preached again, often before the king and the court [[cite:life]].

Under [[person:mary-i-of-england]] he was burned as a heretic at [[place:oxford]] on 16 October 1555, chained to the same stake as [[person:nicholas-ridley]]. Foxe gives him the words to Ridley about lighting a candle in England that would never be put out, and says that he died quickly [[cite:death]]. See [[event:marian-persecutions]].
