Shenouda III bore the titles Pope of Alexandria and Patriarch of the See of St Mark. In May 1973 he met [[person:paul-vi]] at the Vatican; their declaration notes that the meeting followed the return of relics of St Mark to Egypt [[cite:d1973-head]]. Together they confessed Jesus Christ as perfect God and perfect man, his divinity and humanity joined in a real and perfect union [[cite:d1973-3]], while saying that the differences between their churches since 451 could not be ignored and setting up a joint commission to study them [[cite:d1973-7]]. See [[event:christological-declarations]].

In November 2012 [[person:benedict-xvi]] congratulated his successor, Anba Tawadros, on his election as head of the Coptic Orthodox Church, with a tribute to the progress made under Shenouda in relations with the Catholic Church [[cite:successor]].
