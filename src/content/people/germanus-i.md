Theophanes says that Germanus had been patriarch for fourteen years and five months when, on 7 January 730, the emperor [[person:leo-iii-the-isaurian|Leo III]] called him before a council of state and pressed him to subscribe against the icons. He refused, laid down his episcopal stole and retired to his family's house at Platanion; his assistant Anastasius took his place [[cite:resign]]. The Roman *Liber Pontificalis* records the same change and says that Pope [[person:gregory-ii]] would not recognise Anastasius [[cite:lp]].

[[person:john-of-damascus]], writing soon afterwards, speaks of him as punished and in exile [[cite:john]]. The council of 754 anathematized him as a "worshipper of wood" [[cite:hieria]]. See [[event:iconoclasm-begins]].
