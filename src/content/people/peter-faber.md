[[person:ignatius-of-loyola]] says that at Paris he was a friend of Peter Faber and [[person:francis-xavier]] and led them to the service of God by giving them the Exercises [[cite:paris]]. Faber was one of the two companions who lived with Ignatius outside Vicenza while the group waited for a ship to Jerusalem, and he walked with him to visit a sick companion at Bassano [[cite:vicenza]]. The bull of 1540 lists him, as Petrus Faber, immediately after Ignatius among the ten priests of the new Society [[cite:bull]]. See [[event:society-of-jesus]].
