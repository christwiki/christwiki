[[person:bartolome-de-las-casas]], who calls him fray Antón Montesino, describes him as the chief preacher among the Dominicans of Hispaniola after their vicar, sharp in rebuking vice and forceful in the pulpit. The friars agreed on a sermon about the treatment of the Indians, all signed it, and the vicar, Pedro de Córdoba, ordered Montesino to preach it on the fourth Sunday of Advent. By Las Casas's account he asked the settlers by what right they held the Indians in servitude and whether these were not men with rational souls, and the governor and royal officials afterwards demanded that he retract [[cite:sermon]]. Las Casas's book places these events in 1511 [[cite:year]]. See [[event:sublimis-deus]].
