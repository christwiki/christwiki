Thietmar of Merseburg says that she was the sister of Boleslav the elder of Bohemia, that her name means "good", and that she lived up to it. Married to the pagan [[person:mieszko-i]], she broke her Lenten fast at his request so as to win his ear, and by her persistence brought him to baptism. She then bore him a son, whom she named Boleslav after her brother [[cite:thietmar]].

In the Polish court chronicle of the early twelfth century she refuses to marry Mieszko until he promises to give up his seven wives and become a Christian [[cite:gallus]]. The annals of the Kraków chapter enter her coming to Mieszko under 965 [[cite:annals]]. See [[event:baptism-of-poland]].
