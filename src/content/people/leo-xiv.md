Robert Francis Prevost was born in [[place:chicago]] on 14 September 1955 [[cite:bio]]. He entered the Order of Saint Augustine in 1977, was ordained a priest in 1982 and took a doctorate in canon law in Rome [[cite:bio]]. He worked in the order's missions in Peru from 1985, was prior general of the Augustinians from 2001 to 2013, and returned to Peru in 2014 to the diocese of Chiclayo, whose bishop he became in 2015 [[cite:bio]]. Pope [[person:francis]] made him prefect of the Dicastery for Bishops and a cardinal in 2023 [[cite:bio]]. The cardinals elected him pope on 8 May 2025, and the Holy See's biography calls him the first pope from the United States of America and the first Augustinian pope [[cite:bio]]; see [[event:leo-xiv-elected]].

He told the cardinals that he took the name Leo mainly because of Leo XIII's teaching on the social question [[cite:cardinals]]. In November 2025 he prayed at İznik with Patriarch [[person:bartholomew-i]] on the anniversary of the Council of Nicaea [[cite:iznik]] and signed a joint declaration with him the next day [[cite:declaration]]; see [[event:nicaea-1700]].
