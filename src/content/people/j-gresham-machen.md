The title page of *Christianity and Liberalism* describes Machen as assistant professor of New Testament literature and exegesis in Princeton Theological Seminary, and the preface says the book grew from an address he gave to the ruling elders of Chester Presbytery in November 1921 [[cite:machen-title]]. He set out to show that liberalism was a religion of a different kind from Christianity [[cite:machen-7]]. He wrote that he did not presume to say whether any individual liberal was a Christian, but that liberalism was not Christianity and the two ought not to be taught within one organisation [[cite:machen-160]].
