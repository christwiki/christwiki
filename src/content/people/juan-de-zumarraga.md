The *Nican Mopohua* names Don Fray Juan de Zumárraga, a Franciscan, as the bishop, lately arrived, to whom [[person:juan-diego]] went. In that account he listens, asks for a sign, and has Juan Diego followed [[cite:account]]; when the cloak is opened he kneels before the image and keeps it in his chapel [[cite:image]]. See [[event:our-lady-of-guadalupe]].

Joaquín García Icazbalceta wrote in 1883 that the writings of Zumárraga known to him, among them books of doctrine, letters, opinions, a pastoral exhortation and two wills, contain no allusion to the apparition or to the chapels and never use the name Guadalupe, and that the bishop went to Spain in the year after 1531 [[cite:silence]].
