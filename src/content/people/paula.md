Almost all that is known of Paula comes from the memorial letter that [[person:jerome]] wrote for her daughter Eustochium in 404. She belonged to a family that claimed descent from the Gracchi and the Scipios, married Toxotius and bore five children [[cite:family]]. As a widow she left Rome, visited the holy places and settled at [[place:bethlehem]], where she founded a monastery for men and three communities of women [[cite:monastery]].

She read the Scriptures with Jerome and learned Hebrew well enough to chant the psalms in it [[cite:hebrew]]. Several of the prefaces to his translations are addressed to her and Eustochium [[cite:helmeted]] (see [[event:jerome-and-the-vulgate]]). She died on 26 January 404, aged fifty-six years and eight months, which puts her birth in 347 [[cite:death]].
