summary: A man of Manasseh who, according to Judges, routed the Midianites with three hundred men and refused to found a dynasty. He is also called Jerubbaal, from his attack on his father's altar of Baal.
---

Gideon, son of Joash the Abiezrite, of the tribe of Manasseh, was called by an angel at Ophrah in years when Midianite raiders stripped the land at every harvest [[bible:Judg 6:1-6, 11-16]]. After he pulled down the local altar of Baal he was given the name Jerubbaal, "Let Baal contend against him" [[bible:Judg 6:25-32]]. He twice asked for a sign with a fleece of wool [[bible:Judg 6:36-40]], and then attacked the Midianite camp by night with three hundred men [[bible:Judg 7:7, 16-22]].

He refused an offer of hereditary rule with the words "Yahweh shall rule over you" [[bible:Judg 8:22-23]]. But the gold ephod he set up at Ophrah "became a snare" to him and his family [[bible:Judg 8:27]]. Judges gives the land forty years of rest in his days [[bible:Judg 8:28]]. Isaiah recalls "the day of Midian" [[bible:Isa 9:4]], and Hebrews names him among those who acted through faith [[bible:Heb 11:32]]. His story is told in [[event:age-of-the-judges]].
