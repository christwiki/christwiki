Gregory of Tours says that Queen [[person:clotilde]] asked Remigius, bishop of [[place:reims]], to instruct [[person:clovis]] in secret, and that he then baptised the king, telling him to worship what he had burned and to burn what he had worshipped. Gregory calls him a man of great learning, trained in rhetoric, and mentions a written Life that credited him with raising a dead man. He also quotes the opening of a letter in which Remigius consoled the king on the death of his sister Albofled, who had been baptised with him [[cite:baptism]].
