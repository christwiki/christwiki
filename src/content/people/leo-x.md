[[person:erasmus]] dedicated his New Testament to Leo in 1516 and praised the house of Medici from which he came [[cite:dedication]]. The indulgence for the building of St Peter's was granted by Leo [[cite:instructio]].

In the bull *Exsurge Domine* he condemned forty-one propositions from the writings of [[person:martin-luther]]. He said that he had urged Luther by letters and through his legate to give up his errors and had offered him a safe-conduct to [[place:rome]], and he gave him sixty days to recant [[cite:exsurge]]. The bull *Decet Romanum Pontificem*, which declared Luther a heretic, is dated 3 January 1521, in the eighth year of Leo's pontificate [[cite:decet]]. Luther's open letter to him of 1520 speaks of Leo himself with respect, as a lamb and a Daniel among lions, and attacks the Roman court [[cite:freedom]]. See [[event:diet-of-worms]].
