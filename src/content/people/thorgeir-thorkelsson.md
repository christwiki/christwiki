Ari says that Thorgeir, son of Thorkel, of Ljósavatn, held the office of lawspeaker for seventeen summers [[cite:office]]. At the assembly where Christians and heathens had declared themselves out of law with each other, the Christians' spokesman arranged that Thorgeir, though still a heathen, should proclaim the law. He lay under his cloak for a day and a night, and then persuaded both sides to accept in advance whatever he decided: one law and one faith, with a few old customs left standing for the time being [[cite:ruling]].
