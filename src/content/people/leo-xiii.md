The Holy See's page for Leo XIII gives his name before his election as Vincenzo Gioacchino Pecci, his birthplace as Carpineto Romano, and his pontificate as running from 20 February 1878 to 20 July 1903 [[cite:pontificate]]. In 1879 he made [[person:john-henry-newman]] a cardinal [[cite:newman]].

In *Rerum novarum*, dated 15 May 1891 [[cite:dated]], he wrote that a remedy had to be found quickly for the misery pressing unjustly on the majority of the working class [[cite:workers]], and that natural justice requires wages sufficient to support a frugal and well-behaved wage-earner [[cite:wage]]. See [[event:rerum-novarum]]. The patriarch of Constantinople and his synod, replying in August 1895, record that he had invited the Orthodox Church to unite with Rome [[cite:appeal]].
