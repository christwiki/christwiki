Isaac is born when [[person:abraham]] is a hundred years old and is circumcised on the eighth day [[bible:Gen 21:1-5]]. Genesis has God say beforehand that the covenant would be established with him and not with his elder half-brother Ishmael [[bible:Gen 17:19-21]]. In [[event:binding-of-isaac]] he is bound on the altar and a ram is offered in his place [[bible:Gen 22:9-13]]. At forty he marries Rebekah, who is brought from his father's relatives in Mesopotamia [[bible:Gen 24:67]] [[bible:Gen 25:20]], and at sixty he becomes the father of the twins [[person:esau]] and [[person:jacob]] [[bible:Gen 25:24-26]]. The promise made to Abraham is repeated to him at Gerar and at [[place:beersheba]] [[bible:Gen 26:2-5]] [[bible:Gen 26:23-25]]. Old and blind, he gives the blessing of the firstborn to Jacob, who comes to him disguised as Esau [[bible:Gen 27:1-29]]. He dies at 180 at [[place:hebron]] and is buried by his two sons [[bible:Gen 35:27-29]].

[[person:paul]] calls Christians "children of promise" as Isaac was [[bible:Gal 4:28]], and quotes the words "your offspring will be accounted as from Isaac" to show that descent alone does not make a child of the promise [[bible:Rom 9:6-9]]. His dates are worked out from the Bible's own figures; see the dating note at [[event:call-of-abraham]].
