The missionaries' reports of 1907 call him Elder Kil or Mr. Kil. W. B. Hunt writes that at the end of the men's meeting on 6 January, after a time of prayer and confession, Kil stood and asked everyone to sing the doxology, saying that the Spirit had come [[cite:hunt-37]]. Graham Lee, who calls him the mission's most gifted Korean preacher, says that he gave the address on the Tuesday night of the revival [[cite:lee-34]] and preached the following Sunday, acting out a sinner bound by a rope who breaks free and runs to God [[cite:lee-36]].

He then preached in the churches of Seoul [[cite:seoul-41]]. On 17 September 1907 the new Presbytery of Korea ordained him with six others, who had completed a five-year course in theology [[cite:presbytery]]. See [[event:pyongyang-revival]].

The sources cited here are missionary reports in English. No writing of his own is cited.
