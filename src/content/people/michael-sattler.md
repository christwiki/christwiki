The seven articles agreed at [[place:schleitheim]] on 24 February 1527 name no author; their drafting is attributed to Sattler [[cite:schleitheim]].

The *Martyrs Mirror* reproduces an account of his trial in May 1527. The charges were that he had acted against the emperor's mandate, denied that the body and blood of Christ are present in the sacrament, taught that infant baptism does not help to salvation, rejected extreme unction, despised the mother of God and the saints, forbidden oaths, kept the Lord's Supper in a new way, left his order and married, and said that the Turks should not be resisted. Sattler answered the articles one by one; asked why he had not remained a lord in his convent, he said, "According to the flesh I was a lord; but it is better so." He was sentenced to have his tongue cut out and his flesh torn with hot tongs and was burned, and his wife was drowned a few days later [[cite:trial]]. See [[event:anabaptists]].
