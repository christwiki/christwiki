Luke tells of John's birth to the priest Zechariah and his wife Elizabeth, a relative of [[person:mary]] [[bible:Luke 1:5-25, 36, 57-80]]. He appeared in the [[place:judean-wilderness|wilderness of Judea]] in the fifteenth year of [[person:tiberius]], preaching "the baptism of repentance for remission of sins", and baptised those who came to him in the [[place:jordan-river]] [[bible:Luke 3:1-3]] [[bible:Mark 1:4-6]]; see [[event:ministry-of-john-the-baptist]]. He spoke of one mightier than himself who was to come, and he baptised [[person:jesus]] [[bible:Mark 1:7-11]]. The Gospels see in him the messenger foretold by the prophets [[bible:Mark 1:2-3]], and in Matthew Jesus calls him more than a prophet and the [[person:elijah]] who was to come [[bible:Matt 11:9-14]].

Mark tells that [[person:herod-antipas]] imprisoned John, who had condemned his marriage to [[person:herodias]], and had him beheaded [[bible:Mark 6:17-29]]. [[person:josephus]], who writes of John with respect, says that Antipas feared his influence over the people and put him to death at the fortress of [[place:machaerus]] [[cite:josephus]]; see [[event:death-of-john-the-baptist]]. Groups of his disciples continued after his death [[bible:Acts 19:1-7]].
