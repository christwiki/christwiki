Mar Dinkha IV was Catholicos-Patriarch of the Assyrian Church of the East when he and [[person:john-paul-ii]] signed the Common Christological Declaration at St Peter's in [[place:rome]] on 11 November 1994 [[cite:d1994-1]]. The declaration recognised the two churches as sister churches, said that they could not yet celebrate the Eucharist together, and set up a mixed committee for theological dialogue [[cite:d1994-church]]. See [[event:christological-declarations]].

In 2012 [[person:benedict-xvi]] congratulated him on fifty years as a bishop, recalled the declaration and a further visit to Rome in 2007, and spoke of the re-establishment of full communion as the goal of the dialogue [[cite:benedict]].
