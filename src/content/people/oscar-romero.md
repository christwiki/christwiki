Óscar Arnulfo Romero Galdámez was born at Ciudad Barrios in El Salvador on 15 August 1917 and ordained priest in Rome on 4 April 1942. He was named auxiliary bishop of San Salvador in 1970, bishop of Santiago de María in 1974 and archbishop of [[place:san-salvador]] on 3 February 1977 [[cite:profile]].

His homilies carried the reports of human rights violations gathered by the legal aid office of his archdiocese, and the United Nations truth commission records that right-wing civilian and military circles came to see him as a dangerous enemy [[cite:background]]. On 23 March 1980 he appealed to soldiers and police to stop the repression [[cite:appeal]]. He was killed the next day while celebrating Mass [[cite:profile]]; the commission found that former Major Roberto D'Aubuisson gave the order [[cite:findings]]. See [[event:assassination-of-oscar-romero]]. The Catholic Church beatified him as a martyr on 23 May 2015 and canonised him on 14 October 2018 [[cite:profile]].
