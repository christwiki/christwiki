The Anonymus Valesianus says that Odoacer, son of Edico, came with a force of Sciri, killed the patrician Orestes, deposed [[person:romulus-augustulus]] at [[place:ravenna]] and was made king [[cite:av-rise]]. Procopius calls him one of the emperor's bodyguards, raised to power by barbarian troops to whom he then gave a third of the land of Italy [[cite:procopius]]. Eugippius tells how, as a poorly dressed young man on his way to Italy, he visited the monk Severinus in Noricum, who foretold his rise, and how as king he granted the monk's request to recall an exile [[cite:severinus]]. The Anonymus says that he favoured the Arians [[cite:av-arian]].

The emperor Zeno sent Theoderic and his Goths against him. Odoacer was beaten in the field, held out in Ravenna through a siege of three years, came to terms, and was then killed by Theoderic with his own hand [[cite:av-end]] [[cite:procopius-end]].
