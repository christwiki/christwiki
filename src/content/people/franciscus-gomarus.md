[[person:jacobus-arminius]] tells how the two of them held a conference at The Hague by order of the States of Holland, and how Gomarus then told the States that the difference between them was so great that he would not dare to appear before his Maker with the opinions Arminius held, and that the provinces and churches would be set against one another unless something were done [[cite:arminius]]. That is his opponent's report of his words.

At the Synod of Dort he signed the Canons among the Dutch professors of theology, as professor in the university of Groningen [[cite:subscribers]]. The teaching he put his name to is that election is God's unchangeable purpose and does not rest on foreseen faith [[cite:election]]. See [[event:synod-of-dort]].
