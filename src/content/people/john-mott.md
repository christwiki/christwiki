The record of the conference names Mott, of New York, as chairman of its first commission, on carrying the gospel to all the non-Christian world [[cite:commissions]]. It gives his post as general secretary of the World's Student Christian Federation and says that the delegates chose him unanimously to chair the day sessions at which the commission reports were debated [[cite:opening]]. When the conference ended, the Continuation Committee it had appointed elected him chairman [[cite:chairman]]. See [[event:edinburgh-1910]].
