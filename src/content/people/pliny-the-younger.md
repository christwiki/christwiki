Pliny says he was in his eighteenth year when Vesuvius erupted, which was in AD 79, so he was born in 61 or 62 [[cite:vesuvius]]. Nine books of his letters to friends survive (see [[source:pliny-letters]]).

[[person:trajan]] sent him to govern [[place:bithynia-and-pontus]], where he arrived on 17 September of a year between 109 and 111 [[cite:arrival]]. His official letters from the province, with the emperor's answers, make up a tenth book. Among them is his report on the Christians, in which he says that he had never before attended one of their trials [[cite:christians]]. See [[event:pliny-and-trajan]]. The book ends, without a farewell, with an exchange about a travel permit for his wife [[cite:last]].
