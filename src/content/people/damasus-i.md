Jerome's Chronicle records that Damasus was ordained bishop of [[place:rome]] and that a rival, Ursinus, was set up soon afterwards, with bloodshed between their supporters [[cite:chronicle]]. The edict of 380 defines the Catholic faith as that which "the pontiff Damasus" and Peter of Alexandria follow [[cite:edict]] (see [[event:edict-of-thessalonica]]), and the bishops who met at Constantinople in 382 addressed their statement of faith to him and the other bishops gathered at Rome [[cite:letter382]].

[[person:jerome]] served him as secretary and at his request revised the Latin Gospels against the Greek [[cite:secretary]] [[cite:gospels]] (see [[event:jerome-and-the-vulgate]]). Jerome adds that he wrote verse and died in the reign of Theodosius at almost eighty [[cite:catalogue]]. Prosper's chronicle enters his successor Siricius under the year 384 [[cite:successor]].
