Jefferson's draft of a bill for establishing religious freedom in Virginia is dated 1779 in the edition of his papers [[cite:bill]]. [[person:james-madison]] reported its passage to him by letter in January 1786 [[cite:passage]]. On 1 March 1792 he signed at [[place:philadelphia]] the circular that sent the ratified amendments to the Constitution to the governors of the states [[cite:circular]].

In 1801 the Baptists of Danbury, Connecticut, addressed him as President of the United States [[cite:danbury-address]]. His reply of 1 January 1802 says that religion lies solely between a man and his God and that the First Amendment built "a wall of separation between Church & State" [[cite:danbury-reply]]. See [[event:religious-freedom-in-america]].
