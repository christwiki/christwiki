Sukenik heard of the scrolls through an Armenian dealer, went to [[place:bethlehem]] to see the three held by a dealer there, and recognised them as ancient Hebrew manuscripts. His diary records that his hands shook as he unwrapped one. He acquired the three, the War Scroll, the Thanksgiving Scroll and a second copy of Isaiah, and published selections from them. In 1954 his son Yigael Yadin bought the four scrolls of [[person:athanasius-yeshue-samuel]] for the State of Israel [[cite:discovery]]. See [[event:dead-sea-scrolls-discovered]].
