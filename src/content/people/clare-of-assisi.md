Thomas of Celano, in his life of [[person:francis-of-assisi]], calls Clare a native of [[place:assisi]], noble by family, whom Francis turned to the religious life, and the foundation of the order of "Poor Ladies" that began at the church of San Damiano [[cite:celano]]. The Life written after her death tells that on the night after Palm Sunday she left her home, was received by the friars at the Portiuncula and had her hair cut off there [[cite:flight]]. She then lived at San Damiano for forty-two years [[cite:damiano]].

The Life places her death on the day after the feast of Saint Lawrence, which is 11 August [[cite:death]], and her canonization by Pope Alexander IV at Anagni in 1255, two years later [[cite:canonized]]. See [[event:francis-of-assisi]].
