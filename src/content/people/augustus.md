Suetonius records that he was born on the ninth day before the Kalends of October in the consulship of Cicero and Antonius, which is 23 September 63 BC [[cite:birth]]. He took the name Gaius Caesar under the will of his great-uncle Julius Caesar, and the name Augustus by vote of the senate [[cite:name]]. In his own account of his career he says that in his sixth and seventh consulships he returned the state to the senate and people and was named Augustus for it [[cite:settlement]]. See [[event:augustus-becomes-emperor]].

As Octavian he and Mark Antony backed [[person:herod-the-great]] before the senate, which made Herod king [[cite:herod]]. Luke names him as the Caesar whose decree of enrolment brought Joseph and Mary to Bethlehem [[bible:Luke 2:1-5]]. He died on the fourteenth day before the Kalends of September in the consulship of Sextus Pompeius and Sextus Appuleius, which is 19 August AD 14, thirty-five days before his seventy-sixth birthday [[cite:death]].
