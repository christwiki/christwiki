role: Merchant at Philippi; host of Paul and the believers there
summary: A dealer in purple cloth from Thyatira, living at Philippi, who was the first person Acts names as baptised on Paul's mission in Macedonia. Acts shows the believers of the city gathered in her house.
---

Acts mentions Lydia in one episode. She came from the city of Thyatira, sold purple cloth, and was "one who worshiped God". [[person:paul]] and his companions met her on the sabbath at a place of prayer by the river outside [[place:philippi]]. Acts says that the Lord opened her heart to what Paul said. She was baptised with her household and pressed the travellers to stay in her house [[bible:Acts 16:13-15]].

When Paul and [[person:silas|Silas]] were released from prison they went to her house and met the believers there before leaving the city [[bible:Acts 16:40]]. See [[event:second-missionary-journey]]. Paul's letter to the Philippians does not name her.
