John was the brother of the elector [[person:frederick-the-wise]]. When Frederick died in May 1525, [[person:martin-luther]] wrote to John as the new elector, on whom, he said, the misfortune fell in perilous times [[cite:accession]].

He attended the diet of [[place:speyer]] in 1526 [[cite:spalatin]]. At the diet of 1529 the protest against the majority was made in his name first: it recalls that "we, Duke John, Elector of Saxony" had proposed a compromise, and declares the majority's resolution not binding [[cite:protest]]. His is the first signature under the confession presented at [[place:augsburg]] in 1530 [[cite:augsburg]]. See [[event:protestation-at-speyer]] and [[event:augsburg-confession]].
