Benedict XIV names him as Charles Thomas de Tournon, patriarch of Antioch, commissary and apostolic visitor in the kingdom of China, to whom [[person:clement-xi]] sent the Holy Office's answers of 1704 so that he might see them observed. A decree of the Holy Office of 25 September 1710, which Benedict quotes, calls him cardinal and upholds the mandate on the rites that he had issued in China in 1707 [[cite:d1704]]. See [[event:chinese-rites-controversy]].
