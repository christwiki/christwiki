Melchizedek appears in three verses of Genesis. He is king of Salem and priest of God Most High, he brings out bread and wine to [[person:abraham|Abram]] after a battle, blesses him, and receives from him a tenth of everything [[bible:Gen 14:18-20]]. A psalm addressed to one who is to rule from Zion calls the one it addresses "a priest forever in the order of Melchizedek" [[bible:Ps 110:2, 4]].

The letter to the Hebrews takes the psalm as spoken of Jesus and makes Melchizedek the pattern of a priesthood that does not depend on descent from Levi [[bible:Heb 7:1-17]]. Josephus, in the first century AD, explains his name as "the righteous king" and says that his city Salem was afterwards called [[place:jerusalem]] [[cite:josephus]].
