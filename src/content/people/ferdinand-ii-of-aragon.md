Andrés Bernáldez, a priest of his own time, says that he was born in Aragon in March 1452, the son of King Juan of Navarre and Aragon, and that he married [[person:isabella-i-of-castile]] at Valladolid in 1469, when he was king of Sicily and prince of Aragon [[cite:lineage]].

The bull of 1 November 1478 is addressed to him and Isabella as king and queen of Castile and León and lets them choose, remove and replace inquisitors [[cite:bull]]. They used it on 27 September 1480, when they commissioned two Dominicans and reserved the right to remove them [[cite:commission]].

On 18 April 1482 [[person:sixtus-iv]] ordered that in Aragon, Valencia, Mallorca and Catalonia the accused be told who had testified against them and be free to appeal to Rome [[cite:bull-april]]. Ferdinand answered from Córdoba on 13 May, signing as king of Castile and of Aragon. He gave the report of these concessions no credit, he wrote, and if they had been granted he did not intend ever to let them take effect; inquisitors in his kingdoms and lands were to be appointed according to his pleasure and will, and he would never allow others to hold the office against it [[cite:letter]].

Bernáldez describes how the king of [[place:granada]] gave him the keys of the city on 2 January 1492 [[cite:granada]]. On 31 March, in that city, he and the queen signed the edict that ordered every Jew to leave their kingdoms [[cite:edict]]. See [[event:spanish-inquisition]].
