The Edict of Fontainebleau is issued in his name as "king of France and Navarre" and calls Henry IV, who granted the Edict of Nantes, his grandfather [[cite:preamble]]. It is dated in October 1685 and "of our reign the forty-third", which places the beginning of his reign in 1643 [[cite:clause]]. The preamble states his claim that most of his Reformed subjects had become Catholics and that the old edict was therefore no longer needed [[cite:preamble]]. See [[event:revocation-of-the-edict-of-nantes]].
