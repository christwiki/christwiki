name: Miriam
role: Prophetess, sister of Moses and Aaron
summary: The sister of Aaron and Moses, called a prophetess. She led the women's song after the crossing of the sea, was struck with leprosy for speaking against Moses, and died at Kadesh.
---

Miriam is listed with Aaron and Moses as a child of Amram and Jochebed [[bible:Num 26:59]]. The sister who watches over the infant Moses among the reeds is not named [[bible:Exod 2:4]]. After the crossing of the sea "Miriam the prophetess, the sister of Aaron" took a tambourine and led the women in song and dance [[bible:Exod 15:20-21]].

In the wilderness she and Aaron spoke against [[person:moses]] and claimed that God had spoken through them too. She was struck with leprosy, Moses prayed for her, and she was kept outside the camp for seven days while the people waited [[bible:Num 12:1-15]]. She died and was buried at [[place:kadesh-barnea|Kadesh]] [[bible:Num 20:1]]. The prophet Micah names her beside her brothers as one of the three whom God sent to lead Israel out of Egypt [[bible:Mic 6:4]].
