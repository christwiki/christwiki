The Moravian Church's history says that Nicholas Louis, Count Zinzendorf, was brought up by his grandmother at Great Hennersdorf, studied at the academy of [[place:halle]] and the university of Wittenberg, took a post as counsellor at the Saxon court in Dresden to satisfy his family, and bought an estate in Upper Lusatia with the aim of winning souls for Christ [[cite:youth]]. He wrote the statutes of [[place:herrnhut]] and spoke for three hours on the day they were adopted, 12 May 1727, a day on which, he said twenty-one years later, it had been in doubt whether Herrnhut would become a church of the Saviour or a new assembly of sects [[cite:pledge]].

He had resolved while at Halle to work for missions among peoples neglected by others, and on 21 August 1732 he accompanied the first two missionaries on the first stage of their journey and blessed [[person:leonard-dober]] with the laying on of hands [[cite:dober]].
