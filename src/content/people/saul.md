Saul, son of Kish, of the tribe of Benjamin, was anointed by [[person:samuel]] and made king before the people at Mizpah and at [[place:gilgal]] [[bible:1 Sam 9:1-2]] [[bible:1 Sam 10:1, 24]] [[bible:1 Sam 11:15]]. He relieved Jabesh-gilead and fought the Philistines and the neighbouring peoples throughout his reign [[bible:1 Sam 11:11]] [[bible:1 Sam 14:47-52]]. Samuel twice told him that he had lost the kingdom, first for offering sacrifice himself and then for sparing the king and the spoil of Amalek [[bible:1 Sam 13:13-14]] [[bible:1 Sam 15:26-28]].

He took [[person:david]] into his service, grew jealous of him, tried to have him killed and hunted him in the wilderness [[bible:1 Sam 18:8-11]] [[bible:1 Sam 19:1]] [[bible:1 Sam 24:1-2]]. Before his last battle he consulted a medium at Endor [[bible:1 Sam 28:7-19]]. He died with three of his sons on Mount Gilboa [[bible:1 Sam 31:1-6]], and David made a lament for him [[bible:2 Sam 1:17-27]]. The sources do not agree on how long he reigned, and his dates are approximate; see [[event:saul-anointed-king]].
