The bishop of Tarbes describes her in 1862 as a child of fourteen, born at [[place:lourdes]] of a poor family, and says that by her own account she saw and heard a being that called itself the Immaculate Conception [[cite:bishop]]. He judged that Mary had really appeared to her eighteen times in the grotto of Massabielle, from 11 February 1858 [[cite:judgment]]. The sanctuary at Lourdes dates the saying to 25 March 1858 [[cite:words]].

The sanctuary gives her birth as 7 January 1844 and says that she was admitted in 1860 to the hospice managed by the Sisters of Nevers, lived afterwards as Sister Marie-Bernard in their mother house at Nevers, and died there on 16 April 1879; she was canonized on 8 December 1933 [[cite:life]].
