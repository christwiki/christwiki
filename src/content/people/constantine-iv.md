Constantine succeeded his father Constans II, who was murdered in Sicily, and ruled at first with his two brothers [[cite:accession]]. He wrote to Rome asking for delegates to a meeting on the unity of the churches, received the legates of Pope [[person:agatho]] in the palace in November 680, and sat in state at the council's sessions [[cite:lp]].

Theophanes, who calls him "most Christian", says that he gathered 289 bishops for the [[event:third-council-of-constantinople]], and that he died after a reign of seventeen years [[cite:council]].
