The register of baptisms at [[place:pisa|Pisa]] records his baptism on 19 February 1564 [[cite:birth]]. In his letter to the Grand Duchess Christina he argued that the Bible, rightly understood, cannot contradict what observation and demonstration establish about nature [[cite:christina]]. In February 1616 he was warned by Cardinal [[person:robert-bellarmine]] and ordered by the commissary of the Holy Office to give up the opinion that the earth moves [[cite:injunction]].

The sentence of 22 June 1633 calls him a Florentine, seventy years old, declares him vehemently suspected of heresy for his *Dialogue* and condemns him to imprisonment; he abjured the same day [[cite:sentence]]. The pope at once changed the prison to a palace in Rome, then to Siena, and in December to his own country house [[cite:confinement]]. Notes collected by Vincenzio Galilei and printed in the same volume say that he died at his villa at Arcetri on 8 January 1642 [[cite:death]]. See [[event:galileo-affair]].
