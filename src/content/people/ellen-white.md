A publishers' footnote in her *Early Writings* gives her birth as 26 November 1827 at Gorham, Maine [[cite:youth]]. She says that she was converted at eleven, was baptized and joined the Methodist church at twelve, and at thirteen heard [[person:william-miller]] lecture at Portland [[cite:youth]]. She relates that in December 1844, at prayer with four other women, she was shown in a vision the Advent people on a path to the holy city [[cite:vision]]. A vision she dates to March 1849 concerns the heavenly sanctuary and the Sabbath [[cite:sanctuary]].

The Seventh-day Adventist Church teaches that the gift of prophecy was manifested in her ministry, that her writings speak with prophetic authority, and that they make clear that the Bible is the standard by which all teaching must be tested [[cite:belief-18]]. See [[event:great-disappointment]].
