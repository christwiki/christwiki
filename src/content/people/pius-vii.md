Pius VII wrote his first encyclical from the monastery of San Giorgio Maggiore at [[place:venice]] on 15 May 1800, two months after his election; the cardinals had met at Venice to vote [[cite:elected]]. The convention of 1801 with the French government was made in his name and that of the First Consul [[cite:concordat]]. In it he agreed to ask the existing French bishops to resign and to give canonical institution to bishops named by the First Consul [[cite:concordat-bishops]].

On 17 May 1809 [[person:napoleon]], as emperor, decreed the Papal States united to the French Empire and [[place:rome]] a free imperial city [[cite:annexation]]. Pius had allowed the Jesuits to re-form in Russia in 1801 [[cite:jesuits-russia]], and on 7 August 1814, at Rome, he restored the Society of Jesus for all countries [[cite:jesuits]].
