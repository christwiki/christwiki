The chronology that Vida Scudder prefixed to her translation of the letters gives the outline of Catherine's life: born at Siena on 25 March 1347, the youngest child of the dyer Jacopo Benincasa; clothed in the Dominican habit as a tertiary living at home in 1363 or 1364; sent by the Florentines to [[place:avignon]] in 1376; died in April 1380, on the 30th by Scudder's table [[cite:life]].

Her letters are her own voice. To [[person:gregory-xi]] she wrote that he should be "manly" and "not timorous", come to the seat of Peter, and reform the church by giving it good priests [[cite:gregory]]. See [[event:avignon-papacy]]; her part in the dispute that followed is told in [[event:western-schism]].
