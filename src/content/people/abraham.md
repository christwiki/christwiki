Genesis introduces him as Abram, son of Terah, from [[place:ur]] [[bible:Gen 11:27-31]]. At seventy-five he leaves [[place:haran]] for Canaan at God's command ([[event:call-of-abraham]]) [[bible:Gen 12:1-5]]. God makes a covenant with him, renames him Abraham and gives circumcision as its sign ([[event:covenant-with-abraham]]) [[bible:Gen 17:1-11]]. Ishmael is born to him by Hagar and Isaac by [[person:sarah]] [[bible:Gen 16:15-16]] [[bible:Gen 21:1-5]], and he is tested in [[event:binding-of-isaac]]. He dies at 175 and is buried by his two sons in the cave of Machpelah at [[place:hebron]] [[bible:Gen 25:7-10]].

Later Scripture calls him the friend of God [[bible:Isa 41:8]] [[bible:Jas 2:23]]. Matthew's genealogy of Jesus begins with him [[bible:Matt 1:1-2]], and [[person:paul]] calls him the father of all who believe, circumcised or not [[bible:Rom 4:11-12]]. The dates of his birth and death are worked out from the Bible's own figures and have no outside anchor; see the dating note at [[event:call-of-abraham]].
