---
name: Hosea
altNames: [Osee]
role: Prophet in Israel, mid eighth century BC
summary: Prophet of the northern kingdom in its last decades. He read his marriage to an unfaithful wife as a picture of the covenant between the Lord and Israel, and set beside the coming judgment a love that would not give Israel up.
---

Hosea son of Beeri prophesied, according to the heading of his book, from the days of [[person:jeroboam-ii]] of Israel into those of [[person:hezekiah]] of Judah [[bible:Hos 1:1]]. The first three chapters tell of his marriage to Gomer, the naming of their children and the buying of an adulterous woman, all as signs of the Lord's dealings with Israel [[bible:Hos 1:2-9]] [[bible:Hos 3:1-5]]. The rest of the book charges Israel with faithlessness, bloodshed and idols [[bible:Hos 4:1-3]] [[bible:Hos 8:4-7]], and yet holds out healing and return [[bible:Hos 11:8-9]] [[bible:Hos 14:1-4]].

The New Testament quotes him several times, among them "I desire mercy, and not sacrifice" [[bible:Hos 6:6]] [[bible:Matt 9:13]] and "Out of Egypt I called my son" [[bible:Hos 11:1]] [[bible:Matt 2:15]]. See [[event:amos-and-hosea]].
