John Hales, who watched the sessions, reports that when the cited Remonstrants were admitted to the synod on 6 December 1618 Episcopius stood up and spoke for them [[cite:arrival]]. He said that they had come to hold a conference about the cause they had maintained with a good conscience; the synod answered that they had not been called to a conference and that it was not their opponent [[cite:arrival]]. The cause was that of the five articles of 1610 [[cite:remonstrance]].

In January 1619 the president told them that they had treated the synod as an adverse party and refused its questions, and dismissed them [[cite:dismissal]]. Hales records that Episcopius said as he left that the Lord God would judge of frauds and lies [[cite:dismissal]]. The synod went on to depose the cited ministers from their offices [[cite:sentence]]. See [[event:synod-of-dort]].
