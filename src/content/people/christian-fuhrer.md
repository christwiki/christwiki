Christian Führer was pastor of the Nikolaikirche in [[place:leipzig]]. By his own account he had earlier served at Lastau and Colditz [[cite:fuhrer-3]], established the yearly peace decade at the Nikolaikirche from 1981, and saw peace prayers held there every Monday from 20 September 1982 [[cite:fuhrer-1]]. Richard Schröder says that the parish council, which Führer chaired, opened the church to the prayers after many critical questions, and that Führer and the superintendent never cancelled one under pressure from the state [[cite:schroder]].

On 2 October 1989 he asked the other churches of the city to hold peace prayers too [[cite:fuhrer-2]]. A week later the state security service counted more than 5,000 people at prayers in four churches, and 50,000 to 60,000 in the demonstration that set out from the Nikolaikirche [[cite:stasi-100]].
