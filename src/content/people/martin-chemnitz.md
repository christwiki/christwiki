Chemnitz's name, "Martinus Chemnitius D.", closes the list of six signatures under the Epitome of the Formula of Concord, dated at Bergen on 29 May 1577; the others are [[person:jakob-andreae]], Nikolaus Selnecker, Andreas Musculus, Christoph Körner and David Chytraeus [[cite:signatures]]. The document they signed makes Scripture the only judge of doctrine and receives the creeds, the Augsburg Confession and Luther's catechisms as its witnesses [[cite:rule]].
