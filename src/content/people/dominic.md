Jordan of Saxony, his successor, says that Dominic came from the village of Caleruega in the diocese of Osma and became subprior of the cathedral chapter there [[cite:youth]]. After the death of his bishop he stayed on in the region of [[place:toulouse]], preaching, for nearly ten years [[cite:alone]]. [[person:honorius-iii]] addressed him in 1216 as prior of the community at Saint Romanus in Toulouse [[cite:bull]].

He fell ill at [[place:bologna]] in 1221, a year in which the order's general chapter met there, and died in that city. The bishop of Ostia, later [[person:gregory-ix]], who had known him well, conducted his funeral [[cite:death]]. See [[event:dominicans]].
