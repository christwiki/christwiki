Theodosius came of a Spanish family and had made his name as a soldier when [[person:gratian]] proclaimed him emperor at Sirmium on 16 January 379 and shared with him the war against the barbarians [[cite:accession]]. He was baptised at [[place:thessalonica]] during an illness by its bishop Ascholius [[cite:baptism]]. In February 380 he ordered all his subjects to hold the faith of the bishops of Rome and Alexandria [[cite:edict]] (see [[event:edict-of-thessalonica]]), and in 381 he summoned the eastern bishops to [[event:council-of-constantinople-i|a council at Constantinople]] [[cite:council]]. Later laws of his forbade sacrifice and the visiting of temples [[cite:sacrifice]].

After he ordered reprisals at Thessalonica that became a massacre, [[person:ambrose]] of Milan wrote that he could not celebrate the eucharist in his presence until he repented, and Theodosius did public penance [[cite:letter]] [[cite:penance]] (see [[event:ambrose-and-theodosius]]). He died at [[place:milan]] on 17 January 395, and his sons Arcadius and Honorius took the East and the West [[cite:death]] [[cite:sons]].
