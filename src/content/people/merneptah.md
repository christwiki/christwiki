Merneptah was the thirteenth son of Ramesses II and followed him on the throne [[cite:record]]. In his fifth year he turned back an invasion from Libya and had the victory celebrated in a long hymn [[cite:hymn]]. The stele that carries it stood in his mortuary temple at [[place:thebes]], where it was found in 1896 [[cite:find]]. Its closing lines add a list of places and peoples that he claims to have subdued, among them Israel [[cite:israel]]. See [[event:merneptah-stele]].

The Bible does not name him. His reign is usually dated about 1213–1203 BC (so James K. Hoffmeier, *Journal of the Evangelical Theological Society* 50, 2007, p. 232); other reconstructions of Egyptian chronology differ by some years.
