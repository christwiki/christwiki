Benedict XIV's review of the controversy says that the examination of the Chinese rites begun under Innocent XII was cut short by that pope's death, and that Clement XI, who succeeded him, approved the Holy Office's answers forbidding the rites and had them sent to his visitor in China, [[person:charles-maillard-de-tournon]] [[cite:d1704]]. His constitution *Ex illa die*, which Benedict reproduces, repeats the prohibitions [[cite:exilladie]]. See [[event:chinese-rites-controversy]].
