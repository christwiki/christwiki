The *Passion* names her among the young catechumens arrested together and calls her the fellow-servant of Revocatus, which suggests that both were slaves [[cite:intro]]. She was eight months pregnant. Because the law did not allow a pregnant woman to be executed, she feared that she would be held back and die later among criminals. Her companions prayed, and she went into labour three days before the games. When a guard taunted her for crying out, she answered that now she suffered alone, but in the arena another would be in her to suffer for her. She bore a daughter, whom a Christian woman brought up as her own [[cite:birth]].

She entered the arena with [[person:perpetua]], was thrown down by the same animal, and was killed with the sword with the rest [[cite:death]].
