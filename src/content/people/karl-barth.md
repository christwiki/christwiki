The introduction that the Evangelical Church in Germany publishes with the Barmen Declaration names "theologian Karl Barth" beside four pastors as one of the church leaders who opposed what it calls the church's captivity to National Socialism and who brought the synod of May 1934 together [[cite:ekd-en]]. The first thesis of the declaration says that Jesus Christ, as attested in holy Scripture, is the one Word of God, and rejects the teaching that the church must acknowledge other events, powers, figures and truths beside it as God's revelation [[cite:thesis-1]]. The documents cited here do not say who drafted the theses. See [[event:barmen-declaration]].
