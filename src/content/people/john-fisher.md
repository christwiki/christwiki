An Act of the Parliament of 1534 attainted John, bishop of Rochester, of misprision of treason because he had refused the oath to the succession [[cite:attainder]]. A London chronicler of the time records that he was arraigned at Westminster on 17 June 1535 for denying the king to be supreme head of the Church of England and beheaded on Tower Hill on 22 June, and that his head was set on London Bridge [[cite:death]]. See [[event:act-of-supremacy]].
