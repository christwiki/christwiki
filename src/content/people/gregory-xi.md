[[person:catherine-of-siena]] wrote to Gregory repeatedly while he was at [[place:avignon]], begging him to come to Rome, to resist the counsel of those who held him back and to give the church good pastors [[cite:letters]]. The editor of her letters, Vida Scudder, records that he announced his intention of returning in 1372, declared war on Florence in 1376, reached [[place:rome]] on 17 January 1377 and died in March 1378 [[cite:events]]. See [[event:avignon-papacy]] and, for what followed his death, [[event:western-schism]].
