The record of the council of 1274 lists "brother Bonaventure of Albano" among the cardinal bishops seated at the pope's right [[cite:cardinal]]. While the council waited for the Greeks he preached to the assembled prelates on the verse of Baruch that bids Jerusalem arise and look toward the east [[bible:Bar 5:5]], and he preached again at the mass of the feast of Peter and Paul, when the creed was sung in Latin and in Greek [[cite:preaching]].

He died at the hour of matins on Sunday 15 July 1274. The record calls him a man of eminent learning and eloquence, outstanding in holiness, kind, approachable and merciful, beloved of God and men. He was buried the same day in the house of the Friars Minor at [[place:lyon]], with the pope and the whole council present; Peter, cardinal bishop of Ostia, preached on David's lament for Jonathan [[bible:2 Sam 1:26]], and the pope ordered every priest in the world to sing a mass for his soul [[cite:death]].
