Avvakum writes that he was born in the village of Grigorovo, in the country of Nizhny Novgorod, the son of a priest named Peter [[cite:birth]]. When [[person:nikon]] ordered the three-finger sign of the cross he gathered passages from the Fathers against it [[cite:instruction]], and he was sent to Siberia with his wife and children [[cite:siberia]].

Brought back and set before the patriarchs in Moscow, he defended the two-finger sign as the tradition of the Russian saints, would not be reconciled, and was banished to Pustozersk, from where he wrote to the tsar [[cite:hearing]]. See [[event:nikon-and-the-old-believers]].
