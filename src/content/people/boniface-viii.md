The Florentine chronicler Giovanni Villani, who lived through his pontificate, says that Cardinal Benedetto Caetani of Anagni was elected in 1294 after Celestine V laid down the papacy, a resignation Villani says the cardinal had himself advised [[cite:elected]]. Villani calls him learned in Scripture and in canon law, great-hearted, proud and cruel to his enemies, and credits him with the Sixth Book of the Decretals [[cite:character]].

His bull [[event:unam-sanctam]] of 1302 states that it is necessary for salvation for every human creature to be subject to the Roman pontiff [[cite:bull]]. In September 1303 men in the pay of [[person:philip-iv-of-france]] seized him in his palace at Anagni. He was freed by the townspeople after three days, returned to [[place:rome]] and died there on 12 October 1303 [[cite:anagni]].
