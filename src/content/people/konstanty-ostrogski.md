What this wiki reports of Ostrogski comes from a letter addressed to him by [[person:clement-viii]] on 15 January 1605, headed to "Constantius, duke of Ostrog, palatine of Kiev". The pope is replying to a letter of his. He takes up Ostrogski's assertion that the bishops had brought the business of union to Rome in haste and without his knowledge, assures him that the rites and ceremonies of his church had been kept whole, and answers his plan to approach the patriarchs of Antioch and Constantinople by saying that this would be a long and laborious road for a man of his age; he urges the duke to lead by his own example and come into the Roman church [[cite:letter]]. The letter shows that more than eight years after the synod of [[place:brest]] he had not accepted the union [[cite:letter]]. See [[event:union-of-brest]].
