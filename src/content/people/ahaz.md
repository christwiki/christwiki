Ahaz son of Jotham became king at twenty and reigned sixteen years. Kings says that he did not do what was right and that he made his son pass through the fire [[bible:2 Kings 16:1-4]]. When Rezin of Damascus and Pekah of Israel besieged [[place:jerusalem]], [[person:isaiah]] urged him to stand firm and offered him a sign, which he declined [[bible:Isa 7:1-12]]. He appealed instead to Tiglath-pileser of Assyria, sending the treasure of the temple and the palace [[bible:2 Kings 16:5-9]]. An Assyrian tablet lists him, under the fuller name Jehoahaz, among the western kings who paid tribute [[cite:tribute]].

After meeting the Assyrian king at [[place:damascus]] he had a copy of the altar he saw there built in the temple and moved the bronze altar aside [[bible:2 Kings 16:10-16]]. Chronicles adds that Assyria's help brought him no relief [[bible:2 Chr 28:16-21]]. His son [[person:hezekiah]] succeeded him [[bible:2 Kings 16:20]].
