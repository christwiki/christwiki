A London chronicler records the birth of a son to [[person:henry-viii]] and his queen at Hampton Court in 1537, on the eve of St Edward's day [[cite:birth]], and his proclamation as King Edward VI on 31 January 1547, three days after his father's death [[cite:accession]]. The first Act of Uniformity speaks of the king acting with the advice of his uncle the lord protector and his council [[cite:uniformity]]. That Act authorised the first Book of Common Prayer and a second Act the revised book of 1552 [[cite:second-act]]; see [[event:book-of-common-prayer]]. The same chronicler records that Edward died on Thursday 6 July 1553 [[cite:death]].
