The constitution *Unigenitus* does not name him in its title. It names his book: a New Testament in French, in several volumes, with moral reflections on each verse, printed at Paris in 1693, 1694 and 1699 [[cite:title]]. Among the 101 propositions it takes from the book are that it is useful and necessary at all times and for every kind of person to study Scripture, and that the reading of Scripture is for all; the censure says that the propositions renew those of [[person:cornelius-jansen]] [[cite:censure]].

The duc de Saint-Simon, writing of the French court, calls the book "the New Testament of Pere Quesnel" and says that the king's former confessor, the Jesuit La Chaise, kept a copy on his table, saying that he liked what was good wherever he found it [[cite:saint-simon]]. See [[event:jansenism]].
