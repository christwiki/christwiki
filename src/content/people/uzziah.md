Uzziah, whom Kings usually calls Azariah, became king at sixteen and reigned fifty-two years [[bible:2 Kings 15:1-2]]. Chronicles describes his wars against the Philistines, his towers and his army [[bible:2 Chr 26:6-15]]. It says that when he went into the temple to burn incense he was struck with leprosy, and that he lived apart until his death while his son Jotham governed [[bible:2 Chr 26:16-21]]. Kings records the leprosy without giving a cause [[bible:2 Kings 15:5]].

[[person:amos]] and [[person:hosea]] prophesied in his reign [[bible:Amos 1:1]] [[bible:Hos 1:1]], an earthquake in his days was long remembered [[bible:Zech 14:5]], and [[person:isaiah]] dates his vision to the year of his death [[bible:Isa 6:1]]. For that year see [[event:call-of-isaiah]]. He appears in Matthew's genealogy of Jesus [[bible:Matt 1:8-9]].
