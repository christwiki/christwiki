Li Tim Oi was the deaconess in charge of the Anglican congregation at [[place:macau]] in 1943, when the war kept her bishop from reaching her [[cite:hall-1943]]. In January 1944 she travelled to unoccupied China, where Bishop [[person:ronald-hall]] ordained her a priest at [[place:zhaoqing]] [[cite:zhaoqing]]. In 1946 the Chinese House of Bishops asked Hall to accept her resignation from her priestly ministry, which she had already offered [[cite:bishops-1946]]. See [[event:florence-li-tim-oi]].

The Anglican Communion Office's text of the Lambeth resolution on her case carries her name as a subject heading, with the years 1907 and 1992 [[cite:dates]]. Her own account of her life, given in Toronto in 1984, is in [[source:li-tim-oi-much-beloved-daughter]].
