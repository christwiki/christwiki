---
name: Samuel
role: Prophet and judge of Israel
summary: Born in answer to his mother Hannah's vow and raised at Shiloh, Samuel led Israel as prophet and judge after the loss of the ark. He anointed both Saul and David as king.
---

Samuel was the son of Elkanah and Hannah. He was promised to the Lord before his birth and brought up by the priest [[person:eli]] at [[place:shiloh]] [[bible:1 Sam 1:11, 20-28]]. He was called as a boy and came to be known as a prophet throughout Israel [[bible:1 Sam 3:10, 19-20]]. After the Philistines captured the ark he led the people in repentance at Mizpah, and he judged Israel on a yearly circuit from his home at Ramah [[bible:1 Sam 7:3-6, 15-17]].

When the elders asked for a king he warned them and then anointed [[person:saul]] [[bible:1 Sam 8:4-9]] [[bible:1 Sam 10:1]]. He later told Saul that the Lord had rejected him, and he anointed [[person:david]] at Bethlehem [[bible:1 Sam 15:26-28]] [[bible:1 Sam 16:13]]. He died while Saul was still king and was buried at Ramah [[bible:1 Sam 25:1]]. Jeremiah names him beside Moses as an intercessor [[bible:Jer 15:1]]. In Acts, Peter counts the prophets "from Samuel", and Paul ends the time of the judges with "Samuel the prophet" [[bible:Acts 3:24]] [[bible:Acts 13:20]].
