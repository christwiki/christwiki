Roger of Wendover says that [[person:innocent-iii]] was succeeded by Honorius, "formerly called Cencio", who held the see for ten years, seven months and nineteen days [[cite:elected]]. In the first year of his pontificate he issued the privilege that confirmed the community of [[person:dominic]] at [[place:toulouse]] under the Rule of Augustine [[cite:dominic]], and a month later addressed its members as preachers [[cite:preachers]]. In his eighth year he confirmed the Rule of the brothers of [[person:francis-of-assisi]], in which Francis promises obedience to "the Lord Pope Honorius" [[cite:rule]].

Wendover dates his death to 18 March 1227 and names Gregory, bishop of Ostia, as his successor [[cite:death]]. See [[event:dominicans]] and [[event:francis-of-assisi]].
