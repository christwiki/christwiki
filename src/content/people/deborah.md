---
name: Deborah
role: Prophetess and judge of Israel
summary: A prophetess who judged Israel in the hill country of Ephraim. She summoned Barak to fight the Canaanite commander Sisera at Mount Tabor, and the victory song in Judges 5 bears her name.
---

Judges introduces Deborah as a prophetess, the wife of Lappidoth, who sat under a palm tree between Ramah and Bethel, where the Israelites came to her for judgment [[bible:Judg 4:4-5]]. She summoned Barak in the Lord's name to gather the men of Naphtali and Zebulun on [[place:mount-tabor]]. She went with him at his request, and told him that the honour of the day would go to a woman [[bible:Judg 4:6-10]]. It was she who gave the word to attack Sisera's chariots [[bible:Judg 4:14-16]].

The song of Judges 5, which she and Barak sing, calls her "a mother in Israel" [[bible:Judg 5:1, 7]]. After the victory, the book says, the land had rest for forty years [[bible:Judg 5:31]]. Her story is told in [[event:age-of-the-judges]].
