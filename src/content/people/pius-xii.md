Pius XII dated the constitution *Munificentissimus Deus* on 1 November 1950, in the twelfth year of his pontificate [[cite:date]]. In it he defined as a divinely revealed dogma that the Virgin Mary was assumed body and soul into heavenly glory [[cite:definition]]; see [[event:dogma-of-the-assumption]]. In 1954 he wrote to the church in China that those who held to the principles of the "Three Autonomies" could not be considered Catholics [[cite:autonomies]], and in 1958 that bishops consecrated without the mandate of the Apostolic See incurred excommunication [[cite:consecrations]]; see [[event:three-self-movement]].

Before his election he was Eugenio Pacelli. The concordat of 20 July 1933 names Cardinal Eugenio Pacelli, Secretary of State, as the plenipotentiary of [[person:pius-xi]], and he signed it in the Vatican with Franz von Papen [[cite:concordat-sign]]. Cardinal [[person:michael-von-faulhaber]] noted in his diary that during his stay in Rome in January 1937 he was twice with the bishops at Pacelli's [[cite:diary]]. The editors of that diary record that Pacelli had been nuncio in Munich, where the two men became close, and that he was pope from 1939 to 1958 [[cite:biography]]; see [[event:mit-brennender-sorge]].
