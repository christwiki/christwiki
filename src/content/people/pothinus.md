The letter of the churches of Vienne and [[place:lyon]] calls Pothinus the man "entrusted with the bishopric of Lyons". He was over ninety and could hardly breathe when soldiers brought him to the tribunal. Asked by the governor who the God of the Christians was, he answered, "If you are worthy, you shall know." The bystanders struck and pelted him, and he died in prison two days afterwards [[cite:death]]. Eusebius adds that [[person:irenaeus]] followed him as bishop [[cite:successor]]. If he was over ninety in 177, he was born before AD 87. See [[event:martyrs-of-lyon]].
