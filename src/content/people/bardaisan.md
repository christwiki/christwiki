The Chronicle of Edessa gives his birthday as 11 Tammuz in year 465 of the Seleucid era, which is 11 July AD 154 [[cite:birth]]. Eusebius describes him as a most able man and a skilled disputant in Syriac, who wrote dialogues against the followers of [[person:marcion]] and others, among them a dialogue *On Fate*, and whose many pupils turned his works into Greek. He adds that Bardaisan had once followed Valentinus and never wholly freed himself from that teaching [[cite:eusebius]].

In the dialogue from his school cited here, he argues that neither nature nor the stars take away human freedom, and points to the Christians, who keep the same way of life in every country under every sky [[cite:laws]].
