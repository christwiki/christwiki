The Continuator of Theophanes introduces Methodios as a man who had kept his integrity through long imprisonment, in a cell so foul that his hair had fallen out. He was installed when the iconoclast patriarch John the Grammarian was removed [[cite:installed]], and with the empress [[person:theodora-the-empress]] led the procession to Hagia Sophia on the first Sunday of Lent [[cite:procession]]. The same history says that he died after four years as patriarch [[cite:death]].

The Synodikon of Orthodoxy names him with Germanos, Tarasios and Nikephoros as a champion and teacher of orthodoxy [[cite:synodikon]]. See [[event:triumph-of-orthodoxy]].
