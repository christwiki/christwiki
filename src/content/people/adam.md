---
name: Adam
role: The first man in Genesis
summary: "In Genesis, the first human being, formed from the dust and placed in the garden of Eden. Paul sets him beside Christ: through Adam came sin and death, through Christ righteousness and life."
---

Genesis tells that God formed the man from the dust of the ground and put him in the garden of Eden [[bible:Gen 2:7-8]]. His name is also the Hebrew word for humankind, and Genesis uses it of male and female together [[bible:Gen 5:1-2]]. In [[event:the-fall]] he and his wife eat from the forbidden tree and are sent out of the garden [[bible:Gen 3:6]] [[bible:Gen 3:23-24]]. He is the father of Cain, Abel and Seth [[bible:Gen 4:1-2]] [[bible:Gen 4:25]], and Genesis gives his age at death as 930 years [[bible:Gen 5:5]].

Luke's genealogy of Jesus runs back to Adam [[bible:Luke 3:38]]. [[person:paul]] calls Adam a foreshadowing of the one who was to come [[bible:Rom 5:14]] and calls Christ "the last Adam" [[bible:1 Cor 15:45]].
