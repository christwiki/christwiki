When Urban became pope there were two claimants. Fulcher of Chartres says that Guibert of Ravenna, backed by the emperor, for some time kept Urban out of St Peter's in [[place:rome]], and that Urban gained full control only in the year the crusaders passed through, with the help of [[person:matilda-of-tuscany]] [[cite:fulcher]]. In 1095 he held a synod at Piacenza, where envoys of the emperor of [[place:constantinople]] asked for help [[cite:bernold-1095]]. His own letter to Flanders says that at the council in Auvergne he enjoined an expedition to free the churches of the East, for the remission of sins [[cite:letter]]. Bernold of Constance calls him the chief author of the expedition [[cite:bernold-1096]].

He died at Rome on 29 July 1099, after eleven years and five months as pope [[cite:bernold-1099]]. [[place:jerusalem]] had fallen to the crusaders on 15 July [[cite:gesta]].
