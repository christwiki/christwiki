Anna Komnene describes Godfrey as rich and proud of his lineage, says that he had sold his land to make the journey, and tells how he took the oath demanded by [[person:alexios-i-komnenos]] only after a fight outside [[place:constantinople]] [[cite:alexiad-5]] [[cite:alexiad-9]].

At [[place:jerusalem]] the Gesta Francorum puts him and his brother Eustace in the siege tower from which the wall was first crossed [[cite:gesta-capture]]. Eight days after the capture the leaders chose him as head of the city [[cite:gesta-after]]. Raymond of Aguilers, who calls him the duke of Lorraine and names him among the first to enter the city [[cite:raymond-capture]], says that the princes first urged Count Raymond of Saint-Gilles to accept the kingdom, and that he answered that he shrank from the name of king in that city [[cite:raymond]]. The letter sent to the pope that September calls Godfrey, "by the grace of God", defender of the Holy Sepulchre [[cite:letter]].
