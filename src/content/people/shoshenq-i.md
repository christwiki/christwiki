Shoshenq I founded Egypt's Twenty-second Dynasty and built the gate at Karnak now called the Bubastite Portal. Kings names him twice: as the king of Egypt with whom [[person:jeroboam-i]] took refuge from Solomon [[bible:1 Kings 11:40]], and as the invader who took the treasures of the temple in the fifth year of [[person:rehoboam]] [[bible:1 Kings 14:25-26]]; see [[event:shishak-invades]].

His own monuments record a campaign in Palestine. The relief beside the portal lists towns in Israel, Judah and the Negev, among them Megiddo, Beth-shean and Gibeon [[cite:list]] and Arad [[cite:arad]], and a stone fragment bearing his name, published as part of a stela, was found at Megiddo [[cite:megiddo]]. His highest known regnal year is the twenty-first, when he ordered stone for the court at Karnak [[cite:silsila]]. The calendar dates of his reign are not fixed by Egyptian evidence alone.
