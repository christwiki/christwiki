Inter caetera is dated at St Peter's on 4 May 1493, in the first year of Alexander's pontificate. In it he speaks as holder of "the vicarship of Jesus Christ" and, by that authority, gives the kings of Castile and León the islands and mainlands found and to be found beyond a line in the Atlantic, on condition that no Christian prince already possessed them, and orders them to send men to teach the inhabitants the Catholic faith [[cite:bull]].

The Holy See said in 2023 that such bulls were written in a particular historical period and linked to political questions, and that they were never regarded as expressions of the Catholic faith [[cite:vatican]]. See [[event:christianity-reaches-the-americas]].
