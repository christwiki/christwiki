---
name: Naomi
role: Mother-in-law of Ruth
summary: A woman of Bethlehem who lost her husband and both sons in Moab and came home with her Moabite daughter-in-law Ruth. She guided Ruth towards marriage with Boaz and nursed their son Obed.
---

Naomi left [[place:bethlehem]] for Moab with her husband Elimelech and their two sons during a famine, and there she lost all three [[bible:Ruth 1:1-5]]. When she came back with [[person:ruth]], she told the women of the town, "Don't call me Naomi. Call me Mara; for the Almighty has dealt very bitterly with me" [[bible:Ruth 1:19-21]].

She recognised [[person:boaz]] as one of the family's near kinsmen and sent Ruth to him at the threshing floor [[bible:Ruth 2:20]] [[bible:Ruth 3:1-4]]. When Ruth's son Obed was born, the women said, "A son is born to Naomi", and she became the child's nurse [[bible:Ruth 4:14-17]].
