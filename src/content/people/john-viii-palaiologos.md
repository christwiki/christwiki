Doukas says that John had planned the union of the churches beforehand and sailed to Italy with the patriarch and bishops at the pope's expense [[cite:voyage]]. The pope's decree at [[place:ferrara]] records that he reached [[place:venice]] on 8 February 1438 and refused to go to Basel [[cite:ferrara]]. The bull of union states at its head that it is issued with the agreement of John Palaiologos, emperor of the Romans [[cite:laetentur]].

On his return he sent envoys to reassure the Ottoman sultan Murad, who feared that the journey had produced an alliance against him, that it had concerned only the faith [[cite:murad]]. See [[event:council-of-florence]].
