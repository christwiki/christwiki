Otto had his young son elected king before he left for Italy, kept Christmas at Pavia and was made emperor at [[place:rome]] by Pope [[person:john-xii]] [[cite:coronation]] [[cite:rome]]. His privilege of 13 February 962 confirmed the lands of the Roman church and reserved the emperor's part in papal elections [[cite:privilege]]. When the pope turned against him he sat in the synod that declared John deposed and chose Leo VIII [[cite:synod]]. See [[event:otto-the-great]].

He also looked east. A chronicler at his court records that in 959 envoys of the Rus' asked him for a bishop, that the request later proved insincere, and that he nevertheless sent the monk Adalbert in 961 [[cite:rus]].
