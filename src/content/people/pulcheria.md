Sozomen, who wrote in her lifetime, says that Pulcheria was not yet fifteen when she took charge of the government for her younger brother Theodosius II, and that she dedicated her virginity to God and led her sisters to do the same [[cite:youth]]. According to Evagrius it was at her suggestion that [[person:marcian]] was made emperor, and she married him while remaining a virgin [[cite:marriage]].

[[person:leo-i]] wrote to her as well as to the emperor. One letter treats the order to hold a council as hers [[cite:leo-95]], and after the council he asked her help against the canon that raised the see of Constantinople [[cite:leo-105]]. At [[event:council-of-chalcedon]] the bishops acclaimed her beside Marcian as orthodox [[cite:session-6]].
