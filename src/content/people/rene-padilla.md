In 1974 Padilla was associate general secretary for Latin America of the International Fellowship of Evangelical Students, with headquarters in Buenos Aires [[cite:headnote]]. His paper at the congress at [[place:lausanne]], "Evangelism and the World", criticised what he called "culture Christianity", above all the identification of the Gospel with the "American Way of Life" [[cite:culture]], and argued that repentance has a social dimension and that evangelism is not a call to social quietism [[cite:repentance]]. [[person:john-stott]] cites the paper in his commentary on the covenant's section on culture [[cite:stott]]. See [[event:lausanne-congress]].

The relief agency Tearfund, whose international president he was from 1998 to 2010, records that he was born in Ecuador and announced his death, at the age of 88, on 29 April 2021 [[cite:obituary]].
