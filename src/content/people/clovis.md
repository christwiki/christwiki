Clovis succeeded his father Childeric as a king of the Franks and, in the fifth year of his reign, defeated Syagrius, who ruled at Soissons [[cite:accession]]. Gregory of Tours tells how he married the Catholic [[person:clotilde]], vowed in battle against the Alamanni to accept her God, and was baptised by [[person:remigius]] with more than three thousand of his men [[cite:baptism]]. A letter of congratulation from Bishop Avitus of Vienne survives from the time and says the baptism took place at Christmas [[cite:avitus]]; the year is debated ([[event:baptism-of-clovis]]).

He went on to defeat and kill the Visigothic king Alaric at Vouillé, and at Tours he received from the emperor Anastasius an appointment as consul and put on the purple tunic and a diadem [[cite:vouille]]. Gregory says that he died at Paris in the fifth year after Vouillé, aged forty-five, having reigned thirty years, and was buried in the church of the Holy Apostles which he and Clotilde had built [[cite:death]].
