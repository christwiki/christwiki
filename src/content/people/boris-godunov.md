The records of the tsar's foreign office call Godunov the tsar's "near boyar", master of the horse and brother-in-law, and show him handling the whole negotiation with [[person:jeremias-ii-of-constantinople]]. He received the patriarch after his first audience in July 1588, carried the tsar's proposal that Jeremias stay in Russia as patriarch at Vladimir, went back to him many times when he refused, and on 13 January 1589 brought the request that he install a patriarch chosen by the Russian council [[cite:talks]]. The same records show the council of Constantinople and its envoy addressing letters to him as well as to the tsar, and Godunov writing to Jeremias in his own name [[cite:letters]].
