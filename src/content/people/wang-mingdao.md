Wang was pastor of the Christian Tabernacle in [[place:beijing]], an independent church, and he opposed the Three-Self Movement openly [[cite:wang-editors]]. In June 1955 he published "We, Because of Faith", which ends with the declaration that he and those with him would not unite in any way with the movement's leaders, whom he called unbelievers, or join any of their organisations [[cite:wang]]. The editors who translated the pamphlet in 1963 say that he was arrested on 8 August 1955 [[cite:wang-editors]], and speak of his "suppression" by the time of the movement's meeting of 1956 [[cite:suppression]]. See [[event:three-self-movement]].
