Giovanni Battista Montini was born at Concesio, near Brescia, on 26 September 1897 and ordained priest in 1920. He served in the Vatican Secretariat of State under Pius XI and Pius XII and became archbishop of Milan in 1954. He was elected pope on 21 June 1963 and died at Castel Gandolfo on 6 August 1978 [[cite:life]].

He continued the council that [[person:john-xxiii]] had opened and closed it on 8 December 1965 [[cite:closing]]; see [[event:second-vatican-council]]. He met the Ecumenical Patriarch [[person:athenagoras-i]] in [[place:jerusalem]] in January 1964 [[cite:communique]], and on 7 December 1965 the two removed the excommunications of 1054 from the memory of the church [[cite:decl-4]]; see [[event:lifting-of-the-anathemas]]. In August 1968 he opened the conference of Latin American bishops in Bogotá, on what he called the first visit of a pope to Latin America [[cite:bogota]]; see [[event:medellin-and-liberation-theology]]. In May 1973 he and [[person:shenouda-iii]] signed a common confession of faith in Christ [[cite:d1973]]; see [[event:christological-declarations]]. Pope Francis canonised him on 14 October 2018 [[cite:life]].
