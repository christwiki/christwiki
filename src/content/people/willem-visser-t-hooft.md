Visser 't Hooft was general secretary of the World Council of Churches when its first assembly met at [[place:amsterdam]] in 1948. Reporting for the provisional committee on the day the council was constituted, he called it a council of churches and not the council of the one undivided Church, "an emergency solution" and a stage on the road [[cite:secretary]]. He edited the assembly's official report and dated its preface from Geneva in September 1948 [[cite:preface]]. See [[event:world-council-of-churches]].
