Fox's Journal tells how he came to [[place:swarthmoor-hall]] in 1652 and how Margaret Fell, the wife of Judge Fell, was convinced with her children and household [[cite:north]]. When Friends were imprisoned in great numbers after the Fifth Monarchy rising, she went to the king and council with accounts of their sufferings [[cite:rising]].

Her tract *Women's Speaking Justified*, printed in 1666, argues from Scripture for the preaching of women [[cite:fell]]. She and [[person:george-fox]] were married at [[place:bristol]], in a meeting of Friends and with the consent of her children, whom Fox says he asked first [[cite:marriage]].
