Newbigin describes himself on the title page of his book of 1948 as bishop in Madura and Ramnad in the Church of South India and formerly a missionary of the Church of Scotland at Conjeeveram [[cite:newbigin-title]]. He was one of the nine bishops consecrated at the inauguration of the united church [[cite:bishops]]. He wrote *The Reunion of the Church* in the last months of a furlough and dated its foreword from Madura in November 1947 [[cite:newbigin-foreword]]. See [[event:church-of-south-india]].
