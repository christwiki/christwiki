At the end of his *Short Account* Las Casas names himself a friar of St Dominic who goes about the court of Spain trying, as he puts it, to drive hell out of the Indies. He says that he finished the work at Valencia on 8 December 1542, while the killing and oppression were at their height [[cite:author]], and he added in 1546 that the settlers had resisted the laws the emperor made to stop them [[cite:after]].

The title page of the tract he printed in 1552 calls him the former bishop of Chiapa in New Spain and records his dispute at [[place:valladolid]] with [[person:juan-gines-de-sepulveda]], in which he maintained that the conquests had been tyrannical and unjust [[cite:dispute]]. His *History of the Indies* preserves the account of the sermon of [[person:antonio-de-montesinos]] [[cite:sermon]]. See [[event:sublimis-deus]].
