Jehu son of Jehoshaphat, son of Nimshi, was a commander of the army of Israel at Ramoth-gilead when a prophet sent by [[person:elisha]] anointed him king [[bible:2 Kings 9:1-13]]. He killed King Joram, had Ahaziah of Judah shot, ordered the death of [[person:jezebel]], wiped out the family of [[person:ahab]] and destroyed the temple of Baal in [[place:samaria]] [[bible:2 Kings 9:14-10:28]]. Kings praises the purge and blames him for keeping the calves of Bethel and Dan [[bible:2 Kings 10:29-31]]; Hosea later condemns the bloodshed at Jezreel [[bible:Hos 1:4]].

In the year of his revolt he paid tribute to [[person:shalmaneser-iii]] of Assyria, who calls him "son of Omri" [[cite:annals]] [[cite:caption]]; see [[event:jehu-pays-tribute]]. During his reign [[person:hazael]] of Damascus took Israel's territory east of the Jordan [[bible:2 Kings 10:32-33]]. He reigned twenty-eight years and was buried in Samaria [[bible:2 Kings 10:35-36]].
