John Owen describes Charles as an ordained minister of the established church who officiated in connection with the Welsh Calvinistic Methodists, and who knew the wants of his countrymen from his habit of itinerating and promoting Sunday schools [[cite:charles]]. In [[place:london]] in December 1802 he urged the need for "new and extraordinary means" to supply Wales with Bibles [[cite:charles]], and the discussion of his appeal produced the proposal for a society to distribute the Scriptures generally [[cite:hughes]]. The Society's first report records an order for twenty thousand Welsh Bibles [[cite:welsh]]. See [[event:bible-society]].
