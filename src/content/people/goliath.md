Goliath of Gath came out from the Philistine camp at the [[place:valley-of-elah]] and challenged Israel to send a man to fight him. The Hebrew text gives his height as six cubits and a span and describes his bronze helmet, his coat of mail and his iron-headed spear [[bible:1 Sam 17:4-10]]. The Greek Septuagint and Josephus give four cubits and a span [[cite:lxx-height]] [[cite:josephus-height]]. [[person:david]] struck him down with a sling stone and cut off his head with Goliath's own sword [[bible:1 Sam 17:49-51]]. The sword was later kept at the sanctuary of Nob, where David took it again [[bible:1 Sam 21:1, 8-9]].

A notice of David's wars with the Philistines says, in the Hebrew text, that Elhanan of Bethlehem killed "Goliath the Gittite", whose spear shaft was like a weaver's beam [[bible:2 Sam 21:19]]. Chronicles says that Elhanan killed Lahmi, Goliath's brother [[bible:1 Chr 20:5]], and some translations, the World English Bible among them, read "brother" in Samuel as well. The question is taken up under [[event:david-and-goliath]].
