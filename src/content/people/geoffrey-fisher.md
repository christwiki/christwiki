Fisher attended the first assembly of the World Council of Churches as a delegate of the Church of England [[cite:delegates]]. He was in the chair on the morning of 23 August 1948 and, when the resolution had been adopted, declared the formation of the council completed [[cite:constituted]]. The assembly's nominations committee named him one of six presidents of the council [[cite:presidents]]. See [[event:world-council-of-churches]].
