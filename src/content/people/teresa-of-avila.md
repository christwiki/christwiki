Teresa was a nun of the Carmelite convent at [[place:avila]]. In her own account she came to want a stricter life than her house offered, and, after what she describes as a command from the Lord, worked to found a small convent that would keep the order's rule without its later relaxations [[cite:life-32]]. St Joseph's was founded on St Bartholomew's Day 1562, for no more than thirteen nuns living on alms [[cite:life-36]].

By 1573, when her confessor told her to write their history, seven more convents had been founded [[cite:prologue]], and the general of the Carmelites had allowed her to begin houses of friars under the same rule [[cite:friars]]. She wrote the *Interior Castle* in 1577, between Toledo and Ávila, for the nuns of these houses [[cite:castle]]. In its preface she submits her writings to the judgment of the learned men who had commanded them and declares herself faithful to the Roman Catholic Church [[cite:castle]].
