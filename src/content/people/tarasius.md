Theophanes says that when the empress [[person:irene-of-athens|Irene]] asked the people of [[place:constantinople]] to name a patriarch, all called for Tarasius, the imperial secretary. He told them that he was a layman in the emperor's service, that the church of the capital was cut off from the other churches, and that he would accept only if a council were held. He was consecrated on 25 December 784 [[cite:election]].

He was threatened by the soldiers who broke up the first assembly in 786 and went to [[place:nicaea]] for the second in 787 [[cite:council]]. The bishops who returned from iconoclasm at the council made their submission to him and to the pope together [[cite:session1]]. See [[event:second-council-of-nicaea]].
