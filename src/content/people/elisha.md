name: Elisha
altNames: [Eliseus]
role: Prophet in Israel, ninth century BC
summary: Disciple and successor of Elijah. Kings tells of his miracles, among them the healing of Naaman the Syrian, and of his part in the rise of Hazael of Damascus and Jehu of Israel.
---

Elisha son of Shaphat, of Abel-meholah, was called from the plough by [[person:elijah]] and served him [[bible:1 Kings 19:16, 19-21]]. He saw his master taken up at the [[place:jordan-river]] and returned with his cloak as the recognised prophet in his place [[bible:2 Kings 2:9-15]]; see [[event:elisha-succeeds-elijah]].

Kings gathers a series of stories about him: he promised water and victory to the kings marching against Moab [[bible:2 Kings 3:11-20]], restored the son of a woman of Shunem to life [[bible:2 Kings 4:32-37]], and sent Naaman to wash in the Jordan [[bible:2 Kings 5:9-14]], a healing that Jesus recalls in the synagogue at Nazareth [[bible:Luke 4:27]]. He told [[person:hazael]] that he would be king of Aram [[bible:2 Kings 8:7-15]] and sent a prophet to anoint [[person:jehu]] [[bible:2 Kings 9:1-3]]; see [[event:jehu-pays-tribute]]. He died in the reign of Joash, Jehu's grandson, who wept over him as "the chariots of Israel and its horsemen" [[bible:2 Kings 13:14-20]].
