Frederick ruled the Palatinate from 1559 to 1576 [[cite:crc-intro]]. In the preface to the catechism he styles himself elector Palatine on the Rhine and says that he holds it the chief duty of his government to see the young trained in "the pure and consistent doctrine of the holy Gospel". Finding no settled catechism in use, he had one prepared with the advice of the theological faculty at [[place:heidelberg]] and of the leading ministers, and he commanded all pastors and schoolmasters to use it [[cite:preface]].

The addition of question 80, against the mass, to the second and third printings was made, according to the note in the third, by his command [[cite:q80-note]]. See [[event:heidelberg-catechism]].
