Stefan Wyszyński was born at Zuzela in Poland on 3 August 1901 and ordained priest on 3 August 1924. Pius XII named him bishop of Lublin in 1946 and transferred him in November 1948 to Gniezno and Warsaw, the primatial see of Poland. In May 1953 he addressed to the state authorities, in the name of the bishops, the message known by its words "Non possumus". He was arrested on 24 September 1953 and kept in isolation until 28 October 1956. He died in [[place:warsaw]] on 28 May 1981 and was beatified on 12 September 2021 [[cite:profile]].

He was at the head of the Polish bishops who came to [[place:rome]] for the inauguration of [[person:john-paul-ii]] in October 1978 [[cite:inaug-7]]. The new pope wrote, in a letter dated the next day, that he would not be on Peter's chair but for the primate's faith, which had not retreated before prison and suffering [[cite:letter-5]].
