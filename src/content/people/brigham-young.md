A text in the Doctrine and Covenants, headed by the church as given through Brigham Young at Winter Quarters on the Missouri in January 1847, orders the Latter-day Saints to be organized into companies for the journey west, under the direction of the Twelve Apostles [[cite:winter-quarters]].

The general epistle of 23 December 1847 is signed by Young as president, on behalf of the Twelve [[cite:epistle-signed]]. It reports that members of the council and 143 pioneers left Winter Quarters on 14 April 1847 and reached the Great Basin in the latter part of July [[cite:epistle-journey]], and that they chose the site of a city there, to be called Great Salt Lake City [[cite:epistle-city]].
