---
name: Ruth
role: Moabite widow; great-grandmother of David
summary: A Moabite woman who stayed with her widowed mother-in-law Naomi, came with her to Bethlehem and married Boaz. She was the great-grandmother of David, and Matthew names her in the genealogy of Jesus.
---

Ruth was a Moabite, married to one of the sons of [[person:naomi]] while the family lived in Moab. When her husband died she refused to leave Naomi: "Your people will be my people, and your God my God" [[bible:Ruth 1:4, 14-17]]. At [[place:bethlehem]] she supported them both by gleaning. [[person:boaz]], a relative of her husband's family, redeemed the family's land and married her [[bible:Ruth 2:2-3]] [[bible:Ruth 4:9-13]]. The elders at the gate wished that she might be like Rachel and Leah, "which both built the house of Israel" [[bible:Ruth 4:11]].

Her son Obed was the grandfather of [[person:david]] [[bible:Ruth 4:17]]. Matthew includes her by name in the genealogy of Jesus [[bible:Matt 1:5]]. Her story is told in [[event:ruth-and-boaz]].
