The *Liber Pontificalis* describes Hadrian as a Roman of noble family from the Via Lata quarter, who held the see for twenty-three years and ten months [[cite:life]] and was buried at St Peter's on 26 December in the fourth indiction, which is 795 [[cite:end]]. Einhard says that it was at his entreaty that [[person:charlemagne]] made war on the Lombards and ended their kingdom [[cite:einhard]]. In 774 Charlemagne renewed in St Peter's the promise of territory that his father had made to Stephen II [[cite:promise]].

His letter to the rulers at Constantinople, urging the restoration of images and setting out the authority of the Roman see, was read at the [[event:second-council-of-nicaea]] [[cite:letter]]. His biographer says that the council decided in accordance with that letter and that Hadrian had the Greek acts put into Latin and kept in the papal library [[cite:council]].
