---
name: Amos
role: Prophet to Israel, c. 760 BC
summary: A herdsman and tender of sycamore figs from Tekoa in Judah who prophesied in the northern kingdom under Jeroboam II. He denounced the oppression of the poor and worship without justice, and announced Israel's exile.
---

Amos came from Tekoa in Judah, where he kept flocks and tended sycamore figs, and he insisted that he was not a prophet by profession [[bible:Amos 1:1]] [[bible:Amos 7:14-15]]. He preached in the northern kingdom in the days of [[person:jeroboam-ii]], and the priest of [[place:bethel]] ordered him out [[bible:Amos 7:10-13]].

His book charges Israel with selling the poor and rejects its festivals and offerings [[bible:Amos 2:6-8]] [[bible:Amos 5:21-24]]. It reports five visions of judgment [[bible:Amos 7:1-9]] [[bible:Amos 8:1-3]] [[bible:Amos 9:1-4]] and closes with a promise that the fallen tent of David will be raised again [[bible:Amos 9:11-15]], which James quotes in Acts [[bible:Acts 15:13-18]]. See [[event:amos-and-hosea]].
