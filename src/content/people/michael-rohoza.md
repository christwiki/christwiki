Michael's name stands first under the thirty-three articles of 1 June 1595, as "Metropolitan of Kiev and Halych and all Rus'" [[cite:articles]]. The pope's bull of December 1595 names him as the archbishop and metropolitan in whose name, and that of his fellow bishops, the envoys Hypatius and Cyril sought communion with [[place:rome]], and it empowers them to absolve him on their return [[cite:bull]].

Clement VIII wrote in January 1597 that Michael had called and held a provincial synod of the Ruthenian bishops, as the pope had instructed, and that he and the bishops with him had there ratified what was done in Rome and made their own profession of faith and obedience [[cite:brief-1597]].
