Christian Krause was born on 6 January 1940 at Dallgow-Döberitz in Brandenburg and was ordained a pastor of the Evangelical Lutheran Church of Hanover in 1969 [[cite:lwf]]. He was bishop of the Evangelical Lutheran Church in Brunswick from 1994 to 2002, and president of the Lutheran World Federation from its assembly at Hong Kong in 1997 until its assembly at Winnipeg in 2003 [[cite:lwf]].

His is the first of the federation's signatures on the statement that confirmed the Joint Declaration [[cite:ocs]]; see [[event:joint-declaration-on-justification]]. He died at Wolfenbüttel on 28 November 2024 [[cite:lwf]].
