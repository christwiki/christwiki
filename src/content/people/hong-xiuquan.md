The missionary Theodore Hamberg, who had his account from [[person:hong-rengan]], says that Hong was born in 1813 in a village about thirty miles from Canton, taught in the village school and failed the examinations at Canton [[cite:early]]. In 1837, during an illness, he had visions in which an old man gave him a sword against the demons and a man of middle age, whom he called his elder brother, helped him to fight them [[cite:visions]]. In 1843 he read Christian tracts that he had been given years before and understood the two figures as God the Father and Jesus [[cite:tracts]]. The Taiping Trimetrical Classic tells the same story in the movement's own words: God sent his Son, who in 1837 was received into heaven [[cite:classic]].

He was proclaimed emperor of a new dynasty of Taiping in 1851 [[cite:dynasty]], and after his army took [[place:nanjing]] in March 1853 he lived shut up in his palace there [[cite:nanjing]]. The missionary [[person:issachar-roberts]], who had taught him and later lived fifteen months at his court, wrote in 1862 that Hong made himself "equal with Jesus Christ" [[cite:roberts]]. His general Li Xiucheng states that he poisoned himself shortly before the city fell in 1864 [[cite:death]]. See [[event:taiping-rebellion]].
