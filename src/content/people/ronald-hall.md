Hall was the Anglican bishop whose diocese included Hong Kong and [[place:macau]]. In 1943 he told the Archbishop of Canterbury that he had allowed the deaconess [[person:florence-li-tim-oi]] to celebrate the Lord's Supper for her congregation, and that he was no advocate of the ordination of women [[cite:hall-1943]]. In January 1944 he ordained her a priest and reported it to the archbishop two days later [[cite:hall-1944]]. The Chinese House of Bishops voted in 1946 that his action was uncanonical [[cite:bishops-1946]]. See [[event:florence-li-tim-oi]].

The Anglican Communion Office's text of the Lambeth resolution on the case carries his name as a subject heading, with the years 1895 and 1975 [[cite:dates]].
