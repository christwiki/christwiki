According to Gregory of Tours, Clotilde was a daughter of Chilperic, a Burgundian prince killed by his brother, King Gundobad. Envoys whom [[person:clovis]] sent to Burgundy noticed her, and the king asked for her in marriage. She had their sons baptised and argued with her husband against his gods [[cite:marriage]]. When he had made his vow in battle she was the one who called in Bishop [[person:remigius]] to instruct him [[cite:remigius]].

After the king's death she went to live at Tours, where she served at the church of Saint Martin and seldom visited Paris [[cite:widow]]. Her part in the conversion is told here from Gregory, who wrote in the city where she had spent her widowhood; the contemporary letter of Avitus to Clovis does not mention her.
