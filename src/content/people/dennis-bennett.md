The Episcopal Church's dictionary gives his dates as 28 October 1917 to 1 November 1991 and calls him the "father of charismatic renewal in the Episcopal Church". Born in London, he was ordained a Congregational minister in 1949 and an Episcopal priest in 1952, and became rector of St Mark's Church in [[place:van-nuys]], California. On 3 April 1960 he told his congregation in a sermon that he had received the "baptism in the Holy Spirit" and spoken in unknown tongues. The opposition this aroused led him to resign, and in the same year he became vicar of St Luke's Church, Seattle [[cite:bennett-dict]]. His own account is in *Nine O'Clock in the Morning* [[cite:bennett-own]]. See [[event:charismatic-renewal]].
