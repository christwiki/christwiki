---
name: Nathan
role: Prophet at the court of David
summary: Prophet in Jerusalem under David and Solomon. He brought David the promise of a lasting dynasty, confronted him over Bathsheba and Uriah, and helped to secure the throne for Solomon.
---

Nathan first appears when [[person:david]] proposes to build a temple. He brings the answer that the Lord will instead build a house, a dynasty, for David [[bible:2 Sam 7:1-17]]. After David took Bathsheba and had her husband Uriah killed [[bible:2 Sam 11:2-5, 14-17]], Nathan told him a story of a rich man who seized a poor man's only lamb. When the king condemned the man, Nathan said, "You are the man" [[bible:2 Sam 12:1-10]]. David confessed, and Nathan told him that he would live but that the child would die [[bible:2 Sam 12:13-14]].

In David's old age Nathan and Bathsheba moved the king to name [[person:solomon]] his heir against the claim of Adonijah, and Nathan took part with the priest Zadok in Solomon's anointing [[bible:1 Kings 1:11-14, 32-40]]. Chronicles cites a "history of Nathan the prophet" among its sources for both reigns [[bible:1 Chr 29:29]] [[bible:2 Chr 9:29]]. He is not the Nathan who was David's son [[bible:2 Sam 5:14]], through whom Luke traces the descent of Jesus [[bible:Luke 3:31]].
