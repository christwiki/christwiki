Athanasius went to the [[event:council-of-nicaea]] as a deacon of [[person:alexander-of-alexandria]] and, Socrates says, powerfully opposed the supporters of [[person:arius]] there [[cite:deacon]]. He became bishop of [[place:alexandria]] in 328 [[cite:bishop]]. He defended the creed of Nicaea and its word *homoousios*, as in his *Defence of the Nicene Definition* [[cite:terms]]. [[person:constantine]] banished him to Gaul [[cite:gaul]], and under Constantius II he was twice forced to flee his church, in 339 and in 356 [[cite:flights]].

He wrote a *Life* of the hermit Antony ([[source:athanasius-life-of-antony]]). The letter he sent to his churches for Easter 367 lists the books of Scripture, and its New Testament has twenty-seven books, those of the later canon [[cite:letter39]]; see [[event:athanasius-festal-letter]]. He died on 2 May 373 [[cite:death]].
