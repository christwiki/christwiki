---
name: Aaron
role: Brother of Moses, first high priest of Israel
summary: The elder brother of Moses and his spokesman before Pharaoh. He made the golden calf at Sinai and was afterwards ordained as Israel's first high priest; the later priesthood traced its descent from him.
---

Aaron was three years older than his brother [[person:moses]] [[bible:Exod 7:7]] and was appointed to speak for him to the people and to Pharaoh [[bible:Exod 4:14-16]] [[bible:Exod 7:1-2]]. At Sinai, while Moses was on the mountain, he made the golden calf at the people's demand [[bible:Exod 32:1-6]]. He and his sons were nevertheless set apart as priests and ordained when the tabernacle was finished [[bible:Exod 28:1]] [[bible:Lev 8:1-13]]. He died on Mount Hor in the fortieth year after the exodus, aged 123, and his son Eleazar succeeded him [[bible:Num 20:22-29]] [[bible:Num 33:38-39]].

The priesthood was given to Aaron and his sons "by a perpetual statute" [[bible:Exod 29:9]], and Israel's later priests were reckoned as his descendants. Luke describes the mother of John the Baptist as one "of the daughters of Aaron" [[bible:Luke 1:5]]. The letter to the Hebrews takes Aaron as the model of a priest called by God [[bible:Heb 5:1-4]] and argues that Christ's priesthood is of a different order from his [[bible:Heb 7:11]].
