Philip is named second, after [[person:stephen]], among the seven men chosen to serve the [[place:jerusalem|Jerusalem]] church [[bible:Acts 6:5]]. He is a different man from Philip, one of the twelve apostles [[bible:Acts 1:13]]: Acts says that the apostles stayed in Jerusalem when the others were scattered, and that they sent [[person:peter|Peter]] and [[person:john-the-apostle|John]] to follow up Philip's work [[bible:Acts 8:1, 14]]. He preached in [[place:samaria]] and baptised an Ethiopian court official on the road to [[place:gaza]] [[bible:Acts 8:5-40]]; see [[event:philip-in-samaria]].

Later in Acts [[person:paul|Paul]] and his companions stayed at his house in [[place:caesarea-maritima]]. Acts there calls him "Philip the evangelist, who was one of the seven", and says that he had four unmarried daughters who prophesied [[bible:Acts 21:8-9]].
