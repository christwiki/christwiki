The decrees of the Holy Office record meetings held before him at the Quirinal palace in June 1633 [[cite:confinement]]. On 16 June he decided that [[person:galileo-galilei]] should be examined about his intention under threat of torture, should abjure before the full Congregation and be condemned to prison, that the *Dialogue* should be prohibited, and that copies of the sentence should be sent to the nuncios and inquisitors and read to professors of mathematics [[cite:decision]].

On 23 June, the day after the sentence, he ordered Galileo moved from the prison of the Holy Office to the palace of the grand duke of Tuscany in Rome [[cite:confinement]]. On 30 June he sent him to Siena, and on 1 December he allowed him to go to his own country house, to live there in seclusion [[cite:confinement]]. See [[event:galileo-affair]].
