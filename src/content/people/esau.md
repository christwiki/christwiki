Esau is the firstborn of the twin sons of [[person:isaac]] and Rebekah, "a skillful hunter, a man of the field", and his father's favourite [[bible:Gen 25:25-28]]. He sells his birthright to [[person:jacob]] for bread and lentil stew [[bible:Gen 25:29-34]], and weeps aloud when he finds that Jacob has taken his blessing [[bible:Gen 27:30-38]]. When Jacob comes home twenty years later, Esau meets him with four hundred men, runs to him and embraces him [[bible:Gen 33:1-4]]. The brothers bury Isaac together [[bible:Gen 35:29]]. Esau settles in the hill country of Seir, and Genesis calls him Edom, the father of the Edomites [[bible:Gen 36:6-9]]. His death is not recorded.

[[person:malachi]] sets the two brothers against each other as two nations, "I loved Jacob; but Esau I hated" [[bible:Mal 1:2-3]], and [[person:paul]] quotes the words when he writes about God's choice [[bible:Rom 9:10-13]]. Hebrews warns its readers not to be like Esau, "who sold his birthright for one meal" [[bible:Heb 12:16-17]].
