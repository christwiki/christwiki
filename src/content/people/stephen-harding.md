A Brother Stephen is among the monks named in the legate's letter that authorised the move from Molesme in 1098 [[cite:ep-2]]. The Exordium Parvum says that Alberic's successor as abbot was Stephen, "English by birth", who had come from Molesme with the others and loved the Rule and the place. In his time the monks forbade the duke of Burgundy to hold court in their church and reduced its furnishings to the plainest materials [[cite:ep-17]].

[[person:bernard-of-clairvaux]] entered [[place:citeaux]] under Abbot Stephen, and it was Stephen who sent him to found [[place:clairvaux]] [[cite:vp-entry]] [[cite:vp-clairvaux]]. The prologue of the Charter of Charity names Abbot Stephen and his brethren as the authors of the agreement that bound the daughter houses to Cîteaux [[cite:cc]].
