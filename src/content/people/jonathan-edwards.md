Edwards writes that he was the third minister settled at [[place:northampton-massachusetts]], that he had served for about two years beside his grandfather, Mr Stoddard, before the old man's death, and that the awakening in his congregation began in the latter part of December 1734 [[cite:town]]. He signed his account of it at Northampton on 6 November 1736 [[cite:signed]].

[[person:george-whitefield]], who stayed in his house in October 1740, called him "a solid, excellent Christian" and wrote that he had not seen his fellow in all New England [[cite:visit]]. On 8 July 1741 Edwards preached at Enfield in Connecticut the sermon *Sinners in the Hands of an Angry God* [[cite:sinners]]. See [[event:great-awakening]].
