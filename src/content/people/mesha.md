Mesha calls himself a man of [[place:dibon]] and the son of a king who ruled Moab for thirty years, and says that he built a sanctuary for the god Chemosh [[cite:intro]]. Kings describes him as a sheep breeder who paid tribute to Israel and rebelled when [[person:ahab]] died. Besieged in Kir-hareseth by the kings of Israel, Judah and Edom, he sacrificed his eldest son on the wall, and the besiegers withdrew [[bible:2 Kings 3:4-27]].

His stele tells of the same struggle from his side: the oppression of Moab by Omri and his son, and the capture of Ataroth, Nebo and Jahaz from Israel [[cite:wars]]; see [[event:mesha-stele]]. Neither source gives the years of his reign.
