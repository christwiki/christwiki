[[person:john-wesley]]'s Journal names "my brother, Charles Wesley" among those who took boat with him for Georgia in October 1735, and says that on the voyage Charles wrote sermons [[cite:voyage]]. In May 1738 John found him ill in London and at first strongly averse to what he called "the new faith"; on Whitsunday he heard that Charles "had found rest to his soul" [[cite:whitsun]]. When John was asked to go to [[place:bristol]] in March 1739, Charles could scarcely bear the mention of it, John writes, until a text of Scripture changed his mind [[cite:bristol]].

The General Rules of the United Societies are signed "John Wesley, Charles Wesley" and dated 1 May 1743 [[cite:rules]]. See [[event:wesley-and-methodism]].
