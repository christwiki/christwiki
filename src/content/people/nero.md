Nero was born at Antium nine months after the death of Tiberius, that is, in December of AD 37 [[cite:birth]]. Claudius adopted him [[cite:birth]], and he was hailed as emperor at seventeen when Claudius died in 54 [[cite:accession]]. He is the Caesar to whom [[person:paul]] appealed from the court of [[person:festus]] [[bible:Acts 25:10-12]].

Tacitus reports that after the fire of AD 64 Nero fixed the blame on the Christians of [[place:rome]] and had them killed as a public show [[cite:christians]]; Suetonius lists the punishment of the Christians among his measures without mentioning the fire [[cite:measure]]. See [[event:neronian-persecution]]. When Judaea rose in revolt he gave the command against it to [[person:vespasian]] [[cite:judaea]]. In 68 the senate declared him a public enemy and he killed himself [[cite:death]]. Suetonius puts his death in his thirty-second year [[cite:age]]; Cassius Dio gives him a reign of thirteen years and eight months and calls him the last of the line of Augustus [[cite:reign]].

Tertullian, as Eusebius quotes him, called him the first emperor to persecute the Christians. Eusebius, in the early fourth century, records as received tradition that Paul was beheaded and Peter crucified at Rome under him [[cite:apostles]].
