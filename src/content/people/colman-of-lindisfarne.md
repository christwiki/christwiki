Colman was sent from [[place:iona]] to succeed Finan as bishop of the Northumbrians, with his seat on Lindisfarne [[cite:synod]] [[cite:ireland]]. Bede counts three years for his episcopate, ending in 664 [[cite:after]]. At the [[event:synod-of-whitby]] he argued that the Irish Easter came down from the apostle John and from Columba [[cite:synod]].

After the king decided against him he left Northumbria with those who would not accept the Roman Easter and tonsure, taking some of the bones of Aidan. Bede, who thought him wrong on Easter, praises his frugality and says the king loved him for his good sense [[cite:after]]. He settled his monks on the island of Inishbofin off the west of Ireland and later founded a separate house at Mayo for the English among them [[cite:ireland]].
