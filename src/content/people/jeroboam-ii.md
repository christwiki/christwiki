---
name: Jeroboam II
role: King of Israel, first half of the eighth century BC
summary: King of the northern kingdom for forty-one years, of the house of Jehu. Kings says he restored Israel's borders from Hamath to the Sea of the Arabah. Amos and Hosea prophesied during his reign.
---

Jeroboam son of Joash reigned forty-one years in [[place:samaria]]. Kings says that he restored Israel's border from the entrance of Hamath to the Sea of the Arabah, as the prophet [[person:jonah]] had foretold, and judges him, like most northern kings, for keeping up the worship founded by [[person:jeroboam-i]] [[bible:2 Kings 14:23-28]].

[[person:amos]] and [[person:hosea]] both date their work to his reign [[bible:Amos 1:1]] [[bible:Hos 1:1]]. The priest of Bethel reported Amos to him for saying that Jeroboam would die by the sword and Israel go into exile [[bible:Amos 7:10-11]]. Kings records that he died and was succeeded by his son Zechariah, who was murdered after six months; with him the dynasty of [[person:jehu]] ended [[bible:2 Kings 14:29]] [[bible:2 Kings 15:8-12]].
