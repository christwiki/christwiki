Bede says that he was born on the lands of the monastery of Wearmouth and [[place:jarrow]], was given to Abbot Benedict Biscop at seven and brought up by him and by Abbot Ceolfrid, became a deacon at nineteen and a priest at thirty, and lived there all his life, studying Scripture and "learning, or teaching, or writing". He was in his fifty-ninth year when he finished the History in 731, which puts his birth in 672 or 673. The list of his works that follows is mostly biblical commentary [[cite:life]].

His pupil Cuthbert records that he died on the eve of the Ascension, a Wednesday, still dictating a translation of John's Gospel into English, and that Ascension Day fell on 26 May [[cite:death]]. That fits the year 735. See [[event:bede]].
