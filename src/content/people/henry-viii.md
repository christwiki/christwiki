A London chronicle records Henry's birth at Greenwich in June 1491, the second son of Henry VII [[cite:birth]]. In 1533 Archbishop [[person:thomas-cranmer]] gave sentence against his marriage to Katherine of Aragon and crowned Anne Boleyn, whom Henry had already married [[cite:dunstable]]. The Act of Supremacy of 1534 declared him and his successors "the only supreme head in earth of the Church of England" [[cite:supremacy]]; see [[event:act-of-supremacy]].

The lord mayor of London was told on 31 January 1547 that the king had died on the Friday before, 28 January, and his son was proclaimed as [[person:edward-vi]] [[cite:death]].
