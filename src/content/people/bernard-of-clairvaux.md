William of Saint-Thierry says that Bernard entered [[place:citeaux]] in 1113, aged about twenty-two or twenty-three, with more than thirty companions, under Abbot [[person:stephen-harding]] [[cite:vp-entry]]. Stephen then sent him as abbot to found [[place:clairvaux]] [[cite:vp-clairvaux]]. His treatise *On Loving God* begins from the answer that the reason for loving God is God himself [[cite:love-1]].

Geoffrey of Auxerre records that he died on 20 August 1153, aged about sixty-three, the father of more than a hundred and sixty monasteries [[cite:vp-death]]. The two ages together put his birth about 1090.
