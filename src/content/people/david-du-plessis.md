Du Plessis served as secretary and organiser of the Pentecostal World Conferences held in Paris in 1949 and in London in 1952. By his own account he met John Mackay at Princeton early in 1951 and under his guidance became interested in the ecumenical movement, and in 1954 the secretary of the World Council of Churches asked him to go to its assembly at Evanston [[cite:duplessis]].
