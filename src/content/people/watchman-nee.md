The American editors of the Three-Self Movement's documents identify Ni To-sheng, known in English as Watchman Nee, as the founder of the indigenous church usually called the Little Flock [[cite:bio]]. They write that by 1956 the dissenting leaders of the Little Flock had been suppressed, especially those centred on the Nanyang Road meeting house in [[place:shanghai]] [[cite:suppression]]. The same note says that he was imprisoned and, some years later, sentenced to a long term [[cite:bio]]. These are the editors' statements, made in New York in 1963; the dates and the length of the sentence are not repeated here, because no document of the time open to the reader was found to check them. See [[event:three-self-movement]].
