Among the bishops who signed the Spiritual Regulation is Theophanes, bishop of "Pleskow", that is, of Pskov. Its English translator, Thomas Consett, adds in a note of 1729 that this was Theophanes Procopovich, "a learned Prelate", moved to Novgorod in 1725 and by then the chief archbishop of the country [[cite:signature]].

The Regulation itself names no author. A. N. Muravyov, an official of the Synod who wrote a history of the Russian church in 1838, says that its composition was committed to Theophanes, bishop of Pskov [[cite:author]], and that he was one of the two vice-presidents of the Synod at its opening [[cite:vice-president]].
