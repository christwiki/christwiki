The Council of Constance had deposed two claimants to the papacy and accepted the resignation of the third before it allowed an election [[cite:removed]]. On 11 November 1417 the conclave chose Cardinal Oddo Colonna, who took the name Martin V [[cite:election]]. As pope he presided over the last sessions: he had the deposed John XXIII released, issued reform decrees, named Pavia as the place of the next council as the decree Frequens required, and dissolved the council on 22 April 1418 [[cite:acts]].
