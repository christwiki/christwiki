The *Liber Pontificalis* describes Leo as a Roman brought up from childhood in the papal household, elected on St Stephen's day, 26 December [[cite:lp-life]]. The Frankish annals record that on succeeding Hadrian he at once sent [[person:charlemagne]] the keys of St Peter's tomb and the banner of [[place:rome]] [[cite:annals-796]].

In April 799 he was attacked during a procession by a party led by officials of his own court, who tried to blind and silence him. He escaped to the king at Paderborn and was sent back with a Frankish escort [[cite:lp-attack]] [[cite:annals-799]]. In December 800 the bishops assembled in St Peter's declined to sit in judgment on the apostolic see. Leo swore his innocence on the Gospels, and at the Christmas mass he crowned Charles emperor [[cite:lp-crown]]. The Frankish annals enter his death under 816, in the twenty-first year of his pontificate [[cite:annals-816]]. See [[event:coronation-of-charlemagne]].
