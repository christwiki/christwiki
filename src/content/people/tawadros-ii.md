A diocese of his church gives these dates: he was born at Mansoura in Egypt on 4 November 1952, took a degree in pharmacy in 1975, entered the Monastery of Saint Pishoy, was ordained priest in 1989 and consecrated a general bishop by [[person:shenouda-iii]] in 1997, and took office as the 118th Pope of Alexandria on 18 November 2012 [[cite:life]]. [[person:benedict-xvi]] congratulated him on his election in a message of 5 November 2012 [[cite:elected]].

On 21 February 2015 he announced the inclusion of the twenty-one men killed in Libya in the Synaxarium of the Coptic Orthodox Church [[cite:synaxarium]]. He first visited [[person:francis]] on 10 May 2013; on 11 May 2023 he came again, gave him a relic of the twenty-one, and consented to their inclusion in the Roman Martyrology [[cite:rome]]. See [[event:martyrs-of-libya]]. This entry states only what the cited documents say.
