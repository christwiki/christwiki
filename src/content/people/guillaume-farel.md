[[person:john-calvin]] writes that "Popery" had lately been driven from [[place:geneva]] by the exertions of Farel and of Pierre Viret, and that Farel, burning with zeal for the Gospel, kept him in the city by declaring that God would curse his retirement and his studies if he refused to help [[cite:memoir]]. The council register for April 1538 records that Farel and Calvin preached after being forbidden and were ordered to leave the city within three days, and it notes that both accepted the order when it was brought to them, Calvin answering that they served a greater master than men [[cite:banished]]. See [[event:calvin-in-geneva]].
