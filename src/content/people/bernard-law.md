Bernard Francis Law was born at Torreón in Mexico on 4 November 1931, the son of a United States Air Force officer, and was ordained a priest for the diocese of Natchez-Jackson in Mississippi in 1961 [[cite:bio]]. He became bishop of Springfield-Cape Girardeau in 1973, was appointed archbishop of [[place:boston]] by [[person:john-paul-ii]] in January 1984, and was made a cardinal in 1985 [[cite:bio]].

The Massachusetts attorney general's report of 2003 found "overwhelming evidence" that Law and his senior managers had known for many years that substantial numbers of children in the archdiocese had been abused by its priests [[cite:ma-finding-3]]. The same report found no basis for criminal charges against the archdiocese or its senior managers [[cite:ma-charges]]. Law resigned in December 2002 [[cite:ma-law]]; see [[event:clergy-abuse-crisis]]. From 2004 to 2011 he was archpriest of the basilica of St Mary Major in Rome, and he died on 20 December 2017 [[cite:bio]].
