Christoph Schönborn was born on 22 January 1945 at Skalsko, in what is now the Czech Republic. He was ordained for the Dominicans in 1970, took a doctorate in theology in Paris in 1974, and taught dogmatic theology and the theology of the Christian East at the University of Fribourg in Switzerland. From 1987 to 1992 he was secretary of the commission responsible for drafting the Catechism of the Catholic Church [[cite:note]], a work that [[person:john-paul-ii]] describes as carried out by a commission of cardinals and bishops with an editorial committee [[cite:fd-2]]. See [[event:catechism-of-the-catholic-church]].

He was ordained bishop in 1991 as auxiliary of Vienna, became archbishop of Vienna on 14 September 1995 and was made a cardinal on 21 February 1998. The press office of the Holy See now lists him as archbishop emeritus [[cite:note]].
