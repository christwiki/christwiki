Stephen heads the list of seven men chosen by the Jerusalem church to oversee its daily distribution of food. Acts calls him "a man full of faith and of the Holy Spirit" [[bible:Acts 6:1-6]]. He disputed with members of the synagogue of the Freedmen and other Jews from abroad, was charged before the council with speaking against the temple and the law of Moses, and answered with a long speech on the history of Israel [[bible:Acts 6:8-7:53]]. He was dragged out of [[place:jerusalem]] and stoned, while a young man named Saul, the later [[person:paul]], looked after the cloaks of the witnesses [[bible:Acts 7:54-8:1]]. See [[event:martyrdom-of-stephen]].

The persecution that followed his death scattered the believers and carried their preaching to [[place:samaria|Samaria]], Phoenicia, Cyprus and [[place:antioch|Antioch]] [[bible:Acts 8:1-5]] [[bible:Acts 11:19]]. No other New Testament book mentions Stephen; the year of his death is an estimate tied to the date of Paul's conversion.
