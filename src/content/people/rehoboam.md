Rehoboam was the son of [[person:solomon]] and Naamah, an Ammonite. He came to the throne at forty-one and reigned seventeen years in [[place:jerusalem]] [[bible:1 Kings 14:21]]. At [[place:shechem]] he refused to lighten his father's demands, and ten tribes made [[person:jeroboam-i]] their king instead [[bible:1 Kings 12:1-20]]; see [[event:kingdom-divides]]. Chronicles lists the towns he fortified in Judah [[bible:2 Chr 11:5-12]].

In his fifth year the Egyptian king [[person:shoshenq-i|Shishak]] took the treasures of the temple and the palace [[bible:1 Kings 14:25-26]]; see [[event:shishak-invades]]. Kings says there was war between him and Jeroboam throughout his reign [[bible:1 Kings 14:30]]. He is counted in Matthew's genealogy of Jesus [[bible:Matt 1:7]]. His dates are reckoned from his age at accession and the length of his reign, on a division of the kingdom about 930 BC.
