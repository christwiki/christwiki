Eugene was at odds with the council sitting at [[place:basel]], which he declared transferred to [[place:ferrara]]; his legate opened the sessions there in January 1438, and in February he laid church penalties on those who stayed at Basel [[cite:ferrara]]. He paid for the voyage of the emperor and the Greek delegation, according to Doukas [[cite:voyage]].

The bull of union of 6 July 1439 is issued in his name and defines, among other things, that the Roman pontiff holds the primacy over the whole world [[cite:laetentur]]. See [[event:council-of-florence]].
