Serhii Petrovych Dumenko was born on 3 February 1979 in the village of Volkove in the Odesa region of Ukraine [[cite:bio]]. He took monastic vows with the name Epiphanius in 2007 and was ordained a bishop in 2009 in the Ukrainian Orthodox Church of the Kyiv Patriarchate, by its patriarch Filaret [[cite:bio]].

On 15 December 2018 the council held in [[place:kyiv]] elected him, then metropolitan of Pereyaslav and Bila Tserkva, primate of the new Orthodox Church of Ukraine [[cite:ep-december]]. The tomos of 6 January 2019 gives the primate the title Metropolitan of Kyiv and all Ukraine and names Epiphanius as the one to whom it was handed [[cite:tomos]]; see [[event:autocephaly-of-ukraine]]. The Serbian bishops' communiqué of May 2019, which does not recognise the new church, refers to him only by his secular surname [[cite:serbia]].
