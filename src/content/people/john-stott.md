John Stott was rector of All Souls Church, Langham Place, London, from 1950 to 1975. At the congress at [[place:lausanne]] in July 1974 he chaired the committee that drafted the Lausanne Covenant, with Hudson Armerding and Samuel Escobar. He describes in the preface to his commentary how three drafts were written and how the last was revised in the light of many hundreds of comments from the participants [[cite:preface]]. See [[event:lausanne-congress]].

The Lausanne Movement, of which he remained honorary chairman, records that he died at Lingfield in Surrey on 27 July 2011, aged 90 [[cite:obituary]].
