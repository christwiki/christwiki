Xavier is one of the ten priests named in the bull that approved the Society of Jesus [[cite:bull]]. [[person:ignatius-of-loyola]] says that he won him at Paris by giving him the Spiritual Exercises [[cite:paris]]. His own letters record that he left Lisbon on 7 April 1541 and reached India on 6 May 1542 [[cite:arrival]], and that he landed at [[place:kagoshima]] in Japan on 15 August 1549 [[cite:landing]]; see [[event:francis-xavier-in-asia]].

His last letters are dated 13 November 1552 from the island of San Chan, where he was waiting to enter China [[cite:last-letter]]. He died there soon afterwards. H. J. Coleridge, *The Life and Letters of St. Francis Xavier* (1872), vol. 2, pp. 571–572, who names the Chinese youth Antonio among those who attended him, gives the day as 2 December 1552; Pope Francis, in his general audience of 17 May 2023, gives 3 December.
