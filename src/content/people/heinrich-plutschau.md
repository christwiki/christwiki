The English editor of the Tranquebar letters says that Plütschau was born at Wesenberg in Mecklenburg and, like [[person:bartholomaus-ziegenbalg]], was educated at Berlin and at [[place:halle]] [[cite:origins]]. Ziegenbalg calls him his "dear Brother and Fellow-Labourer" [[cite:arrival]] and reports that the two divided the work, Plütschau catechizing for two hours a day in Portuguese [[cite:progress]]. A letter from the mission of December 1713 thanks its English friends for their kindness to him during a stay in England [[cite:england]]. See [[event:tranquebar-mission]].
