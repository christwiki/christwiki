The title page of *Pia Desideria* names its author as Philipp Jacob Spener, doctor, preacher and senior at [[place:frankfurt]] am Main [[cite:title]], and he explains in the prefatory address how the book grew out of a preface he was asked to write for an edition of Johann Arndt's sermons [[cite:reader]]. The first of its six proposals asks for meetings, beside the regular sermon, in which members of a congregation read and discuss Scripture under the preacher's direction [[cite:p1]]. See [[event:pietism]].
