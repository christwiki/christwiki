---
name: Noah
role: Builder of the ark
summary: In Genesis, the righteous man whom God saves from the flood with his family and the animals, and with whom God makes a covenant never again to destroy the earth by water.
---

Noah is the son of Lamech, the tenth name in the line that Genesis traces from Adam [[bible:Gen 5:3-29]]. Genesis calls him righteous and blameless among the people of his time [[bible:Gen 6:9]]. He builds the ark as God commands and is six hundred years old when [[event:the-flood]] comes [[bible:Gen 6:22]] [[bible:Gen 7:6]]. Afterwards God makes a covenant with him and with every living creature [[bible:Gen 9:8-11]]. His sons Shem, Ham and Japheth are presented as the ancestors of all the nations [[bible:Gen 9:18-19]] [[bible:Gen 10:32]]. Genesis gives his age at death as 950 years [[bible:Gen 9:28-29]].

[[person:ezekiel]] names Noah with Daniel and Job as a model of righteousness [[bible:Ezek 14:14]]. In the New Testament he appears in Luke's genealogy of Jesus [[bible:Luke 3:36]], as an example of faith [[bible:Heb 11:7]] and as "a preacher of righteousness" [[bible:2 Pet 2:5]].
