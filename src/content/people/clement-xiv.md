Clement XIV issued the brief *Dominus ac Redemptor* at [[place:rome]] on 21 July 1773, in the fifth year of his pontificate [[cite:date]]. In it he says that the kings of France, Spain, Portugal and the Two Sicilies had asked his predecessor Clement XIII to suppress the Society of Jesus, and that their petitions came to him as soon as he was elected [[cite:petition]]. He writes that he took time to inquire and to pray before deciding [[cite:deliberation]], and that he abolished the Society by his apostolic power for the sake of the peace of the Church [[cite:suppress]]. See [[event:suppression-of-the-jesuits]].
