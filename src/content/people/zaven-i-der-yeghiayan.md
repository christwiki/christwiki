A report of 21 April 1915 from the Armenian Catholicos about events at Zeitun, forwarded by the German consulate at Adana, is addressed to "Archbishop Zaven, Armenian Patriarch in Constantinople" [[cite:lepsius-34]]. After the arrests of 24 and 25 April in the capital, the German embassy reported, the Patriarch asked the Grand Vizier and the Minister of the Interior for the reasons [[cite:lepsius-38]]. Embassy notes of the summer and of December 1915 record what he told its staff: that Vramian, the Armenian deputy for Van, had been arrested and then killed at Bitlis [[cite:lepsius-101]], and that Armenians from Anatolia living in the capital were still being arrested and expelled [[cite:lepsius-214]].

Johannes Lepsius writes that an Ottoman law of 1 August 1916 abolished the patriarchate of Constantinople and joined it with the catholicosates of Sis and Aghtamar in one office, which was removed to Jerusalem [[cite:lepsius-intro]]. See [[event:armenian-genocide]].
