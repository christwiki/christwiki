Athenagoras I was Ecumenical Patriarch of [[place:constantinople]] when he met Pope [[person:paul-vi]] in [[place:jerusalem]] in January 1964, acting, as their communiqué says, with the agreement of his synod [[cite:communique]]. On 7 December 1965 he and his synod declared with the pope that they regretted the excommunications of 1054 and removed them from memory and from the midst of the church [[cite:decl-4]], while saying that this did not end the differences between the Orthodox and Roman Catholic churches [[cite:decl-5]]; see [[event:lifting-of-the-anathemas]].

In October 1967 he visited the pope in [[place:rome]]. Their declaration on that occasion speaks of the two churches as sister churches and of obstacles still to be overcome before full communion [[cite:decl-1967]].
