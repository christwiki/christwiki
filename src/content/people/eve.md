Genesis tells that God made the woman from the man's rib, because no partner was found for him among the animals [[bible:Gen 2:18-23]]. In [[event:the-fall]] the serpent speaks to her; she eats from the forbidden tree and gives the fruit to her husband [[bible:Gen 3:1-6]]. Afterwards the man names her Eve, "because she would be the mother of all the living" [[bible:Gen 3:20]]. She is the mother of Cain, Abel and Seth [[bible:Gen 4:1-2]] [[bible:Gen 4:25]]. Genesis does not record her death.

The New Testament names her twice, in letters that bear [[person:paul]]'s name: as one deceived by the serpent [[bible:2 Cor 11:3]], and in an argument from the order in which the man and the woman were made [[bible:1 Tim 2:13-14]]. From the second century Christian writers set her beside [[person:mary]]. [[person:irenaeus]] writes that the knot of Eve's disobedience was untied by Mary's obedience [[cite:irenaeus]].
