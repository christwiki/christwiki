Hannington wrote that he was born on 3 September 1847 [[cite:birth]]. He was consecrated bishop at Lambeth on 24 June 1884 [[cite:bishop]]. On his way to Buganda he was stopped at Luba's in Busoga and held prisoner [[cite:death]]. His diary, which later reached the missionaries, ends on 29 October 1885, the eighth day of his imprisonment [[cite:death]]. The missionary Robert Ashe tells that [[person:mwanga-ii]]'s messengers then led him out, that he was speared to death and his porters killed, and that one wounded porter survived to tell what had happened [[cite:death]]. Alexander Mackay wrote that Mwanga ordered the execution "in a fit of fear and madness" [[cite:order]].

The Church of England commemorates him on 29 October as a martyr in Uganda [[cite:calendar]]. See [[event:uganda-martyrs]].
