Timothy was a disciple at [[place:lystra]], well spoken of by the believers there. His mother was a Jewish believer and his father a Greek, and [[person:paul]] circumcised him before taking him on his travels [[bible:Acts 16:1-3]]. 2 Timothy names his mother Eunice and his grandmother Lois [[bible:2 Tim 1:5]]. He was with Paul and [[person:silas]] in Macedonia and at [[place:corinth]], and Paul used him as his envoy: he sent him back to [[place:thessalonica|Thessalonica]], and later from [[place:ephesus]] to Corinth and to Macedonia [[bible:1 Thess 3:1-6]] [[bible:1 Cor 4:17]] [[bible:Acts 19:22]]. See [[event:second-missionary-journey]].

Paul calls him "my beloved and faithful child in the Lord" and writes that he has no one else like him [[bible:1 Cor 4:17]] [[bible:Phil 2:19-22]]. Timothy is named with Paul in the opening of 2 Corinthians, Philippians, Colossians, 1 and 2 Thessalonians and Philemon [[bible:2 Cor 1:1]] [[bible:Phil 1:1]] [[bible:Col 1:1]] [[bible:1 Thess 1:1]] [[bible:2 Thess 1:1]] [[bible:Phlm 1]], and 1 and 2 Timothy are addressed to him, the first placing him at Ephesus [[bible:1 Tim 1:2-3]]. [[person:eusebius-of-caesarea|Eusebius]], in the early fourth century, reports as tradition that he was the first bishop of Ephesus [[cite:eusebius]].
