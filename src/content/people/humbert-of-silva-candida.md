The legates' report names him first of the three sent by Pope [[person:leo-ix]], as cardinal bishop of Silva Candida, and the bull opens with his name [[cite:account]] [[cite:bull]]. The report describes how, at the legates' insistence, a Greek monk publicly withdrew his tract against Latin customs and the emperor had it burnt, and how the legates, refused a meeting by the patriarch, excommunicated him and left [[cite:account]].

The synod of [[place:constantinople]] gives the other view: the legates behaved insolently, refused to appear before the patriarch and his synod, and were not sent by the pope at all [[cite:edict]]. See [[event:east-west-schism]].
