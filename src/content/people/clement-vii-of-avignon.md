The English chronicler Knighton records under 1378 that some of the cardinals left [[person:urban-vi]], went to [[place:avignon]] and raised Robert of Geneva to the papacy under the name Clement, and that a great schism followed, with kings and kingdoms on either side [[cite:knighton]]. [[person:catherine-of-siena]], who held to Urban, called him an antipope [[cite:catherine]].

At the time of the Council of Constance the claimant of this line was Peter de Luna, called Benedict XIII, whom the council deposed in 1417 [[cite:three]] [[cite:benedict]]. See [[event:western-schism]].
