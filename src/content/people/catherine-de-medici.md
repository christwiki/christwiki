Catherine was the mother of [[person:charles-ix-of-france]] and had governed as regent during his minority. Her daughter Marguerite, who records this, also describes her arranging Marguerite's marriage to the Protestant king of Navarre [[cite:marguerite-4]].

Marguerite, who says that she herself was told nothing at the time, reports that the king was told his mother and his brother Anjou had shared in the attempt on [[person:gaspard-de-coligny]], that Catherine feared the Huguenots would kill the king and herself in revenge, and that the king then followed her counsel, the killing being resolved on in her apartments [[cite:marguerite-4]] [[cite:marguerite-5]]. De Thou writes that the plan to kill the Protestants was approved by the queen [[cite:de-thou]]. An English report of September 1572 names her first among its "inventors" [[cite:csp-584]].
