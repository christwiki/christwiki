In the foundation charter William calls himself "count and duke by the grace of God" and makes the gift with his wife Ingelberga, for the soul of his lord King Odo, for his parents and kin, and for Ava, from whom he had the estate. He says that he cannot himself renounce the world but can support those who do [[cite:charter]].

The heading written in red over a fourteenth-century copy of the charter calls him William the Pious, duke of the Aquitanians and principal founder of the monastery, and records that he was buried at Saint-Julien of Brioude [[cite:rubric]]. See [[event:cluny]].
