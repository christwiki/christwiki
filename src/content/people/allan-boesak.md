The report of South Africa's Truth and Reconciliation Commission describes Dr Allan Boesak as president of the World Alliance of Reformed Churches at the time of its meeting of 1982, and says that his influence in promoting the resolution that declared apartheid a heresy, adopted by the Alliance and afterwards by the World Council of Churches and many of its member churches, was of far-reaching significance [[cite:warc]]. It lists him among those opponents of apartheid who rose to positions of leadership in their churches [[cite:leaders]].

For the wording of the Alliance's declaration, as a member church quoted it, see [[event:churches-and-apartheid]] [[cite:ottawa]]. This entry states only what those documents say.
