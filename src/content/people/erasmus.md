The title page of the New Testament printed at [[place:basel]] in 1516 names Erasmus of Rotterdam as the scholar who revised and corrected it [[cite:title]]. In the dedication to Pope [[person:leo-x]] he calls himself the least of theologians [[cite:dedication]]. He told the pope later that year that he meant to emend the common Latin text from old manuscripts and the fathers, not to tear it up [[cite:leo]].

In the preface called the *Paraclesis* he wished the Gospels and the letters of Paul translated into all languages and known to the ploughman and the weaver [[cite:paraclesis]]. See [[event:erasmus-greek-new-testament]].
