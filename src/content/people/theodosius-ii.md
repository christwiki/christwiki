Theodosius was born on 10 April 401, the son of the emperor Arcadius [[cite:birth]], and was left sole ruler of the East as a child when his father died on 1 May 408 [[cite:accession]]. He summoned the bishops to [[place:ephesus]] in 431 [[cite:synod]] (see [[event:council-of-ephesus]]). In 438 the body of [[person:john-chrysostom]], whom his parents had exiled, was brought back to [[place:constantinople]]; Theodoret says that the emperor laid his head on the coffin and prayed for their pardon [[cite:relics]] [[cite:parents]].

The collection of imperial laws compiled at his order and issued in 438 is [[source:theodosian-code]]. Sozomen dedicated his church history to him and names [[person:theodosius-i]] as his grandfather [[cite:sozomen]].
