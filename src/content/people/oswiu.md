Oswiu had been instructed and baptized by Irish monks and spoke their language well, while his queen Eanfled followed the Roman usage she had learned in Kent. He opened the [[event:synod-of-whitby]] and gave the verdict himself, saying that he would not oppose Peter, the keeper of the keys of heaven [[cite:synod]]. Bede dates the synod to the twenty-second year of his reign [[cite:year]].

He died on 15 February 670 in his fifty-eighth year. Bede says that by then he was so attached to Roman ways that he had planned, had he recovered, to end his days in Rome, with [[person:wilfrid]] as his guide [[cite:death]].
