Vladimir Mikhailovich Gundyaev was born in Leningrad on 20 November 1946, the son and grandson of priests [[cite:bio]]. From 1989 to 2009 he was chairman of the Moscow Patriarchate's Department for External Church Relations [[cite:bio]]. A local council of the Russian Orthodox Church elected him patriarch on 27 January 2009, and he was enthroned on 1 February [[cite:bio]].

On 12 February 2016 he met Pope [[person:francis]] at Havana, a meeting their joint declaration calls the first in history [[cite:havana]]. In June of that year the synod of his church resolved that its delegation could not take part in the [[event:holy-and-great-council|council on Crete]] unless the council was postponed [[cite:crete]]. In October 2018 the same synod declared eucharistic communion with the Patriarchate of Constantinople impossible after Constantinople's decisions on Ukraine [[cite:ukraine]]; see [[event:autocephaly-of-ukraine]]. In May 2022 the council of the Ukrainian Orthodox Church stated its disagreement with his position on the war in Ukraine [[cite:uoc-2022]].
