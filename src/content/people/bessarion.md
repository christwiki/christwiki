Doukas lists Bessarion of [[place:nicaea]] and Isidore of Russia as the most learned of the Greek bishops who argued their church's case at Florence, and says that all the Greeks except [[person:mark-of-ephesus]] subscribed to the definition of union [[cite:doukas]]. This wiki has not yet cited a primary source for his later life in Italy. See [[event:council-of-florence]].
