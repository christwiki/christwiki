The Lateran Treaty names "Cavaliere Benito Mussolini, Prime Minister and Head of the Government" as the plenipotentiary of the King of Italy, and his signature stands under the treaty beside that of Cardinal [[person:pietro-gasparri]] [[cite:preamble]]. Two days after the signing [[person:pius-xi]], without naming him, spoke of the man whom Providence had caused him to meet, one free of the preoccupations of the liberal school [[cite:address-13]]. See [[event:lateran-treaty]].
