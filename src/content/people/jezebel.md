Jezebel was the daughter of Ethbaal, king of the Sidonians, and married [[person:ahab]] of Israel [[bible:1 Kings 16:31]]. Josephus, citing the historian Menander, knows her father as a king of Tyre [[cite:ethbaal]]. Kings says that she killed prophets of the Lord and kept hundreds of prophets of Baal and Asherah at her table [[bible:1 Kings 18:4, 19]], and that she threatened [[person:elijah]] with death after the contest on Carmel [[bible:1 Kings 19:1-2]]; see [[event:elijah-on-carmel]]. She arranged the trial and stoning of Naboth with letters sealed in the king's name [[bible:1 Kings 21:5-15]].

She outlived Ahab into the reign of her son Joram [[bible:2 Kings 9:22]]. When [[person:jehu]] entered Jezreel she was thrown from a window at his order. Those sent to bury her found little of the body left, which Kings reports as the fulfilment of Elijah's word that dogs would eat her there [[bible:2 Kings 9:30-37]]; see [[event:jehu-pays-tribute]]. The Revelation to John uses her name for a false teacher in the church at Thyatira [[bible:Rev 2:20]].
