The *Liber Pontificalis* describes Stephen as a Roman, orphaned young and brought up in the Lateran. He was elected in 752 after a priest of the same name, chosen first, died within days; because of that priest some lists number him Stephen III. He led processions barefoot through Rome carrying an image of Christ when the Lombard king Aistulf threatened the city, appealed to the emperor without result, and then turned to the Franks [[cite:life]].

He left Rome on 14 October 753, pleaded in vain with Aistulf at Pavia, met King [[person:pepin-the-short|Pepin]] at Ponthion on 6 January 754 and anointed him and his sons at Saint-Denis [[cite:journey]]. After Pepin's two campaigns he received the keys of [[place:ravenna]] and the other cities of the exarchate, laid on the tomb of Peter with the king's deed of gift. He was buried at St Peter's on 26 April in the tenth indiction, 757 [[cite:end]]. See [[event:donation-of-pepin]].
