role: Wife of Uriah, then of David; mother of Solomon
summary: Wife of Uriah the Hittite, taken by David while her husband was at war, and afterwards David's wife and the mother of Solomon. In Kings she helps to secure the throne for her son.
---

Samuel introduces Bathsheba as the daughter of Eliam and the wife of [[person:uriah-the-hittite]]. [[person:david]] saw her from his roof, sent for her and lay with her, and after Uriah's death made her his wife. Their first child died, and she then bore [[person:solomon]] [[bible:2 Sam 11:2-5, 26-27]] [[bible:2 Sam 12:15-24]]. Chronicles calls her Bath-shua, daughter of Ammiel, and lists four sons born to her in [[place:jerusalem]] [[bible:1 Chr 3:5]].

When David was old and his son Adonijah claimed the throne, she went to the king at the prompting of [[person:nathan]] and obtained his oath that Solomon should reign [[bible:1 Kings 1:11-31]]. Under Solomon she sat at the king's right hand as the king's mother, and carried to him Adonijah's request for Abishag, which cost Adonijah his life [[bible:1 Kings 2:13-25]]. Matthew's genealogy of Jesus refers to her without her name, as the woman who had been Uriah's wife [[bible:Matt 1:6]].
