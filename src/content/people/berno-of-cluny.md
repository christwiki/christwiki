The foundation charter places the monks and all their property under "the abbot Berno", who is to preside over them as long as he lives, according to his knowledge and ability [[cite:charter]]. The next document in the abbey's collection of charters shows him at work as abbot of Cluny, exchanging fields in the district of Mâcon with a local couple [[cite:exchange]]. These two charters say nothing of his earlier life. See [[event:cluny]].
