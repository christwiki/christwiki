Justinian was the nephew of the emperor Justin, who made him co-emperor on 1 April 527 and died in the fourth month after that [[cite:accession]]. He survived the Nika riot of 532 [[cite:nika]] and rebuilt the burned cathedral of [[place:constantinople]] as the domed church of Hagia Sophia [[cite:church]]. In a law of 535 he described priesthood and empire as two gifts of God that must work in harmony [[cite:novel-6]].

In doctrine he held to Chalcedon while the empress [[person:theodora]] favoured its opponents [[cite:chalcedon]]. He told the bishops whom he summoned in 553 that his chief task was to reunite the churches, and he asked them to judge three sets of writings linked with Nestorius, the "Three Chapters" [[cite:letter]] ([[event:council-of-constantinople-ii]]). Pope [[person:vigilius]], who was in the city, stayed away from the council [[cite:council]]. Evagrius gives his reign as thirty-eight years and eight months [[cite:death]], which places his death in 565.
