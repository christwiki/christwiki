In his own letters the leader of the revolt is Simeon (Shim'on) bar Kosiba [[cite:letters]], and a coin of the revolt's first year gives Shimon the title "Prince of Israel" [[cite:coin]]. The letters show a commander who gives sharp orders, threatens officers who are slow to obey, and sends for palm branches, citrons, myrtles and willows for his camp [[cite:letters]].

The name Bar Kokhba, "son of the star", comes from others. The Jerusalem Talmud, compiled more than two centuries later, says that Rabbi Akiva applied to him the verse "A star will come out of Jacob" and called him the King Messiah, and that Rabbi Yohanan ben Torta answered that grass would grow from Akiva's jaws before the son of David came [[bible:Num 24:17]] [[cite:talmud]]. The same passage spells his name Bar Koziba. Justin Martyr, his contemporary, says that he had Christians punished unless they denied Jesus [[cite:justin]]. Eusebius says that the name means a star and that he "suffered his just punishment" when the siege of [[place:betar]] ended [[cite:eusebius]]. See [[event:bar-kokhba-revolt]].
