Rufinus speaks only of a captive woman among the Iberians, whose way of life and prayers were the cause of that people's conversion [[cite:rufinus]]. The Georgian texts give her a name and a history. In *The Conversion of Kartli* Nino is a captive who escapes from Armenia when her companions are martyred there, crosses the mountains to [[place:mtskheta]], and lives for three years in a bramble thicket outside its walls, praying before a cross she has made of vine stems [[cite:kartli-flight]] [[cite:kartli]].

The longer *Life* makes her the only child of Zabulon, a Cappadocian, and of Sosana, whose brother Iobenal had become patriarch [[cite:life-title]] [[cite:life-birth]]. It calls her an apostle and the enlightener of Georgia and gives 14 January as her feast [[cite:life-title]]. It tells of her burial at the village of Budi in Kakheti, which the translators' heading calls Bodbe [[cite:life-burial]]. The whole story is told under [[event:conversion-of-georgia]].
