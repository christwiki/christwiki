Stone writes that he was born near Port Tobacco in Maryland on 24 December 1772 [[cite:birth]]. He settled at the end of 1796 as preacher to the congregations of Cane Ridge and Concord in Bourbon County, Kentucky, and was ordained by the Presbyterians although he would not accept their Confession of Faith without qualification [[cite:settled]]. He describes the great meeting at [[place:cane-ridge]] in August 1801 as one who took part in it [[cite:meeting]].

After the Synod of Kentucky moved against him and his colleagues they formed the Springfield Presbytery, renounced all creeds but the Bible, and soon dropped their own name for that of Christians [[cite:presbytery]]. Stone was one of the six who signed the Presbytery's *Last Will and Testament* on 28 June 1804 [[cite:last-will]]. A notice printed with his autobiography records his death at Hannibal, Missouri, on 9 November 1844 [[cite:death]]. See [[event:second-great-awakening]].
