The decision of March 1692, taken by the Board of Rites at his order and, by the Jesuits' account, confirmed by him, left the churches of the empire standing and allowed anyone to worship in them [[cite:toleration]]. The Jesuits at [[place:beijing]] report that on 30 November 1700 they submitted to the emperor a statement of how they understood the honours paid to Heaven, Confucius and the ancestors, and that he replied that it was very well written, agreed with the great doctrine and needed no correction [[cite:declaration]].

In 1721, on reading a proclamation that the sourcebook identifies as the papal decree against the rites, he wrote that Westerners did not understand Chinese matters and should not be allowed to preach in China [[cite:comment]]. See [[event:chinese-rites-controversy]].
