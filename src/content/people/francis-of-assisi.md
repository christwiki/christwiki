Francis traded in cloth at [[place:assisi]] before his conversion [[cite:merchant]]. In his Testament he traces the change in his life to the time he spent among lepers, and says that he worked with his hands and wished all his brothers to work, begging alms only when they were not paid [[cite:testament]]. Thomas of Celano reports that in the thirteenth year after his conversion he went to Syria while Christians and Saracens were at war and presented himself to the sultan, who received him with honour [[cite:sultan]].

Celano dates his death at the Portiuncula, in his native city, to Sunday 4 October 1226 [[cite:death]]. The Testament names the bishop of Ostia as protector of the brotherhood [[cite:testament]]; that bishop, by then Pope [[person:gregory-ix]], declared Francis a saint at Assisi on 16 July 1228 [[cite:canonized]].
