Walter Kasper was born at Heidenheim an der Brenz in Germany on 5 March 1933, was ordained a priest of the diocese of Rottenburg-Stuttgart in 1957, taught dogmatic theology at Münster and Tübingen, and became bishop of Rottenburg-Stuttgart in 1989 [[cite:bio]]. In 1994 he was named co-chair of the international commission for Lutheran–Catholic dialogue, and on 1 June 1999 secretary of the Pontifical Council for Promoting Christian Unity [[cite:bio]].

In that office he signed, with Cardinal Edward Cassidy, the statement that confirmed the Joint Declaration for the Catholic Church [[cite:ocs]]; see [[event:joint-declaration-on-justification]]. [[person:john-paul-ii]] made him a cardinal in February 2001 and president of the council the following month, a post he held until 2010 [[cite:bio]].
