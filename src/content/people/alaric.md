Alaric had fought as an ally of [[person:theodosius-i]] against the usurper Eugenius and held Roman rank before he turned on the empire and marched west [[cite:socrates]]. Orosius calls him an enemy but a Christian king [[cite:christian]]; Sozomen sets him with the Arian party among the Goths [[cite:sozomen]].

In 410 his army entered [[place:rome]]. He let his men plunder, but according to Orosius he ordered that those who took refuge in the basilicas of the apostles Peter and Paul were not to be harmed [[cite:orosius]]; Sozomen says that he made the church of Peter a place of asylum [[cite:sozomen]]. The Goths left on the third day [[cite:orosius]]. Alaric was dead by the time Orosius wrote, a few years later, and his kinsman Athaulf had succeeded him [[cite:successor]].
