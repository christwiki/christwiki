In *The Uneasy Conscience of Modern Fundamentalism* Henry argued that the gospel, once a "world-changing message", had been narrowed by fundamentalism to a "world-resisting" one [[cite:henry-ch2]]. He was the first editor of *Christianity Today* [[cite:founding]], whose first issue, dated 15 October 1956, opened with an editorial saying that the magazine would apply the biblical revelation to the social crisis of the day [[cite:editorial]].
