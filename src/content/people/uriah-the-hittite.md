---
name: Uriah the Hittite
role: Warrior in David's army, husband of Bathsheba
summary: A Hittite among David's warriors and the husband of Bathsheba. When Bathsheba was pregnant by the king and Uriah would not go home, David had him placed in the front of the fighting at Rabbah, where he was killed.
---

Uriah's name closes Samuel's list of [[person:david]]'s warriors, the group called the Thirty [[bible:2 Sam 23:24, 39]]. He was serving under Joab at the siege of Rabbah when David took his wife [[person:bathsheba]]. Called back to [[place:jerusalem]], he would not go home while the ark and the army were in the field. David sent him back to Joab carrying the letter that ordered his death, and he fell under the city wall [[bible:2 Sam 11:6-17, 24]].

[[person:nathan]] charged David with having killed him "with the sword of the children of Ammon" [[bible:2 Sam 12:9]]. Kings names "the matter of Uriah the Hittite" as the one exception to David's obedience [[bible:1 Kings 15:5]], and Matthew's genealogy keeps his name where it omits his wife's [[bible:Matt 1:6]].
