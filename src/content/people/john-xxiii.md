Angelo Giuseppe Roncalli was born on 25 November 1881 at Sotto il Monte, near Bergamo, to a family of sharecroppers. He was ordained in 1904, represented the Holy See in Bulgaria, in Turkey and Greece and in France, and became patriarch of Venice in 1953. The cardinals elected him pope in October 1958, when he was 76 [[cite:life]].

On 25 January 1959 he announced that he would hold a council [[cite:announced]], and at Christmas 1961 he summoned it for the following year [[cite:convoked]]. He opened [[event:second-vatican-council]] on 11 October 1962 [[cite:closing]] with an address that asked for the church's teaching to be handed on whole, in a manner suited to the times [[cite:gaudet-6]]. He died on 3 June 1963 [[cite:life]], and [[person:paul-vi]] carried the council on [[cite:closing]]. Pope Francis canonised him on 27 April 2014 together with [[person:john-paul-ii]] [[cite:john-paul-life]].
