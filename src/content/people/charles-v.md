The Edict of Worms styles Charles elected Roman emperor, king of Germany, Spain, the Two Sicilies, Jerusalem and Hungary, archduke of Austria and duke of Burgundy [[cite:edict]]. The day after [[person:martin-luther]] refused to retract at [[place:worms]], Charles declared that he would stake his dominions, his friends, his body and his soul on the defence of the faith his ancestors had held, and would hear Luther no more [[cite:declaration]]. The edict issued in his name put Luther under the ban of the empire [[cite:edict]].

In 1529 the proposition laid before the diet of [[place:speyer]] in his name annulled the article of 1526 that had left each estate to answer to God and the emperor for its conduct [[cite:proposition]]. The Lutheran estates addressed their confession to him at [[place:augsburg]] in 1530 [[cite:augsburg]]. See [[event:diet-of-worms]], [[event:protestation-at-speyer]] and [[event:augsburg-confession]].
