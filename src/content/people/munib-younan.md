The Lutheran World Federation's list of its presidents gives Munib Younan's country of origin as Palestine, his date of birth as 18 September 1950 and his term as 2010 to 2017 [[cite:lwf-history]]. He was bishop of the Evangelical Lutheran Church in Jordan and the Holy Land [[cite:lwf-report]].

At the common prayer in the cathedral of [[place:lund]] on 31 October 2016 he spoke the words of welcome, saying that what unites Catholics and Lutherans is far greater than what divides them, and signed the joint statement with [[person:francis]] [[cite:lwf-report]]; see [[event:reformation-500]].
