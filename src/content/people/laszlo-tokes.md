László Tőkés was born at Cluj on 1 April 1952, according to the European Parliament's register of its members [[cite:profile]]. The parliament's chronology of 1989 says that protests broke out in Timișoara on 16 December in response to the government's attempt to evict him, a dissident pastor, and that they spread to Bucharest and forced Nicolae Ceaușescu to flee [[cite:ep-december]]. See [[event:peaceful-revolution]].

In the parliament's eighth term, from 2014 to 2019, he sat for a Hungarian party and was vice-chair of the subcommittee on human rights [[cite:profile]]. No document of December 1989 on the events at Timișoara was found on an open institutional site, and this entry says no more about them than the chronology does.
