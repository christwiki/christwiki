---
name: Jonah
altNames: [Jonas]
role: Prophet of Israel, eighth century BC
summary: Prophet from Gath-hepher in the northern kingdom in the time of Jeroboam II. The book named after him tells of his flight from a mission to Nineveh, the great fish and the city's repentance.
---

Kings names Jonah son of Amittai, from Gath-hepher in the territory of Zebulun [[bible:Josh 19:10-13]], as the prophet who foretold that [[person:jeroboam-ii]] would restore Israel's borders [[bible:2 Kings 14:25]]. The book of Jonah tells how he was sent to [[place:nineveh]], fled by ship from [[place:joppa]], was swallowed by a great fish and at last preached in the city, which repented [[bible:Jonah 1:1-3]] [[bible:Jonah 1:17]] [[bible:Jonah 3:1-10]]. The book gives one line of his preaching [[bible:Jonah 3:4]]; the rest is narrative and a prayer.

In the Gospels Jesus speaks of "the sign of Jonah" [[bible:Matt 12:39-41]] [[bible:Luke 11:29-32]]. On the date and character of the book see [[event:jonah-and-nineveh]].
