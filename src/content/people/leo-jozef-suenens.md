The edition of his writings published by the Association FIAT gives his dates as 16 July 1904 to 6 May 1996 [[cite:dates]]. From 21 to 26 May 1974 he gathered a small international team of theologians and lay leaders at Malines in Belgium, who drew up a statement of theological and pastoral guidance on the charismatic renewal among Catholics [[cite:malines-intro]]. See [[event:charismatic-renewal]].
