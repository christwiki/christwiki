Theophanes records that Irene came from Athens in November 769 to be married to the emperor Leo IV [[cite:athens]], and that on 8 September 780 she and her son Constantine, a widow and an orphan child as he puts it, took up the rule of the empire [[cite:accession]]. She put forward Tarasius as patriarch, asked Pope [[person:hadrian-i]] for legates, removed from the capital the regiments that broke up the first meeting, and saw the [[event:second-council-of-nicaea]] through; she and her son signed its definition in the palace [[cite:council]].

In August 797 her son was blinded, by her will and that of her advisers according to Theophanes, and she reigned alone [[cite:blinding]]. It was during her sole reign that Charlemagne was crowned emperor at Rome, and Theophanes says that envoys from Charles and Pope Leo came to propose a marriage between them that would unite East and West [[cite:charles]]. Theophanes, a defender of images, repeatedly calls her "most pious".
