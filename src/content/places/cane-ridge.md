[[person:barton-stone]] became preacher to the congregation of Cane Ridge, in Bourbon County, at the end of 1796 [[cite:settled]]. The meeting that began there in August 1801 drew a crowd that he says was reckoned at twenty to thirty thousand [[cite:meeting]]. The *Last Will and Testament of the Springfield Presbytery* describes that body as "sitting at Caneridge, in the county of Bourbon" [[cite:last-will]]. See [[event:second-great-awakening]].
