The realm is called simply Moravia in the Slavonic Lives. Pope John VIII addressed its ruler Svatopluk in 880, called [[person:methodius]] archbishop of the Moravian church and consecrated a bishop for Nitra within it [[cite:letter]]. The Kyivan chronicle remembered it as the land for which Slavonic books were first written [[cite:chronicle]]. Its chief places are not named in these sources; the marker stands in the valley of the river Morava, from which the land takes its name. See [[event:cyril-and-methodius]].
