Genesis describes Goshen as a district of Egypt with good pasture, near enough to [[person:joseph]]'s residence for his family to be close to him [[bible:Gen 45:10]] [[bible:Gen 47:6]]. [[person:jacob]] reaches it first on the road from Canaan, and Joseph goes up to meet him there [[bible:Gen 46:28-29]]. The same district is called "the land of Rameses" [[bible:Gen 47:11]], and the Israelites later set out from Rameses at the exodus [[bible:Exod 12:37]]. In the plague stories Goshen, where Israel lives, is spared [[bible:Exod 8:22]] [[bible:Exod 9:26]]. These details point to the eastern side of the Nile Delta, toward the Sinai frontier, which is where the district is generally placed. The pin marks that general area and not a known boundary.
