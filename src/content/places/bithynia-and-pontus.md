The province joined two former kingdoms along the southern shore of the Black Sea, from the Bosporus eastward. Its cities included [[place:nicomedia]] and [[place:nicaea]] in the west and Sinope and Amisus on the Pontic coast. Acts says that Paul tried to enter Bithynia and was prevented [[bible:Acts 16:7]], and the First Letter of Peter is addressed to believers in Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia and Bithynia [[bible:1 Pet 1:1]]. Early in the second century [[person:pliny-the-younger]] wrote that the Christian "superstition" had spread through its towns, villages and countryside [[cite:pliny]]. The marker shows the middle of the province.
