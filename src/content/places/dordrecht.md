Dordrecht lies in the south of the province of Holland. The States General of the United Netherlands called the national synod to meet in the city, and the Canons are headed as those of the synod "held at Dordrecht" in 1618 and 1619 [[cite:title]]. The English chaplain John Hales dated his reports of the sessions from "Dort" [[cite:hales]]. See [[event:synod-of-dort]].
