The bishop of Tarbes, in whose diocese Lourdes lay, judged in 1862 that Mary had appeared to [[person:bernadette-soubirous]] in the grotto of Massabielle, near the town, in 1858. He authorized in his diocese the devotion to Our Lady of the Grotto of Lourdes and announced that a sanctuary would be built on the ground of the grotto, which had become the property of the bishops of Tarbes [[cite:judgment]]. See [[event:immaculate-conception]].
