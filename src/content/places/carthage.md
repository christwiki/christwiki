Carthage stood on a peninsula in the Gulf of Tunis. Refounded as a Roman colony, it was the seat of the proconsul of Africa, to whom [[person:tertullian]] addressed his plea for the Christians of the city [[cite:scapula]]. Jerome calls Tertullian a native of "the city of Carthage in the province of Africa" [[cite:jerome]], and Tertullian claimed about 197 that Christians, though "of yesterday", already filled the cities and towns [[cite:apology]]. For later events here see [[event:perpetua-and-felicity]], [[event:cyprian-on-unity]] and [[event:councils-of-hippo-and-carthage]].
