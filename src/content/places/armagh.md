Armagh (Irish *Ard Macha*) stands on a hill in what was the territory of the Airthir. Patrick's own writings do not mention it. Muirchú, writing before 700, tells how a local lord named Dáire first gave [[person:patrick]] a plot at the foot of the hill and later the hill itself, where the city of Armagh then stood [[cite:muirchu]]. The Annals of Ulster enter its foundation under the year 444 [[cite:annals]]. The oldest copy of Patrick's *Confession* is in the Book of Armagh, copied about 807 and now in Trinity College Dublin ([[source:patrick-confession]]).
