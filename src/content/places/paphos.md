Acts places the Roman proconsul of Cyprus at Paphos. It says that [[person:barnabas]] and Saul crossed the whole island from Salamis to reach it, and that the proconsul, Sergius Paulus, sent for them there. It tells how Saul rebuked the magician Elymas, who was struck blind for a time, and how the proconsul believed. From this point Acts calls Saul [[person:paul]]. The party sailed from Paphos to Perga on the coast of Asia Minor [[bible:Acts 13:6-13]]. See [[event:first-missionary-journey]].
