Willibald names the place Gaesmere and sets it in the land of the Hessians. There [[person:boniface]] cut down an oak which the pagans called by the name of Jupiter, and built an oratory of St Peter from the timber [[cite:oak]]. Several places in Hesse are called Geismar, and the translator's note to this passage calls the location uncertain. The village beside Fritzlar, marked here, is the one usually chosen.
