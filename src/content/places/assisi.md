Assisi is a town in Umbria in central Italy. [[person:francis-of-assisi]] and [[person:clare-of-assisi]] were both born there. Thomas of Celano names two churches near it: San Damiano, by the road to the town, which Francis repaired and where Clare's community began [[cite:damiano]], and Saint Mary of the Portiuncula, where the order began and where Francis died in 1226 [[cite:portiuncula]]. See [[event:francis-of-assisi]].
