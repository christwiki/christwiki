Nicomedia stood at the head of a gulf of the Sea of Marmara, in the province of Bithynia. [[person:diocletian]] kept his court there. Lactantius, who taught in the city, says that its church stood on high ground in sight of the palace and was pulled down in a few hours on 23 February 303 [[cite:church]]. Diocletian laid down his office on a hill three miles outside the walls [[cite:abdication]]. The edict by which [[person:galerius]] ended the persecution was posted in the city on 30 April 311 [[cite:edict]], and two years later [[person:licinius]] published there the letter granting freedom of worship [[cite:licinius]]. In 337 [[person:constantine]] was baptised in its suburbs and died there [[cite:baptism]].
