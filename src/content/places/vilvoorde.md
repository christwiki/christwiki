Vilvoorde lies a few miles north of Brussels. John Foxe, who spells the name Filford, tells that [[person:william-tyndale]] was held in its castle for a year and a half and was strangled and burned at the town in 1536 [[cite:foxe]]. See [[event:tyndale-new-testament]].
