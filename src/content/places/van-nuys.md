Van Nuys is a district of Los Angeles in the San Fernando Valley. [[person:dennis-bennett]] was rector of St Mark's Church there, and under him its membership grew from about 500 to 2,500 by 1960 [[cite:bennett-dict]]. The Episcopal Church's dictionary names St Mark's among several Californian parishes where a Pentecostal experience was reported in 1959 [[cite:renewal-dict]]. See [[event:charismatic-renewal]].
