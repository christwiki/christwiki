London stands on the Thames, with the royal palace and the meeting place of Parliament at Westminster a short way upstream. The Parliament that passed the Act of Supremacy sat at Westminster from 3 November 1534 [[cite:parliament]]; see [[event:act-of-supremacy]]. [[person:john-fisher]] and [[person:thomas-more]] were tried at Westminster and beheaded on Tower Hill, and their heads were set on London Bridge [[cite:deaths]]. A London chronicler records that the choir of St Paul's began to use the English prayer book in Lent 1549 and the revised book on 1 November 1552 [[cite:prayer-book]]; see [[event:book-of-common-prayer]].
