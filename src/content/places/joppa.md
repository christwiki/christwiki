Joppa was the port through which goods for [[place:jerusalem]] came by sea. Cedar from Lebanon for Solomon's temple, and again for the second temple, was floated down the coast to it [[bible:2 Chr 2:16]] [[bible:Ezra 3:7]]. [[person:jonah]] went down to Joppa to find a ship for Tarshish [[bible:Jonah 1:3]]. In Acts, [[person:peter]] raised Tabitha there and lodged with Simon the tanner. On the roof of that house he saw the vision of clean and unclean animals, and from there he was fetched to the centurion [[person:cornelius]] [[bible:Acts 9:36-43]] [[bible:Acts 10:5-23]].
