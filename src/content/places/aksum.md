Aksum lies in the highlands of northern Ethiopia. In the fourth century its kings claimed rule over neighbouring peoples on both sides of the Red Sea: [[person:ezana]] styles himself king of the Aksumites and of the Himyarites and Sabaeans of southern Arabia [[cite:inscription]]. Inscriptions set up in the city in Greek and Ge'ez record his wars, and one of them opens with a Christian invocation [[cite:christian]]. Athanasius calls the place Auxumis and names [[person:frumentius]] as its bishop [[cite:athanasius]]. See [[event:ezana-of-aksum]].
