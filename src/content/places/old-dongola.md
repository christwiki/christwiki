Old Dongola lies on the Nile in northern Sudan. The Pleiades gazetteer of ancient places describes it as the late antique capital of the Nubian state of Makuria and a centre of trade through the medieval period ([Pleiades 865674318](https://pleiades.stoa.org/places/865674318)). John of Ephesus mentions the Makoritae as a people living between the Nobadae and the Alodaei whose king tried to stop the bishop Longinus on his way south [[cite:makoritae]]; he does not name their city. A Latin chronicle records under the third year of the emperor Justin II that a people it calls "Maccurritae" received the Christian faith; the name is close to theirs, though the chronicle's editor took it for that of a people in Mauretania [[cite:biclaro]].
