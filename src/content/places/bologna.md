Bologna lies at the foot of the Apennines on the road from the Po plain to Tuscany. The emperor Frederick I's law of 1158 protecting scholars who travel for study does not name it, but Dana C. Munro, who translated the law, reports that it is generally taken to have been made for Bologna and may have been obtained by its doctors of law [[cite:habita]]. He adds that most Italian universities, and some in France and Spain, later copied Bologna as those of the north copied [[place:paris]] [[cite:munro-contents]].
