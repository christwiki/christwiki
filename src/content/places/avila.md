Ávila stands on the high plateau of central Spain, north-west of Madrid. [[person:teresa-of-avila]] founded the convent of St Joseph here in 1562 [[cite:prologue]]. The city's governor and council at first resolved that the new house should not be allowed to exist and took the matter to law [[cite:foundation]]. Teresa finished the *Interior Castle* at St Joseph's in November 1577 [[cite:castle]]. See [[event:teresa-of-avila]].
