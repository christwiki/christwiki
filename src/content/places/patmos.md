Patmos is one of the smaller islands of the eastern Aegean, some sixty kilometres off the coast of Asia Minor, south-west of [[place:miletus]]. The writer of the Revelation says that he was on the island "because of God's Word and the testimony of Jesus Christ" when he was told to write to the seven churches of Asia [[bible:Rev 1:9-11]]. The book does not say more about why he was there. Tertullian, about AD 200, speaks of John's exile on an island [[cite:tertullian]], and Eusebius, in the early fourth century, gives it as something said that he had been condemned to live on Patmos under Domitian [[cite:eusebius]]. See [[person:john-of-patmos]].
