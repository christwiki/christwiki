Trent lies in the Alpine valley of the Adige. In the bull that summoned the council [[person:paul-iii]] says that he would have preferred a city further into Italy but chose Trent because the Germans wished for it, as a place that bishops could reach easily from Germany and the neighbouring nations [[cite:choice]]. In March 1547 the council voted to move to [[place:bologna]] because of disease in the city [[cite:moved]]; it resumed at Trent in May 1551 [[cite:resumed]] and closed there on 4 December 1563 [[cite:close]].
