[[person:athanasius-the-athonite]] describes Athos as a peninsula running out into the sea, steep and without harbours on either side, so cut off in winter that he had to plant his own vineyard. Monks already lived there under a *protos*, who gave a newcomer a place after two or three years [[cite:site]]. The charter of the emperor John Tzimiskes speaks of the superiors of the Mountain meeting in council with the whole body of the brothers [[cite:rule]]. See [[event:mount-athos]].
