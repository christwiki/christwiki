Lund lies in southern Sweden, next to the city of Malmö [[cite:lwf-press]]. The Lutheran World Federation held its first assembly here from 30 June to 6 July 1947 and chose Anders Nygren of Lund as its first president [[cite:lwf-history]]. On 31 October 2016 Pope [[person:francis]] and the federation's leaders prayed together in the cathedral of Lund at the opening of the year that marked five hundred years since the Reformation [[cite:statement]]; see [[event:reformation-500]].
