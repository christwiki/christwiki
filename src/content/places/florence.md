[[person:eugene-iv]] transferred his council here from [[place:ferrara]] in January 1439, giving the plague as the reason and the healthy air of the new site as a condition of an ecumenical council [[cite:transfer]]. The definition of union with the Greeks of 6 July 1439 names Florence as the seat of the council that approved it [[cite:laetentur]]. See [[event:council-of-florence]].
