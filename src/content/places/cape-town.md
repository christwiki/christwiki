The magazine of the Christian Reformed Church in North America reports that the Belhar Confession is named after a suburb of Cape Town, where a general synod of the Dutch Reformed Mission Church met in 1982 [[cite:belhar]]. In his Nobel lecture of 1984 [[person:desmond-tutu]] described the daily demolition of shelters at a squatter camp near the city [[cite:lecture]]. He died at Cape Town on 26 December 2021 [[cite:facts]]. See [[event:churches-and-apartheid]].
