[[person:bede]] calls his house the monastery of the apostles Peter and Paul, "at the mouth of the river Wear, and near the river Tyne, at the place called Ingyruum", that is, Jarrow [[cite:place]]. He says that Benedict Biscop built the house at the mouth of the Wear and brought the chief cantor of St Peter's in Rome to teach its monks the Roman chant [[cite:john]]. Bede was given to the monastery as a child and spent his life there [[cite:life]].
