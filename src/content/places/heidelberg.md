Heidelberg lies on the river Neckar in south-western Germany. In the sixteenth century it was the seat of the electors Palatine and of a university. [[person:frederick-iii-of-the-palatinate]] dated his preface to the catechism here on 19 January 1563 and names "our entire theological faculty in this place" among those who prepared it [[cite:preface]]. See [[event:heidelberg-catechism]].
