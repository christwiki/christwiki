Mylapore is now part of [[place:chennai]]. A chronology published by an eparchy of the Syro-Malabar Church gives it as the place where the apostle [[person:thomas]] was martyred, on 3 July 72, and where his tomb was [[cite:tradition]]. Marco Polo, in 1298, describes a small town in the province of Maabar where the body of St Thomas lay and where Christians and Muslims came on pilgrimage. He does not name it; his editor Henry Yule identifies it with Mylapore [[cite:polo]]. The *Acts of Thomas*, the oldest account cited in this wiki, puts the apostle's death on a mountain in the kingdom of a king Misdeus and names no town; see [[event:thomas-and-india|Thomas and India]].
