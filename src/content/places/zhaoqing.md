Zhaoqing lies on the West River, about eighty kilometres west of Guangzhou. [[person:florence-li-tim-oi]] recalled that early in 1944 she crossed Japanese-held territory into what she calls "Free" China, that she and Bishop [[person:ronald-hall]] came to Zhaoqing from Xingxing, a short distance away, on the day of her ordination, and that the service was held in a small, old Anglican church [[cite:zhaoqing]]. See [[event:florence-li-tim-oi]].
