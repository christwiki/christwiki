Philippi was in Paul's day a Roman colony, as Acts notes [[bible:Acts 16:12]]. [[person:paul]], [[person:silas]] and [[person:timothy]] came to it from Troas by way of the port of Neapolis. [[person:lydia]] and her household were baptised, Paul and Silas were beaten and jailed, and the jailer and his household were baptised in turn [[bible:Acts 16:11-40]]; see [[event:second-missionary-journey]]. Paul recalls the rough treatment he received there [[bible:1 Thess 2:2]]. He passed through again on his way to [[place:jerusalem|Jerusalem]] [[bible:Acts 20:6]]. His letter to the church is addressed to "all the saints in Christ Jesus who are at Philippi, with the overseers and servants", and thanks them for the gifts they had sent him from the beginning [[bible:Phil 1:1]] [[bible:Phil 4:15-16]].
