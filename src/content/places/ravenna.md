Procopius describes Ravenna as lying in a level plain at the head of the Adriatic, a short way from the sea, and hard to approach either by ship, because of the shoals, or by a land army [[cite:procopius]]. [[person:odoacer]] deposed [[person:romulus-augustulus]] here in 476, and here Theoderic besieged Odoacer for three years and killed him [[cite:av]].
