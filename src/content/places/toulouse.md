Toulouse lies on the river Garonne in the south of France. Jordan of Saxony says that when [[person:dominic]] first passed through, the people of the region had long been heretics [[cite:heresy]]. A citizen, Peter Seila, gave Dominic houses near the Narbonne castle in 1215, and the next year the brothers received the church of Saint Romanus in the city [[cite:houses]]. A friar of that convent, William Pelhisson, records that the pope later made two of its members inquisitors against heretics in the dioceses of Toulouse and Cahors [[cite:inquisitors]], and that when the consuls drove the friars out of the city the pope had the count of Toulouse bring them back [[cite:count]]. See [[event:dominicans]] and [[event:papal-inquisition]].
