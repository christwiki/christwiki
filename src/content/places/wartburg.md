The Wartburg is a castle on a hill above Eisenach in Thuringia. [[person:martin-luther]]'s letters from his hiding place carry cover names such as "the land of the birds" in place of a place-name. In one of them, of 12 May 1521, he wrote that he had been brought to the castle by night [[cite:amsdorf]], and after his return he said that he had translated the whole New Testament in his "Patmos" [[cite:patmos]]. The editors of the letters identify the place as the Wartburg. See [[event:diet-of-worms]] and [[event:luther-bible]].
