Osnabrück lies about fifty kilometres north-east of [[place:munster]]. The treaty between the emperor and the queen of Sweden says that the congress of plenipotentiaries was appointed to meet there and at Münster from July 1643 [[cite:congress]], and its closing clause is dated at Osnabrück on 14/24 October 1648 [[cite:signed]]. Its fifth and seventh articles settle the rights of the confessions in the Empire [[cite:articles]]. See [[event:thirty-years-war]].
