Münster lies in Westphalia in north-western Germany. The peace treaty with Sweden records that a congress of plenipotentiaries was appointed to meet from July 1643 at [[place:osnabruck]] and at Münster [[cite:congress]], and the treaty between the emperor and the king of France is dated at Münster on 24 October 1648 [[cite:signed]].
