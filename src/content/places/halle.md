Halle stands on the river Saale. [[person:august-hermann-francke]] was minister at Glaucha, just outside the city, where the foundation of his orphan house was laid in July 1698 [[cite:building]]. [[person:bartholomaus-ziegenbalg]] and [[person:heinrich-plutschau]] were sent to the university of Halle before they went to India [[cite:missionaries]]. See [[event:pietism]] and [[event:tranquebar-mission]].
