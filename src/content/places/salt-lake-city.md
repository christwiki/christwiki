The epistle that the Latter-day Saints' Twelve Apostles issued in December 1847 describes a valley some twenty by thirty miles in extent, with the Great Salt Lake to the north-west, which their pioneer company reached in the latter part of July 1847 [[cite:epistle-journey]]. There, it says, they chose the site of a city, to be called the Great Salt Lake City, surveyed it in blocks of ten acres and reserved one block for a temple [[cite:epistle-city]]. The church's general conference met in the city in April 2020 [[cite:proclamation]]. See [[event:latter-day-saints]] and [[person:brigham-young]].
