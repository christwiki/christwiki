The Jesuit Diego de Pantoja, writing from the city in 1602, calls it "Paquin", the court of the king of China [[cite:court]]. [[person:matteo-ricci]] and his companions reached it on 24 January 1601 [[cite:arrival]]. When Ricci died in 1610 the Jesuits petitioned the emperor for a burial place, since no one might be buried inside the walls, and were granted a site for the grave and for a house [[cite:burial]]. See [[event:matteo-ricci-in-china]].
