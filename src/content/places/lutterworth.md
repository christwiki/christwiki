Lutterworth lay in the medieval diocese of Lincoln. A papal bull of 1377 names [[person:john-wycliffe]] as rector of its church [[cite:bull]]. John Foxe, writing in the sixteenth century, says that Wycliffe returned to the parish and died there on St Silvester's day, 31 December, under the year 1384 [[cite:death]]. In 1415 the Council of Constance ordered his body and bones to be exhumed and cast out of church ground [[cite:constance]]; Foxe reports that this was carried out forty-one years after the burial and that the ashes were thrown into the river [[cite:bones]].
