The ministers' meeting held at Kettering on 2 October 1792 resolved to form a missionary society and chose Andrew Fuller as its secretary [[cite:founding]]. A meeting here on 10 January 1793 agreed to send Thomas and [[person:william-carey]] to the East Indies [[cite:january]]. See [[event:william-carey]].
