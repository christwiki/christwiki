Worms stands on the Rhine between Mainz and Speyer. Ekkehard of Aura calls it the city of the Vangiones, "now called Wormacia", and says that the charters of the concordat of 1122 were read out in a field beside the river because of the size of the crowd [[cite:ekkehard-1122]].
