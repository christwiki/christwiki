San Salvador is the capital of El Salvador in Central America. The United Nations truth commission records that on 24 March 1980 its archbishop, [[person:oscar-romero]], was assassinated while celebrating Mass in the chapel of the Hospital de la Divina Providencia in the city [[cite:case]]. See [[event:assassination-of-oscar-romero]].
