Brighton lies on the English Channel, south of London. [[person:hudson-taylor]] writes that he went there to stay with a friend when his health had broken down, and that on Sunday 25 June 1865 he left a crowded service, walked out alone on the sands, and there prayed for twenty-four workers for the provinces of China that had no missionary [[cite:beach]]. See [[event:hudson-taylor]].
