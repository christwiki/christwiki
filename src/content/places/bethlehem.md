Bethlehem lies in the Judean hills a few kilometres south of [[place:jerusalem]]. Genesis also calls it Ephrath and places Rachel's grave on the road to it [[bible:Gen 35:19]]. It is the home of Naomi and Boaz in the book of Ruth [[bible:Ruth 1:1-2; 2:4]] and of Jesse, whose youngest son [[person:david]] was anointed there by Samuel [[bible:1 Sam 16:1-13]]. Micah looked for a ruler of Israel to come from "Bethlehem Ephrathah" [[bible:Mic 5:2]].

Matthew and Luke both say that [[person:jesus]] was born at Bethlehem, and Matthew quotes Micah's words [[bible:Matt 2:1-6]] [[bible:Luke 2:4-7]]. Eusebius, who lived at the time, reports that [[person:helena]], the mother of the emperor [[person:constantine]], dedicated a church at the cave shown there as the place of the birth [[cite:eusebius]].
