Cîteaux lies in the plain of the Saône, south of Dijon. The Exordium Parvum describes the site in 1098 as a place in the diocese of Chalon so overgrown with wood and thorn that people seldom came there, and says that the monks thought it the better suited to their purpose for that reason [[cite:ep-3]]. The early documents call the house the New Monastery. The Charter of Charity requires every abbot of the order to come there once a year [[cite:cc]].
