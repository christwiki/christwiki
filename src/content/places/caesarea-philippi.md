The place was first called Panium or Paneas. [[person:josephus]] describes a deep cave there, under a mountain, from which springs of the [[place:jordan-river|Jordan]] rise. [[person:herod-the-great]] built a temple of white stone there in honour of [[person:augustus]] [[cite:panium]]. His son Philip, tetrarch of the lands north and east of the lake, built a city at the springs and named it Caesarea [[cite:philip]] [[cite:war]]. It was called Philip's Caesarea to distinguish it from [[place:caesarea-maritima]] on the coast.

Mark says that [[person:jesus]] went with his disciples "into the villages of Caesarea Philippi", and Matthew "into the parts of Caesarea Philippi"; neither says that he entered the city [[bible:Mark 8:27]] [[bible:Matt 16:13]]. It is there that [[person:peter]] says, "You are the Christ"; see [[event:peters-confession]]. The ancient site is at Banias, at the foot of Mount Hermon.
