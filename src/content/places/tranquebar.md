[[person:bartholomaus-ziegenbalg]] dates his first letter from India "at Tranquebar, on the Coast of Coromandel" and says the place lies under the eleventh degree north of the equator [[cite:arrival]]. His letters mention the governor of the settlement and its Danish church, called Sion, in which the missionaries preached before building their own church, Jerusalem, outside the town [[cite:progress]]. See [[event:tranquebar-mission]].
