The council that sat here from 1414 to 1418 declared that it had been rightly summoned to the city, that it could not be dissolved until the schism was healed and the church reformed, and that it could not be moved without good cause [[cite:session3]]. Peter of Mladoňovice, who was present, says that the session of 6 July 1415 was held in the city's great church, that [[person:jan-hus]] was burned in a meadow among the gardens outside the walls on the road to the castle of Gottlieben, and that his ashes were thrown into the Rhine nearby [[cite:burning]]. See [[event:council-of-constance]].
