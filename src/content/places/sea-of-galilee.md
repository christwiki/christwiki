The lake has several names. The Old Testament calls it the sea of Chinnereth [[bible:Num 34:11]]. Mark and Matthew call it the sea of Galilee [[bible:Mark 1:16]], Luke the lake of Gennesaret, after the district beside it [[bible:Luke 5:1]], and John the sea of Tiberias, after the city that [[person:herod-antipas]] built beside it [[bible:John 6:1]] [[cite:tiberias]]. [[person:josephus]] gives its length as 140 stadia and its breadth as 40, praises its fresh water and its fish, and notes that the [[place:jordan-river|Jordan]] runs through the middle of it [[cite:josephus]].

The Gospels place on its shores the call of the first disciples, who were fishermen [[bible:Mark 1:16-20]], and on its waters the stilling of a storm and the night on which the disciples saw [[person:jesus]] walking on the sea [[bible:Mark 4:35-41]] [[bible:Mark 6:45-52]]. John's Gospel sets an appearance of the risen Jesus on its shore [[bible:John 21:1]]. The marker stands at the middle of the lake.
