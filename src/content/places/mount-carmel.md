Carmel is a range of hills that meets the sea at the bay of Haifa and separates the coastal plain from the valley of Jezreel. Kings makes it the scene of the contest between [[person:elijah]] and the prophets of Baal, and has Elijah watch from its summit for the rain cloud rising out of the sea [[bible:1 Kings 18:19-20, 42-44]]; see [[event:elijah-on-carmel]]. [[person:elisha]] also stayed on the mountain [[bible:2 Kings 2:25; 4:25]]. The text does not say where on the ridge the contest took place, and the marker shows the mountain as a whole.
