Gregory the Great describes the town of Cassino as lying on the side of a high mountain that rises for three miles above it. Here stood an old shrine where the country people still sacrificed to Apollo. [[person:benedict-of-nursia]] broke the idol, burned the groves and built two oratories, of Saint Martin and of Saint John [[cite:dialogues]]. He and his sister [[person:scholastica]] were buried in the oratory of Saint John [[cite:burial]].
