The mission from Valaam reached Kodiak in the autumn of 1794; its head wrote the next May that he had lived there since 24 September and that a church had been built [[cite:arrival]]. Spruce Island, where [[person:herman-of-alaska]] lived and died, lies a short distance from it [[cite:spruce]]. The coordinates given are those of the town and harbour of Kodiak. See [[event:russian-mission-to-alaska]].
