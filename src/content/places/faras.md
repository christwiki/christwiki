Faras lay on the Nile in the far north of what is now Sudan. The National Museum in Warsaw, which holds paintings from it, describes it as a large urban centre of the medieval kingdom of Nobadia, known as Pachoras when its first cathedral was built in the seventh century, and says that Polish archaeologists working in the campaign to save the monuments of Nubia found the cathedral of the eighth century there before the valley was flooded [[cite:museum]]. John of Ephesus, who tells of the conversion of the Nobadae, does not name their king's residence in the chapters cited in [[event:conversion-of-nubia]].
