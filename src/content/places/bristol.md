[[person:george-fox]] writes that he and [[person:margaret-fell]] took each other in marriage in the Friends' meeting-house at Broadmead in Bristol; the editor of his Journal gives the year, 1669, from the register of Bristol Friends [[cite:marriage]].

[[person:john-wesley]] reached Bristol on 31 March 1739 and found [[person:george-whitefield]] preaching in the fields. On 2 April he preached "in the highways" himself, from a little eminence in a ground adjoining the city, and in May he took possession of a piece of ground in the Horsefair, where the first stone of a room for the societies of Nicholas Street and Baldwin Street was laid on the twelfth [[cite:wesley]].
