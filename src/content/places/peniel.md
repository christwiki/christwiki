Genesis sets Peniel at the ford of the Jabbok, the modern Zarqa river in Jordan [[bible:Gen 32:22]]. It explains the name by Jacob's words, "I have seen God face to face" [[bible:Gen 32:30]]. Later books spell it Penuel. A town of that name with a tower stood near Succoth in the days of [[person:gideon]], who pulled the tower down [[bible:Judg 8:8-9]] [[bible:Judg 8:17]], and [[person:jeroboam-i]] fortified it [[bible:1 Kgs 12:25]]. No site has been identified with certainty. The pin stands at Tulul adh-Dhahab, twin mounds on the lower Zarqa where the town is most often sought, and marks the general area only.
