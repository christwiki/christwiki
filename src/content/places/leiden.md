[[person:william-bradford]] calls Leiden "a fair & bewtifull citie", made famous by its university, though without the sea trade of [[place:amsterdam]]; his congregation settled there under [[person:john-robinson]] and stayed, he says, for nearly twelve years before part of it left for America [[cite:bradford]]. [[person:jacobus-arminius]] speaks of the doctrine of predestination taught "in the University of Leyden" and of a disputation he held before his own class there [[cite:arminius]]. See [[event:pilgrims-at-plymouth]] and [[event:synod-of-dort]].
