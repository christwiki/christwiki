In 751 [[person:boniface]] wrote to Pope Zacharias that he had settled monks under the Rule of Benedict in a wooded place in a great wilderness, on land obtained with the help of the Frankish ruler Carloman, and that he wished to be buried there. The pope's charter of the same year names the place, Buchonia on the bank of the river Fulda, and puts the monastery under the Roman see alone [[cite:letter]]. After his death in Frisia the monks brought his body there [[cite:burial]]. It was to Baugulf, abbot of Fulda, that Charlemagne addressed his letter on the cultivation of learning [[cite:baugulf]].
