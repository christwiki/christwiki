Before he left Prague in 1414, [[person:jan-hus]] had notices posted in Latin, Czech and German on the doors of its cathedral, parish churches and monasteries, announcing that he was going to Constance to give an account of his faith and inviting anyone who charged him with error to meet him there [[cite:notices]]. John Paul II called him one of the most famous of the scholars to come from the University of Prague [[cite:jp2]]. See [[event:council-of-constance]].
