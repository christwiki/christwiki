Valladolid lies on the plateau of Old Castile in north-western Spain. [[person:bartolome-de-las-casas]] says that the New Laws of 1542 were made after many conferences and debates among learned men assembled in the town [[cite:debates]], and the additions to those laws are dated there on 4 June 1543 [[cite:laws]]. The title page of the tract he printed in 1552 records that the king ordered theologians and jurists to assemble at Valladolid in 1550 to hear his dispute with [[person:juan-gines-de-sepulveda]] [[cite:dispute]]. See [[event:sublimis-deus]].
