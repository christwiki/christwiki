The Life of [[person:sergius-of-radonezh]] describes the place as it was when he and his brother came to it: deep forest with a spring, no road, no village or house nearby [[cite:founding]]. By the time the Life was written it could speak of the house as a great monastery, the lavra in Radonezh [[cite:lavra]]. The modern town bears his name. See [[event:sergius-of-radonezh]].
