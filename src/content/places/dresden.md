Dresden lies on the river Elbe in Saxony. The elector Augustus of Saxony called the theologians to Torgau in 1576 whose work became the Formula of Concord, and his name stands second among the signers of the preface to the Book of Concord [[cite:preface]]. F. Bente's description of the first edition, cited in the dating note of [[event:book-of-concord]], reports the imprint "Dresden MDLXXX" on its title page.
