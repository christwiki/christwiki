Nicopolis ad Istrum lay in the Roman province of Moesia, south of the Danube, in what is now northern Bulgaria. Philostorgius says that the emperor settled the Christian Goths who followed [[person:ulfilas]] in Moesia [[cite:philostorgius]]. Jordanes, in the sixth century, is more exact: the "Lesser Goths", whose bishop Ulfilas had been, were living in the district of Nicopolis at the foot of Mount Haemus [[cite:jordanes]]. No source says that the translation of the Bible was made in the city itself; it is given here as the centre of the community for which Ulfilas worked. See [[event:ulfilas]].
