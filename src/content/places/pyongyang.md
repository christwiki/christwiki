Pyongyang, written Pyeng Yang by the missionaries of the time, is today the capital of North Korea. In January 1907 its four Presbyterian churches met together in the Central Church, which held about fifteen hundred people [[cite:lee-33]]; see [[event:pyongyang-revival]]. The Presbytery of the Presbyterian Church in Korea was constituted in the same building on 17 September 1907 [[cite:presbytery]].
