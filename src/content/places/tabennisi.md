Tabennisi lay in the Thebaid, the southern part of Egypt. The Life of [[person:pachomius]] calls it a village in which no one was living when he came there to pray and was told to build a monastery [[cite:life]]. Palladius, writing about 420, knew it as the place that gave the Pachomian monks their name [[cite:palladius]]. The site has not been identified on the ground; the coordinates are those given for it in the Pleiades gazetteer of ancient places, on the Nile between modern Nag Hammadi and Qena. See [[event:pachomius]].
