Lystra was a town of Lycaonia to which [[person:paul]] and [[person:barnabas]] fled from Iconium. Acts says that after Paul healed a lame man the crowd, speaking Lycaonian, hailed the two as Zeus and Hermes (Jupiter and Mercury in some English versions) and tried to offer sacrifice to them. Then, Acts says, opponents from [[place:pisidian-antioch|Antioch]] and Iconium turned the crowd, and Paul was stoned and left for dead outside the town [[bible:Acts 14:6-20]]. The two came back to strengthen the disciples there [[bible:Acts 14:21-22]]. On his next journey Paul found at Lystra the disciple [[person:timothy]], who became his companion [[bible:Acts 16:1-3]].
