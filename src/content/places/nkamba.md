Nkamba lies in the west of the Democratic Republic of the Congo, in the country between Kinshasa and the sea. In 1921 [[person:simon-kimbangu]] established himself there, in his own village, and the sick were brought to him from every direction [[cite:lerrigo-272]]. A Kimbanguist account published in 2004 says that Kimbanguism was born at Nkamba on 6 April 1921 [[cite:church]]. See [[event:simon-kimbangu]].
