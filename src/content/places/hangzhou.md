The history of the China Inland Mission published in the 1890s describes Hangzhou as the capital of the province of Zhejiang, about a hundred and eighty miles south-west of Shanghai, a city of nearly half a million people then recovering from the Taiping rebellion. Three missionary families of other societies were living there, the first having begun work in 1864 [[cite:city]]. [[person:hudson-taylor]] and the party that had sailed in the *Lammermuir* reached the city at the end of November 1866 and were lent a house [[cite:arrival]]. See [[event:hudson-taylor]].
