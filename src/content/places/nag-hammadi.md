The first report of the find, in 1948, says only that the books came from the region of Nag Hammadi, from a spot at the foot of a hill, and withholds the name of the place [[cite:find]]. James M. Robinson, from interviews in the 1970s, identified the spot as the foot of the cliff of Jabal al-Tarif, and says that the finder lived at al-Qasr, the ancient Chenoboskia, where [[person:pachomius]] began his life as a Christian [[cite:robinson]] [[cite:photo-cliff]]. The coordinates given are those of the modern town. See [[event:nag-hammadi-library]].
