The site lies in the plain north-east of Shumen, a short distance from the modern town that has taken its name. The Byzantine historian Leo the Deacon, writing at the end of the tenth century, names "Pliskouba" among the places that the emperor John Tzimiskes took on his march from Preslav to Dorystolon [[cite:leo]].

It is commonly described as the residence of the Bulgar rulers of the ninth century, and for that reason the timeline places [[event:conversion-of-bulgaria|the baptism of Boris]] here. None of the sources read for that entry names the place. The Annals of St Bertin speak only of the king's palace and of the gates of a city [[cite:palace]].
