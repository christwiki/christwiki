The edict that accompanied the General Admonition of 789 is dated at the public palace of Aachen [[cite:date]]. Einhard says that [[person:charlemagne]] built a basilica there, dedicated to the Mother of God, with columns and marbles brought from [[place:rome]] and [[place:ravenna]], and worshipped in it morning and evening [[cite:church]] [[cite:burial]]. He died at Aachen and was buried in that church [[cite:burial]].
