Gniezno lies in western Poland, north-east of Poznań. Thietmar of Merseburg tells how the emperor Otto III was led there by Mieszko's son Boleslav, entered barefoot, prayed to the martyr Adalbert and founded an archbishopric, with bishops at Kolberg, Kraków and Wrocław under it [[cite:thietmar]]. The Polish court chronicle says that Boleslav had bought Adalbert's body from the Prussians for its weight in gold and laid it at Gniezno, which the chronicler calls a metropolis [[cite:gallus]].

The "city called Schinesghe" that a Roman note of the 1080s records as given to Saint Peter under Pope John XV is read as Gniezno; one manuscript of the note has "Schinesne of the province of the Poles" [[cite:dagome]]. The timeline sets [[event:baptism-of-poland|the baptism of Mieszko]] here by convention; no source read for that entry names the place of the baptism.
