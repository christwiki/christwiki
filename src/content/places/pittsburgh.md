Duquesne University stands in Pittsburgh. In February 1967 about twenty-five of its students made a retreat at The Ark and The Dove, a retreat house on the outskirts of the city, and one of them has described what happened there [[cite:mansfield]]. See [[event:charismatic-renewal]].
