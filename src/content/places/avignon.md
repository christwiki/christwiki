Avignon stands on the Rhône in the south of France. The Florentine chronicler Giovanni Villani writes of "Avignon, where was the Pope's court" in 1310 and says that John XXII, elected pope in 1316, had been bishop of Avignon [[cite:court]]. Petrarch, who lived there, set it "on the shores of the wild Rhone" and called it the Babylon of the West [[cite:petrarch]]. See [[event:avignon-papacy]].
