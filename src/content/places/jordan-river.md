The Jordan flows south through the Sea of Galilee and down a deep valley to the Dead Sea. In Joshua the tribes cross it on dry ground opposite [[place:jericho]] to enter the land [[bible:Josh 3:14-17]]. Kings sets the parting of [[person:elijah]] and [[person:elisha]] on its banks, with the water dividing for each of them in turn [[bible:2 Kings 2:6-14]]; see [[event:elisha-succeeds-elijah]]. Elisha later sent Naaman to wash in it [[bible:2 Kings 5:10-14]]. In the Gospels John baptises in the Jordan, and Jesus comes there from Galilee to be baptised [[bible:Matt 3:13-17]]. The marker stands at the middle of the river's course; the crossings in Joshua and Kings are placed in its lowest stretch, near Jericho.
