Judges tells how the tribe of Dan seized the town of Laish, renamed it and set up a shrine there [[bible:Judg 18:27-31]]. "From Dan to Beersheba" became the phrase for the whole land [[bible:1 Sam 3:20]]. [[person:jeroboam-i]] placed one of his two golden calves at Dan [[bible:1 Kings 12:28-30]]; see [[event:kingdom-divides]]. The calf was still there in the time of [[person:jehu]] [[bible:2 Kings 10:29]], and Amos speaks against those who swear by the god of Dan [[bible:Amos 8:14]]. The site is the mound of Tel Dan, where fragments of an Aramean king's victory stele were found [[cite:stele]]; see [[event:tel-dan-stele]].
