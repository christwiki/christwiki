The historian of the Church Missionary Society, writing in 1899, says that the Egba, scattered by slave raids, had gathered again round a rock called Olumo and named their new town Abeokuta, "Under-stone"; that freed Egba from Sierra Leone settled there and asked for missionaries; and that Henry Townsend and [[person:samuel-ajayi-crowther]] entered the town on 3 August 1846 [[cite:founding]]. Crowther's journal, quoted by his biographer, tells how his mother, from whom he had been taken twenty-five years before, came to him there [[cite:mother]]. See [[event:samuel-ajayi-crowther]].
