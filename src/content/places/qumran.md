Qumran lies near the north-west corner of the Dead Sea, about fourteen kilometres south of [[place:jericho]]. The first scrolls came from a cave about a kilometre north of Wadi Qumran in 1947. By 1956 written remains had been found in eleven caves along the cliffs, and the site itself had been excavated [[cite:caves]]. Pliny the Elder says that Essenes lived on the western side of the Dead Sea, with En-gedi "below" them [[cite:pliny]], and most scholars connect that notice, the ruins and the scrolls.
