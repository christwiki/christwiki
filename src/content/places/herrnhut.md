Herrnhut lies in Upper Lusatia, on the slope of the Hutberg beside the road between Löbau and Zittau. The Moravian Church's history says that the first tree for its first house was felled on 17 June 1722, and that the count's steward gave the place its name, which it explains as "the Lord's Watch" [[cite:founding]]. The same history calls it the firstling of all the church's congregation places [[cite:preface]]. See [[event:moravian-renewal]].
