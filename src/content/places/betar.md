Betar lay at the village of Battir, south-west of [[place:jerusalem]]. Eusebius, following Aristo of Pella, calls it "Bithara", a very strong fortress not far from Jerusalem, where the war was at its height in Hadrian's eighteenth year and where the rebels were worn down by hunger and thirst [[cite:eusebius]]. The Mishnah counts its capture among the disasters remembered on the ninth of Av [[cite:mishnah]]. The coordinates are those of the ruin identified with it, from the Pleiades gazetteer.
