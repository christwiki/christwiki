This is the city in Pennsylvania, not the Philadelphia of Asia Minor addressed in the book of Revelation [[bible:Rev 3:7]]. [[person:richard-allen]] writes that he was born in 1760 a slave of Benjamin Chew of Philadelphia [[cite:allen-birth]]; he opened Bethel church on his own lot here in 1794 [[cite:bethel]], and was received as bishop by the general convention of the African Methodist Episcopal Church held here in April 1816 [[cite:convention]]. [[person:thomas-jefferson]] dated from Philadelphia the circular of 1 March 1792 that sent the ratified amendments to the Constitution to the state governors [[cite:circular]]. See [[event:richard-allen-and-the-ame]] and [[event:religious-freedom-in-america]].
