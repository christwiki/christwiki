Hippo Regius was a harbour town on the coast of Roman Africa, at modern Annaba in Algeria. [[person:augustine]] was ordained presbyter there under its bishop Valerius, founded a monastery within the church, and was then made bishop [[cite:possidius]]. The bishops of the African provinces met in council in its Basilica of Peace on 8 October 393 [[cite:council]]; see [[event:councils-of-hippo-and-carthage]]. In 430 the Vandals laid siege to the city. Augustine died in the third month of the siege, and Possidius adds that Hippo was afterwards abandoned by its people and burned [[cite:siege]].
