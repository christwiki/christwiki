Kodungallur lies on the Malabar coast of south-west India, north of Kochi. A chronology published by an eparchy of the Syro-Malabar Church names it, under the older form Cranganore, as the place where the apostle [[person:thomas]] landed in AD 52 [[cite:tradition]]. The earlier accounts of Thomas cited in this wiki do not name the town, and the place is marked on the map as the site of a tradition; see [[event:thomas-and-india|Thomas and India]].
