[[person:peter-abelard]] came to Paris as a student because, he says, dialectic flourished there above all, and later taught on the Mont Sainte-Geneviève, then outside the city [[cite:hc-2]]. In 1200 King Philip II gave its students the protection of the church courts against his own provost [[cite:philip]]. The statutes of 1215 are addressed to all the masters and scholars at Paris [[cite:courcon]], and in 1231 Pope [[person:gregory-ix]] called the city "the mother of the sciences" [[cite:parens]].
