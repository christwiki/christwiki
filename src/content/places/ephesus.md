Ephesus was a port on the west coast of Asia Minor, in the Roman province of Asia. [[person:paul]] called there briefly at the end of his second journey and left [[person:priscilla]] and [[person:aquila]] in the city [[bible:Acts 18:19-21]]. He came back and stayed more than two years, teaching first in the synagogue and then in the lecture hall of Tyrannus, so that, Acts says, all Asia heard the word [[bible:Acts 19:1, 8-10]]. The silversmiths who made shrines of Artemis raised a riot against him, and the crowd shouted for about two hours, "Great is Artemis of the Ephesians!" [[bible:Acts 19:23-34]]. He wrote 1 Corinthians from Ephesus [[bible:1 Cor 16:8]] and later took leave of its elders at Miletus [[bible:Acts 20:17]]. See [[event:third-missionary-journey]].

A letter in Paul's name is addressed to the saints at Ephesus, 1 Timothy places [[person:timothy|Timothy]] there, and the first of the seven messages in Revelation is to its church [[bible:Eph 1:1]] [[bible:1 Tim 1:3]] [[bible:Rev 2:1]]. In 431 the third general council met in the city; see [[event:council-of-ephesus]].
