Swarthmoor Hall stands just outside Ulverston. [[person:george-fox]] writes that he came "to Swarthmore to Judge Fell's" in 1652, that [[person:margaret-fell]] and her household were convinced there, and that the judge allowed a meeting to be kept in his house, which continued until 1690, when a meeting-house was built nearby [[cite:north]]. See [[event:george-fox-and-the-quakers]].
