Mount Tabor rises at the eastern end of the Jezreel valley in Lower Galilee, about 18 kilometres west of the Sea of Galilee. Joshua names it on the border of the tribe of Issachar [[bible:Josh 19:22]]. [[person:deborah]] told Barak to gather ten thousand men on it, and from it he went down against Sisera's chariots [[bible:Judg 4:6, 12-14]]. A psalm and Jeremiah use it as a landmark, beside Hermon and Carmel [[bible:Ps 89:12]] [[bible:Jer 46:18]].

The Gospels say only that Jesus was transfigured on "a high mountain" [[bible:Matt 17:1-2]]. Cyril of Jerusalem, about AD 350, speaks of the "Transfiguration on Mount Tabor" [[cite:cyril]]. The identification rests on tradition of this kind. The New Testament does not name the place.
