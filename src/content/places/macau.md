Diego de Pantoja, writing in 1602, describes Macau as a city of the Portuguese adjoining the mainland of China, with a college of the Society of Jesus; he waited there for a passage to Japan before he was sent into China instead, and he puts the Jesuit house at Nanjing three hundred leagues from it [[cite:college]].
