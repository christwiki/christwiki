[[person:geert-groote]] preached at Zwolle among the chief towns of the diocese of Utrecht [[cite:preached]]. The chronicle of Mount St Agnes places Windesheim a mile south of the town, and dates the consecration of its first church to 1387 [[cite:windesheim]]. The first four brothers of Mount St Agnes were invested in 1398, and John of Kempen became its prior in 1399 [[cite:agnes]]. [[person:thomas-a-kempis]] dated his manuscript of 1441 at Mount St Agnes near Zwolle (*in monte sancte Agnetis prope Zwollis*) [[cite:autograph]]. See [[event:devotio-moderna]].
