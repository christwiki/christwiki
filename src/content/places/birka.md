Rimbert calls Birka a port of the Swedes, with a king's prefect and its own assembly. There [[person:ansgar]] was given leave to preach, found Christian captives, and baptized the prefect Herigar, who built a church on his own land [[cite:first]]. On his second visit an assembly held in the town agreed that priests might remain [[cite:assembly]]. Its site is on the island of Björkö in Lake Mälaren, west of Stockholm.
