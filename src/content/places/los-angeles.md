Los Angeles lies on the Pacific coast of southern California. In 1906 prayer meetings in a house on Bonnie Brae Street moved to a building on Azusa Street, an old Methodist church partly converted into a tenement, and soon, the mission's paper says, the building could not hold the people who came [[cite:af1-p1]]. See [[event:azusa-street-revival]].
