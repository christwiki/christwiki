The acts of the synod call the place "the Town and Kingdom of Diamper", subject to the king of Cochin, and set the synod in its church of All Saints [[cite:title]]. The summons says that it was chosen as a convenient place near the middle of the diocese [[cite:summons]]. The churches of Kerala use the Malayalam name: a Syro-Malabar chronology speaks of "the Synod of Diamper (Udayamperoor)" [[cite:name]]. See [[event:synod-of-diamper]].
