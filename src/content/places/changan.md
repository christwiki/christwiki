Chang'an was the capital of the Tang emperors. The Xi'an Stele says that Aluoben was received here by [[person:taizong]] in 635 [[cite:arrival]], and that after the edict of 638 a monastery was built for his teaching in the city's Yining ward [[cite:edict]]. The Syriac lines on the stele speak of "Cumdan the royal city" [[cite:syriac]]. The stele itself is kept in the Beilin Museum in modern Xi'an.
