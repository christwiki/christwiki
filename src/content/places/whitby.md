Bede calls the place Streanaeshalch and explains the name as "the Bay of the Lighthouse" [[cite:synod]]. The name Whitby is later; the translator of the edition cited here identifies the two. [[person:hilda-of-whitby|Hilda]] founded the monastery on an estate of ten households about two years after King [[person:oswiu]]'s victory of 655, and Oswiu, his queen Eanfled and their daughter Elfled, who later became abbess, were buried in its church of St Peter [[cite:founding]].
