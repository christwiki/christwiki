Pisa lies near the mouth of the Arno in Tuscany. In 1409 the cardinals called a council here, which declared that their united college had the right to summon it and cited both claimants to hear its sentence [[cite:pisa]]. Its pope, Alexander V, and his successor John XXIII made a third line beside those of Rome and Avignon [[cite:intro]]; John XXIII appealed to the decrees of Pisa when he summoned the Council of Constance [[cite:convocation]]. See [[event:western-schism]].
