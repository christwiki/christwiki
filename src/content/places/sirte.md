The Dicastery for Promoting Christian Unity records that the twenty-one men commemorated as the martyrs of Libya were kidnapped in the city of Sirte in December 2014 and January 2015, and were beheaded on a beach in the western part of the city on 15 February 2015; their remains were found in a mass grave on the Libyan coast in 2017 [[cite:note]]. See [[event:martyrs-of-libya]].
