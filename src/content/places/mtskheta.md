Mtskheta lies where the Aragvi joins the Kura (Mtkvari), about twenty kilometres north of Tbilisi. *The Conversion of Kartli* calls it "a great city, the royal residence", and says that [[person:nino]] lived outside its walls and that the king built a church in the royal garden [[cite:kartli]]. The *Life of Nino* tells how the central pillar of that church, cut from a cedar in the garden, was set in place by a miracle and afterwards honoured as a source of healing [[cite:life-pillar]]. See [[event:conversion-of-georgia]].
