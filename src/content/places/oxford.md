[[person:gregory-xi]] addressed his bull of 1377 against [[person:john-wycliffe]] to the chancellor and university of Oxford. He reminded them of the privileges the apostolic see had granted them and of their learning in the Scriptures, and reproached them for allowing "tares" to grow among the wheat and ordering them to arrest him [[cite:bull]]. The Council of Constance later noted that the masters of Oxford had themselves examined Wycliffe's books and drawn 260 articles from them for condemnation [[cite:constance]].
