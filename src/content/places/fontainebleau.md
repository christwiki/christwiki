Fontainebleau lies about 55 kilometres south-east of Paris. The edict by which [[person:louis-xiv]] revoked the Edict of Nantes closes "Given at Fontainebleau in the month of October, in the year of grace 1685" [[cite:clause]]. See [[event:revocation-of-the-edict-of-nantes]].
