Zurich was a self-governing city of the Swiss Confederation, ruled by a burgomaster and two councils. In January 1523 those councils heard [[person:huldrych-zwingli]], preacher at the Great Minster, defend his teaching and decreed that he should continue [[cite:decree]]. In January 1527 the city's court sentenced [[person:felix-manz]] to be drowned for baptising adults [[cite:manz]]. See [[event:zwingli-in-zurich]] and [[event:anabaptists]].
