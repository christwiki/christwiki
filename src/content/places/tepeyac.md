Tepeyac is a small hill at the northern edge of Mexico City. The *Nican Mopohua* says that the Virgin Mary appeared at Tepeyac, which it calls a little hill and says is now known as Guadalupe [[cite:account]]. It says that her shrine was built there and that the image on [[person:juan-diego]]'s cloak is kept in it [[cite:shrine]]. [[person:john-paul-ii]] canonized Juan Diego in the basilica of Our Lady of Guadalupe on the site in 2002 and called it the Marian heart of Mexico and of America [[cite:basilica]]. See [[event:our-lady-of-guadalupe]].
