Dayton lies in Rhea County, Tennessee. The indictment read to the jury in July 1925 charged John Thomas Scopes, a teacher in the public school of Dayton, with teaching that man is descended from a lower order of animals [[cite:scopes-309]]. He was found guilty and fined [[cite:scopes-313]]. See [[event:the-fundamentals]].
