Clermont, now part of Clermont-Ferrand, was the bishop's city of Auvergne. Fulcher of Chartres says that [[person:urban-ii]] called a council there, that some 310 bishops and abbots attended, and that it met in November [[cite:fulcher-council]] [[cite:fulcher-date]]. Urban himself refers to it simply as the council of Auvergne [[cite:letter]].
