The wilderness of Judah is the eastern slope of the Judean hills, which falls from the ridge near [[place:jerusalem]], [[place:bethlehem]] and [[place:hebron]] to the Dead Sea and the lower [[place:jordan-river|Jordan]] valley. It receives little rain and has few settlements. Joshua lists six towns in it, among them En Gedi [[bible:Josh 15:61-62]], and [[person:david]] took refuge there from Saul [[bible:1 Sam 24:1-2]]. [[place:qumran]] lies on its eastern edge.

Matthew says that [[person:john-the-baptist]] preached "in the wilderness of Judea" [[bible:Matt 3:1]], and the first three Gospels tell that [[person:jesus]] spent forty days in the wilderness after his baptism [[bible:Mark 1:12-13]] [[bible:Matt 4:1-2]]. They name no particular spot, so the marker is set near the middle of the region and shows only its general position.
