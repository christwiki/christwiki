According to the Primary Chronicle, [[person:vladimir-the-great]] returned from [[place:chersonesus]] to Kyiv, had the image of Perun dragged down to the Dnieper and summoned the whole city to the river to be baptized. He built a church of St Basil on the hill where the idols had stood [[cite:baptism]], and then a church of the Mother of God, built by craftsmen from Greece, to which he gave a tenth of his revenues [[cite:church]]. See [[event:baptism-of-rus]].
