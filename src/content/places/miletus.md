Miletus was a harbour city of Ionia near the mouth of the river Maeander, some forty-five kilometres south of [[place:ephesus]]. On his way to Jerusalem [[person:paul]] chose to sail past Ephesus and put in here instead. He sent for the elders of the Ephesian church, reminded them of his years among them, warned them of trouble to come and told them that they would not see him again [[bible:Acts 20:15-38]]. The second letter to Timothy says that Paul left his companion Trophimus at Miletus ill [[bible:2 Tim 4:20]]. The harbour has long since silted up, and the ruins now lie some kilometres inland.
