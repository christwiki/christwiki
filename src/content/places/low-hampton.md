[[person:william-miller]] writes that at the close of the war in which he had served he moved to "my present residence in Low Hampton, N. Y.", and that it was there, with leisure for reading, that he came to believe the Bible and to study its prophecies [[cite:home]]. His first public lecture, he says, was given at Dresden, about sixteen miles from his residence [[cite:lecture]]. See [[event:great-disappointment]].
