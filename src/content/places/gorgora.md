The Ethiopian chronicle says that [[person:susenyos]] established his residence at Gorgora in the sixth year of his reign [[cite:residence]]. The Jesuit [[person:pedro-paez]] died there in 1622 [[cite:paez]], the patriarch [[person:afonso-mendes]] conferred ordinations there on his way to the court [[cite:mendes]], and Jerónimo Lobo tells of coming to Gorgora for the missionaries' yearly meeting and finding the emperor with them [[cite:lobo]]. The coordinates are those of the present town of Gorgora on the north shore of Lake Tana; the sources cited do not fix the site of the seventeenth-century residence more closely. See [[event:jesuits-in-ethiopia]].
