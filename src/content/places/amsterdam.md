Amsterdam is a port in the Dutch province of Holland. [[person:william-bradford]] writes that English people persecuted for their religion had gone to live there, in a country where they heard there was freedom of religion for all, and that his own congregation spent about a year in the city before moving to [[place:leiden]]; he notes that it had the sea trade that Leiden lacked [[cite:bradford]]. The confession issued by [[person:thomas-helwys]] and his party in 1611 is titled as the declaration of "English people remaining at Amsterdam in Holland" [[cite:declaration]], and Helwys wrote the next year against English leaders who had fled to foreign countries to save their lives [[cite:flight]].
