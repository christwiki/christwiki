Genesis calls the homeland of Terah's family "Ur of the Chaldees" [[bible:Gen 11:28]] [[bible:Gen 11:31]], and later texts recall that God brought Abraham out from there [[bible:Gen 15:7]] [[bible:Neh 9:7]]. [[person:stephen]] speaks of Mesopotamia and "the land of the Chaldaeans" [[bible:Acts 7:2-4]]. Most scholars identify the place with the great Sumerian city excavated at Tell el-Muqayyar on the lower Euphrates (C. Leonard Woolley, *Ur of the Chaldees*, 1929). Others argue for a northern Ur, among them Cyrus H. Gordon, "Abraham and the Merchants of Ura", *Journal of Near Eastern Studies* 17 (1958). They point out that Abraham later calls the region of [[place:haran]] "my country" when he sends there for a wife for Isaac [[bible:Gen 24:4]] [[bible:Gen 24:10]]. The text itself does not settle the question.
