Warsaw stands on the Vistula in central Poland. [[person:john-paul-ii]] began his pilgrimage of June 1979 with a Mass in Victory Square, and in his homily called the city the capital of Poland and recalled its rising of 1944, in which it was buried under its own ruins [[cite:homily]]. See [[event:john-paul-ii-in-poland]].
