Frankenhausen lies in northern Thuringia. The printed confession of [[person:thomas-muntzer]], dated 16 May 1525, describes him as found among "the rebellious band at Frankenhausen", and the library that holds the pamphlet notes that it was taken after his capture in the battle there [[cite:confession]]. See [[event:peasants-war]].
