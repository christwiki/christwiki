Brest stands on the river Bug; John Paul II's letter of 1995 calls it Brest-Litovsk and dates the proclamation of the union there to 16 October 1596 [[cite:jp2]]. Clement VIII, writing in January 1597, says that the bishops and magnates who reported the synod to him wrote "from Lithuanian Brest" [[cite:brief-1597]]. The town was also the seat of a bishop: Hypatius Potij, one of the two envoys to Rome, signs as bishop of Volodymyr and Brest, and the articles of 1595 name Brest among the towns with a church brotherhood [[cite:articles]].
