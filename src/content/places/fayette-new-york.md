Fayette is a town in Seneca County, New York. [[person:joseph-smith]]'s manuscript history says that six men met at the house of Peter Whitmer on 6 April 1830 to organize a church, and heads the revelation received at that meeting "Given at Fayette, Seneca Co N Y" [[cite:organized]]. His printed sketch of 1842 places the same event in the town of Manchester, in Ontario County [[cite:manchester]]. The coordinates given are those of the town, not of the Whitmer farm. See [[event:latter-day-saints]].
