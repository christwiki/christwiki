The trial of [[person:joan-of-arc]] opened on 21 February 1431 in the chapel royal of the castle of Rouen, where she was also held prisoner [[cite:castle]]. On 24 May she was brought to a public assembly in the cemetery of the abbey of Saint-Ouen, and on 30 May to the Old Market, near the church of Saint-Sauveur, where the final sentence was read [[cite:places]]. The judges of 1456 gave their sentence in the archbishop's palace and ordered it published that day in the square of Saint-Ouen and the next day in the Old Market, with a cross to be set up there in her memory [[cite:nullity]].
