Memmingen is a town in Swabia, in the south-west of modern Bavaria. The Twelve Articles of 1525 do not say where they were written; the editors of the text cited here attribute them to Sebastian Lotzer, a furrier of Memmingen, with a preface by the town's preacher Christoph Schappeler [[cite:articles]]. Memmingen is named among the fourteen imperial cities whose envoys adhered to the protest at [[place:speyer]] in 1529 [[cite:cities]]. See [[event:peasants-war]] and [[event:protestation-at-speyer]].
