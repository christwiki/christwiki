Chalcedon stood on the Asian side of the Bosporus, in the Roman province of Bithynia. Evagrius describes the shrine of the martyr Euphemia where the council met: a group of three buildings on rising ground about two stadia from the water, with [[place:constantinople]] in view directly opposite [[cite:evagrius]]. At the council's sixth session [[person:marcian]] gave the city the title of metropolis in honour of the martyr and of the bishops, without taking anything from the rights of Nicomedia [[cite:session-6]]. The site is now the Kadıköy district of Istanbul.
