Eusebius calls Lyon and Vienne, both on the Rhône, the chief and most famous cities of Gaul [[cite:gaul]]. A festival held there drew crowds "from all nations", and it was at that festival that the Christians condemned in 177 were killed in the amphitheatre [[cite:festival]]. Two of those named in the church's letter, Attalus of Pergamum and the physician Alexander, a Phrygian, came from Asia Minor [[cite:asians]], and the letter was addressed to the churches of Asia and Phrygia [[cite:gaul]]. After the persecution [[person:irenaeus]] became bishop and wrote *Against Heresies* [[cite:irenaeus]].
