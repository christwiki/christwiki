Venice stands on islands in a lagoon at the northern end of the Adriatic Sea. In the Middle Ages it was a republic governed by a doge and councils, and a naval power. The French envoys who came to hire ships for the Fourth Crusade said they knew of no other people with such power at sea; the doge put their request to his council and then to the people assembled in the church of St Mark [[cite:treaty]]. After the capture of [[place:constantinople]] the booty brought in was divided in equal parts between the Franks and the Venetians [[cite:spoil]]. See [[event:sack-of-constantinople]] and [[person:enrico-dandolo]].
