Dibon appears in an old song about the fall of Moab [[bible:Num 21:30]] and in the lists of towns settled by Gad and Reuben [[bible:Num 32:34; Josh 13:9, 17]]. In the ninth century BC it was Moabite: [[person:mesha]] calls himself "the Dibonite" and describes the walls, gates, palace and cisterns he built in Qarhoh, apparently a quarter of the town [[cite:stele]]. Isaiah and Jeremiah name it in their oracles against Moab [[bible:Isa 15:2; Jer 48:18, 22]]. The stele was found among its ruins, at the modern village of Dhiban; see [[event:mesha-stele]].
