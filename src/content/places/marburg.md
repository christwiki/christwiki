Marburg is a town on the Lahn in Hesse. In October 1529 Landgrave [[person:philip-of-hesse]] received the reformers at his court here; the debate was held in the prince's chamber [[cite:osiander]], and the articles that came of it are headed as written and signed at Marburg [[cite:articles]]. See [[event:marburg-colloquy]].
