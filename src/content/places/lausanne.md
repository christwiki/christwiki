Lausanne stands on the north shore of Lake Geneva. From 16 to 25 July 1974 it was the meeting place of the International Congress on World Evangelization, attended by 2,700 participants from more than 150 nations [[cite:stott-intro]]. Its statement is the Lausanne Covenant. See [[event:lausanne-congress]].
