Kalhu stood on the east bank of the Tigris, at the site now called Nimrud. Ashurnasirpal II made it the capital of Assyria in the ninth century BC, and his son [[person:shalmaneser-iii]] added a great arsenal and completed the temple tower. Genesis lists it, as Calah, among the cities of Assyria [[bible:Gen 10:11-12]].

Austen Henry Layard found the Black Obelisk here in 1846. Its reliefs show the tribute of [[person:jehu]] of Israel being brought before the Assyrian king [[cite:obelisk]]; see [[event:jehu-pays-tribute]]. The tribute itself was received in the west, during the campaign of 841 BC, and not at Kalhu.
