Smyrna stood at the head of a gulf on the west coast of Asia Minor, about 55 kilometres north of Ephesus. The book of Revelation addresses one of its seven letters to the church there and warns it of coming imprisonment [[bible:Rev 2:8-11]]. [[person:ignatius-of-antioch]] wrote four of his letters from the city when [[person:polycarp]] was its bishop [[cite:ignatius]] [[cite:eusebius]], and the church of Smyrna later sent out the account of Polycarp's death in its stadium [[cite:martyrdom]]. [[person:irenaeus]] of Lyon says that as a boy he saw Polycarp "in lower Asia" [[cite:florinus]].
