The acts of the Synod of Diamper speak both of a king of Cochin, "an Infidel", to whom the town of Diamper was subject [[cite:king]], and of a Portuguese governor of Cochin, who came to the synod with the magistrates of the city [[cite:governor]]. The Carmelite Giuseppe Sebastiani puts "Matangeri", Mattancherry, a quarter of a league from Cochin, and tells that the Thomas Christians gathered in the church there swore never again to acknowledge Archbishop Garcia [[cite:oath]]. The declaration of obedience to Rome that he obtained in 1657 is dated from the church of St Thomas outside the walls of Cochin [[cite:declaration]]. See [[event:synod-of-diamper]].
