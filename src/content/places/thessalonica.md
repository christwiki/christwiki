Thessalonica stood at the head of the Thermaic Gulf. [[person:paul]] and [[person:silas]] came to it from [[place:philippi]], and Paul argued in the synagogue on three sabbaths. Some Jews, many Greeks and a number of leading women joined them. A crowd then attacked the house of their host Jason and accused the believers before the city magistrates of proclaiming another king, and Paul and Silas were sent away by night [[bible:Acts 17:1-10]]. See [[event:second-missionary-journey]].

Paul's first letter to the church, sent with Silvanus and [[person:timothy|Timothy]], speaks of its members as having turned from idols to the living God and as suffering at the hands of their own countrymen [[bible:1 Thess 1:1, 9]] [[bible:1 Thess 2:14]]. The Philippians sent him help while he was there [[bible:Phil 4:16]].
