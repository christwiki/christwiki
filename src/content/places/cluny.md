In the foundation charter Cluny is an estate with a manor, a church dedicated to St Mary and St Peter, dependants, vineyards, woods and mills, lying in the county of Mâcon. Duke [[person:william-i-of-aquitaine]] gave it to the apostles Peter and Paul so that a monastery might be built there [[cite:charter]]. See [[event:cluny]].
