[[person:jonathan-edwards]] describes Northampton as an inland town, far from the sea-ports and in a corner of the country, whose first minister was ordained in 1669, and he says that its church had about six hundred and twenty communicants after the awakening [[cite:town]]. [[person:george-whitefield]] reached it by crossing the ferry from Hadley on 17 October 1740 [[cite:visit]]. See [[event:great-awakening]].
