Speyer stands on the left bank of the Rhine in south-western Germany. The diet held here in 1526 left each estate to act on the Edict of Worms as it could answer to God and the emperor [[cite:recess]]. The diet of 1529 revoked that article, and a group of princes and cities protested that the decision did not bind them [[cite:protest]]. See [[event:protestation-at-speyer]].
