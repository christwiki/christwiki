The German court that later tried the prosecutor describes Flossenbürg concentration camp as lying north-east of Weiden in the Upper Palatinate. On 8 April 1945 an SS court-martial sat there, and on the morning of 9 April the five men it had condemned, [[person:dietrich-bonhoeffer]] among them, were hanged [[cite:court]]. See [[event:execution-of-bonhoeffer]].
