The Goa of the sixteenth century is the present Old Goa, on the west coast of India. [[person:francis-xavier]] wrote that he arrived there on 6 May 1542, thirteen months after leaving Lisbon, and describes it as the capital of India, a fine city wholly in Christian hands, with a cathedral and a Franciscan convent [[cite:arrival]]. He later reported three Japanese students at the college of Santa Fe in the city, among them the man he calls Paul [[cite:college]]. See [[event:francis-xavier-in-asia]].
