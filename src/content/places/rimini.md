Rimini, the Roman Ariminum, lies on the Adriatic coast of Italy. In 359 the western half of the council summoned by [[person:constantius-ii]] met here, more than four hundred bishops by the count of Athanasius [[cite:number]]. Sulpicius Severus says that the majority held the church while about eighty of their opponents used a building that stood empty, and that the bishops were still shut up in the city in the seventh month, in winter, when the last of them signed [[cite:sulpicius]]. See [[event:councils-of-rimini-and-seleucia]].
