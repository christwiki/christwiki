This Antioch lay on the high plateau of Asia Minor and was a Roman colony, Colonia Caesarea. It is not the Syrian [[place:antioch]]. [[person:paul]] and [[person:barnabas]] came up to it from Perga. Paul spoke in the synagogue on the sabbath, and on the next sabbath, when they were contradicted, the two said, "we turn to the Gentiles". They were driven out of the district and went on to Iconium [[bible:Acts 13:14-51]]. They passed through again on their way home, and 2 Timothy recalls what Paul suffered "at Antioch, Iconium, and Lystra" [[bible:Acts 14:21]] [[bible:2 Tim 3:11]].
