The Catholic bishops of Uganda record that [[person:charles-lwanga]] was burned at Namugongo on 3 June 1886, at the spot where the altar of the Catholic basilica now stands [[cite:lwanga]]. John Paul II, preaching at the shrine in 1993, called Namugongo the place of the great fire that consumed Lwanga and his companions, and spoke of the crowds that come there on pilgrimage year after year [[cite:fire]]. The Anglican missionary Robert Ashe, who was in Buganda at the time, tells of a great pyre on which the blacksmith Walukaga and some thirty other Christians were burned together; he does not name the place [[cite:pyre]]. Pope Francis celebrated Mass at the Catholic shrine in 2015 and remembered there the Anglican martyrs as well [[cite:francis]]. See [[event:uganda-martyrs]].
