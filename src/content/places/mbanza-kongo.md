Rui de Pina does not name the town; he calls it the court of the king of Congo and says that it lay fifty leagues, a journey of twenty-three days, from the port where the Portuguese landed. He describes the king's palace enclosure with its courtyard, and says that a stone church dedicated to Saint Mary was begun on 6 May 1491 and finished on 1 June, a thousand men carrying the stone [[cite:court]]. King Afonso I dated a letter of 1526 from "our city of Congo" [[cite:afonso-1526]]. See [[event:baptism-of-the-king-of-kongo]].
