The pope's legate declared the council open at Ferrara on 8 January 1438, and on 9 April [[person:eugene-iv]] decreed, with the assent of the emperor and the patriarch of Constantinople, that a universal council existed in the city, which he called free and safe for everyone [[cite:ferrara]]. Doukas says that the Greeks travelled there overland from [[place:venice]] and that a deadly plague struck after the debates had begun, so that the council left for [[place:florence]] [[cite:doukas]]. See [[event:council-of-florence]].
