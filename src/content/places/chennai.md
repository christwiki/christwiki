The services that inaugurated the Church of South India were held in St George's Cathedral, Madras, on 27 September 1947, with a congregation of about three thousand in the cathedral and in a temporary structure in its grounds [[cite:film]]. Madras gave its name to one of the four Anglican dioceses, one of the church councils of the South India United Church and one of the Methodist districts that entered the union [[cite:declaration]]. See [[event:church-of-south-india]].
