The House of Bishops of the Chinese Anglican church met at Shanghai in March 1946 and there passed its resolution on the ordination of [[person:florence-li-tim-oi]] [[cite:bishops-1946]]; see [[event:florence-li-tim-oi]]. The Nanyang Road meeting house of the Little Flock, the church founded by [[person:watchman-nee]], was in the city, and the American editors of the movement's documents say that its dissenting leaders had been suppressed by 1956 [[cite:suppression]]; see [[event:three-self-movement]].
