Arles, the Roman Arelate, stands on the Rhône a short distance above its delta. [[person:constantine]] chose it as the meeting place for the bishops he summoned from "a great many different places" to rehear the case against [[person:caecilian]] of Carthage [[cite:summons]]. Its bishop Marinus, who had already sat as one of the three Gallic judges at Rome [[cite:rome]], is named first in the council's letter to the bishop of Rome [[cite:letter]]. See [[event:donatist-schism]].
