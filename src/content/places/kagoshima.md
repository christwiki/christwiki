Kagoshima lies on a bay at the southern end of the island of Kyushu. [[person:francis-xavier]], who spells the name Cagoxima, wrote that his ship put in there on 15 August 1549 because it could make no other port, that it was the native place of his companion Paul, [[person:anjiro]], and that the people received them kindly [[cite:landing]]. He adds that the lord of the place gave his subjects leave to become Christians [[cite:leave]]. See [[event:francis-xavier-in-asia]].
