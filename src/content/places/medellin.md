Medellín lies in a valley of the Colombian Andes. The Catholic bishops of Latin America published the conclusions of their second general conference as the final documents of Medellín, sixteen texts that set out to apply the Second Vatican Council to their continent [[cite:intro]]. See [[event:medellin-and-liberation-theology]].
