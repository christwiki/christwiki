Basel lies on the Rhine in the north of modern Switzerland. The title page of [[person:erasmus]]'s New Testament of 1516 gives the place of printing in Latin as *apud inclytam Germaniae Basilaeam*, at the renowned Basel of Germany, and the colophon names the house of Johann Froben [[cite:title]]. See [[event:erasmus-greek-new-testament]].
