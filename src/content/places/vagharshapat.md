Vagharshapat lies west of modern Yerevan. Agathangelos, writing in the fifth century, calls it the residence of the Armenian kings and makes it the scene of the central events of the conversion: the nun Hripsime and her companions took refuge there and were put to death there [[cite:royal]]. In the vision he attributes to [[person:gregory-the-illuminator]], a column of fire topped by a cross of light stands in the middle of the city near the palace, and Gregory is told to build a temple to God on that spot [[cite:vision]]. The king and people then walled the place and set up a cross there [[cite:wall]]. The cathedral of Etchmiadzin, the seat of the head of the Armenian Apostolic Church, stands in the town today.
