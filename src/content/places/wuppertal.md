Wuppertal lies on the Wupper east of Düsseldorf. The delegates of the Confessional Synod met in the Gemarke church at Barmen, in the city of Wuppertal, from 29 to 31 May 1934 [[cite:ekd-en]], and a sculpture in the pedestrian street of Wuppertal-Barmen commemorates the declaration adopted there [[cite:ekd-de]]. See [[event:barmen-declaration]].
