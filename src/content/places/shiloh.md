Shiloh lay in the hill country of Ephraim. Judges places it north of Bethel, east of the road from Bethel to Shechem [[bible:Judg 21:19]], and the site is identified with Khirbet Seilun. Joshua says that Israel set up the tent of meeting there once the land was subdued [[bible:Josh 18:1]]. In the time of [[person:eli]] it was the sanctuary where the ark was kept and where families such as Samuel's came each year to sacrifice [[bible:1 Sam 1:3]] [[bible:1 Sam 3:3]]. The ark was taken from it to the battle in which the Philistines captured it [[bible:1 Sam 4:3-11]].

Psalm 78 says that God "abandoned the tent of Shiloh" [[bible:Ps 78:60]], and Jeremiah told those who trusted in the Jerusalem temple to go and see what had been done to Shiloh [[bible:Jer 7:12-14]].
