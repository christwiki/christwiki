The manifesto of [[person:peter-the-great]] establishing the Spiritual College is dated "in Petersburg" on 25 January 1721 [[cite:manifesto]], and the title leaf of the Regulation says that it was first printed at St Petersburg on 16 September of that year [[cite:printing]]. The nineteenth-century church historian Muravyov calls the city the tsar's new capital, built on the banks of the Neva [[cite:muravyov]]. See [[event:holy-synod-of-russia]].
