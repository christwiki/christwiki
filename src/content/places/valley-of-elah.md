The valley of Elah runs through the low hills between the highlands of Judah and the coastal plain where the Philistine cities lay. In 1 Samuel the Philistines camp between Socoh and Azekah, Saul's army camps in the valley, and each holds a hillside with the valley between them [[bible:1 Sam 17:1-3]]. Here [[person:david]] killed [[person:goliath]] [[bible:1 Sam 17:19, 48-51]]. The priest at Nob later speaks of "Goliath the Philistine, whom you killed in the valley of Elah" [[bible:1 Sam 21:1, 9]]. The valley is identified with the Wadi es-Sant. The exact place of the fight is not known, and the point on the map marks the valley only.
