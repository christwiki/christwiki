The Mount of Olives faces [[place:jerusalem]] from the east; Zechariah describes it as "before Jerusalem on the east" [[bible:Zech 14:4]]. David went up it weeping when he fled from Absalom [[bible:2 Sam 15:30]]. Jesus approached the city by way of Bethphage and [[place:bethany]], villages on the mount, and rode down it on a donkey [[bible:Mark 11:1; Luke 19:37]]. In his last week he sat on the mount opposite the temple and spoke of its destruction [[bible:Mark 13:1-3]], and Luke says that he spent the nights there [[bible:Luke 21:37]]. After the last supper he went out to it, to [[place:gethsemane]], where he was arrested [[bible:Mark 14:26, 32, 46]]. Acts places the ascension on the mount, a Sabbath day's journey from the city [[bible:Acts 1:9-12]].

In the fourth century churches were built there. Eusebius says that the mother of the emperor Constantine raised one on the mount of the ascension [[cite:eusebius]], and the pilgrim Egeria describes services at a place on it called the Imbomon, "whence the Lord ascended into heaven" [[cite:egeria]].
