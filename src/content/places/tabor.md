Lawrence of Březová tells that in February 1420 a party of "Taborite brethren" took the town of Ústí by surprise, and a few days later the nearby castle of Hradiště, where the walls of a former town still stood. After a time they burned Ústí and moved with their wives and children to the hill, which they fortified and built over with houses and which they call Mount Tabor [[cite:founded]]. There they chose four captains, among them [[person:jan-zizka]] [[cite:captains]]. It is not to be confused with [[place:mount-tabor]] in Galilee. See [[event:hussite-movement]].
