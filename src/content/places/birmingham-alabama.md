Birmingham lies in north-central Alabama, in the south-eastern United States. Writing from its jail in April 1963, [[person:martin-luther-king-jr]] called it probably the most thoroughly segregated city in the country and said that it had seen more unsolved bombings of Black homes and churches than any other [[cite:letter-1]]. The march of Good Friday 1963 set out from a church in the Black district of the city toward city hall [[cite:good-friday]]. See [[event:letter-from-birmingham-jail]].
