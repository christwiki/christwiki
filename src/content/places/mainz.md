Willibald calls the city Magontia and says that [[person:boniface]] ruled its bishopric by the consent and gift of the Frankish ruler Carloman, and that before leaving for Frisia he ordained his disciple Lul to take his place [[cite:see]]. Lul sent monks to bring the martyr's body from Utrecht; it rested at Mainz on the way to [[place:fulda]] [[cite:body]].
