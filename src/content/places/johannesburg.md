The preface of the Kairos Document is dated from Johannesburg on 25 September 1985 and says that the group which wrote it first met in Soweto at the beginning of July [[cite:kairos]]. The Nobel Foundation's record says that [[person:desmond-tutu]] became dean of St Mary's Cathedral in Johannesburg in 1975 and describes him in 1984 as bishop of Johannesburg [[cite:tutu]].
