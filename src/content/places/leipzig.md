Leipzig lies in Saxony in eastern Germany. On 9 October 1989, by the count of the state security service, more than 5,000 people attended peace prayers in the Nikolaikirche, the Thomaskirche, the Michaeliskirche and the Reformed church in its centre, and 50,000 to 60,000 then marched from the Nikolaikirche round the ring of streets that encloses the old city [[cite:stasi-100]].
