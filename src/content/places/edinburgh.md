Edinburgh stands on the south shore of the Firth of Forth, with its port at Leith. The Parliament held here from July 1560 ratified the Scots Confession on 17 August and a week later ended the pope's jurisdiction in Scotland [[cite:parliament]]. Nobles signed the Book of Discipline in the Tolbooth of Edinburgh the following January [[cite:tolbooth]]. When [[person:mary-queen-of-scots]] landed at Leith in 1561 she went up the same evening to the palace of Holyroodhouse beside the abbey, where mass was said for her in the chapel [[cite:holyrood]]. See [[event:scottish-reformation]].
