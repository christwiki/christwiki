[[person:george-whitefield]]'s journal records sermons in the meeting-houses of Boston and on the Common in September and October 1740, with the governor carrying him to his farewell sermon in his coach [[cite:common]]. Charles Chauncy's *Seasonable Thoughts* was printed in the town in 1743, and its title page calls him pastor of the First Church of Christ in Boston [[cite:chauncy]]. See [[event:great-awakening]].
