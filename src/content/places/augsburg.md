Augsburg lies on the river Lech in Bavaria. The emperor Charles V summoned the diet of the empire to meet there in 1530, and the Lutheran princes presented their confession to him in the city [[cite:confession]]; see [[event:augsburg-confession]]. The diet of 1555 was moved to Augsburg, which its recess calls a city of the emperor, the king and the empire, and the recess containing the religious peace is dated there on 25 September 1555 [[cite:peace]]; see [[event:peace-of-augsburg]].
