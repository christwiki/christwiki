Béziers stands above the river Orb in Languedoc, between Narbonne and Montpellier. In 1209 it belonged to a viscount who was the nephew of the count of Toulouse, and Peter of les Vaux-de-Cernay calls it a most noble city wholly infected with heresy [[cite:peter]]. The crusading army appeared before it on the feast of Mary Magdalene. The legates wrote that it seemed strong enough to hold out against any army for a long time, and that it fell in two or three hours, was plundered and was burnt [[cite:report]]. See [[event:albigensian-crusade]].
