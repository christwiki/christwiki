The Rus' called the city Korsun'. The Primary Chronicle says that [[person:vladimir-the-great]] besieged it, took it by cutting its water pipes, was baptized in its church of St Basil and married the emperor's sister there, and that he gave the city back to the Greeks as a wedding gift [[cite:chronicle]]. Leo the Deacon, who lived through these years, mentions its capture by the Rus' as one of the disasters foretold by signs in the sky [[cite:leo]]. See [[event:baptism-of-rus]].
