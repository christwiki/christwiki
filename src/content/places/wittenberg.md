Wittenberg stands on the Elbe in what was the electorate of Saxony. [[person:martin-luther]]'s theses of 1517 were to be debated "at Wittenberg", where he lectured in theology [[cite:heading]]; [[person:philip-melanchthon]] wrote that he posted them on the church next to the castle [[cite:melanchthon]]. Luther burned the pope's bull at the town's eastern gate in December 1520 [[cite:burning]], and his complete German Bible was printed here in 1534 [[cite:bible]]. See [[event:ninety-five-theses]] and [[event:luther-bible]].
