Shalmaneser III calls Qarqar a royal city of Irhuleni of Hamath. He says that he burned it, fought the allied kings from Qarqar as far as a place called Gilzau, and dammed the Orontes with their dead [[cite:kurkh]]; see [[event:battle-of-qarqar]]. The Bible does not mention the town. It is usually identified with Tell Qarqur, a large mound beside the Orontes, and the marker is placed there.
