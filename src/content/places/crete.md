Crete lies south of the Aegean Sea. Cretans are among those who hear the apostles at Pentecost in Acts [[bible:Acts 2:11]], and the ship carrying Paul as a prisoner to Rome sails under the lee of the island and shelters at Fair Havens on its coast [[bible:Acts 27:1-13]]. The letter to Titus says that Titus was left in Crete to set things in order and appoint elders in every town [[bible:Titus 1:5]].

The primates of the Orthodox churches chose the Orthodox Academy of Crete as the meeting place of the [[event:holy-and-great-council|Holy and Great Council]] in June 2016 [[cite:towards]], and the council's encyclical recalls the island's link with Paul and Titus [[cite:encyclical]].
