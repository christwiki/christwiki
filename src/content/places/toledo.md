The acts of the council of 589 call Toledo the royal city, *civitas regia*, and it was there that King [[person:reccared-i]] gathered the bishops of his kingdom [[cite:acts]]. Isidore of Seville says that Reccared died there [[cite:isidore]]. The collection of Spanish church law in which those acts are preserved contains the acts of more than a dozen councils held at Toledo ([[source:council-of-toledo-589]]).
