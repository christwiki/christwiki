Naples lies on the west coast of southern Italy. William of Tocco says that [[person:thomas-aquinas]] was sent there as a boy to study grammar, logic and natural philosophy, and there took the Dominican habit [[cite:schools]]. At the end of his life he was again in Naples, and it was after a Mass in the chapel of Saint Nicholas there that he gave up writing [[cite:mass]]. The inquiry for his canonization sat in the archbishop's palace at Naples in 1319 [[cite:inquiry]]. See [[event:thomas-aquinas]].
