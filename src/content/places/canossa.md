Canossa is a rock in the northern Apennines, in the hills south-west of Reggio Emilia, on which the ruins of a castle stand. [[person:gregory-vii]] dated the oath of [[person:henry-iv-emperor]] there on 28 January 1077 and wrote that the king had stood three days before its gate [[cite:letter]]. Lampert of Hersfeld calls it a very strong fortress to which the pope withdrew on the advice of [[person:matilda-of-tuscany]], and gives it three rings of wall [[cite:lampert]].
