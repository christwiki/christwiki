The Episcopal Church's Prayer Book prints, under the heading "Chicago, 1886", the declaration in which its House of Bishops named four things as essential to the restoration of unity among Christians [[cite:declaration]]. Two years later the Lambeth Conference's committee on reunion took the American bishops' articles as the basis of its own [[cite:basis]]. See [[event:lambeth-quadrilateral]].
