Gilgal was the place where Israel camped after crossing the Jordan, "on the east border of Jericho", and where [[person:joshua]] set up the twelve stones taken from the riverbed [[bible:Josh 4:19-20]]. The men born in the wilderness were circumcised there and the first Passover in Canaan was kept; the book of Joshua connects the name with the rolling away of "the reproach of Egypt" [[bible:Josh 5:2-10]]. It remained Joshua's base during the campaigns that followed [[bible:Josh 10:6-9]] [[bible:Josh 10:43]]. A Gilgal was on Samuel's yearly circuit and was the place where Saul was confirmed as king [[bible:1 Sam 7:16]] [[bible:1 Sam 11:14-15]], and the prophets Hosea and Amos denounced the worship offered at Gilgal [[bible:Hos 4:15]] [[bible:Amos 4:4]]; more than one place may have borne the name. The site of Joshua's camp has not been identified. The coordinates mark a point just east of ancient [[place:jericho]], where Joshua places the camp.
