Antwerp is a port on the Scheldt in modern Belgium. John Foxe says that [[person:william-tyndale]], after his time in Germany, "had his most abiding in the town of Antwerp" until he was seized there and taken to the castle of [[place:vilvoorde]], eighteen English miles away [[cite:foxe]]. See [[event:tyndale-new-testament]].
