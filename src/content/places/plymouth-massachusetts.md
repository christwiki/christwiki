[[person:william-bradford]] tells how an exploring party sounded the harbour in December 1620, found cornfields and running brooks, and judged the place fit to settle; the ship arrived on 16 December by the old calendar, and the first common house was begun on the 25th [[cite:harbour]]. He says that Squanto was a native of the place and that scarcely any of its people besides him were left alive [[cite:squanto]]. See [[event:pilgrims-at-plymouth]].
