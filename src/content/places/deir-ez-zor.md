Deir ez-Zor lies on the Euphrates in eastern Syria. A German missionary who passed through in the summer of 1915 describes it as a large town in the desert about six days' journey from Aleppo, and found deported Armenians from a village near Zeitun crowded into its inn after four weeks on the road [[cite:bb-145]]. The editor of the British Blue Book of 1916 writes that of the places on the Euphrates where the exiles were quartered, Deir ez-Zor was the largest depot [[cite:bb-645]].
