Constantinople stands on a promontory at the southern end of the Bosphorus, where Europe faces Asia. [[person:constantine]] renamed the older city of Byzantium after himself [[cite:origo]], enlarged and walled it, and decreed that it should be called New Rome [[cite:socrates]]; see [[event:founding-of-constantinople]]. Councils met there in 381 and later, and the first of them gave its bishop a rank of honour second only to that of Rome [[cite:canon3]]. It is the modern Istanbul.
