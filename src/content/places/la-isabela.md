The fleet's physician, Dr Chanca, describes the place in the first weeks: a good harbour with plenty of fish, two rivers close by, and a town being built on the bank of one of them, protected on one side by a rocky ravine and on the other by a dense wood. He calls the town Marta [[cite:chanca]]. Peter Martyr says that houses and a church were built within a few days and that mass was celebrated on the feast of the Three Kings [[cite:mass]]. Ramón Pané calls it Isabella [[cite:pane]]. See [[event:christianity-reaches-the-americas]].
