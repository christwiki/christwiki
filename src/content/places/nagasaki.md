Nagasaki is a port on the island of Kyushu. Antonio de Morga describes it in the 1590s as the harbour between which and the Philippines trade was carried on [[cite:morga-wreck]]. The Christians condemned by [[person:toyotomi-hideyoshi]] in 1597 were brought here and crucified in a row on a hill in sight of the town and port [[cite:morga-crucifixion]]. Luís Fróis adds that the hill lay towards the sea and that the Portuguese of the town, who had asked that the usual place of execution not be used, hoped to build a church on the spot [[cite:frois]]. See [[event:japan-martyrs]].
