Geneva lies where the Rhône leaves Lake Geneva. [[person:john-calvin]], who came there meaning to stay one night, found a city from which "Popery" had lately been driven and which was divided into factions [[cite:memoir]]. Its registers show the Little Council, the Two Hundred and the General Council of the citizens voting to expel him and [[person:guillaume-farel]] in April 1538 [[cite:banished]] and passing the Ecclesiastical Ordinances on 20 November 1541 [[cite:adopted]]. See [[event:calvin-in-geneva]].
