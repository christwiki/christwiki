Malta lies about ninety kilometres south of Sicily. Acts tells how the ship taking [[person:paul]] to Italy ran aground in a bay of an island that the survivors learned was called Malta, *Melite* in the Greek. The islanders lit a fire for them, the chief man, Publius, lodged them for three days, and Paul healed Publius's father and others who were sick. After three months they left on a ship that had wintered there [[bible:Acts 27:39-28:11]]. Acts does not say where on the island the ship struck.
