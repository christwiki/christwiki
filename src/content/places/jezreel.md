Jezreel lay north of [[place:samaria]], in the broad valley that takes its name. [[person:ahab]] had a palace there, next to the vineyard of Naboth [[bible:1 Kings 21:1]], and it was to Jezreel that he drove from [[place:mount-carmel]] when the drought broke [[bible:1 Kings 18:45-46]]. King Joram was recovering there from his wounds when [[person:jehu]] arrived, killed him and had [[person:jezebel]] thrown from a window [[bible:2 Kings 9:15-37]]; see [[event:jehu-pays-tribute]]. Hosea named a son Jezreel as a sign that the blood shed there would be avenged on Jehu's house [[bible:Hos 1:4]].
