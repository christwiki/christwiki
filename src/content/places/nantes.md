Nantes stands on the Loire in historic Brittany, near the Atlantic coast of France. [[person:henry-iv-of-france]] was in the city in April 1598, and the edict on the Reformed religion and the warrants that accompany it are dated from there [[cite:brevet]]. See [[event:edict-of-nantes]].
