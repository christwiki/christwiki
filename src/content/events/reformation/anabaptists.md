In September 1524 [[person:conrad-grebel]], [[person:felix-manz]] and four companions at [[place:zurich]] wrote to [[person:thomas-muntzer]]. They wanted the Lord's Supper kept with ordinary bread and the words of Scripture only, called the baptism of infants senseless and contrary to all Scripture, and held that the gospel and those who follow it are not to be protected with the sword [[cite:grebel]].

The council of Zurich held a disputation and on 18 January 1525 ordered all unbaptised children to be baptised within a week, on pain of banishment. On 21 January it forbade Grebel and Manz to dispute further and expelled four of their associates [[cite:mandates]].

The chronicle of the Hutterian Brethren, written later, tells what the group did. Grebel, Manz and [[person:george-blaurock]], a priest from Chur, were together with others. After prayer Blaurock stood up and asked Grebel to baptise him with true Christian baptism on his faith; Grebel did so, there being no ordained minister for such a work; and the others then asked Blaurock to baptise them [[cite:chronicle]]. Within three weeks the council was questioning villagers of Zollikon whom Manz and Blaurock had baptised [[cite:zollikon]].

On 7 March 1526 the council decreed that anyone who baptised another would be "drowned without mercy" [[cite:drown]]. [[person:huldrych-zwingli]] reported the decision to a friend that day. He would not have wished reborn Christianity to begin with such examples, he wrote, but God had once struck down Ananias to forestall greater evils [[cite:vadian]]. On 5 January 1527 the court sentenced Manz, who had baptised again after swearing to stop, to be bound, taken out in a boat and thrown into the water to die [[cite:manz]]. The same day Blaurock was whipped through the city and banished, on pain of drowning if he returned [[cite:blaurock]].

On 24 February 1527 a meeting at [[place:schleitheim]] agreed seven articles. Baptism is for those who have learned repentance and believe, which excludes all infant baptism. The sword is ordained by God outside the "perfection of Christ" and belongs to the worldly magistrates; within that perfection only the ban is used. Christians are not to swear at all [[cite:schleitheim]]. Their drafting is attributed to [[person:michael-sattler]] [[cite:schleitheim]]. That May he was tried on charges brought in the name of the emperor's governor: breaking the imperial mandate, denying that the body and blood of Christ are present in the sacrament, rejecting infant baptism and oaths, and saying that the Turks should not be resisted. His tongue was cut out, his body was torn with hot tongs and he was burned; his wife was drowned a few days later [[cite:sattler]]. Blaurock was burned near Klausen in Tyrol in 1529 [[cite:klausen]].

The other reformers rejected this teaching: at [[place:marburg]] in 1529 Luther and Zwingli signed together that infant baptism is right and that a Christian may hold civil authority [[cite:marburg]].
