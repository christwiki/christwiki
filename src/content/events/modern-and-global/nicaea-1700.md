The bishops gathered at Nicaea in 325 confessed the Son of God as "of one substance" with the Father [[cite:creed-325]]; see [[event:council-of-nicaea]].

For the 1,700th anniversary, in 2025, the World Council of Churches made a world conference on Faith and Order, its sixth, the centrepiece of its commemoration [[cite:wcc-event]]. It met from 24 to 28 October at the monastery of St Bishoy at Wadi El Natrun in Egypt, on the theme "Where now for visible unity?" [[cite:wcc-event]]. Its message thanks the Coptic Orthodox Church for its hospitality and says that the Nicene creed reminds the churches of a shared gift and call to full, visible unity [[cite:wcc-message]].

On 23 November [[person:leo-xiv]] issued the apostolic letter *In unitate fidei* as he prepared to travel to Türkiye [[cite:iuf-1]]. It calls the creed, with baptism, the foundation of ecumenical dialogue, and describes an ecumenism that neither returns to the state before the divisions nor accepts the present one, but looks to the future [[cite:iuf-12]]. It quotes the creed's article on the Holy Spirit as proceeding "from the Father", and notes that the words "and the Son" were inserted into the Latin creed in 1014 and remain a subject of Orthodox–Catholic dialogue [[cite:iuf-8]].

On 28 November a prayer service was held at İznik, the ancient [[place:nicaea]], near the excavated remains of the basilica of St Neophytos [[cite:programme]]. Leo, the Ecumenical Patriarch [[person:bartholomew-i]], heads of other churches and representatives of the Christian world communions took part [[cite:booklet]] [[cite:iznik]]. Leo said that faith in Christ "consubstantial with the Father" is a bond already uniting all Christians, and thanked Bartholomew for deciding to commemorate the council in the place where it was held [[cite:iznik]]. The booklet for the service has Bartholomew introduce the profession of faith and all say it together, with the Spirit "who proceeds from the Father" [[cite:booklet]].

The next day, at the Phanar in [[place:constantinople]], pope and patriarch signed a joint declaration [[cite:declaration-1-2]]. It names the faith expressed in the creed of Nicaea as what binds Christians together, while acknowledging obstacles that prevent the restoration of full communion among them [[cite:declaration-1-2]]. It notes that in 2025 all Christians kept Easter on the same day and states a shared desire to go on seeking a way to do so every year [[cite:declaration-3]]. It recalls the declaration of 1965 that set aside the excommunications of 1054 (see [[event:lifting-of-the-anathemas]]) and supports the official theological dialogue between the two churches [[cite:declaration-4-5]].
