On the morning of 11 February 2013 [[person:benedict-xvi]] met the cardinals at the Vatican, in [[place:rome]], in a consistory called to decide on three canonizations, and after their vote read a declaration in Latin [[cite:deed]]. He said that he had examined his conscience repeatedly before God, that strength of mind and body were needed to govern the barque of Peter and proclaim the gospel, and that in the last few months his own had declined so far that he had to recognise his incapacity to fulfil the ministry adequately [[cite:declaratio]]. "With full freedom" he renounced the ministry of bishop of Rome, so that the see would be vacant from 20:00 on 28 February and a conclave would have to be called [[cite:declaratio]].

At his last general audience, on 27 February, he said that he had taken the step aware of its gravity "and even its novelty" [[cite:last-audience]]. He was not returning to private life, he said, but would remain in the service of prayer [[cite:last-audience]]. This wiki tells of an earlier resignation, that of Gregory XII in 1415, in its entry on [[event:western-schism|the Western Schism]]. Benedict lived his last years in a monastery in the Vatican and died on 31 December 2022 [[cite:deed]].

On 13 March the cardinals in conclave elected Jorge Mario Bergoglio, archbishop of Buenos Aires and a Jesuit, aged 76 [[cite:bio]]. He took the name [[person:francis]] after [[person:francis-of-assisi]], and the Holy See's biography calls him the first pope to come from the Americas [[cite:bio]]. From the loggia of St Peter's that evening he told the crowd that the cardinals had gone "to the ends of the earth" to find Rome a bishop, prayed with them for "our Bishop Emeritus, Benedict XVI", and asked them to pray for him before he gave his blessing [[cite:first-greeting]].

Francis set out his programme that November in the exhortation *Evangelii gaudium* [[cite:bio]]. He wrote of a "missionary option" that would direct the church's customs and structures to evangelization rather than to its own preservation [[cite:eg-27]]. He said that he must think about "a conversion of the papacy", recalled the hopes of the [[event:second-vatican-council|Second Vatican Council]] for bishops' conferences, and held that excessive centralization complicates the church's life [[cite:eg-32]]. He preferred, he wrote, a church "bruised, hurting and dirty" from being out on the streets to one confined and clinging to its own security [[cite:eg-49]].

The Holy See's biography of Francis says that Francis met his predecessor periodically until two days before Benedict's death [[cite:bio]].
