  Acts gives intervals, not years: three months in the synagogue at Ephesus and two years in the hall of Tyrannus [[bible:Acts 19:8-10]], three years in all by the reckoning of the Miletus speech [[bible:Acts 20:31]], three months in Greece [[bible:Acts 20:3]], a departure from Philippi after the days of Unleavened Bread and a wish to reach Jerusalem by Pentecost [[bible:Acts 20:6, 16]].

  These intervals hang between two outside points. The earlier is Gallio's term as proconsul of Achaia, which falls during Paul's previous stay at Corinth [[bible:Acts 18:12]]; see [[event:second-missionary-journey]]. The later is the replacement of Felix by Festus, two years after Paul's arrest [[bible:Acts 24:27]], and that year is itself disputed; see [[event:paul-arrested-in-jerusalem]]. Rainer Riesner, *Paul's Early Period: Chronology, Mission Strategy, Theology* (1998), puts Paul in Ephesus in 52–55 and his arrest in Jerusalem in 57. Other reconstructions move the whole sequence by a year or two in either direction.

After some time at [[place:antioch]], [[person:paul]] set out again through Galatia and Phrygia [[bible:Acts 18:22-23]]. Before he reached [[place:ephesus]], a Jew from Alexandria named [[person:apollos]] had come there, an eloquent man who knew the Scriptures well and taught about Jesus but knew only the baptism of John. [[person:priscilla]] and [[person:aquila]] took him aside and explained the way more accurately, and he went on to Achaia [[bible:Acts 18:24-19:1]].

Paul taught at Ephesus for three months in the synagogue and then for two years in the hall of Tyrannus, so that, Acts says, all who lived in the province of Asia heard the message [[bible:Acts 19:8-10]]. Acts tells of healings, of Jewish exorcists who tried to use the name of Jesus, and of magicians who burned their books [[bible:Acts 19:11-20]]. From Ephesus Paul wrote to [[place:corinth]], where the church had split into parties that used the names of Paul, Apollos and Cephas [[bible:1 Cor 1:11-12]] [[bible:1 Cor 16:8]]. He speaks of "many adversaries" in Ephesus and of a danger in Asia so grave that he despaired of life [[bible:1 Cor 16:9]] [[bible:2 Cor 1:8-10]]. Acts tells of a riot: the silversmith Demetrius, whose trade was in shrines of Artemis, roused the craftsmen, and a crowd filled the theatre and shouted for two hours, "Great is Artemis of the Ephesians!", until the town clerk dismissed them [[bible:Acts 19:23-41]].

Through these years Paul was organising a collection. He told the churches of Galatia and Corinth to set money aside on the first day of each week [[bible:1 Cor 16:1-4]]. He went on to Macedonia, where the churches gave beyond their means, and pressed Corinth to finish what it had begun a year before [[bible:Acts 20:1]] [[bible:2 Cor 8:1-11]]. He then spent three months in Greece [[bible:Acts 20:2-3]]. His letter to the Christians of [[place:rome]], written as he was about to leave for [[place:jerusalem]], says that he is carrying the gift of Macedonia and Achaia to the poor among the believers there, and that he hopes afterwards to visit Rome on his way to Spain [[bible:Rom 15:23-28]]. The Gentiles, he wrote, had shared in the spiritual goods of the Jerusalem believers and owed them a share of their material ones. He asked for prayer that he would be rescued from the unbelievers in Judea and that the gift would be accepted [[bible:Rom 15:27, 30-31]].

A plot made him return through Macedonia. He sailed from [[place:philippi]] after the feast of Unleavened Bread and at [[place:miletus]] sent for the elders of Ephesus, telling them that they would not see him again [[bible:Acts 20:3-6, 17-38]]. At [[place:caesarea-maritima|Caesarea]] the prophet Agabus bound his own hands and feet with Paul's belt as a sign of what awaited him, and Paul went up to Jerusalem [[bible:Acts 21:8-17]].

The letters to Corinth and to Rome come from these years. In the chapters of Acts cited here the collection is not described. In Paul's later defence before [[person:felix]] he says that he came to bring alms and offerings to his nation [[bible:Acts 24:17]].
