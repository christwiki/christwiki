### B · dating · src/content/people/yi-byeok.md
- **Text:** "died:\n  start: 1786\n  confidence: firm" and "he died of an epidemic in the spring of 1786, aged thirty-three"
- **Problem:** The year of Yi Byeok's death is not secure. 1786 is Dallet's figure (1874, from Daveluy's notes); Korean scholarship working from the Yi family genealogy gives the summer of 1785 (14th day of the 6th lunar month), and standard references now print 1754–1785. `firm` overstates it, and the entry (like the event `first-korean-baptism`, which also says "Yi Byeok died in 1786") gives no hint of the other year. The `born: c. 1754` figure is "reckoned from that age" and so inherits the same doubt.
- **Should be:** `died: { start: 1786, confidence: estimated }` (or 1785) with a sentence such as "Dallet gives 1786; Korean genealogical sources, followed by most modern reference works, give 1785."
- **Evidence:** en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yi_Byeok (opened): headline dates "1754-1785"; body: "Dallet … says … he died of the plague in 1786. A different account … reported that he died on 14 June 1785". The CBCK page cited by the entry (opened) gives no years for him.
- **Confidence:** medium

### C · dating · src/content/people/herman-of-alaska.md
- **Text:** "died:\n  start: 1837\n  confidence: firm" and "he died on 13 December 1837, by the Julian calendar, in the eighty-first year of his life"
- **Problem:** The entry attributes the date to the Valaam history, correctly, but the day is not undisputed and the entry (and the event `russian-mission-to-alaska`, which says in the wiki's own voice "He died there on 13 December 1837") gives no hint of that. The Orthodox Church in America's own life notes that "there are some records which state that he died on November 28th" (N.S., i.e. 15 November O.S.); the OCA keeps a second feast of his repose on 15 November, and current reference works take 15 November for the death and 13 December for the burial or for a clerical error. A minority (the archivist M. Vinokouroff, from Russian-American Company papers) puts it in 1836. The birth year "reckoned from that" (c. 1757) is one of several in print (1756 "assumed" by the OCA life; 1760; Korsun argues for 1751).
- **Should be:** Keep `died: 1837` but add to the body: "Company records, and the church's later calendar, put his death on 15 November; 13 December may be the day of his burial"; in the event, attribute the day ("the Valaam history says …").
- **Evidence:** oca.org/saints/all-lives/2025/12/13 (opened): "the 13th of December according to the Julian Calendar … there are some records which state that he died on November 28th … if we assume that Herman was born in 1756, although sometimes 1760 is given"; en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herman_of_Alaska (opened): "c. 1756 – 27 November [O.S. 15 November] 1837 … December 13/26 … Due to an error in recordkeeping, this was originally thought to be the day of Herman's death … more likely … the day he was buried"; web search (orthochristian.com/88215.html) for Vinokouroff's "November 15, 1836".
- **Confidence:** medium

### B · historical · src/content/people/stefan-wyszynski.md
- **Text:** "He was arrested on 24 September 1953 and kept in isolation until 28 October 1956."
- **Problem:** The entry reproduces the Dicastery profile exactly (I opened it: "Il 24 settembre 1953, fu arrestato … Venne liberato il 28 ottobre 1956"), but those are not the dates in the historical record. Wyszyński was arrested late on the night of 25 September 1953 (after preaching at St Anne's, Warsaw); he was released from Komańcza on 26 October 1956 and returned to Warsaw on 28 October. Also "kept in isolation" until 1956 is too strong: from October 1955 he was interned at the Komańcza convent under much lighter conditions. The same sentence is repeated in the event `john-paul-ii-in-poland`.
- **Should be:** "He was arrested on the night of 25 September 1953 and interned, at first in strict isolation, until October 1956 (released 26 October, back in Warsaw on the 28th); the Dicastery's profile gives 24 September 1953 and 28 October 1956."
- **Evidence:** causesanti.va profile (opened, quoted above); en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stefan_Wyszyński (opened): "On 25 September 1953 he was imprisoned at Rywałd … He was released on 26 October 1956"; own knowledge of the Polish literature (Zapiski więzienne begin 25 IX 1953).
- **Confidence:** medium

### C · completeness · src/content/people/brigham-young.md
- **Text:** "role: President of the Twelve Apostles of the Latter-day Saints"
- **Problem:** The role and the body stop at December 1847. Young was sustained as president of the church that same month and led it for thirty years, until his death on 29 August 1877 (born 1 June 1801); he was also first governor of Utah Territory (1851–58). - **Evidence:** en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brigham_Young (opened): "June 1, 1801 – August 29, 1877 … second president … from 1847 until his death in 1877 … first governor of the Utah Territory".
- **Confidence:** high

### C · completeness · src/content/people/karl-barth.md
- **Text:** "The documents cited here do not say who drafted the theses." / role "Theologian"
- **Problem:** The entry reduces Barth to a name in the EKD's introduction. It omits what a reader most needs: that he was Swiss and Reformed (b. Basel 1886, d. 1968), that he was the principal drafter of the Barmen theses (his own repeated statement, and undisputed), that his *Epistle to the Romans* (1919, rewritten 1922) and *Church Dogmatics* made him the leading Protestant theologian of the century, and that he was dismissed from his chair at Bonn in 1935 for refusing the unconditional oath to Hitler. - **Should be:** Add dates, nationality/confession, the authorship of the Barmen draft (cite Barth's own account, e.g. his letter printed in *Karl Barth zum Kirchenkampf* or the synod minutes naming the committee Barth–Asmussen–Breit), and one sentence on *Römerbrief*/*Kirchliche Dogmatik*.
- **Evidence:** ekd.de/en/The-Barmen-Declaration-133.htm (opened; confirms the entry's quotation, names no drafter); own knowledge for the rest.
- **Confidence:** high
